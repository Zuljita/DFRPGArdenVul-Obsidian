# Survivors of the Stone

## Summary
The Survivors of the Stone are a dwarven adventuring party active in [[locations/Arden Vul.md|Arden Vul]]. They were encountered buying provisions in the [[locations/Goblin Great Hall.md|Goblin Great Hall]] in Session 32.

## Known Members
- **[[npcs/Hallsted.md|Hallsted]]** — cleric of [[npcs/Zodarrim.md|Zodarrim]]
- **Ljot** — owner of the war dog Ummchark; warned the party not to pet him without asking
- **Ummchark** ("Chisel" in Dwarvish) — their war dog

## Notes
- Their stated goal is finding "something of ours but I can't say what" — they were deliberately vague about the nature of their objective.
- Encountered in the Goblin Great Hall purchasing provisions from a merchant.
- Ljot had to call off Ummchark when Vael tried to pet the dog without asking — told him to go stand next to a goat and not eat it either.
- Hallsted offered to carry the [[items/Life-Stone.md|life-stone]] of the Corundum clan back to Kazildor if they departed before Uvash did; would look for Uvash at the House of the Gods in Gosterwick first.
