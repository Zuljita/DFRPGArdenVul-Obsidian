# Archontean Empire

## History
- Fought wars with the [[factions/Varumani.md|Varumani]].
- Fought a civil war against the [[factions/Sortians.md|Sortians]].
- The civil war against the Sortians resulted in reduced borders.

## Notes
- Controls the Great Cavern.
- A political entity with 20 Worthies (including Arden).
- The 'The Philosopher' archetype originates from this culture.
- Uses the Archontean Imperial Calendar, also known as the Ennian Calendar.
- The calendar year consists of 372 days, divided into 12 months of 30 days each, with two six-day intercalary periods.
