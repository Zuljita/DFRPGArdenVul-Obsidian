# Fungal Foresters

## Summary
Subterranean fungal creatures inhabiting a bioluminescent cave ecosystem on the second level. [[npcs/Ptarmis.md|Ptarmis]] claims familiarity and some degree of influence over them; they clearly respect him. ([[notes/Discord Summary 2025-W42.md|Discord Summary 2025-W42]])

## Description
- Inhabit a cave filled with giant fungi and other subterranean plant life, adjacent to a secret door the Foresters themselves were unaware of.
- Speak their own language; communicate via Gift of Tongues.
- Trade in mushrooms (e.g. cloud caps), though supplies are reduced because beastmen keep taking them. ([[sessions/Session 25 - Looking for the Back Door to the Forum of Set.md|Session 25]])

## Leadership & Members
- Led or represented by a "lead forester" who negotiated with the party.
- Ptarmis is described as having lived among them "like a weird Druid" and breeding giant mushrooms. ([[npcs/Ptarmis.md|Ptarmis]])

## Notes
- Their cave is adjacent to a beastman-occupied room via a hidden secret door.
- After the party left through the secret door, Ioannes warned the Foresters not to open it due to hungry beastmen on the other side.
- Many Fungal Foresters were present when the party passed through. ([[sessions/Session 36 - Rescuing Deino's Kids.md|Session 36]])
