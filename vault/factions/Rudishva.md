# Rudishva

## Culture

### Funeral Rites
According to [[Akla-Chah]], the [[factions/Rudishva.md|Rudishva]] have several funeral traditions, ranked by preference:
1. **Full Recycling:** Considered the best method, but the necessary technology is not available at the [[Beacon]].
2. **Spacing:** The second best method, also not possible at the [[Beacon]].
3. **Cremation:** The third best and most practical option available to the party, requiring a very hot fire.

### Technology and Language
- **Identity Plaques:** Also known as identity cards, ID badges, or access cards, these are Rudishva credential devices used for access control, security overrides, and system authentication throughout Rudishva installations, including the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]] and [[locations/Rudishva Bastion.md|Rudishva Bastion]]. Made of various materials such as plastic, metal, or crystalline, they feature color-coded security clearance levels:
    - **Yellow:** High-priority captain's credentials; can open iris doors, activate teleporter panels, bypass force-field controls, and pacify security constructs.
    - **Rust/Brown:** Standard crew credentials for basic access to non-secure areas and common spaces.
    - **Sky Blue:** An unknown security level mentioned in Beacon records.
    - **Silver:** A variant recovered from burned remains; exact privileges are uncertain.
    - **Oval Keycard:** A physical backup mechanism used for access.
- **Language:** A written script associated with Rudishva technology, such as identity plaques and teleportation pads. Vaelitharon Sunshadow has studied this script under the tutelage of Akla-Chah.
- **Power Discs:** Power supply discs used to activate the Rudishva glyph on devices (such as the GnuCoder 1000 Data Reader). They are available in greater and lesser sizes, and the size must match the device being powered.
- Two yellow captain's plaques were registered in the Beacon system.
- Some identity plaque variants, such as those used for the Beacon's reading room closet, are oval keycards.
