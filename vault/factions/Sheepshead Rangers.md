# Sheepshead Rangers

## Summary
A military unit of [[lore/Archontean.md|Archontean]] legionaries, five cohorts of which participated in the [[Adrienic Expedition]] to retake [[Arden Vul]] about 250 years ago.

A survivor's memoir claims the legionaries were destroyed by giant trolls wielding bolts of lightning.

## Goals
Military service for the [[lore/Archontean Empire.md|Archontean Empire]].
