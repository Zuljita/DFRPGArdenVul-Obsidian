# Imperial Tagmata

The Emperor's own guard unit. The third cohort was wiped out in Arden Vul during Adrienic's failed expedition over 200 years ago. Their insignia is a mailed fist, which has been adopted by some of the [[factions/Children of Deino.md|Children of Deino]].
