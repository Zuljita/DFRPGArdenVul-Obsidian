# Kaliyani

## Summary
A faction or group of people associated with the Kaliyani territory.
- Described as a loyal species with reptilian features, 6 limbs, intelligence, and dexterity, making them excellent science officers.
- They utilize the Forge of Creation within Arden Vul.
- Some members are considered 'betrayers' whose treason led to the crash of the *Beacon*.
