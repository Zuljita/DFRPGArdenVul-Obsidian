# Halflings

## Summary
Smallfolk with an organized gang presence in the Halls (e.g., [[npcs/Phlebotomas Plumthorn.md|Phlebotomas Plumthorn]]'s gang) and various toll collectors; active adversaries in early delves.

They are assimilated into the Archontean Empire.

Plumthorn's Halflings were recently defeated by the United Goblins, resulting in the Halls of Thoth being renamed Upper Goblintown.
