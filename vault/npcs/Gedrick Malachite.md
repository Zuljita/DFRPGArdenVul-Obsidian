# Gedrick Malachite

## Summary
A Priest of [[Zodarrim]] who warned a party member about doppelgangers.

## Notes
- He warned that doppelgangers have been showing up and that one should give every dwarf a good look over to ensure they are real.
- [[sessions/Session 14 - Behind the Waterfall Again.md|Session 14 - Behind the Waterfall Again]]
- Met [[npcs/Uvash Edzuson|Uvash]] in [[locations/Newmarket|Newmarket]].
- He is a wizard the party saved and could potentially serve as a lackey if enough gold is acquired.
- [[npcs/Uvash Edzuson|Uvash]] plans to seek grants or alms from Brother Malachite to support his new parish.
- His warning regarding doppelgangers is connected to the [[lore/War of Dwarves and Doppelgangers|War of Dwarves and Doppelgangers]].
- Uvash's planned parish in Gosterwick may seek grants or alms from him.
