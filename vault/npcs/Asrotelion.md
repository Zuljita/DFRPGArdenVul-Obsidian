# Asrotelion

## Summary
A posh wizard and Assistant Department Head from the [[College of Cinders]].

## First Appearance
He was seen at [[The Stunned Acolyte]] in [[Gosterwick]].

## Notes
- He tracked the rogue archmage [[Cerbactos]] to [[Arden Vul]].
- He believes that when he returns the priceless tomes [[Cerbactos]] stole, he will be promoted to Associate Department Head.
- He found Cerbactos by casting a `Seeker` spell.
- His departure to confront Cerbactos leaves his ultimate success or failure unknown.
- There is a lingering desire to recover his corpse should his mission end in tragedy.
- His disappearance and the loss of the books was a stain upon the honor of the College of Cinders. - [[npcs/Cerbactos.md|Cerbactos]]
- Previous attempts to locate Cerbactos and the stolen books failed due to ineffective searches by functionaries. - [[factions/College of Cinders.md|College of Cinders]]
