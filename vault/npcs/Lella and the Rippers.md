# Lella and the Rippers

## Summary
Lella and the Rippers are a group of Thorcin mercenaries led by Lella the Ripper. They came to the [[locations/Forum of Set.md|Forum of Set]] following a treasure map and became involved in the conflict against the [[factions/Cult of Set.md|Cult of Set]].

## Notes
- Led by Lella the Ripper, a Thorcin mercenary commander.
- Followed a treasure map to the area around the Forum of Set.
- Initially stopped in the Forum to gather information and supplies.
- Were disgusted by the treatment of slaves in the Forum and decided to join the fight against the Settites.
- Assisted the Right for Riches company and goblins in defeating the Cult of Set forces.
- Helped supervise the freed former slaves in the Forum after the battle.
- Had departed the Forum by the time the party returned from their trip to Gosterwick.
- Made a big score and sold some of the treasure to the merchants in the Forum.
