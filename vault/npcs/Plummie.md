# Plummie

A psychotic halfling thug (also referred to as "Plumit" or "Plum-something") who is rumored to have taken over the top level just under the [[locations/Pyramid of Thoth.md|Pyramid of Thoth]]. He reportedly refuses to let anyone pass without buying an "exploration license," and his goons are said to be armed with potent magic wands.
