# Gaius Probus

Source canonical: [[lore/The Book of Priors.md|The Book of Priors]]

## Summary

The Thothian prior who began writing [[lore/The Book of Priors.md|The Book of Priors]] in **937 AEP**, the foundational chronicle of Arden Vul and the [[Halls of Arden Vul]].

## Mentioned In

Named in the opening of [[lore/The Book of Priors.md|The Book of Priors]] as the author who started the 6-volume Mithric history, continued by a succession of Thothian priors until **1782 AEP**.
