# Clavinia

## Summary
Clavinia is a red-haired mage encountered in the [[locations/Goblintown.md|Goblintown]] market. She travels with bodyguards and deals in relics.

## Notes
- Encountered in the Goblin Market in Session 35, where she was attempting to sell relics to [[npcs/Claudine.md|Claudine]].
- Claudine declined some of her items; Vael introduced himself and purchased the items Claudine had not bought.
- She traveled with at least one bodyguard (who opened the door when the party came to talk to the wizards in their room nearby).
- Faction affiliation unknown. The wizard named **Lukor** (met in the same area, behind a secret door through ogre guards) refused to name his faction, saying his leader had not permitted him to share that information; whether Clavinia is connected to that group is unclear.
