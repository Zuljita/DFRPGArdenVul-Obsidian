# Hallsted

## Summary
Hallsted is a dwarven cleric of [[npcs/Zodarrim.md|Zodarrim]] and a member of the [[factions/Survivors of the Stone.md|Survivors of the Stone]] adventuring party, encountered in the [[locations/Goblin Great Hall.md|Goblin Great Hall]] in Session 32.

## Notes
- Encountered in the Goblin Great Hall buying provisions with the rest of the Survivors of the Stone.
- Uvash mentioned the party had found a life-stone (belonging to the Corundum clan); Hallsted didn't know them but offered to take the life-stone back to **Kazildor** if the Survivors of the Stone finished their mission and headed back that way before Uvash did. He would look for Uvash at the **House of the Gods in Gosterwick** before leaving the area.
