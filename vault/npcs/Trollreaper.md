# Trollreaper

## Summary
Trollreaper was a great goblin king of the ancient past, credited with creating the [[items/Scepter of the Goblins.md|Scepter of the Goblins]]. He is a figure of reverence among goblin culture, particularly the [[factions/United Goblin Tribes.md|United Goblin Tribes]] under [[npcs/Weskenim.md|King Weskenim]].

## Notes
- His name is not mentioned among the Varumani — [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]] said he would only show the Scepter to the goblin people when no Varumani were present, since the Scepter is made from what appears to be a Varumani legbone and Weskenim did not want to offend the Varumani Thegn.
- The Scepter of the Goblins was authenticated by Weskenim's witch doctors in Session 36 as "definitely magical" and "100% the real Scepter of the Goblins, made by the great goblin king Trollreaper."
