# Dworm

## Summary
Dworm is a guard and fighter who works for [[npcs/Galadnor.md|Galadnor]], the Half-Elven merchant and spy. He assists with security and has been tasked with managing merchant stall operations in the Goblin Forum.

## Notes
- Works as [[npcs/Galadnor.md|Galadnor]]'s guard and assistant.
- Was asked by [[npcs/Gribble.md|Gribble]] to run the food stall in the Goblin Forum until another merchant moved in. He noted that he was a fighter, not a merchant, but could keep an eye on the stall.
- Successfully sold food provided by the Right for Riches company to both goblins and Varumani ambassadors.
- Paid the company their share of the proceeds from food sales.

## Relationships
- [[npcs/Galadnor.md|Galadnor]]: His employer
