# Alexandros

## Summary
Alexandros is an aged human librarian who served the [[factions/Cult of Set.md|Cult of Set]] in the [[locations/Forum of Set.md|Forum of Set]]. He appears to have been largely oblivious to the violent conflicts around him, focused primarily on his duties organizing and maintaining the cult's collection of books.

## Notes
- Was found in the Library of [[npcs/Set.md|Set]] after the Right for Riches company defeated most of the cultists in [[sessions/Session 43c - Looting the Cult of Set.md|Session 43c]].
- Seems to have mostly missed the commotion of the battle, remaining focused on his librarian duties.
- Gave the party a list of all books available in the library.
- Is the author of "On the Location of Priscus Pulcher" - a book in his own collection.
- Can read the secret language of [[npcs/Set.md|Set]].
- Helped translate Mithric writing on a skeleton's notes containing information about many ancient nobles.
