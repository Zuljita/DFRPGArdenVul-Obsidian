# Killik

## Summary
Killik is the Goblin Boss of the [[locations/Wet Caves.md|Wet Caves]], responsible for overseeing the area and reporting to King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]]. He has interacted with the Right for Riches company on multiple occasions.

## Appears In
- [[sessions/Session 42a - Neferet.md|Session 42a - Neferet]]

## Session Notes
- Encountered the party in the Wet Caves during their transit to the Goblin Market
- Asked [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] about the whereabouts of the rest of his company
- Warned the party about unseen visitors when [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] spoke while invisible
- Appears to be a subordinate to King Weskenim but has authority in the Wet Caves area
