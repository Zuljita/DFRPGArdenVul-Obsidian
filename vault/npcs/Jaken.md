# Jaken

## Summary
An ancient warrior and companion of [[npcs/Arden.md|Arden]] and [[npcs/Vul the Sorcerer.md|Vul the Sorcerer]], from roughly 2,000 years before the current campaign era.

## Notes
- Spoke through a set of magical bones in the House of the Gods.
- Communicated in audible Mithric.
- Can be summoned at the top of a waterfall to ask where Vul made his home.
