# Astabelon

## Summary
Astabelon is the owner of [[Astabelon's Bookstore]] in [[Gosterwick]]. He offers a selection of reference materials, including single-volume encyclopedias, dictionaries for [[lore/Archontean.md|Archontean]] and [[Mithric]], grammar references, bestiaries, and herbaries.

He carries titles such as The Archontean Empire and Dickios and Janius Learn Mithric, and can select books to match a given budget.

He is aware of a field guide to mushrooms, though it is not currently in his stock.
