# Dobby

## Summary
Dobby was an Imperial Goblin thief hired by the [[factions/Cult of Set.md|Cult of Set]] to defend the Forum of Set. He attempted to backstab [[npcs/Merenuithiel Lacrymosa Armaris.md|Lacrymosa]] during the battle but was killed after his invisibility was broken.

## Appears In
- [[sessions/Session 43b - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set.md|Session 43b - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set]]

## Session Notes
- Was made invisible by Jadeel the Soulless
- Snuck through the battle to attempt a backstab on Lacrymosa
- Used Luck to survive, but was then shot to pieces by the party after becoming visible
- Worked alongside Jadeel the Soulless during the defense
