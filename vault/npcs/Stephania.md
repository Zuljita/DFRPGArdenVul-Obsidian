# Stephania

## Summary
An individual who, according to a rumor, pays well for basic supplies delivered to "The Forum".

## Notes
- Access to her is rumored to be via a basket lift on a cliff face, summoned by shining two lanterns from the left side of the river at night.
- A red-haired woman was seen briefly when a door opened.
- The High Priestess of Set, if they are the same person, is described as a hostile figure.

## Identity Ambiguity Note
⚠️ **Canonical Uncertainty**: This Stephania (supplier to The Forum) may or may not be the same person as [[npcs/Stephania, High Priestess of Set.md|Stephania, High Priestess of Set]], a major leader in the Cult of Set encountered in Session 41. Until confirmed by additional evidence, these are treated as potentially distinct individuals.
