# Varboka

## Summary
The Thegn of the Varumani — ruler of all loyal Varumani within Arden Vul and the most powerful faction leader in the Halls. Controls the [[locations/Varumani Lifts.md|Varumani Lifts]] and the Varumani Market. Admission to Varumani territory requires a [[items/Varumani Passport.md|Varumani passport]].

## Notes
- Rules all loyal Varumani of Arden Vul; the Varumani are considered the most powerful faction in the Halls.
- Hard to find much information about Varboka outside the Halls.
- The party intends to meet him; Ioannes has researched him in preparation.
