# Demma

[[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]], the "Great Nurturer," is a goddess of fertility, crops, households, childbirth, and magic. She is part of the new pantheon of gods, and the fifth day of the week, [[The Archontean Calendar#Demmasday|Demmasday]], is named in her honor.

## Mythology
Ancient myths portray [[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]] as the one who sowed the first seeds of life and ensures the continuation of the cycles of growth, harvest, and renewal. She is said to have woven the world's fertility into the land itself, her touch bringing forth golden fields, strong children, and thriving households. It is believed she stole the secret of magic from the void and planted it within the soil and stars, granting mortals the ability to shape the world through wisdom and will.

Though her blessings are gentle, her wrath is unyielding. When her gifts are hoarded, abused, or defiled, she strikes with famine, blight, and barrenness. Her sacred weapon is the flail, symbolizing both the harvest and divine retribution. To understand her will is to understand the balance of nature, hearth, and spirit.

## Theology & Worship
The theology of [[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]] centers on balance, stewardship, and the sacred duty of cultivation—whether of land, family, or knowledge. She teaches that fertility and abundance are gifts given only to those who nurture them with wisdom and hard work; to plant without care, hoard without sharing, or wield magic recklessly is to invite ruin.

Her followers are called to protect the cycles of nature, strengthen households, and guide new life. Magic is viewed as a seed of creation—a natural force to be cultivated responsibly rather than an unnatural power. True wisdom is believed to come from action, not just study. Worshipers often bring offerings of seeds, bread, and woven charms to honor her blessings, and gilded crops are frequently used as a form of sacred art by her devotees.

## The Priesthood of [[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]]
The priesthood is hierarchical yet practical, structured to emphasize stewardship and active service rather than rigid authority.

### Clergy and Vestments
The standard attire for [[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]]'s clergy consists of a long tunic in earthy tones (deep greens, golden yellows, or rich browns) with intricate seed-like embroidery along the hems and cuffs. Over this, they wear a sleeveless outer robe or mantle in deep red or russet, representing the life-giving blood of birth and the warmth of the hearth. A broad, gold-threaded sash or belt is worn at the waist, often adorned with woven charms, seed pouches, or small flail-shaped talismans. For ceremonial occasions, high-ranking priests may don a hooded cloak fastened with a bronze or gold brooch shaped like a seed. The vestments are designed to be practical for labor, reinforcing the belief that faith is meant to be lived through work.

### Branches of the Faith
- **[[factions/Seedkeepers|Seedkeepers]]:** A scholarly, more esoteric branch of the priesthood that guards ancient agricultural knowledge, sacred fertility rites, and studies the interplay between magic and the natural world.
- **[[factions/Harvest Guardians|Harvest Guardians]]:** The dedicated warrior arm of the faith. They defend temples and enforce [[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]]'s will, wielding the flail. Each major temple typically maintains a contingent of six to twelve fighters trained in flail combat.
- A temple dedicated to her is located on the Hill of the Gods.
- Local temples are led by a Chief Priest, such as Vivian in Gosterwick, who oversees a council of four to eight assisting clerics responsible for daily rituals, agricultural blessings, and midwifery.

## Notable Followers
- **[[pcs/Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios|Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios]]:** A player character and a scholarly, Archontean cleric of [[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]]. His personal attire is more ornate than that of the rural clergy, consisting of a deep blue tunic with golden embroidery and a red woolen cloak fastened with a bronze brooch.
- **[[npcs/Vivian|Vivian]]:** The stern but fair Thorcin chief priest of the [[locations/Temple of Demma|Temple of Demma]] in [[locations/Gosterwick|Gosterwick]]. He is a practical leader who prioritizes aid to the rural poor and is capable of casting powerful spells, such as *Remove Curse*.

## Associated Locations
- **[[locations/Temple of Demma|Temple of Demma]]:** The temple in [[locations/Gosterwick|Gosterwick]] is a modest but well-maintained structure that serves as a center for healing, wisdom, and rural devotion. It is a frequent destination for the [[factions/Right for Riches Company|Right for Riches Company]] to receive healing and find refuge. The temple contains areas of 'Continual Light' and provides services at a 'book rate' under certain conditions.
- **Demmasday Market:** The central market in [[locations/Gosterwick|Gosterwick]] is held on Totsday and all of [[The Archontean Calendar#Demmasday|Demmasday]], with the latter being the largest market of the week.

## History & Key Events
[[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]]'s influence is felt frequently in the party's adventures, primarily through her cleric, [[pcs/Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios|Ioannes]]. The [[locations/Temple of Demma|Temple in Gosterwick]] has proven to be a crucial resource, providing vital services like curse removal on multiple occasions.

An early incident at the [[locations/Yellow Cloak Inn|Yellow Cloak Inn]], where an accidental *Continual Darkness* spell was cast on some bedsheets, resulted in clerics of [[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]] being forbidden from casting spells on the premises. The party also regularly visits the Demmasday market in town to procure supplies.
