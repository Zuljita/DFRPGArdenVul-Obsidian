# Ida and Odile

## Summary
Ida and Odile are twin female gamblers who frequent the [[locations/Arena.md|Arena]] on the Arena Level of the Halls of Arden Vul.

## Notes
- Ioannes noticed them in the gamblers' area after a Spectacle Day match and tried to ask them questions about the Arena Lord. After a few minutes, they grew tired of answering his questions and brushed him off.
