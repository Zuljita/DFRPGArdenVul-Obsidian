# Second Overseer

## Summary
Assistant to [[Kel Bormol]], Overseer of [[Thoth]]. He is described as cowardly for retreating before a [[factions/Sortians.md|Sortian]] onslaught and is currently cowering within the [[Tower of Scrutiny]].

## First Appearance
Mentioned in a message from [[Uriel Basileon]] to [[Kel Bormol]].

## Disposition
Cowardly.

## Known Associates
- [[Kel Bormol]] (his superior)
- [[Uriel Basileon]] (Archon)

## Notes
[[Archon Uriel Basileon]] intends to "smoke him out of his lair" and deliver him for punishment once conditions improve.
- A messenger from [[Hithra Paleologos]] has recently been seen entering the Tower of Scrutiny.
- An ancient letter from Uriel Basileon to Kel Bormol says he cowered within the Tower of Scrutiny after retreating before the Sortian onslaught, refusing entry to all. ([[notes/Discord Summary 2026-W00.md|Discord Summary 2026-W00]])
