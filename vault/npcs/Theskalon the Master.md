# Theskalon the Master

## Summary
- Honored figure associated with the Tomb of Theskalon; artifacts recovered nearby. Exact biography unknown.
- A found note identifies him as being part of the [[Cult of Set]].
- A figure who used to protect the Howling Caves.

## Appears In
- [[sessions/Session 30 - The Tomb of Theskalon.md|Session 30 - The Tomb of Theskalon]]

## Notes
- His tomb is located to the east of the [[Southern Necropolis of Set]].
- Intact statues found in the tomb include "Licinia the Seer", "Plotinus Quartus Claudino, Archon", "Yeth, Who Sought to Bring Law to Chaos", and "Horatius Interfector, Sorcerer."
- The Thane did not dare to enter the Howling Caves while The Master was there.
- A headless statue found in the tomb was "Thrygga the Mighty".
