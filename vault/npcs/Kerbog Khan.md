# Kerbog Khan

## Summary
Kerbog Khan is a small bipedal construct of unknown origin encountered in the [[locations/Goblintown.md|Goblin Market]]. He claims to possess knowledge of the Halls of Arden Vul surpassing that of anyone else living, and seeks to trade information — but only under very specific terms.

He has also been described as an evil sorcerer.

## Appearance
- Small bipedal construct (not humanoid-sized).
- Exact appearance not further described.

## Known Behavior
- **"All Deals Must Be Sealed in Blood"** — requires a literal blood pact from anyone he does business with.
- **"Principals only"** — insists on dealing with the decision-maker directly, not their guards or proxies. When a Thorcin merchant offered his guard's blood instead of his own, Kerbog Khan rejected this.
- Claims to know more about the Halls of Arden Vul than anyone else living.

## First Encounter (Session 42a)
Kerbog Khan was observed in the Goblin Market attempting to negotiate with Thorcin merchants. The merchant was unwilling to give a blood sample and tried to have his guard deal with the construct, but Kerbog Khan refused on "principals only" grounds. [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] noticed the encounter but was busy at the time and did not engage.

## Notes
- Potentially a significant source of information about the Halls if the party is willing to deal on his terms.
- His origins and true nature are unknown.
- Wanted by one of the [[College of Cinders|Collegium]].
