# Kerbog Khan

## Summary
Kerbog Khan is a small bipedal construct and self-described archmage, of unknown origin, operating deep within [[locations/Goblintown.md|Arden Vul]]. He claims knowledge of the Halls surpassing that of anyone else living, maintains a fortress near a Rudishva surgical bay, and commands an array of constructs including combat constructs, a Surgical Construct, and clockwork dragonfly scouts. He is also described as an evil sorcerer.

He kidnapped [[npcs/Gevorda.md|Gevorda]], the sister of [[npcs/Varboka.md|Varboka the Varumani Thegn]], and subjected her to unauthorized medical experiments. As of Session 55, the [[factions/Right for Riches Company.md|Right for Riches Company]] has accepted a formal kill contract from Varboka and is actively hunting the Khan. He is the party's primary quest target.

He is additionally wanted by the [[factions/College of Cinders.md|College of Cinders]] (the Collegium).

## Appearance
- Small bipedal construct (not humanoid-sized).
- Exact physical appearance not further described in person; typically interacts remotely through dragonfly constructs.

## Known Behavior
- **"All Deals Must Be Sealed in Blood"** — requires a literal blood pact from anyone he does business with.
- **"Principals only"** — insists on dealing with the decision-maker directly, not their guards or proxies. When a Thorcin merchant offered his guard's blood instead of his own, the Khan rejected this.
- Claims to know more about the Halls of Arden Vul than anyone else living.
- Offers information, spells, and even teleportation as trade goods.
- Demonstrates knowledge of the Rudishva language — Vael tested him by speaking Rudishva in Session 52a, and the Khan responded appropriately.
- Deploys dragonfly constructs as scouts and remote communication relays; he speaks through them to parties he encounters.
- Actively spies on areas of interest: his dragonfly constructs are known to surveil the Cloister area and the Temple of Set caverns.
- Willing to kidnap individuals and conduct non-consensual medical experiments on them.
- His constructs are known as popular visitors to the Varumani Market — he appears to maintain at least nominally civil relations with the Varumani as a cover.

## History

### Session 42a — First Sighting, Goblin Market
Kerbog Khan was observed in the [[locations/Goblintown.md|Goblin Market]] attempting to negotiate with Thorcin merchants. The merchant was unwilling to give a blood sample and tried to have his guard substitute; the Khan refused on "principals only" grounds. [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] noticed the encounter but was occupied and did not engage.

### Session 52a — Dragonfly Contact, Cloister Approach
As the party emerged from a secret passage near the Temple of Set, they were spotted by a clockwork dragonfly construct. The dragonfly flew to them, and the Khan spoke through it. He congratulated the party for revealing a secret door he had not previously known about. He offered information, spells, and teleportation — "all deals must be sealed in blood" — and identified himself as an archmage. Vael tested him in Rudishva; the Khan appeared to understand. The party did not deal with him and departed.

Separately, the party later noticed a dragonfly construct hovering in one of the northern lobes of the cavern near the Temple of Set, actively watching them. Concerned about the Khan's spies finding their approach to the Cloister, they deliberately took an alternate route to avoid it.

### Sessions 52b/53 — Surgical Construct and Rescue of Gevorda
The party discovered a Rudishva surgical chamber deep within a perfectly-bored passage south of the Cloister level. Inside, [[npcs/Gevorda.md|Gevorda]] — a Varumani warrior and sister of the Thegn — was strapped to a table and encased in medicaderm gel, with Khan's multi-armed Surgical Construct operating on her. Two Invisible Stalkers guarded the room.

The party fought and defeated both Invisible Stalkers and the Surgical Construct. [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] used the room's Rudishva consoles and [[npcs/Akla-Chah.md|Akla-Chah]]'s guidance to safely release Gevorda from the gel. Upon waking, Gevorda immediately declared her intent to kill the Khan. She confirmed that she had come to negotiate with him; he had knocked her unconscious with a spell, kidnapped her, and had his construct perform medical experiments on her without consent.

[[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] salvaged the wrecked Surgical Construct and brought it to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]].

### Session 55 — Kill Contract, Dragonfly in the Cloister
In the slime-pit chamber south of the Cloister, [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] spotted one of the Khan's dragonfly constructs watching and waiting. When Vael spoke to it, the Khan's voice emerged. The Khan expressed pleasure that the party had led his dragonflies to that secret area. Vael told the Khan not to follow them; the dragonfly did not visibly pursue, but the encounter confirmed the Khan is actively mapping the party's routes.

Later that session, the party met with [[npcs/Varboka.md|Varboka the Thegn]], who formally commissioned them to kill Kerbog Khan. See [[#Kill Contract]] below.

## Threat Assessment
- **Archmage-level caster**: The Khan is described as an archmage and evil sorcerer. Gevorda herself — a Varumani warrior — acknowledged he was dangerous enough that she needed more equipment before confronting him. He is capable of knocking opponents unconscious with a spell at minimum.
- **Fortress**: The Khan maintains a fortress near the surgical bay where Gevorda was held, location not yet precisely mapped.
- **Construct army**: Commands numerous constructs, including combat constructs. Varboka warned the party to expect heavy construct defenses.
- **Dragonfly scouts**: Multiple clockwork dragonfly constructs used for scouting and remote communication. At least one was operating in the Cloister area as recently as Session 55.
- **Surgical Construct**: The one encountered in Sessions 52b/53 was defeated, but the Khan may have additional such constructs.
- **Invisible Stalkers**: Deployed two as guards in the surgical bay; both were defeated by the party.
- **Intelligence and awareness**: The Khan is actively mapping the party's movements and aware they destroyed his constructs. Varboka noted the Khan probably considers the party a target as well.
- **Rudishva knowledge**: Understands the Rudishva language and presumably their technology; maintains a large stash of Rudishva power supplies.
- **Wanted by the Collegium**: The [[factions/College of Cinders.md|College of Cinders]] seeks him for unknown reasons, suggesting additional criminal or rogue arcane activity.

## Kill Contract

### Commissioning Party
[[npcs/Varboka.md|Varboka]], Thegn of the Varumani, commissioned the [[factions/Right for Riches Company.md|Right for Riches Company]] in Session 55 to kill Kerbog Khan. Varboka stated that varumani politics made it a bad time to declare war directly — the Khan's constructs are popular visitors to the Varumani Market — and so he preferred to have outsiders carry out the execution.

### Terms
- **Proof of death**: The Khan's head must be delivered to Varboka.
- **Primary reward**: The party may select **one item** from Varboka's personal hoard. Confirmed items in the hoard include:
  - Rod of Resurrection
  - Girdle of Strength
  - Magic armor and weapons (sized for non-varumani)
  - Rudishva relics, including a Sky Sled
  - Other items not yet enumerated
  - Before selecting, Varboka will allow the party to personally analyze the items for magical potency.
- **Secondary condition**: Varboka requests **half of any Rudishva power supplies** found on the Khan's person or in his possession.
- **Additional benefit**: Varboka's friendship. Varboka noted the Khan likely considers the party a target already, making this a convergent interest.

### Party's Location Plan
The party intends to cast Seeker on [[npcs/Asrotelion.md|Asrotelion]] — a mage from the [[factions/College of Cinders.md|College of Cinders]] who was sent into Arden Vul tracking the rogue archmage [[npcs/Cerbactos.md|Cerbactos]], who was believed to be heading toward the Khan's area — in order to locate Asrotelion and thereby triangulate the Khan's position. Asrotelion's fate after his departure into the Halls is unknown.

## Notes
- Khan has confirmed knowledge of at least one secret door near the Temple of Set caverns (the party inadvertently revealed it in Session 52a).
- Described as both a construct and an evil sorcerer — the exact nature of this dual identity is unresolved.
- Reports suggest he kidnaps Varumani (and possibly others) for unauthorized medical experiments; Gevorda's case confirms this.
- His constructs are welcome in the Varumani Market, suggesting he has cultivated a public-facing relationship with the Varumani even while victimizing them.
- Varboka suspects the Khan holds a large stash of Rudishva power supplies.
- The destroyed Surgical Construct is at the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]]; it may yield intelligence on the Khan's methods or technology.
