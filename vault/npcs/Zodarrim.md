# Zodarrim

[[npcs/Zodarrim.md|Zodarrim]] is the sole and central deity in modern Dwarven monotheistic religion.

## Theology
The faith of [[npcs/Zodarrim.md|Zodarrim]] is a monotheistic religion practiced by dwarves. It appears to be the dominant, if not sole, religious institution for the dwarven people.
- A sacred text exists that Uvash plans to include in his church ([[notes/Holy Book of Zorradrim.md|Holy Book of Zodarrim]]).
- Uvash discusses the deity with his dwarven faithful in the [[notes/House of the Gods.md|House of the Gods]] in [[locations/Gosterwick|Gosterwick]].
- Uvash has attempted to convert humans to dwarven monotheism.
- Uvash has been observed attempting to convert humans to the dwarven monotheistic faith.
- [[sessions/Session 15 - The Great Cavern Re-Revisited.md|Session 15]] — Uvash attempted to convert humans to dwarven monotheism.
## The Clergy of [[npcs/Zodarrim.md|Zodarrim]]
Several clerics of [[npcs/Zodarrim.md|Zodarrim]] have been encountered by the party, suggesting an organized priesthood.

- **[[pcs/Uvash Edzuson|Uvash Edzuson]]:** A member of the [[factions/Right for Riches Company|Right for Riches Company]], Uvash is a devout cleric of [[npcs/Zodarrim.md|Zodarrim]] and has been seen actively attempting to convert others, such as [[npcs/Torunn the Smith|Torunn the Smith]], to his faith. He has successfully conducted theological outreach, converting two dwarves and one human to the faith ([[notes/Discord Summary 2026-W05.md|Discord Summary 2026-W05]]).
- **[[npcs/Gedrick Malachite|Gedrick Malachite]]:** A Priest of [[npcs/Zodarrim.md|Zodarrim]] whom Uvash met in [[locations/Newmarket|Newmarket]]. He warned Uvash to be wary of doppelgangers, a threat connected to the [[lore/War of Dwarves and Doppelgangers|War of Dwarves and Doppelgangers]].
- **[[npcs/Hallsted|Hallsted]]:** A cleric of [[npcs/Zodarrim.md|Zodarrim]] and a member of the dwarven adventuring party known as the [[factions/Survivors of the Stone|Survivors of the Stone]]. He offered to take a life-stone found by the party back to the dwarven city of **Kazildor**.
