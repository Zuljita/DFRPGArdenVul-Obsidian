# Craastonistorex

## Summary
Craastonistorex (known as **Craas**) is a huge green dragon who lairs in the ruins of [[locations/Arden Vul.md|Arden Vul]] and considers himself the apex predator of the area. He has at least one pet wyvern. He has negotiated an arrangement with the Right for Riches company to serve as "Wardens of the Dam" in exchange for regular tribute.

## Disposition
Hostile and extremely dangerous, but willing to negotiate if it serves his interests. Prone to sarcasm and condescension when displeased.

## Tribute Arrangement
The Right for Riches company agreed to serve as **Wardens of the Dam** in exchange for Craas's non-interference. They leave tribute at the top of the dam when passing through.

### Session 35 — First Tribute Attempt
- The party was coming up short on treasure due to the apartment burglary.
- They left a **single earring** (Vallium intended to split a pair, giving one to Craas), plus contributions of coins from all party members.
- Vael included an apology note explaining the meager tribute was due to the robbery.

### Session 36 — Tribute Rejected
After the next delve, the party found a sack waiting for them on top of the dam, containing a **single earring** (returned) and a **large pile of small bones** — the skeletal remains of about a banda (unit) of beastmen.
- The accompanying note from Craas mocked the tribute in detail:
  - Called the earring **"a single earring that someone's ex-girlfriend left in their apartment"**
  - Complained about **"chess pieces with no chess board"** (pieces from the Tower of the Ape that Vallium had thrown in as tribute)
  - Dismissed **"small piles of tiny little modern coins"** as unacceptable
  - Declared this their **"one more chance"** — he was giving them another opportunity only because eating them would require training new Wardens of the Dam
  - Told them to **tell [[npcs/Deino.md|Deino]] to resume paying her tribute or he would eat more of her children**; noted "the piggies tasted the best"
- Vael wrote an **insincere apology note** and put it back in the sack.
- A **wyvern** was spotted flying far to the southwest shortly after — presumably Craas's pet, the delivery agent.
- The party gathered the beastman bones and returned them to [[npcs/Count Skleros.md|Count Skleros]].

## Notes
- An account from approximately 50 years ago details an encounter between the dragon and a party of 12 of Lord Burdock's men-at-arms, where he killed 10 of them. This event led Lord Burdock to forbid exploration of the ruins.
- **Rumor:** The dragon rules the surface ruins and extorts a toll on those he catches exiting the Halls.
- **Rumor:** He is rumored to be the son of a more ancient wyrm that lives at the bottom of the [[locations/Great Chasm.md|Great Chasm]].

## Relationships
- **Wyvern**: A pet wyvern that acts as a scout and delivery agent.
- **PCs ("Wardens of the Dam")**: Negotiated arrangement trading tribute for limited non-interference.
- **[[npcs/Deino.md|Deino]]**: Deino was paying Craas tribute; he has been eating her beastmen children when she fails to pay.
