# Razor Face

## Summary
Razor Face is a male human gambler who frequents the [[locations/Arena.md|Arena]] on the Arena Level of the Halls of Arden Vul.

## Appearance
- Heavily scarred face with no nose or ears.
- Compensates with a large beard.

## Notes
- Ioannes spoke to him in the gamblers' area after a Spectacle Day match, trying to learn about the Arena Lord. Razor Face did not provide much useful information.
