# Kel Bormol

## Summary
Kel Bormol was the Overseer of [[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]] at the time of the collapse of Arden Vul (approximately 1200 years ago). He also held the titles of Archmage of Thoth and Eye of Thoth. He received a message from [[npcs/Archon Uriel Basileon.md|Archon Uriel Basileon]] concerning a cowardly Second Overseer who retreated before a Sortian onslaught.

## Notes
- **Chess Diary**: A book titled "Kel Bormol's Chess Diary" (in Mithric) was found on the work table in the teleporter room of the [[locations/Tower of the Ape.md|Tower of the Ape]] in Session 34a. Most of the diary records chess games from approximately 1100 years ago, written in Mithric. The last few pages appear to be records of some kind of experiments, written in Archontean and in a different handwriting — suggesting the book was used or annotated by someone else after Kel Bormol.
- Received a letter from [[npcs/Archon Uriel Basileon.md|Archon Uriel Basileon]] indicating that the Archon intended to "smoke out" the Second Overseer (his assistant, who had cowered in the [[locations/Tower of Scrutiny.md|Tower of Scrutiny]] during a Sortian onslaught) and deliver him to Kel Bormol for punishment once conditions improved.
