# Helena

## Summary
Helena is an accountant for the [[factions/Cult of Set.md|Cult of Set]], found working in her office during the Right for Riches company's raid on the Forum of Set. She surrendered her keys and ledger books when confronted.

## Appears In
- [[sessions/Session 43c - Looting the Cult of Set.md|Session 43c - Looting the Cult of Set]]

## Session Notes
- Found working behind her desk in an office/apartment in the Forum of Set
- After some intimidation ("sword-waving"), she gave up her keys and ledger books
- Was sent to join the other former slaves in the Forum
- Her ledgers likely contained financial records of the Cult's operations
- Had a significant speaking role.
