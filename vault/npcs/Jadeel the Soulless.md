# Jadeel the Soulless

## Summary
Jadeel the Soulless was an Archontean wizard hired by the [[factions/Cult of Set.md|Cult of Set]] to help defend the Forum of Set against the Right for Riches company. She was killed during the battle.

## Appears In
- [[sessions/Session 43b - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set.md|Session 43b - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set]]

## Session Notes
- Cast Invisibility on Dobby the thief to allow him to sneak behind enemy lines
- Used Blink to escape when pursued by [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]]
- Cast Command spell causing Vallium to face-plant
- Was eventually killed by arrows after exhausting her blocking spells
- Had "Soulless" as an epithet, suggesting a connection to soulless magic or state
