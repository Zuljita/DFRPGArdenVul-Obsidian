# Thrainor "Thronebreaker" Ironvein

## Summary
Thrainor "Thronebreaker" Ironvein is a Dwarven mercenary fighter affiliated with the [[factions/Grudge Brigade.md|Grudge Brigade]]. He was hired (along with [[npcs/Lasselanta Chrysalis Ashcroft.md|Chrysalis]]) by the Right for Riches company for the battle against [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] in Sessions 42a–42b. Hiring two Grudge Brigade mercenaries for the same mission was against policy, but business was slow during the winter and Coinbase was out of town.

## Known Abilities
- **Power Blow** — used to knock sarcophagus lids off with extreme force.
- High Will score — resisted wraith ranged attacks that felled more magically-inclined party members.
- Can interact with force spheres; discovered they could be pushed around the room by pressing against them.
- Uses a meteoric axe to cut through things.
- Can facilitate quick exits using well-timed Intimidate checks.

## Actions in Sessions 42a–42b

### Session 42a — Neferet (Part 1)
- Blessed by [[pcs/Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios.md|Ioannes]] before entering.
- Went first into the narrow dispel hallway; his Continual Light and Bless spells were dispelled, but he continued.
- Used Power Blow to knock the lid off the sarcophagus in the northern alcove, releasing a lesser mummy.
- Fought the lesser mummy and shadows alongside the party.
- Trapped in a Bead of Force sphere with Uvash and Mariposa. Discovered the sphere could be pushed.
- Tried to push the force sphere toward Neferet to pin her against a wall.
- Tried breaking the dome by force — could not penetrate it but could move it.
- Pursued Neferet down the hallway after being freed.

### Session 42b — Neferet and the Wraiths
- Ran down the hall after Neferet following her escape through the Dispel corridor.
- Bless spells survived due to excellent rolls by Ioannes.
- Forced open the spiked western door in the Tower of Scrutiny.
- Stood in the front line against the wraiths; resisted their ranged Will-based attacks effectively due to his high Will.
- Suffered multiple wraith attacks but remained standing ("implacable").
- Retreated down the stairs with the party after Uvash and Vael were badly wounded.
