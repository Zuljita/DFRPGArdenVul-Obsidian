# Hal

## Summary
Hal is a Varumani bartender who runs the Corner Bar at the [[locations/Arena.md|Arena]] on the Arena Level of the Halls of Arden Vul.

## Notes
- Sells drinks and food at the Arena Corner Bar. Sold an ale called Hak's (Session 32).
- On Spectacle Days, he encourages patrons to get drinks, place their bets, and take their seats for the show.
- Does not stock milk. When Ioannes asked, he offered water instead.
- Sold mushroom cakes as a food option.
