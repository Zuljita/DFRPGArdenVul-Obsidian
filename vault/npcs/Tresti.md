# Tresti Iredell

## Summary
Tresti Iredell is a Half-Elven mage who was originally encountered as a prisoner in Gerrilad's dungeon and rescued by the party. She later became a wizard working at the [[factions/Rarities Factor.md|Rarities Factor]] in [[locations/Gosterwick.md|Gosterwick]], working for [[npcs/Wicktrimmer.md|Wicktrimmer]]. She subleases one of the Right for Riches company's apartments at the Arcane Practitioners' Club and provides informal security monitoring in exchange.

## History

### Prisoner (Early Sessions)
- Rescued from [[npcs/Gerrilad.md|Gerrilad]]'s prison by the party in Session 16.
- Her spellbook was found in the Library of Thoth (Session 21) and eventually returned to her (Session 45).
- Was a member of the Second Chance Company.

### Rarities Factor Employee (Sessions 38+)
- Wicktrimmer inquired about hiring her in Session 38; the Right for Riches company gave her a positive assessment.
- She now works for Wicktrimmer at the Rarities Factor.
- In Session 39, she approached the party about subleasing one of their four apartments at the Arcane Practitioners' Club. They had taken all four when they became available, jumping her place in line. They agreed to sublease one apartment in exchange for utility spellcasting services (Message spell monitoring, etc.).
- **Apartment monitoring**: Tresti is usually home and agreed to use the Message spell to alert the party if she detects any intruders in their apartment area. This was valuable after the Jarnno the False apartment break-in.
- She later sent a Message to Ioannes when Onyx the cat arrived at the Rarities Factor (Session 41), saying she'd check the Arcane Practitioners' Club.
- Has been encountered at [[locations/Muddy Puddle.md|Muddy Puddle]] as part of the Second Chance Company.

## Services Available
- Utility spellcasting (Message spells, monitoring)
- Apartment security watch via Message spell alerts
