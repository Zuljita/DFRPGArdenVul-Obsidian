# Killick

## Summary
[[npcs/Killick.md|Killick]] is a goblin leader, known as the "Big Boss of the [[locations/Wet Caves.md|Wet Caves]]."

He reports to King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]] and has interacted with the [[factions/Right for Riches Company.md|Right for Riches company]] on multiple occasions.

## Appears In
- [[sessions/Session 23c - Set Jailbreak and Down to Goblintown.md|Session 23c - Set Jailbreak and Down to Goblintown]]
- [[sessions/Session 26 - The Scouring of the Shire.md|Session 26 - The Scouring of the Shire]]
- [[sessions/Session 32 - Fast Exploration.md|Session 32 - Fast Exploration]]

## Notes
- He can provide directions to the [[Troll Lifts]].
- He granted the party safe passage through his territory to reach [[Gog]]'s cave.
- Led the party through a confusing maze of passages in [[locations/Goblintown.md|Goblintown]].

## Relationships
- [[Gog]]: neighboring contact; facilitates access to the hermit’s grotto.
- Goblin leadership (e.g., [[Weskenim]], [[Bottleneck]]): operates within broader goblin structures but controls his own turf.

## Additional Appearances
- [[sessions/Session 39 - Diving for the Yellow Card.md|Session 39 - Diving for the Yellow Card]]
- [[sessions/Session 42a - Neferet.md|Session 42a - Neferet]]

## Session Notes
- Encountered the party in the Wet Caves during their transit to the Goblin Market.
- Asked [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] about the whereabouts of the rest of his company.
- Warned the party about unseen visitors when [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] spoke while invisible.
- Appears to be a subordinate to King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]] but has authority in the Wet Caves area.
