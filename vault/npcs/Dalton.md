# Dalton

## Summary
Leader of [[factions/Dalton's Darlings.md|Dalton's Darlings]], a delving company active around the [[Hall of Judgment]] and adjacent levels.

## Notes
- **Session 32 — Stocks at the Arena**: Found with his entire group in punishment stocks near the Arena Lord's throne. They had fought an arena battle the previous day under the **no-magic rule**. Isidor the halfling was injured during the fight and reflexively drank a healing potion — the Arena Lord ruled this cheating, awarded the win to the goblins they were fighting, and sentenced Dalton's Darlings to a few days in the stocks (rather than execution, as it was a first offense and they were "stupid surfacers"). An arena guard prevented further conversation ("no talking to the prisoners").
