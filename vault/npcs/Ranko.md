# Ranko

## Summary
Ranko is a giant four-armed baboon who controls territory in the caverns south of the [[locations/Howling Caves.md|Howling Caves]], between the Howling Caves and the exit to the [[locations/Cliff Face.md|Cliff Face]]. [[npcs/Yamki.md|Yamki]] warned the party about him by name before they entered his territory.

## Notes
- Aggressive and appeared ready to fight when the Right for Riches company encountered him and his followers.
- The party bribed him and his followers with the meat they were carrying (stored in Larel's Sack), which satisfied him enough to allow safe passage through his territory.
- Distinct from [[npcs/Inkor.md|Inkor]], another four-armed baboon encountered in the same cave system (Inkor presides over a 40' cliff to the south).
