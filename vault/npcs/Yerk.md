# Yerk

## Summary
Yerk is a human gladiator at the [[locations/Arena.md|Arena]] in the Halls of Arden Vul. He is currently ranked **#3** among the Arena's gladiators and is a crowd favorite.

## Description
- Known for extreme efficiency in combat rather than showmanship — while his fellow gladiators spun, kicked, and twirled their weapons for the crowd, Yerk simply closed with the enemy and brutally killed them as fast as possible.
- Described by a goblin spectator as "a total psycho."

## Known Feats
- During a Spectacle Day witnessed in [[sessions/Session 46 - The Arena Lord and the Inn of the Lost.md|Session 46]], Yerk personally killed four out of six giant beetles in a single match (the other two gladiators killed one each).
