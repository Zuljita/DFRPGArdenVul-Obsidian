# Thegan White-Mane

## Summary
Thegan White-Mane is the proprietor of the [[locations/Inn of the Lost.md|Inn of the Lost]] (also known as the House of Isocorax) on the Arena Level of the Halls of Arden Vul. He is an old man with white hair.

## Notes
- Greeted Ioannes when he entered the inn, offering private rooms, bunkroom beds, and food and drink.
- The inn had no other guests in the common room during Ioannes's visit — the barkeeper explained they were probably all at the Arena for Spectacle Day.
