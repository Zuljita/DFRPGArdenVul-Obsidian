# Neferet

## Summary
A powerful greater mummy spellcaster encountered in a hidden tomb complex off the [[Great Hall]]. She relied on Blink, darkness, invisibility beads, and force beads to split and evade the party.

## Notes
- Fought the Right for Riches Company across a multi-phase engagement.
- Retreated through a dispel corridor and fled toward the [[Tower of Scrutiny]].
- Demonstrated high mobility and deception even when pressured and low on resources.
