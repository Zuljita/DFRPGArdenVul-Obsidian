# Ashe Maykum

## Summary
Ashe Maykum (nickname: **"GOAT"**) is a goblin mercenary druid affiliated with the [[factions/Grudge Brigade.md|Grudge Brigade]]. He was hired by the Right for Riches company for Sessions 31 and 32 after the departure of Michael J. Dundee. He is accompanied by a summoned bat named **[[npcs/Camilla.md|Camilla]]**.

## Abilities
- **Druid spellcasting**: Shape Earth, Walk on Air (cast on others), Water Vision, Levitate, Summon Animals, Mass Daze.
- **Summon Animals**: Can call bears, bat swarms, wild boar, hammerhead sharks. Note: **cave bears require Nature's Sanctity**, which is weak underground — he could not summon them inside the Beacon area.
- **Camilla**: A bat companion/familiar used for scouting; magically relays visual images back to Ashe Maykum.

## Actions

### Session 31 — Beacon Discovery
- Levitated up the Cliff Face with Vael (rather than climbing) to save time.
- Used **Water Vision** to scout the lake near the ruins, spotting the wrecked barge (~50' down), a huge snapping turtle, and many crocodiles.
- Summoned **two hammerhead sharks** to lure the turtle away from the barge so Vallium could dive in and loot it.
- Sent **Camilla** ahead to scout the bronze doors at the bottom of the Beacon stairway.
- Cast multiple **Shape Earth** spells (along with Vael) to build a stone cover over the Beacon stairway entrance, then threw dirt and sticks on top to camouflage it.
- Cast **Walk on Air** on Vallium and Ioannes to help them cross the swamp to the Star Obelisk faster.

### Session 32 — Exploration
- Levitated with Vael to move between levels.
- When ambushed by vanishing earth elementals, recovered quickly and fired arrows into likely hiding spots (along with Lacrymosa).
- Used **Gift of Tongues** from Ioannes to allow Vael to speak to the Varumani miner in the fungal forest cavern.
- Was cautious about sending summoned bears across the narrow iron footbridge (unsure of their weight capacity).
