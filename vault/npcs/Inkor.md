# Inkor

## Summary
Inkor is a giant four-armed baboon who presides over a 40-foot cliff in the caverns south of the [[locations/Howling Caves.md|Howling Caves]]. He carries a huge bow and commands a group of smaller baboons in the area.

## Notes
- When the Right for Riches company approached, he gave them a chance to explain their business before attacking.
- He consulted with the smaller baboons under his watch, then let the party pass after they explained they were looking for [[npcs/Bobo.md|Bobo]] and [[npcs/Bifki.md|Bifki]] on behalf of [[npcs/Yamki.md|Yamki]].
- Distinct from [[npcs/Ranko.md|Ranko]], another four-armed baboon further north in the same cave system who is more aggressive and requires food tribute.
