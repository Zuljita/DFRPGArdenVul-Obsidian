# Choumnos

## Summary
A character who formerly owned a tower in Arden Vul and was once enslaved.

He is currently the Castellan of Pol.

His former tower contains a teleporter bank connecting to the Astral Plane, the City of Brass, Archontos, and the roof of the Tower of Scrutiny.

The tower also features an alchemy lab and a high-tech chamber attached to a Rudishva power supply.

A lower level of the tower contains bookshelves with spellbooks belonging to Choumnos, Gemma, and Pol.

The top floor also contains a chest containing several magical items that Choumnos described as dangerous.

His recent enslavement was facilitated by the use of the Collar of Servitude.
