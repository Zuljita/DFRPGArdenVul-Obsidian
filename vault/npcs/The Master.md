# The Master

## Summary
A figure who used to protect the [[Howling Caves]].

He is an honored figure associated with the Tomb of Theskalon, where artifacts have been recovered nearby.

Exact biography unknown.

## First Appearance
Mentioned in [[sessions/Session 33 - Nyema.md|Session 33 - Nyema]]

## Notes
- [[The Thane]] did not dare to enter the [[Howling Caves]] while [[npcs/The Master.md|The Master]] was there.
- A found note identifies him as being part of the Cult of Set.
- His tomb is located to the east of the Southern Necropolis of Set.
- Intact statues found in his tomb include "Licinia the Seer", "Plotinus Quartus Claudino, Archon", "Yeth, Who Sought to Bring Law to Chaos", and "Horatius Interfector, Sorcerer."
- A headless statue found in the tomb was "Thrygga the Mighty".
