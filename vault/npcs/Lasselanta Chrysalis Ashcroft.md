# Lasselanta "Chrysalis" Ashcroft

## Summary
Lasselanta "Chrysalis" Ashcroft is a Half-Elven mercenary martial artist affiliated with the [[factions/Grudge Brigade.md|Grudge Brigade]]. Previously contracted under the name **Thorne**; an internal dispute over contracting names was resolved with an exchange of money on 2025-04-29, after which she contracts exclusively as **Chrysalis**. She was hired (along with [[npcs/Thrainor Thronebreaker Ironvein.md|Thronebreaker]]) by the Right for Riches company for the battle against [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] in Sessions 42a–42b. She has a monkey familiar named **Mariposa**.

## Known Abilities
- **Blind Fighting** — can fight effectively while blinded; Neferet's blinding spells were only a minor inconvenience to her.
- Uses a **Staff of Blue Light** — carries permanent light that was *not* extinguished by the dispel corridor (unlike other light sources).
- Relentless melee combatant; kept pursuing Neferet even while blinded and low on resources.

## Actions in Sessions 42a–42b

### Session 42a — Neferet (Part 1)
- Blessed by [[pcs/Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios.md|Ioannes]] before entering.
- Went first into the narrow dispel hallway (Bless was dispelled, but the Staff's light was not).
- Scouted the tomb chamber and reported the layout back to the party.
- Was blinded by one of Neferet's spells during the battle, but continued fighting with Blind Fighting.
- Continued pursuing Neferet relentlessly through the room despite blindness.
- Trapped in a Bead of Force sphere by Neferet; later freed by Vael's Blink Other.
- Damaged by a second Bead of Force thrown at her; treated by Ioannes with Stop Bleeding.

### Session 42b — Neferet and the Wraiths
- Freed from the force sphere early by Vael's Blink Other; immediately resumed pursuit of Neferet.
- Forced Neferet to abandon her charging Explosive Fireball to Blink away.
- Steadily burned Neferet's mana reserves through relentless pursuit.
- Was hit by a critical success Command spell from Neferet commanding her to flee — fled until Ioannes cast Bravery on her.
- Spotted Neferet moving the spider webs in the Great Hall while invisible and gave chase.
- Tried to push the last Bead of Force after Neferet through the web-filled hallway.
- In the Tower of Scrutiny, fought wraiths with her staff enchanted with Affect Spirits by Ioannes — killed several wraiths.
- Was badly wounded by wraith ranged attacks; this contributed to the party's retreat.
- [[npcs/Thrainor Thronebreaker Ironvein.md|Thronebreaker]], Uvash, and Mariposa were trapped in a separate sphere of force.
- Pursued Neferet down the hall, striking at her with her staff.

## Companion: Mariposa
Mariposa is Chrysalis's monkey familiar. A gargoyle in the Great Hall swooped at Mariposa, but she dodged the attack. Mariposa was trapped in the Bead of Force sphere in Session 42a alongside Thronebreaker and Uvash.
