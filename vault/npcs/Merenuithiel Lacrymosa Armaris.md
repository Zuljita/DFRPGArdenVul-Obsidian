# Merenuithiel Lacrymosa Armaris

## Summary
Merenuithiel Lacrymosa Armaris (known as **Lacrymosa**) is an elven mercenary archer affiliated with the [[factions/Grudge Brigade.md|Grudge Brigade]]. She was hired by the Right for Riches company for the invasion of the [[locations/Forum of Set.md|Forum of Set]] in Sessions 43a–43c.

## Abilities
- Highly skilled archer with rapid rate of fire; under [[spells/Great Haste.md|Great Haste]] (cast by Vael) she was essentially a one-woman firing line.
- Carries alchemical grenades: Concussion, Flash, and Smoke nagetempos.
- Drinks Agility and Strength potions before major engagements.
- Has **Luck** (used it to survive Dobby's invisible backstab attempt in Session 43b).
- Can wear magical goggles to see through Settite magical darkness.
- Uses the bow *Tears of the Forsaken*
- Known for a relentless desire to confront and kill halflings.

## Actions in Sessions 38–39

### Session 38 — Another Attack on the Temple of Set
- Hired as the party's archer for the attack on the [[locations/Temple of Set.md|Temple of Set]] from the northern cave entrance.
- Fought an archery duel against a line of Settite guards with bows: shot multiple Settites in the arms before they could effectively return fire.
- After the party descended the cliff, stood back and peppered cultists and guards with arrows while also throwing flash nagetempos to blind them.
- Vael cast Missile Shield on her as a precaution.
- Tracked fleeing Settites east.

### Session 39 — Diving for the Yellow Card
- Hired for the Yellow Card expedition into the Rudishva Bastion.
- Helped [[npcs/Thrainor Thronebreaker Ironvein.md|Thronebreaker]] breach the Rudishva security door using a portable battering ram.
- Fought the mimic and the sentry gun turrets in the Rudishva room.

## Actions in Sessions 43a–43c

### Session 43a — Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set
- Hired by Right for Riches for the Forum of Set invasion.
- Entered through the cliff-face entrance.
- Shot four of six cultists on the torchlit chasm balcony.
- Shot the last fleeing cultist out of the basket on the cliff.
- Shot multiple Settite guards and acolytes in the kitchen battle.
- Wore magical goggles to see through Settite darkness; shot multiple goggled guards.
- Shot a Settite mage who made the mistake of not knowing Blink.

### Session 43b — Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set (continued)
- Under Great Haste, kept up continuous fire as Settite reinforcements poured in.
- Survived Dobby's invisible backstab using Luck, then shot Dobby to pieces.
- In the "Last Stand of the Settites": opened with Concussion spell, then Flash and Smoke nagetempos, then sustained archery.
- Fought a long-range duel with a Settite warrior using a returning javelin; outlasted him due to superior rate of fire.
- Was exhausted after the battle; shoulder wound to [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] left her as the only functional ranged attacker.
- Noted Stephania was cut down from behind by one of her own priests during the final battle.

### Session 43c — Looting the Cult of Set
- Assigned to guard the entry hallway while the party looted the Settite vault.
- Shot the bipedal boar construct guarding the vault three times in center mass, finishing it off.
- Present for the freeing of many slaves.
