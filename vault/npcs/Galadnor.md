# Galadnor

## Summary
Galadnor is a Half-Elven merchant and spy who operated a stall in the [[locations/Forum of Set.md|Forum of Set]]. He claimed to be spying on behalf of the elven rulers while conducting business selling magical supplies.

## Notes
- Maintained a merchant stall in the Forum of Set selling magical supplies.
- Claimed to be no friend of the Settites and was spying on behalf of the elven rulers.
- Has a guard named [[npcs/Dworm.md|Dworm]] who assists with security and stall operations.
- Revealed the location of the Settites' vault to the southwest of the Forum, warning that it was locked and probably trapped.
- Had items stored in the Settite vault that were returned to him after the party looted it.
- Sold several Sortian eyes, Thothian teleportation squares, magic black sand, and a Rudishva power disc to Vael.

## Relationships
- [[npcs/Dworm.md|Dworm]]: His guard and assistant
