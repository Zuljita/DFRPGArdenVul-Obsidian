# Bastet

## Summary
A cat-associated divine figure invoked by the party during an offering in Gosterwick.

Bastet is the Goddess of Cats.

## Notes
- In Session 42a, the party made an offering to Bastet while seeking information related to Neferet.
- Bastet answered questions but did not know Neferet.
- The Avatar of Bastet can be summoned once a month using the Statuette of Feline Friendship.
- Her Avatar confirms a hidden temple of Bastet exists beneath [[locations/Arden Vul.md|Arden Vul]].
- The Avatar speaks in the language of cats and was translated by [[npcs/Fael.md|Fael]].
