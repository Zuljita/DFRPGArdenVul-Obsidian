# Gevorda

## Summary
A Varumani warrior who came to negotiate with [[npcs/Kerbog Khan.md|Kerbog Khan]], was instead knocked unconscious by him, kidnapped, and subjected to unauthorized medical experiments by his [[monsters/Surgical Construct.md|Surgical Construct]]. Rescued by the Right for Riches company in Session 52b/53. Upon waking, her immediate goal was to kill the Khan.

## Events in Session 52b/53
- Rescued after [[pcs/grudge-brigade/Sister Valya -Basilisk- Hushbreaker.md|Sister Valya]] cast Awaken on her.
- Confirmed she had come to make a deal with the Khan; he betrayed and kidnapped her instead.
- Agreed to delay killing the Khan at Vael's urging — acknowledging he was an archmage and she needed better equipment first.
- Distinguished the "renegade" hostile Varumani the party described from "proper Thegn-following varumani."
- Asked to be escorted to the [[locations/Varumani Lifts.md|Varumani Lifts]]; confirmed she knows [[npcs/Varboka.md|Varboka the Thegn]] personally ("all proper respectable varumani know the Thegn").
- At the lift station, commandeered the operator Gorlen's sword and armor rather than arrive in Varumani territory unequipped, then descended without paying.

## Notes
- Stated she could put in a good word with the Thegn in exchange for the rescue.
- Her arrival in Varumani territory after this session is untracked — potential future hook.
