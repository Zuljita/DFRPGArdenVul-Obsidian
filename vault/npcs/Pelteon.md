# Pelteon

## Summary
A young, vain mage and co-owner of the [[Arcane Practitioners' Club]]. She was formerly the apprentice of her partner, [[Lyssandra Astorion]]. Along with [[Lyssandra Astorion]], she identified the [[items/Gem of Insight.md|Gem of Insight]] for the party.

## Notes

- A young, vain mage who is a co-owner of the [[Arcane Practitioners' Club]].
- She was formerly the apprentice of her partner, [[Lyssandra Astorion]].
- When [[pcs/Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios.md|Ioannes]] attempted to flirt with her, she appeared not to notice.
- Canonical name: "Pelteon" (the "Palteon" spelling was a typo).
- Youngish Archontean woman with brown eyes, frizzy blonde hair, and very stylish wizard's robes.
- She charges [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]]'s robes.
- She cannot afford the [[items/Gem of Insight.md|Gem of Insight]].
- Onyx noted that she was 'insufficiently attentive because of her own problems with some human.'
- Along with Lyssandra, she identified the [[items/Rod of Imperium.md|Rod of Imperium]] as a significant artifact of Archonal power. ([[notes/Discord Summary 2025-W17.md|Discord Summary 2025-W17]])
