# Virgoolian

## Summary
Virgoolian is a goblin chemist who operates a merchant stall near the [[locations/Inn of the Lost.md|Inn of the Lost]] on the Arena Level of the Halls of Arden Vul.

## Wares
- Standard healing potions and similar alchemical goods.
- **Virgoolian's Special Brew** — A personal concoction that causes hallucinations. Sold to curious adventurers; The GOAT drank one and immediately began hallucinating and had to take a nap.

## Notes
- Warned Ioannes and GOAT that the passage to the north of the merchant stalls leads to "death."
- His stall is located in a Rudishva-architecture room northeast of the Varumani Lifts.
