# Tikun Thane

## Summary
A hostile wizard based in the [[Tower of the Ape]], linked by party suspicion to "The Thane" in Newmarket.

## Notes
- Commanded groups of [[Disciplined]] guards and trained baboons from the [[locations/Tower of the Ape.md|Tower of the Ape]].
- Demonstrated high-mobility wizard tactics (Blink, missile pressure, counter-mage actions including Purify Air after Magebane deployment).
- Survived multiple near-catches by retreating between levels and eventually abandoning the tower.
- In Session 34c, Vael's Seeker/Trace placed him in [[locations/Narsileon.md|Narsileon]] shortly after the tower actions, implying fast long-range relocation (likely magical transit).
