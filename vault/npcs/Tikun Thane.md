# Tikun Thane

## Summary
A hostile wizard based in the [[Tower of the Ape]], linked by party suspicion to "The Thane" in Newmarket.

## Notes
- Commanded groups of [[Disciplined]] guards and trained baboons.
- Used Blink and forceful missile magic to evade direct takedown.
- Withdrew from the tower after heavy losses and appears to have relocated to Narsileon.
