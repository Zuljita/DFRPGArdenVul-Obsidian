# Gresta

## Summary
Gresta is a cook found in the Forum of Set's kitchen during the Right for Riches company's assault. She was tasked with feeding the former slaves after the cult's defeat.

## Appears In
- [[sessions/Session 43c - Looting the Cult of Set.md|Session 43c - Looting the Cult of Set]]

## Session Notes
- Found in the kitchen of the Forum of Set
- Ran away when the fighting started
- Was later tasked by the Right for Riches company with cooking for the freed slaves gathered in the Forum
