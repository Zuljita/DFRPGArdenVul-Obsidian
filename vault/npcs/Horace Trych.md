# Horace Trych

## Summary
Horace Trych is an Archontean merchant and spy who operated in the [[locations/Forum of Set.md|Forum of Set]]. He claimed to be working for [[npcs/Lady Alexia Basileon.md|Lady Alexia]], the ruler of [[locations/Gosterwick.md|Gosterwick]].

## Notes
- Operated a merchant stall in the Forum of Set.
- Claimed to be spying on behalf of [[npcs/Lady Alexia Basileon.md|Lady Alexia]].
- Approached the party along with [[npcs/Galadnor.md|Galadnor]] when they were attempting to open the Settite vault, requesting that their personal items in the vault not be taken.
- Made a list of his belongings in the vault for Vael.
- His items were returned to him after the vault was opened.
- Sold various magical items from his stock including Thothian teleportation squares.

## Relationships
- [[npcs/Lady Alexia Basileon.md|Lady Alexia]]: His claimed employer
- [[npcs/Galadnor.md|Galadnor]]: Fellow merchant/spy operating in the same area
