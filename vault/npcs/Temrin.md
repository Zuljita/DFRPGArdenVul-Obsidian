# Temrin

## Summary
Temrin is a goblin artist based in the [[locations/Goblintown.md|Goblintown]] market area. He paints portraits and creates **magical maps** that sometimes actually lead to the object depicted. He was later promoted to **Royal Artist** by [[npcs/Weskenim.md|King Weskenim]] after completing the "Halfling Slayer" patches. His magical maps use a physical focus object to help him locate the target.

## Abilities
- **Portraits**: Standard portrait painting. Takes about an hour per group/subject.
- **Magical Maps**: Maps he draws sometimes actually lead to the depicted object. He uses a focus object (a physical item tied to the target) to improve accuracy. He charged the party for both the map and the return of any focus items they loaned him.
- **Royal Patches**: Created the "Halfling Slayer" patches (and later other award patches) for King Weskenim.

## History

### Session 32 — First Meeting
Vallium encountered Temrin sitting in the Merchant's Corner of the [[locations/Goblin Great Hall.md|Goblin Great Hall]], waiting for customers. He primarily did portraits. Vallium:
- Commissioned a **group portrait** of the Right for Riches company, asking Temrin to include Michael (Vallium used his Ring of Change Self to appear as Michael). Portrait took about an hour.
- Purchased a **portrait of Weskenim** and a **portrait of Gribble** from Temrin's existing stock.
- Commissioned a **magic map to "Nyema's necklace"** (to be ready the following day).

Vael independently cast Seeker for Nyema's necklace and got a vision of a necklace hanging on a hook on a wall, somewhere down and to the east.

### Session 33 — Map Delivered
Temrin completed the magic map to Nyema's necklace; the party retrieved it. Vael also commissioned another map (to find the gold Rudishva oval ID). The map to Nyema's body led to the [[locations/Temple of Set.md|Temple of Set]] crocodile pool.

### Session 35 — Royal Artist Promotion
Temrin was promoted to **Royal Artist** by King Weskenim and given a new patch depicting himself holding a paintbrush. He was commissioned by the King to create a **map to the Scepter of the Goblins** and told to accept no other commissions until it was complete. He retrieved the map and the **rust-colored oval plaque** he had used as a focus from Master Leifcrim's vault. Vallium tried to commission another map, but Temrin declined due to the royal commission.

### Session 36 — Scepter Map Completed
After the Scepter was recovered, Temrin's map had served its purpose. King Weskenim confirmed the Scepter was the genuine article. Temrin's new patch for goblins who participated in the Five Fingers of Destiny's treasury find was a sack overflowing with golden coins.
