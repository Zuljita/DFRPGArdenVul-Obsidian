# Lord Burdock

## Summary

A local lord whose domain included the [[Burdock Valley]]. He was active approximately 50 years ago and died 25 years ago.

## First Appearance

Mentioned in a historical research account regarding the [[Huge Green Dragon]] of [[Arden Vul]].

## Disposition

Cautious.

## Known Associates

TBD

## Notes

- After a disastrous encounter where 10 of his men were killed by the [[Huge Green Dragon]], Lord Burdock forbade his subjects from going up the [[Long Stair]] to the ruins.
- He also actively discouraged outsiders from using his valley to explore the ruins.
- Because of his actions, there was very little exploration of the ruins for about 25 years.
- The party of 12 men-at-arms included three knights; the only survivors were two young men kept back to hold horses, who lost most of the horses and baggage as they fled.
