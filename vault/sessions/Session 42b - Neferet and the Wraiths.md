# 42b: Neferet and the Wraiths

Date: Tothsday, 4th Mercedonian Day, 2994 AE

Weather: 
Cold, mostly sunny, snowy

Player Characters: 

[[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] Grammatikos Byzantios, Archontean cleric of Demma (Demented Avenger)[[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] Sunshadow, Half-Elven mage[[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] Edzuson, Dwarven cleric of ZodarrimThrainor "Thronebreaker" Ironvein, Dwarven mercenary fighter (Frank)Lasselanta "Chrysalis" Ashcroft, Half-Elven mercenary martial artist

Significant NPCs:
Mariposa, Chyrsalis's monkey familiarNeferet, Greater MummySeveral beastmenMany wraithsImperial stone guardian

The Plan:- Kill [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]]
- Take the Circlet of Ghanor

What Happened:
We resumed in mid-battle.

Battle of [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]]'s Tomb

Chrysalis was trapped in one sphere of force.  [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]], Thronebreaker, and Mariposa were trapped in another.  [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] and [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] were free, with [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] invisible and [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] controlling a lot of Created Fire using the Shape Fire spell.  [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] had been pushed back into the entrance hallway, but the only visible and free opponent was [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]], so she was focused on killing him.  Chrysalis was still blind (though this affected her less than most because of her Blind Fighting).
[[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] started growing an Explosive Fireball, planning to chuck it at [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] after it reached lethal size.  However, [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] had other ideas.  He used Blink Other to pop Chrysalis out of the force sphere.  After a second to get her bearings, she started running down the hall at [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]], striking with her staff.  [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] had to drop her Explosive Fireball to be able to Blink away.  This continued for several turns, with Chrysalis keeping [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] on the run and steadily burning her mana with Blink spells, while [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] freed Thronebreaker and [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] from the domes.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] also started sending the Created Fire down the hall after [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]].
[[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] eventually used a Command spell on Chyrsalis to flee, and rolled a critical success.  Chrysalis fled.  This bought [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] a couple of seconds, but when Chrysalis reached [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]], he cast Bravery on her, fixing the problem.  Meanwhile Thronebreaker and [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] ran down the hall after [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]].
[[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] eventually reached the end of the hall, with its Dispel Magic field.  After clearing it and entering the [[locations/Great Hall.md|Great Hall]], she popped another Invisibility Bead from her necklace and disappeared.  Thronebreaker, [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]], and Chrysalis ran out after her, with their Bless spells surprisingly intact due to some good rolls by [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]], but some of their other spells dispelled.
The PCs used a combination of Chrysalis's Blind Fighting and [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]]'s Sense Spirit to find [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]].  She was running low on mana, and decided to retreat and recover.  Invisibly, she went south across a barricade and started pushing through a hallway clogged with spider webs.  In the [[locations/Great Hall.md|Great Hall]], Chyrsalis spotted the moving spider webs and gave chase, but [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] threw a Bead of Force to stop her.  The bead failed to trap or damage Chrysalis, but it did block the hallway.  Many beastmen warriors watched the fight from behind their barricades and arrow slits, but [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] wasn't visible for long, and none took a shot with their bows.
Chrysalis tried pushing the force bead after [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]], but [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] tired of slowly pushing through the web and instead set them on fire with a Flame Jet, then was able to run at full speed.  The PCs ended up waiting a minute for the Force Spheres to dissipate.  By then [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] and [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] had caught up, with [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] using Blink Other then Blink to get them past the Dispel area, and Mariposa appeared a few seconds later.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] cast Seeker on [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]], then followed up with Trace, so he knew exactly where she was: well up some stairs to the southwest.
The Right for Riches company debated whether to go back and loot [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]]'s Tomb first and chase her later, or chase her immediately and loot later.  They decided to press the chase.  They went up the spiral stairs to the level immediately above the [[locations/Great Hall.md|Great Hall]], west of the southern baboon caves.  The Trace spell showed her going up more stairs to the west, so they followed that way.  A wall blocked their way, but they remembered a secret door they had found and bashed open before, and bashed it open again.  (At this point it had been bashed enough that it wasn't so secret.)
Another spiral staircase up took them to the [[locations/Tower of Scrutiny.md|Tower of Scrutiny]], to a room with spiked doors north and west.  Trace showed [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] about ten yards northwest and not moving.  Thronebreaker forced the western door open, revealing a hallway with several wraiths and one of the Imperial stone constructs.  The wraiths and construct reacted started heading at the intruders.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] put up a Wall of Lightning, which killed one wraith.  He then used Shape Air to try to blow the other wraiths in the hallway toward the Wall.  This was less effective than he'd hoped, as the diffuse wraiths were hard to blow around.  One more wraith got blown into the Wall and destroyed, while the construct stupidly ran right into it and took a severe shock, while Chrysalis smashed it through the Wall with a wooden staff.
The remaining wraiths regrouped out of sight and waited for the Wall and the Shape Air spells to expire, with one wraith far away peeking around the corner to watch.  When the Wall went down, a swarm of wraiths came down the hallway.  Thronebreaker, standing in front, got attacked a lot, but the wraiths' ranged attack was resisted by Will and he was implacable.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] and Chyrsalis also got attacked, and didn't resist as well.  [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] cast Affect Spirits on Chrysalis's staff, which then became more effective against the diffuse wraiths, and she killed a couple.  By then, however, the Right for Riches Company was in trouble, with [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] and [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] badly wounded and several PCs without Luck or Bless.  They fled down the stairs, and [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] put up another Wall of Lightning to block pursuit.
They staggered back to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]], intending to come back and fight their way past the wraiths to [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] as soon as possible.

GM's Comments:

[[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] was pretty badass, but she did have limited FP, and once it was almost gone, she had to run to recover it.
Hilariously, the players never figured out that [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] had Resist Fire up for the entire fight, and kept trying to burn her.  She pretended to be hurt to encourage such tactics.
Wraiths are no joke.  Especially swarms of them.  Wall of Lightning was a good way to slow them down, but the wraiths were too smart to just charge into it, and had effective ranged attacks that go past it.
[[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] was pretty sure [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]] was only about ten yards away and not moving, but they never actually spotted her in the [[locations/Tower of Scrutiny.md|Tower of Scrutiny]].
