# Session 42b - Neferet and the Wraiths

## Summary

The party nearly finishes Neferet in a running fight, but she escapes through the dispel corridor and retreats toward the Tower of Scrutiny. Pursuit leads into a dangerous wraith swarm that forces a tactical withdrawal.

## Full Recap

- Combat resumes with the party split by force spheres and Neferet focused on Vael.
- Vael uses Blink Other to free Chrysalis, who pressures Neferet in melee and forces repeated Blink use.
- Ioannes removes a critical Command effect with Bravery.
- Neferet retreats through the dispel hallway into the Great Hall, then uses another invisibility bead.
- The party tracks her using Chrysalis's blind-fighting reads + Uvash's Sense Spirit + Vael's Seeker/Trace.
- They chase up through known routes and a repeatedly bashed "secret" door toward the [[Tower of Scrutiny]].
- In upper halls they engage wraiths plus an imperial stone guardian.
- Vael's Wall of Lightning + Shape Air helps but does not hard-counter coordinated wraith tactics.
- The wraith swarm pressures the party with ranged spirit attacks; injuries mount.
- The group retreats to the [[Beacon]] to recover, intending to return quickly.
