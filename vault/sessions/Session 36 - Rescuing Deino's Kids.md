# 36: Rescuing [[npcs/Deino.md|Deino]]'s Kids

Date:
Basilsday, 4th of Fidios, 2993 AE

Weather:
Cold, overcast, flurries

Player Characters:
[[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] Halcyon, Archontean fighter (Greybrown)[[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] Grammatikos Byzantios, Archontean cleric of Demma (Demented Avenger)[[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] Sunshadow, Half-Elven mage (Kyle)[[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] Edzuson, Dwarven cleric of Zodarrim (Cosmo)[[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]], Mercenary monk (ArchonShiva)

Significant NPCs:
- [[npcs/Basil of Narsileon.md|Basil of Narsileon]], [[factions/Right for Riches.md|Right for Riches]] employee
- Many goblins
- [[npcs/Gog.md|Gog]], exiled varumani
- Several ogre guards
- A [[npcs/Set.md|Set]] deacon and several [[npcs/Set.md|Set]] guards
- [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]], King of the United Goblin Tribes
- [[npcs/Skimmel.md|Skimmel]], [[npcs/Reeflik.md|Reeflik]], [[npcs/Palestrim.md|Palestrim]], and [[npcs/Domo Gribble.md|Gribble]] (the King's advisors)
- [[npcs/Temrin.md|Temrin]], goblin Royal Artist
- [[npcs/Hama.md|Hama]] and company, adventurers
- 6 skeletons
- 6 skeletal stirges
- Many fungal foresters
- Many beastmen, loyal to [[npcs/Deino.md|Deino]]
- 6 beastmen, loyal to [[npcs/Ambrosia.md|Ambrosia]]
- 2 phase salamanders
- [[npcs/Craastonistorex.md|Craastonistorex]], green dragon
- Wyvern, in service to Craastonistorex
- [[npcs/Count Skleros.md|Count Skleros]], beastman commander
- Guardian of [[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]]
- 6 construct spearmen

The Plan:- Bring the Scepter / Flute of the Goblins to King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]] for a reward
- See if the beastmen's stone chest in the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]] could be taken
- Explore the northeast part of the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]]
- Go to the northwest part of the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]], and defeat [[npcs/Ambrosia.md|Ambrosia]] if possible
- Bring [[npcs/Ambrosia.md|Ambrosia]]'s (possibly charmed) beastmen back to the Children of [[npcs/Deino.md|Deino]]

What Happened:
With two pairs of Rugs of Instant Access Available, the [[factions/Right for Riches.md|Right for Riches]] company set up one pair between their apartments in the Arcane Practitioners' Club in [[locations/Gosterwick.md|Gosterwick]] and the secret room of the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]].  They put one Rug from the other pair in the library of the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]], and took the other one with them.  They instructed [[npcs/Basil of Narsileon.md|Basil of Narsileon]] to be ready in the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]] on adventuring days in case they needed help.
From the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]], [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] used *Blink Other* to teleport [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] through the roof over the stairs to the surface, then used *Blink* to follow a few seconds later.  There was no trouble, so they set out the Rug to let the others join them.  Everyone walked to the [[locations/Pyramid of Thoth.md|Pyramid of Thoth]] and descended to Upper [[locations/Goblintown.md|Goblintown]], then down the secret stairs behind  the baboon fresco to [[npcs/Gog.md|Gog]]'s Cave, then through the goblins' Wet Caves and down to [[locations/Goblintown.md|Goblintown]].  They took the long, safe way around the level to King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]]'s court.  There were various goblins and even a group of Settites waiting outside, but [[npcs/Palestrim.md|Palestrim]] was not sitting in his chair.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] asked an ogre on guard if they could see King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]], and the ogre eventually told him that [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]] was down in the Goblin [[locations/Great Hall.md|Great Hall]] dealing with his subjects.
The group circled all the way back around the level to the spiral stairs leading to the Goblin [[locations/Great Hall.md|Great Hall]].  There, King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]] was telling a human adventurer named [[npcs/Hama.md|Hama]] that if he wanted his elven friend freed, he needed to find the [[items/Scepter of the Goblins.md|Scepter of the Goblins]].  Or, if he couldn't do that, perform another great service such as opening the Goblin Great Gates to the west.  [[npcs/Hama.md|Hama]] bowed low to the King then took his companions west, presumably to check out the Gates.
[[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] told the King that he had important news and asked for a word in private.  After shaking the hands of a few followers and making a speech about the new patches that [[npcs/Temrin.md|Temrin]] had completed to award to whichever brave goblins (or possibly goblin allies) found the [[items/Scepter of the Goblins.md|Scepter of the Goblins]], the King stepped around the corner to talk to [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]].  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] pulled the Scepter / Flute out of his bag.  The king and his advisors gasped, then discussed whether that was a varumani bone, and how they could keep the Varumani Thegn from being offended by it.  King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]] said he would like to take it to his Witch Doctors for a close look, then he would meet them in his throne room.
About 30 minutes later, [[npcs/Palestrim.md|Palestrim]] admitted the [[factions/Right for Riches.md|Right for Riches]] company to the King's throne room.  The throne room had new sacks and chests lining the walls, a gold statue of a cat on a shelf of honor, and many copper coins just piled up.  King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]] said the Scepter was definitely magical and was definitely 100% the real [[items/Scepter of the Goblins.md|Scepter of the Goblins]], made by the great goblin king [[npcs/Trollreaper.md|Trollreaper]].  He said that [[npcs/Trollreaper.md|Trollreaper]]'s name was not one they mentioned among the varumani, so he would probably only show the Scepter to the goblin people once when no varumani were around, then hide it.  He then pointed at his new cat statue, chests and sacks of loot, and piles of coins, and said that the [[factions/Five Fingers of Destiny.md|Five Fingers of Destiny]] had shared a great hoard with him, and that he would be passing much of it on to them as a reward for finding his Scepter.
King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]] asked if they had means of transporting many coins.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] said they did, but asked for a private room.  The King said he didn't have any extra rooms right now, but they could maybe borrow one for a little while.  [[npcs/Reeflik.md|Reeflik]], one of his advisors, left for a few minutes, then returned and led the group north to a room with two doors, containing some large bins of mushrooms ([[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] thought they were mostly white ribs, but one bin contained  cloud caps) and some long poles with hooks on the end.  He opened the far door, summoned the mushroom-pickers, and told them to not come back into this room until they were told these people were done in there.  The [[factions/Right for Riches.md|Right for Riches]] company used their Rug to go back to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]] and get two wheelbarrows (one normal, one Living) and two shovels.  They then used the wheelbarrows to transport many copper coins to the mushroom storage room, and then used the Rug to take them to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]].  It took a while, but they eventually got all their coins back to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]].
The [[factions/Right for Riches.md|Right for Riches]] company said farewell to the King, went back to the surface, and then went south to the Long Stair.  Not wanting to climb down it in the icy weather, everyone except [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] and [[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]] stepped through the Rug, and [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] and [[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]] *Levitated* down to the secret door.  They put the Rug down, and eventually the others joined them, describing how [[npcs/Basil of Narsileon.md|Basil of Narsileon]] had been industriously sorting all their coins into neatly counted piles, until they told him not to bother since they were just going to put them back in the wheelbarrows and Rug-teleport them to the Rarities Factor for deposit.
[[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] opened the secret door, went west to investigate the big stone beastman chest, and said it was too heavy to lift.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] searched it for traps, found freshly applied poison on the lock, and used *Lockmaster* and *Apportation* spells to unlock and open the chest without touching it.  It was full of supplies, nothing very valuable, so they refrained from looting it, and [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] magically closed and locked the chest.  [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] then led everyone east through the side caves, then north through the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]] to the Lake.  There everyone again stepped through the Rug, while [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] and [[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]] flew over the lake and up the river to the northeastern tunnels.
Once everyone stepped back out the Rug, [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] led them east, to a cavern full of skeletons, with some armor behind them that [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] and [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] though might be magical.  Before they could approach the armor, the skeletons started to rise.  Half of the skeletons appeared to be human, the other half stirges, and they all attacked.  The battle was fairly quick, with [[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]] stunning skeletons with *Kiai*, [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] and [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] smashing skeletons, [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] using *Shape Air* to smash skeletal stirges against the wall, and [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] mostly hanging back with his shield up.
When all the skeletons were defeated, the group picked up the magic scale armor they had spotted, as well as a few coins and gems and flasks and a scroll case.  They then explored a cave to the north, which contained a pool of water.  [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] asked them to have the Rug ready so he could go back to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]] to warm up, then dived into the nearly-freezing water, holding his glowing stone in front of him.  On the bottom he spotted a limed-over skeleton, wearing a pouch, with some kind of bronze rod on the bottom next to him.  He dived twice more, first bringing up the rod, then the skeleton.  Freezing, he excused himself and went back through the Rug to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]], where he asked [[npcs/Basil of Narsileon.md|Basil of Narsileon]] to set up the magical Tripod of Heating.  After a few minutes to warm up and dry his clothes, he went back through the Rug where the others were standing guard.  The group then took all the treasure through the Rug into the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]], and [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] and [[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]] took the Rug and flew out of the side caves, to the northern part of the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]], above the cliff.  They put the Rug down.
As the group reassembled, they spotted some beastmen moving within their caged-off area to the north.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] mentioned to them that they were going to try to rescue some of their comrades, and asked if they would still be there in a few minutes.  Excited, the beastman sergeant said they would remain there on alert.  [[factions/Right for Riches.md|Right for Riches]] then followed [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] through a secret door in the west wall of the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]], then through some western side passages until they reached the cavern of the fungal foresters.  [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] gestured a desire to go past them to the secret door.  The largest forester said something to the others in their strange language, and they all moved south to make room.  [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] opened the secret door into [[npcs/Ambrosia.md|Ambrosia]]'s cave.  [[npcs/Ambrosia.md|Ambrosia]] was not there, but a half-dozen of her beastmen were, and they were looking even skinnier than last time.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] said something about [[npcs/Ambrosia.md|Ambrosia]] having some food for them back in the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]].  One beastman was skeptical and said they had guard duty, but the rest said they were going for food and might bring him some back if there was enough, so he relented and said he would go with them.
[[factions/Right for Riches.md|Right for Riches]] escorted the beastmen back through the side caves to the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]].  As [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] opened the secret door to the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]], the Children of [[npcs/Ambrosia.md|Ambrosia]] spotted the Children of [[npcs/Deino.md|Deino]] on guard in their supply cage, and their sergeant started to yell, but then [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] dropped a *Mass Daze* spell on the whole group.  The sergeant stopped yelling and the beastmen started placidly staring into space.  The other beastmen came over, thanked the company, and led their former and possibly future comrades into the cage, then through a door on the north wall of the cage, back toward [[npcs/Deino.md|Deino]]'s territory.
Before leaving the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]], [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] suggested exploring the island to the northeast where they had seen a statue and some big lizards.  Everyone walked along the edge of the cliff until they were above the island.  After some discussion, [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] attached his magic rope to a handhold and everyone climbed down.  It was still too far to the island to safely jump, so [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] cast *Flight* on [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]].  [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]], [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]], and [[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]] flew over to the island, while both clerics stayed at the base of the cliff.  The two phase salamanders tried to invisibly ambush the intruders, but they had been spotted on a previous visit so the intruders were too alert.  [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] blocked a couple of attacks, Basiliks stunned both salamanders, and [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] chopped them up.  While [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] skinned the salamanders in case their skins or organs were valuable, [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] investigated the statue, and found a small pile of gear at its base, including a couple of magic torches and a magic statuette of a cat.  They once again used the Rug to teleport all the loot to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]].  There was some discussion of messing with the vaguely reptilian statue, perhaps putting its head back on top of its body.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] and [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] both wanted to try, but [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]], the only one strong enough to lift the stone head, declined.
The group then went south across the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]], again using the Rug to teleport to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]] as [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] and [[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]] flew across the lake.  [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] led everyone through the southeastern caves back to the secret exit to the Cliff Face, and [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] and [[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]] *Levitated* back up to the ruins.  The group reformed and started hiking north toward the [[locations/Pyramid of Thoth.md|Pyramid of Thoth]], when they heard a loud crash to the west.  Walking over to the dam to check on the noise, they saw a sack on top of the dam, along with a lot of small bones.  The bones were the skeletons of about a banda of beastmen.  The sack contained a single earring and a long note from [[npcs/Craastonistorex.md|Craastonistorex]].  It said that their tribute was sub-par, with trash like a single earring that someone's ex-girlfriend left in their apartment and some chess pieces with no chess board and small piles of tiny little modern coins not being acceptable.  It said that Craas was giving them one more chance, as eating them would require training new Wardens of the Dam, but the tribute had better improve.  It also said to tell [[npcs/Deino.md|Deino]] to resume paying her tribute or he'd eat more of her kids, and that the piggies tasted the best.
[[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] wrote an insincere apology note to the dragon and put it in the sack.  They gathered up all the bones to return to Count Skleros.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] *Levitated* up to see if anything else could have caused the loud noise, and spotted a wyvern flying far to the southwest, but nothing else suspicious.  They resumed hiking east then north, then descended through the pyramid to the Glory of [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]].  They proceded south to the border of beastman territory, where [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] made a lot of noise until a beastman guard came to investigate.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] asked to talk to Count Skleros.  The Count showed up a few minutes later and thanked the group for rescuing his men, who were currently being de-charmed by Mother.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] gave him the last page of the dragon's note and the sack of bones.  Skleros was suddenly much less happy, but said he would bring the note to Mother and they would honorably inter the dead.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] asked for a meeting in seven days to address the dragon problem, and Skleros said he could do that.
[[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] next wanted to return to the Chamber of Painful Knowledge and ask [[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]] a question.  They got there, through the gray-green mist, and [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] asked both clerics to be ready in case he was badly hurt.  He then grabbed the obelisk and asked [[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]] for one of his secret names.  [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] heard "The Font of Magic!" as he received a severe electrical shock.  Somehow he avoided passing out.  [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] cast *Stop Bleeding*, followed by *Major Healing*, and [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] was better, except for some scorched-off hair.
After a few minutes to recover, [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] said they should explore the glowing room to the northwest, and led everyone there.  There was obvious magical light from the ceiling, and frescoes of the goddess [[npcs/Maat.md|Maat]] on the wall, handing a feather to a worshipper.  There was a marble dais in the center of the room, with an onyx platform on top, and then a magical feature floating in the air above the platform.  After some discussion, [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] grabbed the feather, and another one appeared.  Three more people each took a feather.
With that room discovered, [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] said they should explore the northeast part of the level next.  They went east to the room with the locked bronze doors with peepholes.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] had everyone else go through the Rug, then Blinked through the door, using the peephole to see where he was going, then dropped the Rug and had the others rejoin them.  They crossed the bridge over the Great Chasm (not so wide this far north, but very deep) to the room with the statue of [[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]] with the movable arms.  They started manipulating statue arms to every combination of positions to try to open another door.  This summoned an ibis guardian of [[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]], which [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] dispatched, but then the door eventually popped open.
Going through the door, they found passages connecting to the magic fountain where [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] got his donkey ears and to a room with some looted sarcophagi and a tunnel leading south to the Great Chasm.  From the pool room, there was a door south, which led to a cave containing many bones and a creepy obelisk, with the ceiling well out of sight.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] thought he remembered this room, and flew up to verify that it was the same obelisk he'd seen a level up. They avoided touching the obelisk, but [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] took some notes of the odd runes on it.  They also searched the bones and found a spear.
From the obelisk room, they went back north past the pool room, then east to a door, a corridor, and another door.  At the second door they heard a sizzling sound and felt heat.  Beyond was a large round pool of magical fire.  Some investigation showed a golden key in the middle of the fire, along with some kind of fire creature, and some Mithric writing: "The fire of Truth cleanses the mind and transports the body of those faithful who proclaim that [[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]] Rules!"  After multiple failed attempts to talk to the fire creature, [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] yelled "[[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]] Rules!" and a path appeared through the fire to the key.  [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] entered the fire and heard a voice in his head "[[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]] values your love.  Do you wish to be cleansed or to be transported?"  [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] answered "cleansed" and felt something magical and pleasant wash over him, but did not notice any real effects.  He was able to grab the golden key, though.
Nobody else played with the fire, and they searched the walls for secret doors.  [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] found one, with a small hidden keyhole.  [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] tried the newly found key in it, and it fit.  Opening the door revealed a passage south that turned east and revealed a wide passage between two rows of statues.  There were three mosaics on the floor, each showing a blue knot.  As [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] stepped out to investigate, the statues animated into constructs, and one tried to spear him from behind.  As [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] strugged to get his shield up, [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] used *Blink Other* and moved [[pcs/Ioannes.md|Ioannes]] out of the line of fire.
A fight began against 6 spearman constructs.   [[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]] and [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] both used their holy abilities to become faster and tougher.  [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] cast *Blur* on [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]].  [[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]] then rushed around at high-speed repeatedly stunning the constructs with *Kiai*, while [[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium]] and [[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash]] bashed them.  The constructs were tough, but had a hard time getting any attacks in, as they kept getting stunned.  Two of were destroyed outright, and the other four knocked out.  [[pcs/Basilisk.md|Basilisk]] tried her *Vampiric Bite* on an unconscious construct and was disappointed that it did not work.  At that point they destroyed the four unconscious constructs.
The eastern wall had a large picture of a middle aged man with empty eye sockets, very similar to those they had seen on the magical face statues, but this one was not magical.  There were also two doors.  But at that point the group felt like they had explored enough for one day, and turned back for the exit.

GM's Comments:
King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]] seemed to appreciate the Scepter / Flute, though the form of his reward was a bit inconvenient, and would have been even less convenient if the group had lacked teleportation magic.
Count Skleros seemed to appreciate getting his lost troopers back, though this did not yield an immediate reward, and the news about the dragon attacks seemed to upset him.
This session involved a bit of combat, a bit of exploration, and a bit of treasure, but no huge discoveries.  [[factions/Right for Riches.md|Right for Riches]] seems committed to tying up loose ends
