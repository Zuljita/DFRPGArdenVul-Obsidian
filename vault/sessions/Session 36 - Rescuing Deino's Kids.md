# Session 36 - Rescuing Deino's Kids

## Summary
A goblin-politics and movement session where the party pushed through Goblintown districts, discussed prisoner-release leverage tied to western goblin doors, and handled follow-on logistics tied to the Beacon and town-side storage routes.

## Full Recap (evidence-backed)

- The party traveled by the [[locations/Pyramid of Thoth.md|Pyramid of Thoth]] approach, moved through the [[locations/Forum of Set.md|Forum of Set]], and entered [[locations/Upper Goblintown.md|Upper Goblintown]] before continuing deeper.
- They navigated between upper/lower goblin-held areas under visible goblin guard control.
- In goblin leadership discussions, the party heard an explicit deal framework: opening the western great goblin doors could secure release of an elven prisoner.
- A formal recognition moment referenced the "Order of the Goblin Scepter," the [[items/Scepter of the Goblins.md|Scepter of the Goblins]], and [[factions/Five Fingers of Destiny.md|Five Fingers of Destiny]], with [[npcs/Temrin.md|Temrin]] tied to ceremonial patches.
- The group discussed coin-movement logistics using the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]] plus rug transit toward the [[factions/Rarities Factor.md|Rarities Factor]] in [[locations/Gosterwick.md|Gosterwick]].
