A reconnaissance-and-control session centered on structured exploration from the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]], with the party prioritizing bastion mapping/iris-door checks before teleport-pad testing and recovering additional access/control gear from burned remains.

- The party also recovered a heavy-looking [[items/Rudishva Command Bracelet.md|Rudishva Command Bracelet]] believed to be more than ornamentation.
