# Session 43a - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set

## Summary

The party shifts from Neferet pursuit to a deliberate assault on the [[Cult of Set]], infiltrating from the cliff-face entrance and methodically carving through multiple defensive layers of the Forum.

## Full Recap

- Vallium returns from Newmarket; party hires [[Lacrymosa]].
- Team stages at the [[Beacon]], executes stealth/invisibility insertion down the cliff.
- Entry cultists are neutralized quickly; party avoids unnecessary infrastructure damage.
- They clear multiple rooms including a kitchen and patrol corridors.
- Ioannes triggers trapped lever combinations; Vael repeatedly mitigates poison with Purify Air.
- A trapped hanging chest is opened (wind trap), yielding valuables.
- Party uses Rug logistics heavily: bodies/loot/furniture/food moved to Beacon and processed by Basil.
- Zombie room is identified and temporarily bypassed/marked instead of engaged.
- Goblin noncombatants are warned and supplied; told to report to King Weskenim.
- The assault pushes into magical darkness; Vael retrieves anti-darkness goggles and coordinates breach.
- Session pauses with a running battle still active and additional Settite defenders converging.
