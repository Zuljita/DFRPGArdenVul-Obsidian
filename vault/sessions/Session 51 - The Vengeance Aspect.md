The Vengeance Aspect was nasty, with its blindness and Symbol of Death and fire attacks and ability to raise lots of ghouls.Â  The combination of Salamander Amulets, Dispel Magic, Affect Spirits, and Vallium and Uvash having enough armor to be mostly ghoul-proof carried the battle.Â  Dispel Magic is a rather blunt instrument though, as it tries to dispel all the magic, not just the magic you want gone.

The book of teleporter addresses was a welcome find.Â  The party has been discussing trying all the permutations of addresses for a long time, but having a directory should save them some time and effort.
