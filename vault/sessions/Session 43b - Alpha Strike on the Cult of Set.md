# Session 43b - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set

## Summary

The continuation of the Forum offensive turns into a high-intensity mass battle. The party breaks major Settite leadership and gains local support from goblins and Thorcin mercenaries, but leaves major unresolved objectives in place.

## Full Recap

- Vael opens with [[Great Haste]] on Lacrymosa; ranged attrition dominates opening exchanges.
- Dobby + Jadeel counterplay (invisibility/backstab + command control) fails under pressure.
- Frontline anchors (Vallium/Uvash) hold while Lacrymosa dismantles waves.
- As Settite collapse starts, goblins and Thorcin mercs join against slavers.
- Slaves are freed from chains/stakes while forum fighting winds down.
- The party identifies and defers a trapped stone chest at the Beacon.
- Intel reveals the Settite vault (3-key lock) but insufficient keys are available yet.
- Vael runs Seeker/Trace on Stephania and pushes into follow-on engagement.
- A larger final confrontation fractures internally; even Settite leadership turns on itself.
- Party returns to Forum with allies still holding space; many Settites dead or routed.
