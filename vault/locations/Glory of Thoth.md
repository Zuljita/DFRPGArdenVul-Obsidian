# Glory of Thoth

## Summary
A major transit and control area in the upper Arden Vul complex near the Great Chasm. Historically tied to Thothian-era structure and routes, but in the current campaign period often referred to as **Glory of Weskenim** after goblin consolidation.

## Current Naming
- **Historical/common map name:** Glory of Thoth
- **Current in-world usage (recent GM recaps):** Glory of Weskenim
