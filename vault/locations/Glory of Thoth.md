# Glory of Thoth

## Summary
A major transit and control area in the upper Arden Vul complex near the Great Chasm. Historically tied to Thothian-era structure and routes, but in the current campaign period often referred to as **Glory of Weskenim** after goblin consolidation.

## Current Naming
- **Historical/common map name:** Glory of Thoth
- **Current in-world usage (recent GM recaps):** Glory of Weskenim

## Connections
- [[locations/Great Chasm.md|Great Chasm]]
- [[locations/Temple of Thoth.md|Temple of Thoth]]
- [[locations/Goblintown.md|Goblintown]]
- Routes toward [[locations/Howling Caves.md|Howling Caves]] and [[locations/Tower of Scrutiny.md|Tower of Scrutiny]]

## Control / Residents
- Predominantly goblin-controlled in recent sessions.
- Authority aligned with [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]] and allied goblin structures.

## Notes
- The party has used this area as a movement corridor during goblin diplomacy and anti-Set operations.
- In Session 33 context, Bottleneck was surprised that Vallium had a key relevant to a door in this area.
