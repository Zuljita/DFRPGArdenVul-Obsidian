# Shining Well

## Summary
A well rumored to lead directly to valuable loot in [[Arden Vul]] which are thought to be picked clean.

The rumor suggests that because the primary entrance via the [[Pyramid of Thoth]] is frequently picked clean by other adventurers, this well may provide an alternative route to significant treasure.

The area is known to be inhabited by albino baboons and intelligent 4-armed baboon cooks.

The well features a mirrored shaft that can be blindingly bright without the use of magical darkness.
