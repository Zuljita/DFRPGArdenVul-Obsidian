# Varumani Lifts

## Summary
The Varumani Lifts are a mechanical lift system operating within the [[locations/Great Chasm.md|Great Chasm]], operated by [[Varumani]] staff. They allow travel between multiple levels of the Chasm, including the Arena level, the Varumani level, and the floating garden island. The Lifts also have cross-chasm ferry service to the garden island and to the far (west) side of the Chasm.

## Structure

### Upper Lift Station (Sundered Span Level)
- Located at the top of the Varumani Lifts, accessible via the [[locations/Sundered Span.md|Sundered Span]] from the [[locations/Wet Caves.md|Wet Caves]].
- A Varumani operator runs this station.
- **Pricing**: 10 silver per "leg" of travel (or 20 per person — the operator quipped this was because people have two legs). First-time riders are charged a surcharge; this is waived for returning customers.

### Arena Level Station
- The operator here directs visitors: Inn to the north, Arena to the northeast.
- To continue down, passengers must take a **cross-chasm ferry** to the garden island floating in the center of the Chasm.
- The cross-chasm ferry carries **maximum four people** per trip.

### Floating Garden Island
- A garden of tropical plants, lit by very bright (non-magical) globes overhead.
- Has a hill in the center with a **pool of green water** on top. (Sample taken but not yet analyzed.)
- Gold and magic detected inside the hill via Seek Earth/Seek Magic (Session 39).
- Two Varumani ferry operators watched visitors; the party didn't want to poke around too conspicuously.

### Far (West) Side — Varumani Control Station
- Has a giant stone chair and a gate behind it.
- Operated by a senior Varumani with a **gold baton of office**.
- Beyond the gate: described as housing "scary ice monsters" — the operator strongly discouraged entering without paying a surcharge (and sarcastically suggested leaving their money with him for "safekeeping").
- A Lift here descends to the Varumani level.

### Varumani Level Station
- Has the Lift landing but no machinery of its own — end of this Lift line.
- **Ogre guards** prevent entry into Varumani territory (to the west) without a passport.
- The officer-in-charge said they might not be issuing any more passports and would ask the thegn.
- A staircase descends further down the Chasm wall from this level.

## Passports
A Varumani Passport (discovered in Session 45 as a Rudishva plasteel card with a Varumani image on one side) is required to enter actual Varumani territory. Riding the Lifts does not require a passport — just payment.
