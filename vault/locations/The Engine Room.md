# The Engine Room

## Summary
A room featuring an iris door and a Rudishva teleporter that connects to the Cloister vault.

To the south, the area features a slated-paved section with mosaics, three burned bodies, and a porphyry sarcophagus inscribed with Mithric writing.

A pit in the center of the room contains two skeletonized Rudishva, three identity plaques, and a GnuCoder 1000 data tablet.
- Six Rudishva drive cores were removed from the engines during Session 57b.
