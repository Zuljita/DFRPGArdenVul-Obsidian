# Red Bridge of Set

The Red Bridge of Set is a prominent location within the domain of the Cult of Set. 

## Appears In
- [[sessions/Session 43a - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set.md|Session 43a - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set]]
- [[sessions/Session 43c - Looting the Cult of Set.md|Session 43c - Looting the Cult of Set]]
- A red granite bridge flanked by Settite statues that spans a chasm.
- A force field at the bridge's Forum-side entrance blocks access from the chasm side.
- The force field at the Forum-side entrance can be bypassed by flying across to the balcony.
- A hanging chest (containing platinum bars, a magic helmet, and wrapped stone) was suspended in the chasm here, protected by a wind trap.
- A torchlit balcony in the far corner of the chasm served as a leisure area for cultists.
- The torchlit balcony in the chasm corner has levers that can release poison gas depending on the combination used.
