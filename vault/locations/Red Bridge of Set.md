# Red Bridge of Set

The Red Bridge of Set is a red granite bridge that spans a chasm within the domain of the Cult of Set. The bridge is flanked by Settite statues. In the far corner of the chasm, there is a torchlit balcony that cultists used as a leisure area.

## Appears In
- [[sessions/Session 43a - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set.md|Session 43a - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set]]
- [[sessions/Session 43c - Looting the Cult of Set.md|Session 43c - Looting the Cult of Set]]

## Notes
- A force field at the Forum-side entrance blocks access from the chasm side, though it can be bypassed by flying to the balcony.
- The torchlit balcony contains levers that can release poison gas depending on the combination used.
- A hanging chest containing platinum bars, a magic helmet, and wrapped stone was suspended in the chasm here, protected by a wind trap.
