# Dam ([[locations/Arden Vul.md|Arden Vul]])

## Summary
A dam separating a large pond or small lake (part of the [[Swift River]]) from the main flow of the river, located above the [[Waterfall]] in [[Arden Vul]].

## Level

## Notes
- In [[sessions/Session 2 - Halfling Rent-Seekers.md|Session 2 - Halfling Rent-Seekers]], the party forced open a locked dam door and found two control wheels inside; field-testing indicated one wheel changes water flow through the dam.
- During that same Session 2 dam test, the party observed a huge barbed-tail flying reptile approach, drop waste near the lake by the dam, and withdraw toward the southwest ruins.
- The dragon [[Craastonistorex]] considers the dam control vital to his swamp.
- In [[sessions/Session 42a - Neferet.md|Session 42a - Neferet]], the party used the dam area as a logistics drop point for an Archontean chess set before moving back toward the [[locations/Pyramid of Thoth.md|Pyramid of Thoth]].
