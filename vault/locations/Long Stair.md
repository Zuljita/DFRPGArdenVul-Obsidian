# Long Stair

## Summary
A major exterior ascent route on the [[locations/Cliff Face.md|Cliff Face]] leading up toward the surface ruins of [[locations/Arden Vul.md|Arden Vul]].

## Notes
- In Session 34a, the party located a high cave exit overlooking the Long Stair, confirming additional cave-to-exterior connectivity.
- A room off the stairs contains the skeleton of a dwarven warrior with mail armor, red leather boots, and blue powder. ([[sessions/Session 1 - First Visit to the Ruins of Arden Vul.md|Session 1]])
- The route includes a secret entrance to the Great Cavern approximately two-thirds of the way up. ([[sessions/Session 11 - The Great Cavern Redux.md|Session 11]])
- The stairs feature a stone cover that can be bypassed with magic such as *Blink*.
