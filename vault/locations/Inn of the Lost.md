# Inn of the Lost

## Summary
The Inn of the Lost is an underground inn located on the Arena Level of the Halls of Arden Vul, near the [[locations/Arena.md|Arena]] and the Varumani Lifts. It is accessible from the Varumani Lifts by heading east past a passage northeast of the Lifts. The inn has a sign in Mithric reading **"House of Isocorax"** and another sign in Archontean reading **"Inn of the Lost."**

## Staff
- **[[npcs/Thegan White-Mane.md|Thegan White-Mane]]** — Proprietor. An old man with white hair.
- **[[npcs/Boots.md|Boots]]** — Barkeeper.
- **[[npcs/Aethgrim.md|Aethgrim]]** — Entertainer/bard.

## Amenities
- Private rooms available for rent.
- Bunkroom with shared beds.
- Dining room with food and drink.
- Common room (tends to be empty on Spectacle Days, when guests are at the Arena).

## Location Notes
- A large door is marked with a sign in Mithric ("House of Isocorax") before the passage turns toward the inn entrance.
- East of the inn's merchant stalls (chemist, baker, cleric, weapons dealer), there is a cave-in. Beyond the cave-in is a passage leading to a Rudishva-architecture room with a kaliyani statue.
- The area to the north of the merchant stalls was described by the chemist [[npcs/Virgoolian.md|Virgoolian]] as leading to "death."
