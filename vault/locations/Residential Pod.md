# Residential Pod

## Summary
A location associated with Rudishva architecture where interviews with homesick Rudishva were conducted.

The floor inside was corroded, as if something had melted the high-tech flooring.

The building contains one intact iris door and several rooms.

The passage leading to the pod featured an iris valve along one wall and a hole high up on the opposite wall.


The area includes cold rooms with floors covered in ice and snow, where the Frost Mother was encountered alongside several frost ghouls, giant lizards, slime zombies, and slimes.

A cavern southeast from the frost rooms contained dead giant lizards that appeared to have been killed by powerful fire magic or dragon breath.

Inside the intact iris door, a desiccated Rudishva corpse identified as Mol-Prev, the Beacon's Morale Officer, was found hanging from a lighting fixture alongside a communication bracelet.

One of the rooms contained a large constrictor snake hiding in a bed.
