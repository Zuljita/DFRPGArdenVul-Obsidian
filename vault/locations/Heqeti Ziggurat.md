# Heqeti Ziggurat

## Description

The main fortress of the [[Chasm Floor]]
- The destruction of the Heqeti Upper Temple was a key event in the historical conflict involving the Rudishva, Varumani, Varuda, and Kalyani forces.
- Following its conquest, survivors retreated into the Underearth.

## Inhabitants

- [[Marius Tricotor]].
- [[Heqeti]] (some of whom are mages or demon-anti-priests)
