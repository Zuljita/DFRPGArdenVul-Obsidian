# The Tomb of Ptoh-Ristus

## Summary
The burial site of [[npcs/Ptoh-Ristus.md|Ptoh-Ristus]], a Thothian servant described as "Knower of Secrets and Founder of these Halls." Accessed via a "back door" from the chasm — the front entrance was considered more dangerous. First explored in Session 27.

## Layout
- **Approach**: A porch-like area with a secret door discovered by Ioannes; Vallium fell into a pit trap beyond it, which was full of corpses and loot.
- **Main Chamber**: Accessed via a Shape Earth spell through a blocked passage. Contains two normal-sized white marble sarcophagi, a large black basalt sarcophagus, and two man-sized spearman statues that animated and attacked.
- **Sarcophagus Inscription**: The large black basalt sarcophagus bears a Mithric inscription: *"Ptoh-Ristus, Servant of Thoth, Knower of Secrets and Founder of these Halls."*
- **Secondary Passage**: A secret door in the southeast of the main chamber leads past a large falling stone block trap and poison dart traps along two walls, eventually connecting back to a known area.

## Contents (Looted in Session 27)
- **[[npcs/Ptoh-Ristus.md|Ptoh-Ristus]] (mummy)** — inert, found in the large sarcophagus with a magical golden death mask and doll-sized furniture including a tiny magic chair.
- **Two additional mummies** — emerged from the white marble sarcophagi when the large lid was removed; defeated.
- **[[items/Teleport Rug.md|Rugs of Instant Access]]** — a pair of teleportation rugs; very useful, exact limits initially unknown.
- **Death Mask of Ptoh-Ristus** — magical; provides Infravision, See Secrets, Will bonus, and bonuses to detect lies and illusions. Also contains an unidentified residual magic that defeated three separate identification attempts.
- Various coins and pit-trap loot.
