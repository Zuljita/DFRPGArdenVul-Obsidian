# Druid's Retreat

## Summary
A mostly unmapped dungeon area on its own level, connected to the [[locations/Well of Light.md|Well of Light]] level via a secret passage near the [[items/Ruby Chair.md|Ruby Chair]] throne room. The name "Druid's Retreat" is the GM's title for this map; the "druid" in question is most likely [[npcs/Gerrilad.md|Gerrilad]], the eccentric half-elven druid who claimed ownership of areas near the [[locations/Library of Thoth.md|Library of Thoth]].

## Access
- **Secret passage from the Well of Light level**: In Session 22, two secret doors were found near the Ruby Chair — one northeast and one northwest. The northwest door leads to Druid's Retreat and was explored in Session 29 (without Vael). The passage connects to the throne room area.
- The area is on a **different level** from the Well of Light.

## Known Features
- A southern section ("Druid's Retreat S") partially mapped.
- A northern section ("Druid's Retreat N") reached during Session 29 but the party moved on before a screenshot could be taken.
- The eastern exit on the known map leads both **down and east** and represents a one-time free Fast Travel point if used at the top of a session.
- Given the elevation of the Well of Light, this level may connect to the surface — a potential additional point of ingress to the dungeon.

## Notes
- Not connected to [[npcs/Fael.md|Fael]] or Gosterwick. The name derives from the dungeon map itself.
- Several unexplored passages remain, including connections that may lead to useful areas.
- The cemetery level (with mummies) is at a similar elevation and was accessed from the northern map in the same session.

## Sessions
