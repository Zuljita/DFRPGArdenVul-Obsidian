# Druid's Retreat

## Summary
A secluded location outside [[locations/Gosterwick.md|Gosterwick]] used by [[npcs/Fael.md|Fael]] the druid. Fael insists on meeting in natural settings away from urban influence when conducting druidic services such as Speak with Animals.

## Notes
- [[npcs/Fael.md|Fael]] brought the party here in Session 54 to cast Speak with Animals for [[pcs/Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios.md|Ioannes]], explaining that the town's urban sprawl interferes with nature's karma.
- Mapped on a document from the #Unfinished Business channel.
