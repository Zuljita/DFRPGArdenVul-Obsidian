# Muddy Puddle

## Summary
A tavern in [[Gosterwick]]. The [[Second Chance Company]] is known to frequent this establishment.

Members of the [[Second Chance Company]], including Geleg, Tresti, Klisko, and Samantha the Red, have been encountered here.
