# Hall of Mysteries

## Summary
A dungeon area within Arden Vul, visible on player-recovered maps alongside the Second Temple and Pit of Fire. The Hall's precise level and access route remain under active investigation.

## Notes
- Named on at least two player maps recovered from the dungeon, one showing it alongside a "Second Temple" and another showing proximity to a "Pit of Fire."
- Likely situated within or near the Thothian complex given association with temple areas on the maps.
