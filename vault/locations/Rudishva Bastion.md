# Rudishva Bastion

## Summary
A Rudishva-architecture complex reached from passages behind the Settite prison area, containing iris doors, teleportation discs, advanced devices, and multiple secured rooms.

## Notes
- Accessed with Rudishva identity plaques (notably the yellow card) for doors, force-field controls, and command permissions.
- Linked by teleporter network to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]] and other areas in the Halls.
- Contains surviving security hazards (traps, sentry turrets, undead, and hostile flora).
- Session 40 established command-tablet authority and mapped multiple teleporter links from Bastion-side control points.
- Session 41 used Bastion-connected treasury routes for deeper movement and secure fallback.
