# The Obsidian Gates

Source canonical: [[lore/The Book of Priors.md|The Book of Priors]]

## Mentioned In

Listed in the **Architecture** section of [[lore/The Book of Priors.md|The Book of Priors]] as a significant feature of the [[Halls of Arden Vul]], between the [[Forum of Set]] and the [[Lower Halls of the Archons]]. The Book of Priors also records the **Discovery of The Great Chasm and The Obsidian Gates** as a foundational event in Arden Vul's history.

## Notes

- An individual named [[Amhit]] found the Gates but was unable to teleport behind them.
- Contains a green dragon's hoard.
- A way to open the Gates is through the sequence: Lockmaster, Shape Earth, Blink.

## Inhabitants

A green dragon (per the hoard).
