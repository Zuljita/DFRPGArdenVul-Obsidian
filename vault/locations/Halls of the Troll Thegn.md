# Halls of the Troll Thegn

## Description

An area within the [[Halls of Arden Vul]], ruled by the Varumani [[npcs/Varboka.md|Thegn]]. "Troll Thegn" is the outsider Archontean name; the Varumani themselves do not use the word "troll."

## Notes

- Requires a [[items/Varumani Passport.md|Varumani passport]] for access.
- Accessible via a free 'fast travel' through the Great Cavern route at the start of a session.
- "Troll" is the Archontean word used to describe the Varumani — considered derogatory by the Varumani themselves. See [[lore/Varumani.md|Varumani lore]].
- The loyal faction controls the Varumani Lifts and the Varumani Market.
- The Varumani are a spacefaring people.
- The Varumani are big, strong, smart, and can regenerate unless killed by fire or acid.
- The faction is currently threatened by Kerbog Khan, who reportedly kidnaps Varumani.
- They are considered the most powerful faction in the Halls.
- Renegade Varumani who operate outside the Thegn's authority have been encountered as hostiles (Session 52b/53).
- They eventually became loyal Imperial subjects to Marius Tricotor.
