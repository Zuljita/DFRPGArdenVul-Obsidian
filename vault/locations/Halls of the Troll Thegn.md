# Halls of the Troll Thegn

## Description

An area within the [[Halls of Arden Vul]], ruled by the Varumani [[npcs/Varboka.md|Thegn]]. "Troll Thegn" is the outsider Archontean name; the Varumani themselves do not use the word "troll."

## Notes

- Requires a [[items/Varumani Passport.md|Varumani passport]] for access.
- Accessible via a free 'fast travel' through the Great Cavern route at the start of a session.
- "Troll" is the Archontean word used to describe the Varumani — considered derogatory by the Varumani themselves. See [[lore/Varumani.md|Varumani lore]].
