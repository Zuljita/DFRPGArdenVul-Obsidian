# Forum of Set

## Summary
The Forum of Set was a large Settite compound in the Halls of Arden Vul, operated by the [[factions/Cult of Set.md|Cult of Set]]. It served as a marketplace, administrative hub, slave market, and fortified base for the cult. In Sessions 43a–43c, the Right for Riches company, allied with King [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]]'s goblins, the Thorcin mercenaries (led by [[npcs/Lella and the Rippers.md|Lella the Ripper]]), and allied merchants, swept the cult out and liberated its slaves. The Forum is now under goblin control, administered by [[npcs/Gribble.md|Gribble]], Lord of the Goblin Forum, on behalf of King Weskenim.

## Access
- **Cliff Face Entrance**: Approximately 800 feet down the cliff face, marked with a red lantern. Guarded by cultists; has a basket for lowering/raising people.
- **Sighing Stair**: From the former [[locations/Temple of Set.md|Temple of Set]] above (now cleared).
- **Thothian Teleportation Circle**: Located in the finished room near the cliff entrance (stairs up).

## Layout (Sessions 43a–43c)

### Entry from Cliff Face
- **Rough cavern passages** north to a finished Rudishva/Settite room with Settite statues and stairs up.
- **Thothian teleportation circle** at the top of the stairs.
- **Red granite bridge** ([[locations/Red Bridge of Set.md|Red Bridge of Set]]) flanked by Settite statues, spanning a chasm.
- **Torchlit balcony** in the far corner of the chasm — cultists used it as a leisure area. Has levers: combinations release poison gas or nothing useful. A **hanging chest** (with platinum bars, magic helmet, wrapped stone) was suspended in the chasm here; the chest had a wind trap.
- **Force field** at the bridge's Forum-side entrance, blocking access from the chasm side (bypassed by flying across to the balcony).

### Northern Passages
- **Small Settite temple** (empty of personnel on invasion day).
- **Lounge area** (empty).
- **Kitchen** — location of the first major battle; many cultists and guards made a stand here. A cook escaped.
- **Storeroom** (north of kitchen, locked) — full of dried foods.
- **Dormitory rooms** (3, northeast) — bunk beds; two empty, one occupied by goblins buying food on invasion day.
- **Locked door east** — led to a room full of zombies (Settite business, left alone).
- **Wide, well-lit north-south passage** — primary artery of the Forum.
- **Guard rooms** (south of passage) — tables, chairs, dice.
- **Door spiked shut** (west wall of passage) — Mithric lettering: "Set Vanquishes the Serpent"; painted warning in Archontean: "No access! Stay out! This means you!"
- **Magical Darkness area** + plaque listing the Laws of the Forum. Goggles allow sight through the darkness.

### The Forum Proper
- **Central forum area** — large chamber, the heart of the compound. Contains a **Set animal statue** in the center (was emitting strange gas; neutralized in Session 46 by The GOAT). 
- **Slaves** were chained to the statue and to stakes in the northeast corner at the time of invasion.
- **Merchant stalls** in the southeast: [[npcs/Galadnor.md|Galadnor]] (magical supplies, Elven spy), [[npcs/Horace Trych.md|Horace Trych]] (goods, Lady Alexia's agent), moneychanger's stall (with stone chest, trapped).
- **Pantry** to the north of the Forum (found in Session 44) — contained food given to goblins.

### Western/Northwestern Territory
- **Guest rooms** (unoccupied) — suitable for Teleport Rug use.
- **Settite vault** (southwest, three keyholes) — contained coin, gems, magic items, weapons, supplies; guarded by a bipedal boar construct. Looted in Session 43c.
- **Spy tunnels** behind a secret door (via boar statue room) — peepholes into several rooms; doors leading to: a map room, a room with a wraith (not defeated), and a room with a skeleton holding a platinum goblet.
- **Boar statue room** with caged boars (killed by Vallium with the Returning Javelin in Session 44).
- **Sandy-floored area** — hid giant scorpions and a buried strongbox (Session 44).
- **Library of Set** — contains [[npcs/Alexandros.md|Alexandros]] the librarian; significant book collection.
- **Settite bedrooms** — thoroughly searched in Session 44.
- **Helena the accountant's office** — She was captured in Session 43c.
- **Guard barracks** and map room to the north.
- **Rough caverns** to the north (not yet explored).

### Ambassador Quarters (East/Southeast)
- Rooms occupied by a **Varumani ambassador** ([[npcs/Yoburra.md|Yoburra]]) and **Frost Giant ambassadors** (asked about a human female wizard with long black hair).

### Guild of Service Area (East)
- Slave pits — four types found: better-fed slaves, average slaves, low-value slaves, and a pit of mostly dead/dying slaves.
- All survivors freed and directed to Gosterwick.

## Current Status (Session 44+)
Under goblin control. [[npcs/Gribble.md|Gribble]] (Lord of the Goblin Forum) oversees security with a dozen goblin guards. The goblins are focused on making the route from [[locations/Upper Goblintown.md|Upper Goblintown]] to the Forum safe. A food stall is operated by [[npcs/Dworm.md|Dworm]] (standing in until a proper merchant moves in). Varumani ambassadors are buying food from the surface and providing economic support. Several surface merchants (including [[npcs/Galadnor.md|Galadnor]], [[npcs/Horace Trych.md|Horace Trych]], and Claudine of Narsileon) have stalls here.
