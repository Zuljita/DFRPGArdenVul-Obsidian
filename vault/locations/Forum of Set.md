# Forum of Set

## Summary
A Setite-controlled forum/compound in Arden Vul tied to the [[factions/Cult of Set.md|Cult of Set]].

## Notes
- Serves as an operational/social space connected to Setite activity and personnel.
- In Session 34c prisoner testimony, Settite slavers sold captives to Tikun Thane through a slave market operating in/through the forum.
