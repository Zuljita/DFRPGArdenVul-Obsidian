## Discord Insights

- A location under Arden Vul that contains seven statues, where archons built their tombs. ([[notes/Discord Summary 2025-W28.md|Discord Summary 2025-W28]])
- Have been renamed Upper G... ([[notes/Discord Summary 2025-W44.md|Discord Summary 2025-W44]])
- The old pyramid of Thoth is used by everyone to enter the halls.
- West to map where the 3 portcullis are, south, down stairwell, find yourself on a map that is one map east of the Well of Light map that has the Library on it.
- The Library of Thoth is located near the Well of Light.
- There is an Overseer of Thoth ([[notes/ancient letter from Uriel Basileon to Kel Bormol.md|ancient letter from Uriel Basileon to Kel Bormol]])
- The main library room was cleared and its books moved to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]] in Session 37.
- Adjacent to the Library of Thoth is a secret room — the **Secret Library of Thoth** — containing a much rarer and more dangerous collection.
- A lever on top of the indoor pyramid can be used to teleport to another indoor pyramid on the Well of Light level.
- The location features a Chief Librarian named Hellas.
- Stamelis, the Librarian of Thoth, exists as a magically animated head.
- The location was an ancient place where Kel Bormol served as Overseer and Archmage, associated with the collapse of Arden Vul approximately 1200 years ago. ([[npcs/Thoth.md#Discord Insights|Thoth]])
- Recent encounters have brought new political shifts in the Halls of Thoth to light. ([[notes/Discord Summary 2025-W44.md|Discord Summary 2025-W44]])
- Halflings under the pyramid were engaged in a major construction project, building new doors around the Glory of Thoth. ([[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]])
- The Church of Thoth maintains a teleportation network in Arden Vul using glass activation tiles.
- Travelers are warned not to enter the Temple of Thoth without a holy symbol of Thoth due to hostile Ibis Guardians.
- Numerous statues of Thoth are rumored to have eyes made of huge gemstones; removing them allows contact with the deity but causes the statues to animate and attack. ([[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]])
