[[sessions/Session 29 - The Tower of Scrutiny.md|Session 29 - The Tower of Scrutiny]]]]; halfling-slayer patches awarded.

- A room to the west contains three sarcophagi (two open, one magical/closed) and a giant centipede.
- The area is part of a goblin territory with at least two distinct elevations (Upper and Lower).
- An iconic location associated with a group achievement.
- The secret room reached via burial niches in Session 23c contained a stashed wheelbarrow of food.
- During navigation, Vael used Shape Earth to cover the ends of the passage created next to the one-way door.
- The territory includes the Wet Caves.
- The goblin military force includes officers such as Killik and Bottleneck, while the court is managed by the major-domo Palestrim. ([[factions/Goblins.md#Summary]])
- The goblin military force includes ogre guards and warriors led by officers such as Killik and Bottleneck.
- The area was formerly known as the Glory of Thoth.
- The goblin territory has recently expanded to include the conquered Forum of Set, where Weskenim has appointed his brother Gribble as Lord. ([[factions/Goblins.md#Summary]])
- Efforts are underway to clear the area south of the basket elevator to ensure it is safe for goblin patrols. ([[factions/Goblins.md#Summary]])
- The territory also encompasses Goblintown. ([[factions/United Goblins.md#Summary]])
- [[npcs/Hidlat.md|Hidlat]] was present during the events of [[sessions/Session 23c - Set Jailbreak and Down to Goblintown.md|Session 23c]].
- The Eighth Collegium, a group associated with Lukor, has been encountered in the area.
- Considered a more formal area compared to the Goblin Warrens.
