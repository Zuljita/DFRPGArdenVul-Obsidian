# Upper Goblintown

[[sessions/Session 29 - The Tower of Scrutiny.md|Session 29 - The Tower of Scrutiny]]]]; halfling-slayer patches awarded.
- Session 36 — Party re-entered through this district en route to lower goblin-held areas during prisoner/door negotiations.
- Session 38 — Party moved back through this district during anti-Set operations; goblin troop rotation and veteran "halfling slayer" badges were discussed.
