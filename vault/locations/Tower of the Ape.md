# Tower of the Ape

## Summary
The Tower of the Ape is a fortified tower complex in [[locations/Newmarket.md|Newmarket]], owned and operated by the wizard [[npcs/Tikun Thane.md|Tikun Thane]]. It was connected to Arden Vul via a teleporter, allowing Thane to use it as a staging base for operations in the Halls. Thane fled to [[locations/Narsileon.md|Narsileon]] following the Right for Riches company's assault and the tower was abandoned. A **low-mana zone** surrounds the tower.

## Access
The tower was reached via a concealed teleporter in the caverns beneath Arden Vul (in a room Thane used as a research base). The teleporter command word is **"noobab"** (determined by Vael's repeated Analyze Magic castings in Session 34a). The teleporter exits into a small closet with a heavy, secure metal door and a sliding peephole.

## Layout

### Teleporter Arrival Room
- Small closet-sized room with stone walls and a large metal door (secure, with sliding peephole).
- The inactive teleporter frame is mounted on the back wall.
- The guards on the other side are visible through the peephole.

### Ground/Lower Levels
- **Murder room** (entry combat zone): Site of the initial fight against [[factions/Disciplined.md|Disciplined]] guards and trained albino baboons with spears. A murder hole in the ceiling allowed grenade drops — a Disciplined dropped an Alchemist's Fire grenade through it; Ioannes blocked it with his shield and survived via his Salamander Amulet.
- **Weapon rack**: A disarmed Disciplined ran to a northeast-corner weapon rack to rearm.
- **Barracks, storeroom, magical cold storage**: Found during Session 34c search. Cold storage was full of fresh meat.

### Mid Levels
- **Balcony**: Wraps around the middle of the tower. Has a small shack/outhouse on it.
- **Library**: One magical book (a spellbook; cover warned that anyone taking it would be killed) taken by the party. About half the books were gone when revisited in Session 34c — Tikun Thane had taken them.
- **Dining room and kitchen**: Below the library level. A few jars of spices taken from the kitchen.
- **Spiral stairs** running between levels.

### Upper/Attic Level
- An attic room with no exits except the staircase, containing a backpack and clothes. Vael gathered these.

### Teleporter Research Room (Arden Vul Side)
This was not in the tower itself but in the Arden Vul caverns — the room where the party found the teleporter portal:
- **Chessboard**: Covered by a linen cloth and a pyramidal glass cover. Black-and-white squares (non-standard; traditional Archontean is purple and orange). Six black pieces, some piles of dust, and one non-standard piece — a copper-skinned little man wearing robes covered with eyes, carrying a twisted staff. He was looking up and gesturing upward. (The party replaced the cloth.)
- **Baboon statue**: Thothian style, not obviously magical.
- **Manacles on the wall**: Covered in white fur (similar manacles in the secret corridor outside).
- **Work table**: Potions, scrolls, a notebook with spells, a padded bag with a complete set of **6 glass activation squares**, a decent alchemy lab, and the book "**[[npcs/Kel Bormol.md|Kel Bormol]]'s Chess Diary**" (in Mithric).
- **Magic sink** along the north wall.
- **Chest**: Behind a screen with a bed. Contained human men's clothes, gold coins, and a small box with **6 black opals**.
- **Teleporter frame**: A large magical wooden frame with Thothian decorations mounted to the southwest wall. Command word "noobab."

### Basement / Sub-Levels (Session 34c)
- **Secret door** in the tower (found by Ioannes): locked, resisted Lockmaster → Vael used Shape Earth to punch through the adjacent wall.
- **Wizard's lab**: Mostly emptied. Some lab equipment remaining, a few broken pieces of magical items.
- **Locked door** in the lab: Resisted Vael's Lockmaster spell entirely.
- **Stairs down** to prison level.
- **Prison level** (two cells):
  - Cell 1: Three human men — captured by Settite slavers, sold to Tikun Thane at the slave market in the [[locations/Forum of Set.md|Forum of Set]], trained as Disciplined fighters under coercion (fed raw meat, forced to fight each other with sticks while Disciplined guards gave instructions and whipped losers).
  - Cell 2: Two baboons (Bobo and Bifki). A **no-mana zone** was found in one corner of the baboons' cell.
- **Storeroom** (accessed through Shape Earth hole next to the resistant door): Shelves mostly emptied of valuables. Ioannes took mushrooms; Vael took empty chests.

## Current Status (After Session 34c)
- Tikun Thane fled to Narsileon via apparent magical transit (Vael's Seeker/Trace located him there 3 days after the battle).
- The tower-side teleporter was **deactivated** (not destroyed) by Tikun Thane before leaving. Vael later removed the physical portal assembly and transported it back to Gosterwick.
- The tower is abandoned — no signs of Disciplined or baboons when revisited.
- Three human prisoners were freed. Two of the freed men returned to their farms. The third, **[[npcs/Basil.md|Basil of Narsileon]]**, was hired by the Right for Riches company as their first employee.
- The two baboons (Bobo and Bifki) were escorted via the Rug back to the Beacon, then Blinked to the surface and released — they found their own way back to the [[locations/Howling Caves.md|Howling Caves]].
