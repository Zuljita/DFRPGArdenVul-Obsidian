# Tower of the Ape

## Summary
A fortified tower near the cliff face used by [[Tikun Thane]] and his followers.

## Notes
- Contains a teleporter frame that connected to deep ruins access routes, but the far-side frame was later disabled during Thane's withdrawal.
- Included multiple defended levels, a library, barracks, cells, and a laboratory area.
- Served as holding site for kidnapped baboons and enslaved human prisoners.
- Heavy fighting in Sessions 34a/34b killed many Disciplined defenders and trained baboons, but Tikun Thane escaped.
- In Session 34c, the party returned, freed surviving prisoners, and removed the non-functioning portal frame for transport.
