# Tower of the Ape

## Summary
A fortified tower near the cliff face used by [[Tikun Thane]] and his followers.

## Notes
- Contains a teleporter frame that connected to deep ruins access routes.
- Included multiple defended levels, a library, barracks, cells, and a laboratory area.
- Served as holding site for kidnapped baboons and enslaved human prisoners.
