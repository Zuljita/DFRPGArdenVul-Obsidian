# Tower of the Ape

## Summary
A fortified tower complex connected to the caverns south of the [[locations/Howling Caves.md|Howling Caves]]. In Session 34a, the party reached it via a teleporter and encountered human guards with scimitars and trained albino baboons with spears.

## Notes
- A concealed route from nearby caverns led into worked passages and a teleporter-enabled chamber tied to tower defenses.
- The tower-side arrival room had a secure metal door and visible guard post beyond the peephole.
- Defenders used layered internal defenses including murder-hole grenade drops, locked doors, and stair choke points with rotating reinforcement groups.
- Explored levels included a mid-level balcony, attic room, and lower domestic/study spaces (library, dining room, kitchen), indicating sustained habitation and logistics capacity.
- In Session 34c, the tower-side teleporter was confirmed deactivated (not destroyed), and the party later removed the physical portal assembly for transport.
- In Session 34c, deeper searches found mostly-emptied lab/storage spaces, a concealed access route, and prison cells holding three human captives plus two baboons used in Thane's training operation.
- The detached portal frame was carried back to Gosterwick by Vael.
- As of February 2026, the tower's gates were closed and the snow outside was undisturbed.
- The tower's defenders included 'Disciplined' soldiers.
- During the battle, Tikun Thane was forced to retreat three times.
- At the conclusion of the battle, Ioannes was left unconscious on the Arden Vul side of the teleporter.
