# Temple of Demma

## Discord Insights

- A temple dedicated to Demma, where services can be cast at a 'book rate' under certain conditions; has full-time clerics and is one of the individual temples on the Hill of the Gods. ([[notes/Discord Summary 2025-W11.md|Discord Summary 2025-W11]])
- A modest but well-maintained structure, it serves as a center for healing and rural devotion and is a frequent destination for the Right for Riches Company to find refuge. ([[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]])
- The temple contains torches and areas of 'Continual Light'. ([[notes/Temple.md|Temple]])
- The priesthood includes branches such as the Seedkeepers, who guard ancient agricultural knowledge and sacred fertility rites. ([[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]])
- An incident at the Yellow Cloak Inn involving an accidental Continual Darkness spell resulted in Demma's clerics being forbidden from casting spells on those premises. ([[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]])
- The party plans to return to the temple at night. ([[notes/Temple.md|Temple]])
- Worshipers often bring offerings of seeds, bread, and woven charms to honor her blessings. ([[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]])
- The goddess's sacred weapon is the flail, symbolizing both the harvest and divine retribution. ([[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]])
