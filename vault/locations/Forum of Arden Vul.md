# Forum of Arden Vul

## Summary
An outdoor surface-level plaza area in the ruins of Arden Vul. Contains [[npcs/Tuftwillig.md|Tuftwillig]], a sentient ancient oak tree, as its most notable inhabitant. Located near the entrance to the [[locations/Pyramid of Thoth.md|Pyramid of Thoth]] and used as a travel route through the surface ruins.

## Notable Features
- **[[npcs/Tuftwillig.md|Tuftwillig]]** — A large sentient ancient oak tree that can converse when Beast Speech or Plant Speech is active. Dislikes [[npcs/Craastonistorex.md|Craastonistorex]] the green dragon and the undead in the [[locations/Tower of Scrutiny.md|Tower of Scrutiny]]. Claimed he might be able to summon bears and crocodiles to aid against the dragon, though he does not trust crocodiles. Complained that the Tower of Scrutiny blocks his view of the sunrise.

## Connections
- [[locations/Pyramid of Thoth.md|Pyramid of Thoth]] — entrance to Thothian underground areas is accessible from the Forum area
- [[locations/Tower of Scrutiny.md|Tower of Scrutiny]] — visible from the Forum; Tuftwillig wants it knocked down
