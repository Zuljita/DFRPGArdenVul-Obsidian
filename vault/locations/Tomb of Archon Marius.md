# Tomb of Archon Marius

## Summary
A heavily guarded tomb complex beneath Arden Vul dedicated to Marius Tricotor, a great Archontean archon. Accessed via a secret trap door from a hall of Marius statues, the tomb is actively watched over by the Oathbound Knights — ancient soldiers in yellow "MI" tabards who have stood guard since the Archontean era. First fully entered in Session 54.

## Layout

### Hall of Statues (Approach)
A large room filled with statues of Marius Tricotor, including one lesser statue of a younger man bowing to Marius, identified by inscription as Lucius Cornelius, scribe and confidant. A twist-ring mechanism on one statue's hand opens a trap door in the ceiling.

### Antechamber / Oathbound Knights' Post
Accessed via a very tight passage from the trap door. Eight Oathbound Knights stand guard here under a Knight Commander. They permit honorable visitors who do not steal, break anything, or make excessive noise. The knights wear ancient yellow tabards marked "MI."

### Main Chamber
Contains four statues of spearmen, four statues of Marius in various poses (with indentations around skull, eyes, and chest), and a speaking head of Marius that delivers a magical speech. Doors lead to two stairways and locked side rooms.

### Locked Knight Room
A room containing the corpses of ancient knights, magical armor and weapons, a key, and three maps:
- A map of the City of Arden Vul before its destruction
- A map of Burdock's Valley, long ago
- A map of the area around the Tomb of Marius

### Spiral Stairs
Lead up hundreds of feet to a cellar beneath the surface on the northern island containing the Donjon — a Thothian teleportation ring is present. The island is reportedly guarded by deadly constructs and patrolled near a dragon.

### Straight Stairs
Lead to a shorter passage ending in a flooded area. Fishman corpses found; narrow stairs lead down into water.

### Yellow Pool Room
Hidden behind a secret door off the main varumani area. A pool of yellow water that grants temporary magical boons (Shield, Might, Protection from Evil) with brief individual effects; Ioannes gained a permanent +1 ST from drinking.

### Frozen Passages
A door marked "Entrance Forbidden — This means you!" (Varumani script) leads south to snowy passages and an icy room with a thawed fountain guarded by four animating spearman statues.

## Notes
- The tomb is accessed via bestial varumani territory; the party cleared the varumani to reach it in Session 54.
- The Oathbound Knights appear to be magically bound guardians, not truly hostile unless their rules are violated.
- Vallium had a key copied by Theopilos the Locksmith and replaced it before the knights noticed.
- Three maps were found and copied by Vael using the Copy spell.
- Confirmed connection to the Drowned Canyon via a constructed spillway visible from a passage near the Varumani Lifts barrier.
