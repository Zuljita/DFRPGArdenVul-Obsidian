# Arena

## Summary
The Arena is a large underground entertainment complex on the Arena Level of the Halls of Arden Vul. It is run by the [[npcs/Arena Lord.md|Arena Lord]], a [[Rudishva]] being who hosts gladiatorial spectacles. The Arena is located near the Varumani Lifts and the [[locations/Inn of the Lost.md|Inn of the Lost]].

The Arena Lord is tentatively identified as Crellik-Var, the Entertainment Officer of the crashed starship the Beacon.

## Access
- Located near [[npcs/Gog.md|Gog]]'s underground grotto.
- Reachable from the Goblin Forum level via a stairway to the south.
- The Varumani Lifts can take visitors to the Floating Gardens or all the way across the Great Chasm.
- Located above the level of the Troll Thegn.

## Layout
- **Bleachers:** Seating for spectators, mostly goblins. The Arena Lord has his own box on the far side of the Arena.
- **Arena Sands:** The combat floor where gladiatorial matches take place. Violence is not permitted outside the sands.
- **Corner Bar:** A bar run by [[npcs/Hal.md|Hal]], a Varumani bartender. Sells drinks and food (mushroom cakes, etc.) before and during spectacles.
- **Gamblers' Area:** Where bettors settle their bets after matches.
- **Monster Cages:** A room with rows of cages containing arena monsters: giant beetles, hell hounds, mephits, undead, a manticore, a chimera, and owlbears.
- **Varumani Lifts:** Nearby lifts operated by Varumani staff, giving access to the Floating Gardens and across the Great Chasm.

## The Arena Lord
The Arena is presided over by the [[npcs/Arena Lord.md|Arena Lord]], a Rudishva known as [[npcs/Arena Lord.md|Rudishva]], who announces spectacles from his box. He wears a purple jumpsuit, a white scarf, and a red bracelet (possibly a Rudishva Command Bracelet).
- Temrin's map indicated a second Rudishva Command Bracelet was located in the Arena.

## Spectacle Days
The Arena holds regular "Spectacle Days," during which gladiators fight monsters or each other. Bets are placed and settled in the gamblers' area. Known gladiators include:
- **[[npcs/Yerk.md|Yerk]]** — Currently ranked #3 in the Arena. Human. Crowd favorite, described as "a total psycho." Known for efficiency over showmanship.
- **Basil** — Gladiator (distinct from [[npcs/Basil of Narsileon.md|Basil of Narsileon]]).
- **Nisdis** — Elven gladiator.

## Welcome Message
Near the older statues at the arena's entrance, a booming voice welcomes visitors and announces that violence is not permitted outside the sands — repeated in two other unknown languages.

## Nearby: Merchant Stalls (House of Isocorax Passage)
A passage northeast of the Varumani Lifts leads to a Rudishva-architecture room with four merchant stalls. The chemist warned that the passage to the north leads to death.
- **[[npcs/Virgoolian.md|Virgoolian]]** — Goblin chemist. Sells standard healing potions and his own "Virgoolian's Special Brew" (causes hallucinations).
- **[[npcs/Swiddy.md|Swiddy]]** — Baker.
- **[[npcs/Aebbe Long-Nose.md|Aebbe Long-Nose]]** — Cleric.
- **[[npcs/Philippous.md|Philippous]]** — Weapons dealer.

## Connections
- [[locations/Inn of the Lost.md|Inn of the Lost]] — Located east of the merchant stalls, accessible through an unlocked door.
- [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]] — The pool of green liquid in the Rudishva command chamber southeast of the Arena level displays camera feeds including one of the Arena.
- A second Rudishva [[notes/Command Bracelet.md|Command Bracelet]] is believed to be located somewhere in the Arena complex, per [[npcs/Temrin.md|Temrin]]'s map.
