# Great Cavern

## Summary
The Great Cavern is a vast underground cavern system accessible from Arden Vul, below the surface ruins and connected to multiple dungeon levels. It contains a large underground lake, a cliff dividing the northern and southern sections, several distinct cave systems (northeastern, western, southeastern), and zones controlled by different factions. The Scepter of the Goblins was discovered here in Session 35.

## Geography

### The Lake and Cliff
- A large underground lake occupies the central/southern portion.
- A cliff divides the northern (higher) and southern sections.
- There is a **pillar** rising from the lake.
- An **island in the northeast** portion of the lake (below the cliff) contains a reptilian statue, two phase salamanders (Session 36: killed by the party), and a small pile of gear including magic torches and a magic cat statuette.

### Access Routes
- From Arden Vul: Through beastman territory (requires negotiating passage with [[npcs/Count Skleros.md|Count Skleros]] or crossing at the barricade border), or via the secret entrance at the Cliff Face (southeastern side caves → secret exit).
- From the Beacon: Via Rug of Instant Access to the Beacon, then through the relevant cave routes.
- To Upper Goblintown: Through beastman rooms and northern passages to the Pyramid of Thoth.

## Key Areas

### Northeastern Cave System (Sessions 35–36)
Accessed by flying/levitating over the lake or via cliff routes from the north:
- **Stirge cave**: Connects to the northeast complex. A dozen stirges ambushed Uvash here (Session 35); they fled through a crack in the south wall, which Vael sealed with Shape Earth.
- **Urn room** (six urns, Session 35):
  - Urn 1: Contained the **[[items/Scepter of the Goblins.md|Scepter of the Goblins]]** (surrounded by wooden sticks/scraps)
  - Urn 2: Black sand
  - Urn 3: Small white rocks (likely calcite, not valuable)
  - Urn 4: Disgusting rotten plant material
  - Urn 5: Oil floating on water
  - Urn 6: Water floating on a large block of gold (heavy — Vallium could barely move the urn even with Lighten Burden)
  - Wooden ladder remains on east wall; holes in floor where it was based
- **Secret door** high on the east wall of the urn room: Required a ladder to reach. Beyond was a **room with zombies and construct spearmen** (Session 35):
  - Two zombies (quickly killed) and two construct spearmen (tougher; three good hits to destroy one, then surrounded and smashed the other)
  - **Sarcophagus** on the far wall: Decayed remains buried with many ancient silver pennies, a **magical torc**, a **golden ceremonial helmet**, an **ebony magic wand**, and a few potions
- **Skeleton cavern** (Session 36, explored northeast via different route): Full of animated skeletons — half human, half stirge. Defeated by Basilisk (Kiai), Vallium/Uvash (smashing), Vael (Shape Air to smash stirge skeletons against walls). Loot: magical scale armor, coins, gems, flasks, scroll case.
- **Cold water pool** (cave to the north of skeleton cavern): Near-freezing pool. Vallium dove (three times) and recovered a **limed-over skeleton** with a pouch and a **bronze rod** from the bottom.

### Western Cave System
- Accessed through a secret door in the west wall of the Great Cavern (known to Vallium).
- Contains the **fungal forester** cave: The foresters made room for the party to pass through toward [[npcs/Ambrosia.md|Ambrosia]]'s cave.
- **Ambrosia's cave** (behind a secret door in the fungal forester cave): Session 36 — Ambrosia was not present; six of her beastmen were, looking malnourished. Vael lured them back to the Great Cavern with a promise of food, then used Mass Daze to pacify them while Deino's beastmen led them north to Deino's territory.

### Southeastern Cave System
- Contains a **large beastman stone chest** (in side caves west of the secret exit route): Too heavy to lift; freshly applied poison on the lock (Session 36: Vael unlocked and opened with Lockmaster/Apportation without touching it; found only supplies, not valuables; magically relocked).
- A **secret exit** on the Cliff Face side accessed via southeastern passages.

## Faction Zones

### Children of Deino (North/East)
- Controlled the northern areas including a **supply cage** with a door on the north wall leading deeper into their territory.
- A beastman sergeant and guards were present in the cage area during Session 36 rescue operation.
- A squad of Deino's beastmen maintained an alert post at the cage.

### Children of Ambrosia (West, fragmented)
- Located in caves to the west, beyond the fungal foresters.
- Six of Ambrosia's beastmen were recovered and returned to Deino's territory in Session 36 after Ambrosia's disappearance.

### Fungal Foresters (West Cave)
- Inhabit the western cave between the Great Cavern and Ambrosia's cave.
- Do not speak Common but moved to make room for the party (apparently on good terms with Ioannes's gestures).

## Notable Events

### Session 35 — Scepter Discovery
The Right for Riches company located the [[items/Scepter of the Goblins.md|Scepter of the Goblins]] using Vael's Seeker/Trace spell. It was a three-foot-long hollowed legbone (probably ogre or troll bone), with holes drilled along one side — effectively a giant flute. It was confirmed magical. The gold-filled urn from the same cache was too heavy to easily carry; Coinbase's Lighten Burden spell let Vallium move it through the Rug to the Beacon.

### Session 36 — Beastman Rescue
The Children of Ambrosia's beastmen (charmed or displaced) were recovered and handed off to Deino's people. Count Skleros thanked the party for recovering his lost troopers (who were being de-charmed by "Mother"). The operation was complicated by a Mass Daze needed to stop the sergeant from raising the alarm.
