# Vault

## Summary
A location reachable via the Rudishva Teleportation Pad, part of an exploration route involving the Beacon and the Well of Light.
- The route to the Vault via the Rudishva Teleportation Pad includes passing through an acidic pool room and a room containing Chaotic Sweat.
- Certain items, such as the Pale Green Horn, are secured within the Vault and require a Yellow Captain's Card for access.
- The secure vault is located near the Cloister.
