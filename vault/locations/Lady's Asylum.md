# Lady's Asylum

## Summary
A site of historical importance dedicated to Lady Clementia.

Lady Clementia was the wife of Marius.

Vallium Halcyon conducted research into the site using the *Book of Priors*.

The party planned to use *Scry Gate* to observe if the snow at the asylum had melted.
- The party intended to use the [[notes/Thothian Teleportation Circle Network.md|Archon's Palace teleportation circle]] to *Scry Gate* the site.
- The site is considered one of the suspected haunts of the "Bad Neighbours", with plans to investigate it using *See Invisible*, *Darkvision*, and *Potion of Clairvoyance*.
- Teleportation Code: `BL-GR-BK-WH-RE-YE`
- Upon arrival, the party encountered invisible halflings armed with Rudishva laser rifles.
