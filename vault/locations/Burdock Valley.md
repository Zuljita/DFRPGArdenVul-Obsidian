# Burdock Valley

## Description

A valley that was under the control of [[Arden Vul]].
- The area covers approximately 90x80 miles and contains [[Gosterwick]], [[Newmarket]], six small villages, and a couple of castles.
- Most inhabitants are Thorcin.
- It was the former domain of [[Lord Iskander Burdock]], who died approximately 25 years ago.
- [[Gosterwick]] is located 10 miles south of the ruins of Arden Vul.
- [[Newmarket]] is located three days south of [[Gosterwick]].
- The dragon attack on his men-at-arms resulted in 10 deaths, including three knights.
- Following the dragon attack, Lord Burdock forbade subjects from using the [[Long Stair]] to reach the ruins and discouraged outsiders from using the valley as a base for exploration.
- The only survivors of the encounter were two young men who had been kept back to hold horses.
