# Statue of Vul

## Summary
One of two colossal statues of the ancient heroes [[Waterfall]] and [[Long Stair]].

## Notes
The statue does not appear to be magical.
- The head of the statue is currently missing.
- There is a theory that searching for a secret entrance behind the statue might be fruitful.
- The site of Arden Vul was discovered in 829 AEP.
