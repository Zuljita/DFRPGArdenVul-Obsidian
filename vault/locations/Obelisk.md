# Obelisk
- Contains writing about the [[Beacon]].

## Notes
- Features writing listing the gods of the old pantheon and old emperors.
- Has an ankh-shaped recess.
- The top section is full of iconography related to the sun.
- Discovered by Thothians while building temples under Arden Vul.
- Related to demon-worshipping hoppers.
- Built around rather than destroyed.
