# Neferet's Tomb

## Summary
A hidden tomb complex accessed from secret doors near the [[Great Hall]], used as Neferet's lair.

## Notes
- Included a dispel corridor that Neferet used during her retreat.
- Site of a multi-phase battle involving mummies, shadows, darkness, and force effects.
