# Neferet's Tomb

## Summary
A hidden tomb complex accessed from secret doors near the [[sessions/Session 42a - Neferet.md|Session 42a - Neferet]]

The tomb is located off the [[locations/Great Hall.md|Great Hall]].

An expedition into the tomb encountered a magical suppression zone and [[npcs/Neferet.md|Neferet]].
