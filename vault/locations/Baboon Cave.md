# Baboon Cave

## Summary
Cave areas adjacent to or within the [[locations/Howling Caves.md|Howling Caves]] network, inhabited by the baboon factions under [[npcs/Yamki.md|Yamki]] and [[npcs/Bumpko.md|Bumpko]]. The Baboon Cave and the associated Lesser Baboon Caves to the south form the baboons' core territory.

## Notes
- [[npcs/Yamki.md|Yamki]] (3-armed giant intelligent baboon) holds the main cave; [[npcs/Bumpko.md|Bumpko]] (4-armed) is a key associate.
- The party regularly delivers food to the baboons here, maintaining a friendly relationship.
- [[npcs/The Master.md|The Master]] previously protected this territory; [[npcs/The Thane.md|The Thane]] raided it to kidnap young baboons.
- The Lesser Baboon Caves extend south of the main cave area.
