# Lower Shrines and Tombs

## Summary
A large room on a level below the main dungeon entrance, accessed via stairs near the Forum of Set area. Explored in Session 24b.

## Key Features
- Two rows of statues of Archontean heroes (some statues missing).
- Eight shrines along the walls; seven closed behind magical force fields. The one open shrine contains a book, a quill, and Mithric writing.
- A large ibis-headed statue of [[npcs/Thoth.md|Thoth]] with orange gas descending from its beak and Mithric writing about pneuma.
- Several old corpses, apparently violently killed; contained weapons, armor, coins, potions, a scroll, a magic sling, and a magic shield. ([[sessions/Session 24b - The Set Cult Strikes Back, Larel's Stuff, and the Hall of Shrines.md|Session 24b]])

## Notes
- Vael had theories about the Thoth statue and the shrine but the group deferred deeper investigation.
- The seven force-field-closed shrines remain unexplored.
