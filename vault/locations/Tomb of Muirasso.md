# Tomb of Muirasso
- Undead (zombies, ghouls)

## Notes
A dangerous location due to the powerful undead within.
- Has a secret entrance where corpses containing crystals and pearls were found outside.
- A green necklace was found on a corpse within the decoy burial chamber.
- Zombies emerged from bas reliefs in the walls during encounters.
- The tomb contained gas that required Purify Air to clear.
- The wight Muirasso jumped out of his sarcophagus holding a glowing broadsword to attack Vallium.
- Muirasso, a powerful undead wight, attacks with a glowing broadsword.
- Muirasso jumped out of his sarcophagus to attack the party upon being disturbed.
- Muirasso is a former cleric of Thoth.
- He is hostile and territorial, draining life force.
- The party defeated Muirasso in a major battle within the tomb.
