# Gosterwick

## Summary
Gosterwick is a boom town in the foothills near the ruins of [[locations/Arden Vul.md|Arden Vul]], ruled by [[npcs/Lady Alexia Basileon.md|Lady Alexia Basileon]] (the Green Lady). Originally a tiny Thorcin farming village, it was transformed about 20 years ago when Lady Alexia arrived with her [[factions/Knights of the Azure Shield.md|Knights of the Azure Shield]], built a keep, and began expanding it into a proper town. Population is now probably a couple thousand. The local name "Gosterwick" stuck even though the Archonteans tried to give it a formal name.

The nearby ruins of Arden Vul are clearly the source of the money flowing into the area, drawing adventurers, merchants, and craftspeople who hope to get a share of it.

## Layout
- **Keep**: In the northeast corner, by the lake. Walled.
- **Town walls**: Marked by a dirt berm but not yet built except for two gatehouses and a couple of large wooden guard houses in the empty part of town.
- **Main buildings**: Clustered in the northeast corner near the keep.
- **Isolated buildings**: A few structures are deliberately set apart from the main cluster, including the stables and the [[factions/Arcane Practitioners' Club.md|Arcane Practitioners' Club]].
- **Hill of the Gods (NW)**: Home to all temples. Four of the Ten Gods have individual temples here with full-time clerics: [[npcs/Demma.md|Demma]], [[npcs/Mitra.md|Mitra]], Lucreon, and Heschius Ban. The other gods share the **House of the Gods**.
- **Dwarven area**: Small dwarven population. No formal Dwarven Quarter, but a street exists with about 6 dwarven buildings clustered together.

## Inns & Taverns
**2 Inns:**
- **Yellow Cloak Inn**: Good quality at a reasonable price. 5-story building near Central Market with a 3-story addition. Features: reception area, lounge, upper-class common room, main common room, individual guest rooms (max occupancy 3), and dormitories with bunkbeds. The starting location for the Right for Riches company.
- **Stunned Acolyte**: Much more expensive — the kind of place where you buy dinner with gold instead of silver. Where rich people stay.

**9 Taverns** (roughly ascending quality): Baliff's Truncheon, Dripping Hog, Rusty Gauntlet, Old Well, Pyrite, Grinning Skull, Muddy Puddle, Three Hanged Men, and the Pig and Whistle. **"The Gauntlet"** is an unofficial activity: one signature drink at each establishment costs $500 total and grants a Carousing +3 roll.

## Key Businesses & Services

### Arcane Practitioners' Club
Run by [[npcs/Lyssandra Astorion.md|Lyssandra Astorion]] and [[npcs/Pelteon.md|Pelteon]]. Deliberately called the "Club" to skirt the Collegium's legal monopoly on magical instruction. Earns thousands monthly in training, identification, and membership fees. Located in an isolated building within the future town walls. For full details see [[factions/Arcane Practitioners' Club.md|Arcane Practitioners' Club]].

### Rarities Factor
Founded and run by [[npcs/Wicktrimmer.md|Wicktrimmer]], an Imperial Goblin who is probably the second richest person in town. Maintains a secure vault. The main place the party sells loot. See also: Prosperity Factor and Silent Factor (the other two merchant factors in town).

### Craftspeople & Services
- **Torunn the Smith**: Big Wiskin woman, reputedly the best smith in town. Personally crafts highest-quality items; apprentices handle standard work under her supervision.
- **Astableon's Bookstore**: Modest selection. Books typically cost $10–$50; $100 budget builds a very small library; special orders possible. Currently stocks *The Archontean Empire* and *Dickios and Janius Learn Mithric*.
- **Tasha's Tailor Shop**: Custom patches for $25. Works with unusual materials (e.g., Giant Spider Silk, though still learning).
- **Jeremias Smalleaf** (money changer): Identifies well-known coins for free; charges fees for actual moneychanging. Handles coins and gems; also lends money.
- **Freydis the Stern**: Lady Alexia's steward. Manages the ruler's affairs and acts as gatekeeper. Limits the party's healing potion purchases.
- **Sakeon the Horse Whisperer**: Can arrange carriage hire for long trips (no regular scheduled service; has carriage, horses, and a driver available).
- **Barnaby Goodbarrel**: Halfling lawyer. The party's legal counsel.
- **Jador the Just**: Temple of Mitra. Can perform Resurrection (requires very large donation, plus assistance from all clerics of Mitra and Demma in town).

### Other Services
- **Arcane Practitioners' Club**: Identification ($100), spell training at book cost, library/lab/lounge access ($50/month, $13/week, or $600/year), apartments for rent ($150/week over membership), recharges Mage Power Items.
- **House of Sight** (The Drome): Investigative authority for the town. Handles murder cases and crime.
- Healing potion availability: Minor Healing Potions cost $100 each; limited weekly supply.
- Key copies: $10 for standard keys; $25 for large elaborate bronze keys (copy made in steel).
- Building costs: range from ~$2,000 (small used house) to $100,000+ (custom high-end).

## Historical Context
- **~2150 years ago**: Arden and Vul discovered the site.
- **~2100 years ago**: The city of Arden Vul was founded.
- **~1950 years ago**: Marius Tricotor finished conquering everything beneath the Halls.
- **~1200 years ago**: Arden Vul collapsed.
- **~20 years ago (2973 AE)**: Lady Alexia arrived and began building Gosterwick from the old Thorcin farming village.

## Notes
- The Collegium of Cinders has not yet established a presence here; Lady Alexia's town operates without their monopoly oversight.
- Lady Alexia recently banned slavery in Gosterwick.
- No records of the Beacon exist in any documents found in town.
- Ioannes failed to find any written record of the Thothian Teleportation Rings in Gosterwick.
- [[npcs/Wicktrimmer.md|Wicktrimmer]] may be temporarily absent when the party visits (he travels to Narsileon for procurement).
