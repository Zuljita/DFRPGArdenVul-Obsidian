# Flying Monkey Statues

## Summary
Animated monkey-shaped constructs encountered in Arden Vul. Current evidence suggests a shared creature family with observed visual variants.

## Known Variants

### Huge-Ears Variant
- Noted for oversized ears and shrieking behavior.
- Potential alarm/auditory specialization.

### Huge-Eyes Variant
- Noted for oversized eyes.
- Potential visual/sensory specialization.

## Significant Notes
