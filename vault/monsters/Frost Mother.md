# Frost Mother

## Summary
A significant entity encountered during the events of Session 65 near the Residential Pod.

The entity was encountered in cold rooms with floors covered in ice and snow.

The entity was encountered alongside several frost ghouls, giant lizards, slime zombies, and slimes.

The Frost Mother was found in a stone throne and unleashed a Cone of Cold at Vallium.

Upon her death, all accompanying frost ghouls also collapsed into bones.
