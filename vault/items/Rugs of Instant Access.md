# Rugs of Instant Access

## Summary
A set of linked teleport rugs used by the party as its primary logistics and extraction network between the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]], [[locations/Gosterwick.md|Gosterwick]], and deep-delve staging points.

## Historical Context — The Sligo Affair
Rugs of Instant Access have a dark history in the Archontean Empire, stemming from an incident approximately 1400 years ago:
- **Sligo the Devious** gifted **Leonidas of Archontos** a Rug of Instant Access as a peace offering.
- Sligo then had assassins come through the rug at night to murder Leonidas.
- Sligo was convicted of murder and sentenced to death by **Emperor Basil**, who also **confiscated all Rugs of Instant Access** and **banned enchanting more**.

The cultural aftermath persists to the present day:
- **Carpets are never given as gifts in the Empire** — doing so is considered deeply suspicious.
- Rug-weavers maintain the slogan **"A Carpet Is Forever"** to encourage the wealthy to only use custom-made carpets and never buy used ones.

## Range
The Rugs have a range of approximately **60 miles** — confirmed when the party escorted Michael south toward Newmarket in Session 31. The rug stopped working just south of Newmarket (~60 miles from Gosterwick) and worked again when they backed up slightly.

## Notes
- Central to rapid loot movement, casualty management, and secure withdrawal.
- In Session 34a, the rugs repeatedly failed to open despite command-word retries and a magical gate check, indicating temporary network disruption or suppression.
- In Session 34c, a rug survived collateral fire damage in the party apartment and enabled emergency casualty movement between Arden Vul and Gosterwick during Uvash's resurrection response.
- In Session 41, one green rug was stolen by Settite-aligned actors, triggering a recovery operation and escalation.

- A second set of these rugs was recovered from a hidden storeroom near the goblin market. ([[sessions/Session 35 - The Scepter - Flute of the Goblins.md|Session 35 - The Scepter / Flute of the Goblins]])
