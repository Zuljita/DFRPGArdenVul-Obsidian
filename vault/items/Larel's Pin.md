# Larel's Pin

A wicked-looking magic stiletto belonging to [[npcs/Larel One-Eye.md|Larel One-Eye]]. When found, it was disguised as an ordinary silver letter opener on a writing desk in Larel's funerary chamber. Vael cast Analyze Magic and identified it as Larel's legendary pin.

## Properties

## Known History

The party made a deal with the [[factions/Rarities Factor.md|Rarities Factor]] for free lifetime storage as part of the sale of this item. Sold to [[npcs/Wicktrimmer.md|Wicktrimmer]] for $20,000; its revelation is expected to deeply affect [[npcs/Balthazar the Blue.md|Balthazar the Blue]].

## Notes

- Found in a funerary complex featuring frescoes of Larel One-Eye — depicted in his iconic green cap with his famous dagger.
- The chamber also contained a weapons rack with various knives, a cloak rack, a golden apple, and identical love notes to three differently named women.
- Larel is an Archontean folk hero.
