# Rudishva Power Disc

## Summary
A power supply disc used to activate the Rudishva glyph on devices such as the GnuCoder 1000 Data Reader. Come in greater and lesser sizes.

## Notes
- Come in greater and lesser sizes; the appropriate size must match the device being powered.
