# Mirror of Life Trapping

## Summary
A magical mirror capable of trapping a person's essence within their reflection, as demonstrated by Vallium in Session 63.

It is too large to be stored within a Bag of Holding or Larel's Sack.

Breaking the mirror releases all captives but inflicts "Seven Years of Ill Luck" upon the breaker.
- The mirror can hold up to fifteen individuals; a Warden can use a command word to view prisoners, call one to the foreground for conversation, or release them.
- The command word must be used before looking into the glass to ensure safety.
- Two mirrors were sold (one to Wicktrimmer, one to Lady Alexia), while one remains in company possession.
