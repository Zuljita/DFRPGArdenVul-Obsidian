# Rudishva Devices

## Description
"[[items/Rudishva Devices.md|Rudishva Devices]]" is a book that serves as a reference guide for various artifacts created by the [[Rudishva]].

## Properties
- Contains pictures of many [[factions/Rudishva.md|Rudishva]] devices, which can be used to identify unknown artifacts.
- The book contains warnings related to the devices. Reading the correct page before experimenting with an artifact may make the process safer.
- Includes names, functions, and properties for various devices.
- Allows for the identification of items in the loot sheet.

## Notes
- The writing in the book is described as confusing, and some of the devices pictured look very similar, making identification uncertain.
- It is suggested that multiple people should cross-reference their research in the book to prioritize safety over speed when dealing with these artifacts.
- Uvash Edzuson conducted an intensive study of the text.
- [[notes/Discord Summary 2025-W46.md|Discord Summary 2025-W46]] - Research into Rudishva artifacts provided vital nomenclature for recently recovered items.
