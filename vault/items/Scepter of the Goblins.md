# Scepter of the Goblins

## Summary
A magical three-foot hollow legbone drilled like a flute. In play it is also called the "Flute of the Goblins."

## Notes
- Recovered in [[sessions/Session 35 - The Scepter - Flute of the Goblins.md|Session 35]] from an urn in side caves off the northeastern [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]] while Vael maintained Seeker/Trace.
- Physical description: a magical, hollow, three-foot legbone with flute-like holes.
- Discovery context: the party was racing other groups (especially after news of Five Fingers treasure finds) and prioritized immediate retrieval.
- Session 36 recording notes reference a goblin ceremonial context around the scepter (including finder recognition framing), but do not add new confirmed mechanical properties.
