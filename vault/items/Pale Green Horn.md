# Pale Green Horn

## Summary
An artifact recovered from the Heqeti ziggurat, featuring a broad spiral and irregular black bristly tufts.

Its removal is believed to have diminished the magical potency of the Heqeti, prompting Akla-Chah to order that it be secured within the Vault (accessible only via Yellow Captain's Card) and its existence kept secret.

During Session 31, Vael used this disc to open a suspicious door featuring an ibis fresco and later used it to open a round door that opened like an iris.

The disc was discovered falling from a book while looting during Session 31.

The party recorded acquisitions on 2025-11-14 for $25 and 2026-02-06 for $100.
