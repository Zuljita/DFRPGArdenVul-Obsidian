# Teleport Rug

## Summary
Magical rugs (also called **Rugs of Instant Access**) used for teleportation between specific campaign locations. Each rug is **linked to a fixed partner location**; stepping onto a rug and speaking the command word **'viator'** teleports the user to its partner. The party uses a set of these as its primary logistics and extraction network between the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]], [[locations/Gosterwick.md|Gosterwick]], and deep-delve staging points. Specific named rugs in use include the **"Exequor"** and **"Viator"**.

They can bypass the long climb up the [[locations/Long Stair.md|Long Stair]], provided someone with Levitation is available, and can be used while holding [[npcs/Onyx.md|Onyx]] the cat.

## Tactical Constraint — using a rug to leave means leaving the rug
A rug teleports you **off** it to its partner; **you cannot carry a rug through itself.** To use a deployed rug to evacuate a site, the **last person through must leave that rug behind** — there is no one remaining to pick it up and carry it out. So "withdraw via the Teleport Rug" costs you the rug at the departure end.

- An abandoned rug is exposed: in Session 41 a green rug left in play was **stolen by Settite-aligned actors**, triggering a recovery operation.
- The established pairs (e.g. the [[factions/Arcane Practitioners' Club.md|Arcane Practitioners' Club]] ↔ [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]] link) stay reusable both ways precisely because **neither end is removed** — that is the exception, not evacuation behaviour.
- [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] used the *History* skill on the recovered (stolen) rug to reconstruct its recent movements.

## Notes
- Central to rapid loot movement, casualty management, and secure withdrawal.
- In Session 34a, the rugs repeatedly failed to open despite command-word retries and a magical gate check, indicating temporary network disruption or suppression.
- In Session 34c, a rug survived collateral fire damage in the party's Gosterwick apartment and enabled emergency casualty movement between [[locations/Arden Vul.md|Arden Vul]] and Gosterwick during Uvash's resurrection response.
- A second set of these rugs was recovered from a hidden storeroom near the goblin market. ([[sessions/Session 35 - The Scepter - Flute of the Goblins.md|Session 35 - The Scepter / Flute of the Goblins]])
- The party has used the rugs to descend toward the [[locations/Well of Light.md|Well of Light]].

## History
- Approximately 1400 years ago, a rug was used by [[npcs/Sligo the Devious.md|Sligo the Devious]] to send assassins to kill [[npcs/Leonidas of Archontos.md|Leonidas of Archontos]].
- Following the murder of Leonidas, Emperor Basil confiscated the rugs and banned further enchanting of them.
