# Personal Defense Array

## Summary
A Rudishva defensive belt that projected a shimmering field but proved unsafe in field use.

## Notes
- Successfully generated a barrier on activation.
- Nearly suffocated the wearer when the field trapped stale air and resisted immediate shutdown.
- Marked as dangerous/broken after emergency spell support was needed to keep breathing.
