# Rudishva Identity Cards

Canonical page: [[items/Rudishva Identity Plaque.md|Rudishva Identity Plaque]]

## Summary
A family of Rudishva credentials (cards/plaques) used for access control, teleporter activation, and security overrides in bastion systems.

This page documents specific known variants. For complete information on identity plaque types and functions, see the [[items/Rudishva Identity Plaque.md|canonical Rudishva Identity Plaque page]].

## Known Variants

### Yellow Rudishva Identity Plaque
- High-priority credential ("yellow card").
- Opens multiple iris doors in the [[locations/Rudishva Bastion.md|Rudishva Bastion]] and related secure areas.
- Can pacify/suppress some Rudishva security constructs when presented.
- Used to activate specific teleporter panels and bypass force-field controls.
- **See also:** [[items/Yellow Rudishva Identity Plaque.md|Yellow Rudishva Identity Plaque]]

### Silver ID Card
- Silver-colored access credential recovered from burned remains.
- Treated by the party as a known-use credential variant.
- Exact privilege level vs yellow plaque remains uncertain.

### Other Colors
- Rust/Brown - Standard crew access
- Sky Blue - Unknown security level
