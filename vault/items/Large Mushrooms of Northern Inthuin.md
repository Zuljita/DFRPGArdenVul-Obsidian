# Large Mushrooms of Northern Inthuin

## Summary
A reference guide compiled by **Fael** of the Gosterwick Nature Foundation, documenting the large mushrooms found in the caverns beneath and around Arden Vul. Many of these species grow in the [[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]] and surrounding fungal cave systems. Giant specimens of several of these species were cultivated by the historical Thothian priest [[npcs/Ptarmis.md|Ptarmis]].

⚠️ Several species are dangerous. **Do not eat White Bulbs, Mind-Eaters, or Red Veil.** Exercise caution with Eye-Openers and Slow Steppers.

---

## Species Guide

### ✅ Aqua Vitae
- **Height**: ~4 ft (brown stem); caps 5–8" diameter, white, heavy
- **Taste**: Juicy and delicious
- **Nutrition**: One mushroom feeds and hydrates a person for a full day
- **Medicinal use**: Sautéeing the cap releases liquid usable as a **minor healing potion** (2-week shelf life)

### ✅ Blue Trumpet
- **Height**: ~14 ft; 2 ft diameter cup-shaped blue caps
- **Taste**: Edible and nutritious, but bitter (like tree bark)
- **Nutrition**: One stalk feeds a person for 3 days
- **Other**: Caps can hold several gallons of water

### ✅ Cloud Cap
- **Height**: ~9 ft; 2 ft diameter white and blue mottled caps
- **Taste**: Tasty
- **Nutrition**: One mushroom feeds a person for 3 days
- **Medicinal use**: Can be cooked to produce approximately **6 minor healing potions** (shelf life: a few weeks)

### ⚠️ Eye-Opener — CAUTION
- **Height**: ~9 ft; 5 ft diameter light blue caps with yellow spots
- **Nutrition**: Non-nutritious
- **Effect if eaten**: Enhances senses but **reduces judgment** for several hours

### ❌ Mind-Eater — DO NOT EAT
- **Height**: ~1 ft; 2" pale yellow caps with a red dot in the center
- **Appearance**: Looks nutritious
- **Effect if eaten**: Contains a **toxin that causes insanity**

### ✅ Orange Lamps
- **Height**: ~10 ft; 3 ft diameter orange phosphorescent caps
- **Taste**: Edible
- **Nutrition**: One mushroom feeds a person for about 2 days
- **Light source**: Glows from phosphorescence — continues to glow for **a few hours after picking** (useful emergency light)

### ❌ Red Veil — DO NOT EAT
- **Height**: ~3 ft; yellow stem; 1 ft diameter blood red cap
- **Effect if eaten**: Causes **temporary violent madness**

### ⚠️ Slow Stepper — CAUTION
- **Height**: ~2 ft; 6" corpse-white caps with black flecks
- **Effect if crushed/damaged**: Emits spores that **slow anyone within ~10 ft** for a few minutes
- **Effect if eaten**: Slowness lasts for **hours**

### ❌ White Bulbs — DO NOT EAT
- **Height**: ~11 ft; 2 ft diameter off-white caps
- **Fatal**: Fatally poisonous

### ⚠️ White Ribs — CAUTION (may be confused with White Bulbs)
- **Height**: ~15 ft; 4 ft diameter off-white caps
- **Taste**: Large, fast-growing, nutritious, and tasty
- **Nutrition**: One mushroom feeds a person for about 3 days
- **WARNING**: Looks fairly similar to poisonous **White Bulbs** — only eat if you can tell the difference

## Other Notes
- **Blue Haze, Green Friend, and Red Lotus** are not mushrooms. Fael described Green Friend as "Green Invasive Algae That Doesn't Belong Here" and refused to add it to the guide.
- The **Green Friend** sample is distinct from the green glowing cave lichen gathered in the Halls — it does not glow.
- Fael expressed interest in seeing a small cutting of the **Red Lotus** flower (without destroying it).
