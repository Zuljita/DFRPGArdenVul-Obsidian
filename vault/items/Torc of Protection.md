# Torc of Protection

## Summary
A magical torc that provides Force DR from the neck upward but prevents the wearer from using a helmet.

It provides a magical force field over the head with a general Damage Resistance (DR) of 6.
- An Amulet occupies a separate equipment slot from the Torc of Protection; they may be worn simultaneously.
