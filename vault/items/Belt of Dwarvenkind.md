# Belt of Dwarvenkind

## Summary
A magical belt that bestows traits or abilities associated with dwarves upon its wearer.

## Description
A sturdy belt, often crafted with dwarven motifs, that magically imbues the wearer with characteristics or powers reminiscent of dwarves, such as enhanced resilience, mining prowess, or specific resistances.

## Properties
- Magical.
- Grants dwarven traits or abilities.
- Provides +1 Charisma vs dwarves and causes rapid beard growth.

## Known History
A classic magic item in fantasy settings, likely present in the world of [[locations/Arden Vul.md|Arden Vul]].
