# Blue glass square

## Description
A magical blue glass square, part of a set of six colors of teleportation squares.

## Properties
- Used for teleportation.

## Known History

## Notes
- The party completed their set of six colors with the discovery of this square.
- Identified by Pelteon as a key to a teleportation device.
- The sequence of colors in the set is red, black, yellow, blue, green, and white.
