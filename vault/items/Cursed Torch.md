# Cursed Torch

## Summary
A magical item that functions as a mobile Darkness spell and is currently stored in a wheelbarrow with a 'Do Not Touch' sign.

It features an Area of 6 and allows the holder to see through the darkness while others cannot.
- The curse has been removed via *Remove Curse*, though it must be handled with extreme caution to prevent accidental re-curse or mishap.
- This item originated in Session 02.

Players are currently considering its fate.
