# Imperial Field Plate

## Summary
A piece of armor whose possession may attract robbers, official attention, or 'officially connected robbers'.
- This specific suit of armor is a priceless historical relic stolen from the Emperor; its possession is strictly illegal for anyone below the rank of high noble.
- Noted for its high efficiency (8 DR at only 36 lbs).
