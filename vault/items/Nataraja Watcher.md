# Nataraja Watcher

## Description
A dancing statue that can be used as a guard and remote viewing device.

## Properties
- Acts as a guard.
- Can be used for remote viewing.
- Does not send notifications and requires a light source.
- Requires periodic looking through its eyes.
- Requires a command word for activation.
- Can be made to vanish by presenting a [[library/yellow Rudishva ID.md|yellow Rudishva identity plaque]].
- Picking up its eyes delivers a serious but not life-threatening electrical shock.

## Known History
- Upon his disappearance, Yrtol left behind two large sapphires known as the [[items/Watcher's eyes.md|Watcher's eyes]].

## Notes
- [[Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow]] placed one on the stairs to the [[Beacon]].
