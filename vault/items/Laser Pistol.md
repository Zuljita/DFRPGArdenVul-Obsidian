# Laser Pistol

## Summary
A captured weapon that utilizes small energy cells and requires recharging via the stolen charger from Kerbog Khan.

Basilisk Sister Valya has used the weapon for weapons training.

The weapon is a piece of Rudishva technology.

[[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash Edzuson]] also underwent training with [[npcs/Basil.md|Basil]] regarding its use, safety, and maintenance.

[[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow]] used the weapon in an experiment involving a goat and a *Potion of Fire Resistance*.

The experiment resulted in the survival of the goat, though $500 and one potion were lost.

The weapon was acquired on 2026-02-06 and is valued at $10.

Basil was seen using the weapon to watch a hole in the ceiling during Session 63.
