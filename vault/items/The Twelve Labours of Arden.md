# The Twelve Labours of [[npcs/Arden.md|Arden]]

## Summary
A historical or mythological text detailing the legendary feats of [[Arden]], a prominent figure in the history of [[Arden Vul]]. It is said to be a guide for a puzzle that starts with purification at the fountain underneath the [[waterfall]].

It also contains information regarding the four summonings of Vul.

## Description
A book or manuscript recounting the "Twelve Labors" performed by the historical figure [[Arden]]. It likely contains significant lore and details about the early history and challenges of the region.

## Properties
- Historical/mythological text.
- Details the deeds of [[Arden]].
- Details battles against owlbears, trolls, ogre magi, Wiskin berserkers, frost giants, evil Thorcin priests, water elemental, evil priestess, undead bard, lich, and dragons.

## Known History
- Mentioned in connection with [[Arden]] and the lore of [[Arden Vul]].
- It details 11 of the 12 Labors. The 12th is prophesied to be completed at a future time of need.

## Notes
- Provides insight into the origins and legends surrounding the setting.
