# Plumthorn's Keys

## Summary
A set of three keys acquired by Vaelitharon Sunshadow, as recorded in the party armory and session recaps.
- Vallium Halcyon commissioned a local locksmith to produce three copies at a cost of $30 ($10 per key).
- One copy was used by Vallium to unlock a door leading towards the Glory of Weskenim.
- The original keys were stripped from Phlebotomas Plumthorn and copies were subsequently provided to the goblins.
