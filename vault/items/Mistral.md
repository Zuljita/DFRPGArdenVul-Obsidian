# Mistral

## Summary
A fine balanced scimitar (curved non-thrusting broadsword) featuring +2 Acc/Puissance and mysterious additional powers.

It is an intelligent scimitar capable of speech and detecting nearby items like Cers.

During Session 61, Mistral was discovered in a chest where it requested to speak with Vallium.

In Session 62, Mistral suggested that Cers be melted down or at least disenchanted.

When told Vallium was unavailable, Mistral offered to speak with Ioannes instead.

It had remained silent until recently.

It is currently carried by Vallium in Larel's Sack.
- The scimitar was originally found months prior to Session 61.
- The chest in which Mistral was found was located in the bridge of the Beacon.
