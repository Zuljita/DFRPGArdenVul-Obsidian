# Mortality Poison

## Summary
A vial of poison that deals 20 HP damage with an 8-second onset, requiring a HT roll for half effect.

Produced in the halfling alchemy labs, it is highly valuable and potentially useful against significant threats like dragons.
- Does not deal increased damage based on hit location.
- Destroyed in an apartment fire on 2025-12-29.
