# Collar of Servitude

## Summary
A highly dangerous artifact that functions as magical shackles to command a target to follow the wearer and prevent magic use or harmful actions.

No trusted buyer exists who would agree to its destruction.

During Session 57b, the party planned to use the collar on the statue of Basil Choumnos.

The plan included casting *Stone to Flesh* on the statue to interrogate it regarding Kerbog Khan and the Tower of Pol.
