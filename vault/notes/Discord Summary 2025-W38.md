# Weekly Chronicle: Right for Riches Company
**Period Ending:** September 26, 2025

## 1. Summary
This week was characterized by the redistribution of recent spoils and intense strategic debate regarding the company's next moves within the Arden Vul. While no active delving occurred due to a scheduled hiatus in operations, the company focused on managing assets and weighing the merits of direct confrontation against the Cult of Set versus opportunistic manipulation of local bandit factions.

**Uvash Edzuson**
*   **Loot Distribution:** Received 3,210 gold pieces as part of the recent wealth redistribution.

**Vallium Halcyon**
*   **Loot Distribution:** Received 4,050 gold pieces.
*   **Research Proposals:** Proposed that the company undertake research into an individual known as Androsia to determine her nature and potential threats.

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**
*   **Loot Distribution:** Received 1,304 gold pieces.
*   **Strategic Theory:** Proposed a method of utilizing high Charisma to recruit kobold or halfling forces to act as a proxy against more dangerous entities, such as ghasts.

**Zuljita**
*   **Logistics & Crafting:** Has ordered a third custom transport device (a wheelbarrow) to improve the efficiency of hauling loot from the dungeon.
*   **Loot Distribution:** Managed the redistribution of recent wealth.

**/ Mercenaries**
*   No mercenary activity recorded this week.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Settite Presence:** Estimates regarding the number of Settites in the immediate area vary; Zuljita estimates approximately 50 members, while Greybrown suggests a lower count of roughly 30. The company has successfully killed 8 Settites (5 on the first trip, 3 on the second) and knocked 2 others unconscious during recent encounters.
*   **Halfling Relations:** There is speculation that the Settites may have placed a bounty (potentially 5,000 gold pieces per head) on the company following recent incursions.
*   **Androsia Hypothesis:** Greybrown has hypothesized that Androsia is likely a witch, hag, or some other form of magic-using nonhuman.

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Spell Interruption via Falling:** Dropping or dodging while under the effect of certain spells breaks the spell in the same manner as a fall. Unless *Slow Fall*, *Levitation*, or *Walk on Air* is cast before impact (the window for casting such a spell is approximately 5 yards), the caster will suffer falling damage.

## 5. Item Intelligence
*   **Bag of Holding:** Retained by the company. Identified as a critical strategic asset for bulk loot extraction during prolonged delves.
*   **Scarab of Protection from Death Magic:** Recovered during the recent delve; contained within the company's current hoard.
*   **Magic Glass Eyes:** Identified as being made of common glass and possessing negligible market value.
*   **Settite Gear:** Four sets of equipment were recovered from the recent encounter with Settite forces.

## 6. Tactical Planning
*   **The "Sabotage" Doctrine:** Greybrown proposed a shift toward more subtle disruption of the Cult of Set, including destroying basket elevators (and backups), poisoning water supplies, or using *Mystic Mist* to create dissent and discomfort within their quarters.
*   **Halfling Management:** The company is debating how to address the halfling presence, with options ranging from assassination/relocation to leveraging them as a buffer against the Settites.
*   **Future Research & Coordination:** The company intends to coordinate research into Androsia and determine which specific potions must be brewed for upcoming expeditions. There remains an open question regarding whether other town activities should be coordinated alongside these efforts.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
*   **The Halfling/Settite Conflict:** The outcome of the clash between the Settites and halflings from two sessions prior remains unknown.
*   **Bounty Status:** It is unconfirmed if the Settites have officially placed a bounty on the company's heads.
*   **Androsia:** The identity, nature, and potential threat level of Androsia remain uninvestigated.
*   **Resource Requirements:** The specific list of necessary potions for future delves has yet to be determined.
