# Weekly Knowledge Base: 2025-06-20

The company focused on the recovery and care of the rescued woman, Lytta (later identified as Versania), whose sanity was restored through a significant donation to the Temple of Mitra. Following the liquidation of recent spoils, the company has finalized a new distribution of wealth and items, prioritizing the acquisition of the Belt of Dwarvenkind for Ioannes and the retention of one platinum Arden token as a key.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Identity of Lytta:** The rescued woman is actually named Versania; she had been surviving in a cave for several years, avoiding a giant turtle. (Source: Recovery/Temple of Mitra)
* **Gosterwick Demographics:** There are approximately six dwarves currently residing in Gosterwick. (Source: Local knowledge)
* **Botanical Magic:** In the traditions of ancient magic, fungi are classified as plants. (Source: Arcane theory)
* **The Forum of Set Access:** A rumor suggests that the Forum of Set may be accessed at night by shining two lanterns from the left side of the river toward a cliff face to reveal a basket. (Source: Local rumors/Tinkers)

## 4. World Rulings
* **Loot XP Thresholds:** One XP is earned by reaching a value of $(CP^2)/100$ or the weekly cost of $150, whichever is higher. Two XP requires ten times that amount.
* **Alchemy & Potions:** Generally, only minor and major healing potions are commercially available; other effects must be brewed by those with Alchemy or Herb Lore.
* **Training Costs:** Training for Combat Reflexes is free if the character has been actively fighting; training for Luck carries no cost as it is considered an unrealistic skill to train.
* **Clerical Conversion:** Converting citizens to a faith at the House of the Gods requires a critical success on a Theology or Fast-talk roll, attempted once per week.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Belt of Dwarvenkind:** Acquired by Ioannes. Provides benefits including +1 Charisma to dwarves and increased Lifting ST and FP. Note: Regular wearers will develop a fast-growing, thick beard.
* **Platinum Arden Tokens:** These function as keys. One was retained by Vallium; the other was sold.

## 6. Tactical Planning
* The company is considering several expedition targets, including the Forum of Set, Surface Ruins, and the Well of Light.
* There is an ongoing discussion regarding the necessity of increasing Perception-based skills or hiring mercenaries with high Perception to mitigate the threat of being surprised in combat.
* A plan was discussed to potentially utilize a night-time approach for the Forum of Set to utilize the lantern-lit basket method.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
* The fate of the halflings (the "Roskelly" matter) remains a point of contention, specifically whether the company should take on the task of killing them.
* Whether to prioritize the Forum of Set at night to utilize the lantern-lit basket method.
* Uvash's plan to seek grants or alms from Brother Malachite to support his new parish.
