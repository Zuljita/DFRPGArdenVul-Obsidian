**Lore & Discoveries**
* **Lizardmen**: Known to inhabit swampy regions; some are semi-civilized traders of swamp goods for metal, while others are predatory savages. (Source: Vallium's Research)
* ***A History of Dragonkind***: An ancient text by the gold dragon Yasidoranicil detailing chromatic and metallic dragons, the Pact of Ennius, and the civil war between Sortians and Theosophs. (Source: Ioannes' Reading)
* ***Divine Breath of Thoth***: Describes a ritual involving an orange vapor that grants increased intelligence and openness to Thoth at the cost of physical weakness. (Source: Ioannes' Reading)
* ***Practical Liturgies of Lord Thoth***: A collection of 111 rituals, notably missing the "Litany of Light." (Source: Ioannes' Reading)
* ***Seventeen Uses of a Dead Troll***: Details various uses for troll components, including blood for regeneration and eyes for alchemy. (Source: Vallium's Reading)
* **Theological Status of Constructs**: The Church of Demma does not recognize constructs as "people," though assisting them is permitted provided they do not cause disruption. (Source: Vivian, Priest of Demma)

**World Rulings**
* **Dragon Hide Crafting**: Nikandros can craft items from dragon hide but requires the hides and will retain all scraps. The cloak takes one week, light armor two weeks, and heavy armor one month to complete.
* **Jewelry Production**: Chairduster can produce dragon tooth necklaces for $100–$1000 each depending on silver/gem usage; work begins approximately one week after the commission.
* **Tailoring**: Tasha produces embroidered badges for $100 each, provided accurate reference imagery is supplied.
* **Item Maintenance**: Certain magical items cannot be maintained through standard means.

**Item Intelligence**
* **The Scrying Globe**: A device containing maps that correspond to the known geography of Magae. Attuning the globe requires an advanced degree; the skull-based variant must be held openly and emits smoke during use. (Source: Loot findings)
* **Dagger of Venom**: A smaller implement than a Large Knife of Venom, capable of magically generating poison. (Source: Loot findings)
