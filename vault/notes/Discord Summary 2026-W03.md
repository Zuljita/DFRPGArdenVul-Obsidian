## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Slave Route:** Through interviews with Rolf and Basil, the company has mapped a general path of abduction: from western farms/Newmarket, via wagons through caves, into a basket system leading to slave pits near the Forum of Set, and eventually to auctions overseen by figures such as Belisarius or Tikun Thane.
*   **The Temple Raid:** Rolf reported that during the recent attack on the Temple of Set, a red-haired woman in scale armor and her bodyguards were present and actively engaged in the chaos.
*   **The Wizard Khan:** According to local rumors, a wizard named Khan rules the second level from the top with an army of ghouls to procure souls under the guise of being helpful.
*   **Rudishva Glyphs:** The stone slab contains imagery and text in Rudili/Rudishva Glyphis. Standing on the ramp, the glyphs translate to "Shining Beacon of Glorious Heaven" and depict four beings: a Rudishva, a Varumani, a Varuda, and a "Traitorous Eater of Its Own Toxic Filth."
*   **The Vessel:** Akla-Chah indicated the vessel depicted on the slab is the "Beacon," shown in its intact state prior to its crash.

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Temple Destruction:** The sanctity of a temple can be broken via physical vandalism or the *Hallow* spell.
*   **Large Bronze Keys:** There are distinct sizes for these keys; one is approximately 12 inches, while another is 8 inches. Duplication in steel costs $50.
*   **Thoth's Palanquin (Levitation Disc):** The disc permanently levitates exactly 2 feet off the ground and cannot be moved higher or lower. It has a capacity of 5,000 lbs and operates at two speeds: stationary and Move 2 (approx. 4 mph). It responds to specific commands in Mithric.
*   **Magic Detection:** The *Analyze Magic* spell failed to detect any magical properties on the jeweled snake, suggesting it is either shielded or not a permanent magic item. However, *Dispel Magic* was effective in neutralizing the animation of the snake statue.

## 5. Item Intelligence
*   **Thoth's Palan/Levitation Disc:** A massive, 6-foot diameter, 6-inch thick disc. It is highly valuable; Wicktrimmer has offered $20,000 for it. Due to its size and weight, it cannot be transported via teleportation rugs. The company has proposed a rental agreement with Wicktrimmer at $2,000 per day, provided 6 weeks' notice is given.
*   **The Stone Slab:** A heavy object (4,200 lbs) featuring Rudishva art. It is currently considered a "package deal" with the Levitation Disc. Wicktrimmer has offered $3,000 for it. The company intends to take rubbings of the entire surface before any sale.
*   **Jeweled Snake:** A captured, animated object that attempts to bite those nearby. While *Analyze Magic* was inconclusive, *Dislund Magic* rendered the statue inert and non-magical.
*   **Serpent's Amulet:** A known item available for purchase for $9,590, which grants immunity to poison.

## 6. Tactical Planning
*   **The Next Expedition:** The company intends to use the "Viator" Rug to transport Vallium specifically to the surface ruins via *Blink Other*. Plans include using a *Clairvoyance* potion to scout the southwestern tower for dragon activity and descending through the Pyramid/Troll lift toward the Sundered Span.
*   **Exploration Goals:** The company aims to follow Temrin’s map toward the gold ID card and explore the area west of the Well of the Light, specifically looking for a secondary teleportation circle.
*   **Mercenary Deployment:** Thronebreaker will be utilized for tasks requiring heavy lifting or the destruction of obstacles like obelisks.
