# Weekly Knowledge Base: The Company Chronicles
**Week Ending:** 9/12/2025

## 1. Summary
The company engaged in a heavy skirmish within the Cult of Set's territory, resulting in over twenty casualties among the enemy forces. Upon reaching a four-way intersection, the party found themselves facing overwhelming numbers from multiple directions. To prevent their only known exit from being cut off, the company executed a tactical retreat. During the withdrawal, the party successfully evaded direct pursuit, inadvertently driving the pursuing Cult of Set forces into a confrontation with a group of halflings.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Magebane Potions:** When smashed, these potions emit a sickly smoke that prevents the casting of wizard spells within a short radius; however, this effect does not affect clerics.
* **Potions and Dispel Magic:** Uncorked potions are not affected by *Dispel Magic*.
