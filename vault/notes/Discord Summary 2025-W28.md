# Weekly Chronicle: The Grudge Brigade
**Date:** Week Ending 2025-07-18
**Subject:** Summary of Operations and Alchemical Developments

## 1. Summary
The company spent much of the week engaged in commercial negotiations, alchemically driven logistical innovation, and managing newly acquired magical assets. While significant progress was made in developing new technologies for transporting heavy loot, tensions remain regarding the ultimate fate of the Arcanum reserves and how to best utilize them for future enchantments.

* **Equipment Changes:** Currently carries an amulet providing +2 to Will against Fright, which provides protection to allies within three yards of the wearer.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **Regional Geopolitics:** It has been confirmed that halfling forces are currently occupying territories previously held by goblin tribes.
* **The Tribe of Egrk:** The caveman known as Egrk belongs to an isolated society; his people speak a language unique to their tribe and possess little knowledge of outside "invader" cultures.
* **Properties of Arcanum:** Arcanum is an orange magical metal found within the Arden Vul. It can be utilized to recharge magic items, create new magic items, enhance the range, duration, or effect of spells, and serve as a material component for specific incantations. 
* **Gosterwick Commerce:** Wicktrimmer is identified as a goblin who operates "The Rarities Factor" and is noted as being the second wealthiest individual in Gosterwick.
* **Archon Tombs:** Rumors suggest that many Archons of Arden Vul constructed their tombs beneath the city, often within the Halls. Specifically, the tomb of Julian Grex is rumored to be located near a grove of underground almond trees.
* **Teleportation Research:** Recent research into regional teleportation systems has yielded new rumors regarding the network.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Combat Morale:** Killing a leader in combat grants the party a free Intimidate roll; however, enemies may refuse to fail morale checks until their resolve is sufficiently broken.
* **Scroll Usage:** A spell scroll can be used either to cast the spell once or to learn the spell permanently, after which the scroll is consumed. If multiple individuals wish to learn a spell from a single scroll, at least one must possess the Teaching skill.
* **Familiar Mechanics:** Familiars are treated as a special class of Ally; therefore, all rules regarding the death of an Ally apply. Familiars grant Energy Resistance (ER) rather than Fatigue Points (FP), with the limitation that the process "drains the familiar." A familiar cannot enter a zone of no mana or sanctity if it is not already summoned, though it will be forcibly unsummoned if moved into such a zone involuntarily.
* **Gem Valuation:** Gems may be sold at 100% of their assessed value provided the assessment is accurate.
* **Levitation Mechanics:** When cast on the self, *Levitation* allows for movement of 3 hexes per turn without concentration. When cast on others, the caster must use a Concentrate maneuver to move the subject; otherwise, the subject remains stationary.
* **Concentration Maneuvers:** A caster may perform a Concentrate maneuver to cast one spell while maintaining others. This imposes a -3 penalty for each additional spell being concentrated upon, and a -1 penalty for any other active spells.
* **Initiative:** A combatant may voluntarily move themselves lower in the initiative order for the remainder of a combat encounter without utilizing a Wait maneuver.
* **Power Items:** Any owned item can be designated as a "power item." Adding aesthetic enhancements ("bling") to such an item is free of charge.
* **Equipment Properties:** Boots of Elvenkind provide DR 2.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Arcanum (Mass/Chunks):** A highly valuable orange metal. It is a finite resource and cannot be manufactured. Its presence in Gosterwick has attracted the attention of major traders like Wicktrimmer. The party possesses Arcanum acquired during the April 25th session which remains unspent.
* **Find Familiar Scroll:** An item of significant strategic value. While it can be sold, it is proposed that gifting this to the APC (the company's contacts) may secure long enough favors and future training opportunities.
* **Living Wheelbarrow (Prototype):** Developed by Ashe. 
    * **Cost/Weight:** Costs $300; weighs 30 lbs (compared to the standard 18 lbs).
    * **Durability:** Fragile, with a resistance of HT 11. It requires regular maintenance (water and sunlight) and will die if abandoned in a dungeon for extended periods. Note that a dead wheelbarrow is still usable as a standard wheelbarrow but loses its levitation capability.
    * **Handling:** When levitated, the caster must concentrate to move it up or down elevations. On level ground, if the caster maintains the spell without concentrating, the weight is effectively reduced to 1/20th for pushing purposes.
* **Amulet of Courage:** Currently held by the party but remains unequipped/unworn.

## 6. Tactical Planning
* **The Arcanum Dilemma:** The company is debating whether to liquidate the Arcanum for immediate wealth or retain it to facilitate the creation of magic items and enchantments.
* **Logistical Upgrades:** There is an intention to transition toward using Living Wheelbarrows to ease the burden of transporting heavy loot.
* **Diplomatic Strategy:** The company intends to maintain relations with the freed hostages by providing trade goods and visiting their lands.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
* **The Wicktrimmer Inquiry:** What are the intentions of Wicktrimmer regarding the purchase of the Arcanum, and how should the company respond to his interest?
* **Skill Investment:** The party has yet to decide between investing a point into *Resist Fire* or *Naturalist*.
* **The Halls of Thoth:** The identities/names of the seven statues within the Halls of Thoth remain unrecorded.
* **Archaic Knowledge:** Whether the company will pursue research into the teleportation system mentioned in recovered texts.
