# Weekly Chronicle: The Arena Reconnaissance and the Beacon’s Defense
**Week Ending 2/4/2026**

## 1. Summary
The company focused heavily on logistical preparations and reconnaissance, specifically regarding a planned expedition to the Arena via the Goblin Market. While some members engaged in research into magical fortifications for the Beacon, others prepared for a low-profile intelligence mission to observe local factions and rumors.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

### Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios
* **Town Actions:** Purchased three Flash Nagateppos ($40 each), Delver's Webbing ($160), and Goat's Foot ($50). Allocated $1,000 for food supplies and paid $10 to a bard and $10 for a mushroom meal. Additionally, purchased a flask of unidentified substance for the GOAT ($50).
* **Alchemy/Crafting:** Successfully brewed an alkahest potion despite a momentary distraction during the process.
* **Research:** Conducted research at the Arcane Practitioners' Club regarding methods to prevent teleportation and scrying. Findings included:

    * Mundane lead-lined walls (noted as expensive and structurally challenging, but effective against both scrying and teleportation).

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Arena Lord:** Described as taller and thinner than a human, possessing horns, wearing a purple jumpsuit, a white silk scarf, and a red bracelet.
* **The Goblin Market Route:** Gribble indicates that a direct path exists from the Goblin Market to the Arena via the stairs.
* **Varumani Passport:** A passport of Varum            mani origin has been recovered by the company.
* **The Settite Statue:** Decapitating the statue in the Forum with an elephant successfully prevented the release of poisonous gas.
* **Arena Rumors:** A person named Waleed approached Ioannes, being questioned by two twin gambling ladies at the Arena.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Market Caps:** Most items available within Gosterwick are subject to a price cap of $20,000.
* **Magic vs. Enchantment:** The "Perpetual Light" effect on certain weaponry is a spell effect rather than a permanent enchantment; the cost for such an effect is $100.
* **Downtime Policy:** No downtime rolls (alchemy, research, or study) are permitted during weeks when a player is not present for the session.

## 5. Tactical Planning
The company has established a formal plan for the upcoming mission to the Arena:
1.  **Phase 1 (Forum):** Deliver food to the Forum and consult with Gribble regarding recent changes.
2.  **Phase 2 (Navigation):** Attempt to navigate the route from the Goblin Market to the Arena, marking the path.
3.  **Phase 3 (Intelligence):** Act as observers rather than combatants. The goal is to identify factions and gather rumors regarding the Command Bracelet and recent local disturbances.
4.  **Phase 4 (Exploration):** Utilize "See Secrets" items to identify hidden opportunities or treasures without engaging in direct conflict.
5.  **Phase 5 (Exfiltration):** Depart the Arena immediately once objectives are met; do not linger or investigate suspicious areas deeply.

Additionally, there is a long-term plan to fortify the Beacon using stone and lead to prevent "blink" entries, establishing a point defense array with a mithric password and offering a $10,000 guided tour to any authorized visitors (via Right for Riches) to discourage trespassers.
