# Weekly Chronicle: 2026-03-13 to 2026-03-20

## 1. Summary
Following the successful raid on the Cult of Set, the company has focused its efforts on the monumental task of auditing and cataloging the vast spoils recovered from the site. While much of the week was dedicated to logistical management and the proposed liquidation of certain assets, preparations have begun for a secondary expedition into the Forum of Set.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

### Uvash Edzuson
* **Town Actions:** During upcoming downtime, Uvash intends to engage in several religious and social endeavors: spreading news of his recent triumphs to his followers through the power of Zorradrim; tidying his quarters in anticipation of his family's arrival this spring; and attempting to proselytize to newly liberated freemen. Additionally, he plans to investigate the cost of hiring a cook to prepare Dwarven Meals for his companions as part of a new healthy eating regimen.
* **Research:** Uvash intends to research further aspects of Zorradrim to better serve his faith within Arden Vul, as well as investigating the history of Clan Obsidian and Gokkick Obsidian.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Chasm Monsters:** An inscription on the base of an elvish-scripted amphibian statue warns: *"Beware these crouching monsters. They may be found in hidden caves along the chasm."* (Source: Statue inscription).
* **Imperial Field Plate:** This specific suit of armor is a priceless historical relic stolen from the Emperor; its possession is strictly illegal for anyone below the rank of high noble. (Source: Local authorities/World Fact).
* **The Scriptor's Path:** A stone tablet contains instructions regarding the location of holy objects: *"To find the holy objects seek the resting place of the scriptor."* (Source: Stone tablets).
* **Temrin’s Map:** A recovered map provides directions to a communication bracelet. (Source: Recovered map).

## 4. World Rulings
* **Legal Restrictions:** The ownership of Imperial Field Plate is restricted to high nobility; possession by commoners is a criminal offense.
* **Magical Destruction:** The Salamander statue possesses the capability to destroy magical enchantments on items placed within it.
* **Dwarven Physiology:** Due to racial characteristics, dwarves are unable to wear armor that has been sized or refitted for other races.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Imperial Field Plate:** This item is a high-profile stolen relic of the Emperor. While highly functional (Heavy Plate, Lighten -50%, Fortify 1), wearing it in Arden Vul may attract robbers, official investigators, or even state-connected thieves.
* **Salamander Statue:** A significant tactical asset or threat; it is capable of destroying magic items. The company is considering using it to "neutralize" cursed items that are too cumbersome to discard.
* **Helms of Underwater Action:** The company has confirmed possession of two such helms.
* **Signet Rings:** A large quantity of signint rings was recovered. While their specific identities are currently unknown to the company's experts, the sheer number of them in one location is considered highly unusual.
* **Recovered Keys:** Several keys were recovered from the Forum of Set area during the looting process.

## 6. Tactical Planning
The company intends to execute the following plan for the next expedition:
1.  **Logistics:** Purchase sufficient food stores in town to sustain a week-long operation within the Forum of Set.
2.  **Transit:** Utilize recovered rugs to travel to the Beacon, using *Blink Other* to transport the party across the surface ruins.
3.  **Exploration:** Navigate through the Pyramid and Glory of Thoth levels to locate Temrin and commission maps for the Command Bracelet and the Tablet of the Beacon.
4.  **Forum Operations:** Re-establish presence in the Forum of Set; unload food stores (offered at cost) and purchase necessary supplies from Horace and Galadnor.
5.  **Direct Objectives:** Explore the unexplored room (#4 on the central map), search the Library of Set for valuable texts, and use *Seek* or **See Secrets* spells within the sand-filled chamber.
6.  **Intelligence Gathering:** Investigate the southern stairwell to determine the fate of those who fled the Guild of Service area.
