**World Rulings**
* **Spell Mechanics (Area):** Area spells extend 4 yards upward; vertical expansion is fixed unless obstructed by impassable objects.
* **Spell: Knit Bones:** A Clerical spell (PI 5) that restores crippled body parts, hearing, sight, memory, and speech, and stops paralysis/spasms; however, it does not restore HP or cure poisons, diseases, or curses.
* **Spell: Warding Chain of Glyphs:** A Fire-based Area spell (Clerical PI 5). The caster inscribes up to PI glyphs on a surface; they are invisible after casting and deal 1d burning damage per glyph upon trigger. Each trigger consumes one glyph.
* **Item Weights:** Short scrolls weigh 1/20th lb; rings weigh 1/50th lb; data crystals weigh 0.2 lbs (naked) or 0.5 lbs (in protective cases). Books have variable weights (e.g., *Book of Priors* is 14 lbs).
* **Currency:** Platinum coins are large (2" diameter, 1/2" thick), weighing 1.5 lbs each.

**Item Intelligence**

Regn. est"* (The Archontean Emperor shall rule). The reverse bears the legend *"Auc. est otest."* (Authority is power). They were issued for significant political events such as coronations or victories.
