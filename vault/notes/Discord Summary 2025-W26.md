# Weekly Chronicle: The Grudge Brigade
**Week Ending:** 2025-07-04

## 1. Summary
The company spent the week negotiating the distribution of significant spoils from recent expeditions, specifically focusing on a proposal to allocate magical artifacts and coin to ensure all members meet their necessary wealth thresholds. While much of the loot was successfully allocated—with Vallium Halcyon acquiring the *Amulet of Missile Shield*—the period was marked by the sudden onset of "Stirge Fever" in the mercenary Thronebreaker and the discovery of new rumors regarding goblin territories within the middle levels.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

### Vallium Halcyon
* **Alchemy:** Attempted to brew a Potion of Fire Resistance; the attempt resulted in failure, incurring additional costs.
* **Town Actions:** Engaged in carousing at a cost of $125, which successfully generated new rumors for the company.
* **Research/Acquisition:** Conducted a search for a Potion Belt. The item was unavailable at Creon's Curios and could not be found among the peddlers at the Totsday market; further inquiries are required on Demmasday.
* **Equipment Changes:** Acquired the *Amulet of Missile Shield*.

### Ioannes Grammatric Byzantios

* **Loot Distribution:** Voted to prioritize company wealth (CP) over secondary magical items.

### Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow
* **Town Actions:** Returned to town following travel; participated in the identification of recent loot.

### Uvash Edzuson
* **Town Actions:** None this week.

### Mercenaries
* **Thronebreaker:** Suffered a single injury during a recent encounter with stirges. Following his return to town, he has been afflicted with "Stirge Fever." He has recently undergone training in the skill *Levitation* (Skill 10).
* **Chrysalis:** Assigned to assist the company; noted as being "Blessed" alongside Tubbs.
* **Coinbase:** Noted as a significant force multiplier for the company's tactical strength.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Goblin City (Rumor 1s5):** Intelligence suggests a goblin city exists within the middle levels. The primary entrance is a massive iron gate set into the wall of the Great Chasm. Access may be gained by locating a great stone seat near the Sundered Span and rappelling down, though the Goblin King reportedly demands tribute for entry.
* **The Altar of Thoth:** Observations made on the secret path exiting the halfling territories revealed several lore-intensive mosaics and a hidden, trapped panel located beneath the altar.
* **Gerrilad’s Spoils:** It has been confirmed that several "weird" or unusual items recovered from the individual known as Gerrilad are of questionable origin or nature.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Spell Mechanics (Concussion):** The spell *Concussion* creates a ball of pressurized air with a maximum blast radius of 10. It inflicts crushing explosive damage and requires a HT roll (at -1 per die of damage, up to HT-3) to avoid physical stun. Hearing protection provides bonuses to this roll; earplants provide +1 per -1 to the roll, up to a maximum of +5.
* **Alchemy:** It is possible to reverse-engineer potions if one possesses a full alchemy lab.
* **Attribute Purchases:** It is valid to purchase points of HT as standalone increments (e.g., +1 FP or +0.25 Basic Speed) provided they are not part of a racial package.
* **Item Properties:** The *Amulet of Missile Shield* functions as an "always-on" item.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Amulet of Missile Shield (Value: $2,000):** A vital defensive tool for the company. While casters can self-buff, this amulet is most significant for frontline fighters who rely on parry-based defense and are vulnerable to ambushes.
* **Beech Club (Value: $4,000):** A one-handed wizard staff measuring one yard in length. It provides a +2 bonus to Parry.
* **Cloak of Bark (Value: $2,000):** A large cloak weighing 5 lbs; retained by the company for its defensive utility.
* **Potion of Diminution:** Identified as a highly significant item. While its exact nature is suspected to be a recurring phenomenon in local lore, it remains a reproducible asset for tactical size manipulation.
* **The "Weird" Toy Bed:** A magical item recovered from Gerrilad. It is noted to be extremely delicate; using it as a projectile (e.g., via "Throwing Art") carries a high risk of destruction, even if protected by *Shatterproof*.
* **Unidentified Loot (2025-06-27):** The recent haul included a minor healing potion, an invisibility potion, and a wand. A large knife was also recovered but remains unidentified due to the prohibitive cost of appraisal.

## 6. Tactical Planning
* **Dungeon Expedition:** The company intends to push forward into the dungeon, with potential plans to encounter a wizard and subsequently resolve matters regarding Newmarket. There is an open question as to whether the party is prepared for the complications of such an encounter.
* **Security:** There is an ongoing discussion regarding the necessity of securing the corridor previously used for the party's exit.

## 7. Unresolved Threads

* **Supply Chain:** The continued search for a Potion Belt during the upcoming Demmasday market.
