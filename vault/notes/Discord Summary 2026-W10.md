# Weekly Chronicle: The Aftermath of the Alpha Strike
**Week Ending:** 2026-03-13

## 1. Summary
Following the high-intensity assault on the Cult of Set, the company has turned its attention to the logistical and ethical challenges of the aftermath. Discussions have centered on the liberation of captives held by the cult and the potential for establishing a refugee settlement or aid program in Gosterwick.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

**Uvash Edlevsson**
*   **Alignment Note:** It is noted that Uvash maintains a Lawful Good alignment.

**Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow**
*   **Alignment Note:** Vaelitharon's alignment is characterized as Chaotic Good, though his adherence to good principles is noted as being somewhat thin.

**Zuljita**
*   **Tactical Planning:** Proposed a plan to provide financial grants (estimated at several hundred coins) to liberated individuals to help them restart their lives.
*   **Potential Projects:** Is considering using the *Shape Earth* spell to construct a temporary refugee house, provided that land can be secured through an agreement with Lady Alexia.
*   **Surveillance/Infrastructure:** Plans to implement a "closed-circuit hack" in their Gosterwick apartment by placing an eye within a book, left open to the eye page on the bridge, to allow for remote monitoring.

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**
*   **Social/Strategic Discussion:** Participated in deliberations regarding the company's standing and "credentials" following the recent bloodshed, noting the visible evidence of the conflict left upon the party.

**Vallium Halcyon**
*   **Social/Strategic Discussion:** Contributed to discussions regarding the resettlement of liberated captives and the potential for them to return to their original homes or settle near the company's operations.

**Lacrymosa**
*   **Equipment/Finances:** Expected to receive significant loot from the recent operation, which may be sufficient to fund a long-term excursion to the eastern countries.

**Mercenary (Unspecified)**
*   **Social/Strategic Discussion:** Engaged in philosophical debates regarding the alignment of various factions—including the Brigade (primarily Chaotic Neutral) and others—and the ethical implications of the party's recent actions.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **Cult Demographics:** Observations from the recent raid suggest the Cult of Set consists of well over one hundred members.
*   **Cult Logistics:** Within the cult's operations, there appears to be a consistent ratio of approximately two guards for every one slave.

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Legal Status of Captives:** The legal standing of individuals held as slaves by the illegal Cult of Set—specifically whether they are considered legally free or remain the property of others under local law—is currently unknown to Vaelitharon Sunshadow.

## 5. Item Intelligence
*   **Gokkick Obsidian / Lifestone:** These materials were confirmed to be present within the headquarters of the Cult of Set. No specific instructions for handling or further investigation were established this week.

## 6. Tactical Planning
*   **Evacuation Protocols:** The company intends to devise a method for the safe evacuation of former slaves from cult territory and their subsequent transport to a secure location.
*   **Refugee Management:** There is an ongoing discussion regarding how to integrate liberated individuals into local populations without them being subject to re-enslavement or being claimed by local powers, such as Lady Alexia.
*   **Infrastructure Development:** The company is weighing the possibility of requesting land from Lady Alexia to build a permanent or semi-permanent refuge for those displaced by the conflict.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
*   **The Slave Count:** The exact number of slaves currently held by the Cult of Set remains unconfirmed.
*   **Legal Ambiguity:** The question of whether the liberated captives are legally free under local law remains unanswered.
*   **Resettlement Logistics:** It is yet to be determined if the liberated individuals will prefer to return to their original homes or resettle near the company's current base of operations.
*   **Lady Alexia’s Cooperation:** The party has not yet approached Lady Alexia regarding land grants for a refugee house.
