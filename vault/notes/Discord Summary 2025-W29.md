## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **Stirge Lair Notes:** Recovered text details a failed expedition involving the Obsidian Gate, the Chamber of Life, and the Drowned Canyon. The notes recount the death of a hero named Gregor near the Troll Lift and the loss of an individual's spellbook to fire. (Source: Recovered adventuring notes).
*   **Rumor 17:** Red Guardsmen in the Forum of Set have been overheard whispering about escaped slaves from the Guild of Service, though official company policy denies any such occurrence. (Source: Overheard conversation).
*   **The Was Stick:** This item holds low value as a curiosity because its use is restricted to Clerics of Set, a deity currently banned within the region. (Source: Item analysis).

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Magic & Spells:** The "Jump" spell is categorized under the Magic skill rather than a standard DFRPG spell.
*   **Shield Mechanics:** Using a Large Shield without the *Shield-Wall Training* advantage imposes a -2 penalty to attacks. Medium shields do not carry this specific penalty.
*   **Training Requirements:** The skills *Search* and *Traps*, as well as the *Energy Reserve* advantage, require formal training; experience gained through dungeon exploration does not substitute for professional instruction.
*   **Item Availability:** "Balanced" sticks/batons are not available for purchase or acquisition in the current market.
*   **Trade & Commission:** Coinbase transactions involve a 70% commission fee.

## 5. Item Intelligence
*   **The Lucky Penny:** Formerly a cursed item that caused the bearer to be distracted by shiny objects; after a *Remove Curse* ritual, it is now considered "overly fixed" and functions as a beneficial magical coin.
*   **Lover's Token:** A single-use item that allows for two-way voice communication for up to one hour. The user must concentrate to activate the effect. The connection lasts until the hour expires or the token is broken.
*   **Fine Mail (with Fortify 1):** An extremely high-value item, though its utility is debated due to its weight and cost compared to "Lighten" variants. The company has proposed a plan to liquidate this asset for Character Points.
*   **The Living Wheelbarrow:** A prototype developed by Ashe Maykum. It features living plants on its underside, allowing it to be moved via *Levitation* rather than *Apportation*. It can be pushed or pulled with only a quarter of the strength required for standard wheelbarrows and ignores difficult terrain (Note: Requires caster concentration).
*   **Healing Sludge:** Found in a pool within the "Orange Room." It is noted to be highly unpalatable, which artificially lowers its market value.

## 6. Tactical Planning
*   The company is debating whether to sell recent treasures (such as the gold censer and Thoth statue) for immediate profit or to offer them as tribute to the dragon or local churches to ensure continued peace or favorable relations.
*   Plans are being considered to return to the Great Cave to investigate the locked round metal door in the exploding hexagon hallway, the cave passages north of the Fidelity stone guardian, and potential leads behind the waterfall.
