## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **Rugs of Instant Access:** Testing has confirmed that by speaking the command word "viato," a user teleports between two rugs one second after activation, provided both rugs are flat and unoccupied. The effect functions while carrying heavy loads but cannot transport two people simultaneously. No cooldown period has been identified, and the range is confirmed to be at least 250 yards without requiring line of sight.
* **Sanctity & Hallowing:** The *Hallow* spell increases the Sanctity (for Clerics) or Nature's Strength (for Druids) of an area by one level (up to High Sanctity or +3 Nature's Strength, respectively). This spell can be used to de-sanctify opposing temples as an act of war, though it only functions for the caster's specific deity. While physical desecration of a temple takes time to diminish sanctity, the *Hallow* spell accelerates this process.
* **Regional Intelligence:** 
    * Trefko, the baboon guardian of the mirror, possesses a dwarven life stone necklace and is willing to trade it for a live goat.
    * Lycandus's remains were discovered via *Continual Light* rocks held at the top of a pit; his remains are otherwise hidden by illusions.
    * Ptarmis claims authority over the Fungal Foresters.
    * The tomb of Great Theskelon (Set cult) is located east of the Southern Necropolis of Set.
    * Amhit has discovered the Obsidian Gates but has been unable to teleport behind them.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Equipment Slots:** An Amulet occupies a separate equipment slot from the Torc of Protection; they may be worn simultaneously. However, the Torc of Protection prevents the wearing of a helmet.
* **Magic Item Pricing & Mechanics:** The Incandescent Blue Ioun Stone (+1 Will) is valued at $8,000. The *Windstorm* spell family allows for an optional "eye" within the radius; this eye can be reduced to zero (effectively non-existent) for smaller area spells.
* **Commerce & Social Mechanics:** 
    * Locksmith services are priced at $10 per key.
    * Carousing bonuses: Spending $125 on alcohol provides a +2 bonus to carousing rolls; spending $500 provides a +3 bonus.
* **Alchemy:** Failure in alchemy may result in increased costs for future attempts due to required supervision or damage remediation.
* **Teleportation Utility:** "Pebbles" can be used as portable teleportation gates via the "trace and toss" method.
