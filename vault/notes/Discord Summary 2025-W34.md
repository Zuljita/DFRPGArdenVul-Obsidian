## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **Theological Precedent for Weaponry:** It is established that a piercing tool, such as a mattock or warhammer, may be treated as a blunt weapon if the user utilizes only the non-bladed or "pointy" end. This precedent was recognized by the Council of Seven in Kazildor following the actions of Father Durin Obsidian.
* **Linguistic Instruction:** Instructors for the Mithric tongue are available within the known regions.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Specialized Knowledge Availability:** Highly specific knowledge skills, such as Hidden Lore (Arden Vul) or Area Knowledge (Gosterwick), are not available through standard training; they require the discovery of particular, specialized instructors.
* **Training Requirements:** Skill and attribute training requires dedicated downtime. However, the requirement for formal training may be established by an authority if a character has demonstrated consistent proficiency through active use in combat or exploration.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Warhammer/Pick:** The classification of these weapons as blunt or edged is subject to usage and theological interpretation. As established by the precedent of Father Durin Obsidian, using the "pointy end" of a tool allows it to be treated as a blunt instrument for certain purposes.

## 6. Tactical Planning
* **Halfling Relations:** Following the recent destruction of much of their cult, discussions have turned toward potentially declaring all-out war on the halfling factions to secure entrances without taxation.
* **Post-Delve Objectives:** Upon the successful rescue of Lillian, the company plans to travel to Newmarket to collect the bounty for her safe return. 
* **Newmarket Investigations:** Future objectives include investigating the Tower of the Ape and seeking out a Thothian priest or librarian at the Temple of Thoth to gain access to the Library of Thoth.
* **Settlement Logistics:** The company is considering the potential for establishing a "friendly lair" within the dungeon, potentially comprised of escaped slaves, though the viability of trade and survival in such a location remains unconfirmed.
