The company spent the week engaged in intensive logistical preparation and scholarly research within Archontos. While efforts to locate Zhorak's Forge yielded no new geographic leads, significant progress was made in decoding ancient Setite communications and mapping the Rudishva teleporter network.

**Lore & Discoveries**
* **The Constantius Code:** The diary of the Setite spymaster Constantius utilizes a pattern of "Significant Capitals" to hide messages, specifically regarding documents hidden near a Black Boar statue. (Source: *Diary of Constantius*)
* **Settite Theology:** Early Settite doctrine is characterized by an emphasis on blind obedience and the enforcement of rules through punishment. (Source: *Dogmatics of Father Set*)
* **Historical Archontos:** The city's social landscape was significantly different 1,300 years ago, as evidenced by Meson's records. (Source: *Meson's Guide to the Brothels of Archontos*)

**World Rulings**
* **Flexible Light Armor (Dragonhide):** This armor provides DR 2 and is entirely immune to acid damage. However, wearing it imposes a -3 reaction penalty when interacting with dragons. It has a weight of 125% of standard light armor and a value of 30x base cost.
* **The Dwarven Forge:** While confirmed to exist somewhere beneath the Arden Vul complex, its exact location remains unmapped.

**Item Intelligence**
* **Large Metal Chest:** Located in a Rudishva-architecture room reached via teleporter. The chest features a "hand reader" mechanism and is currently unopenable. It is hypothesized that specific hands (perhaps from the Morale Officer in the Residential Pod) are required to act as keys.
* **Flexible Light Armor (Dragonhide):** Acquired from Nikandros. While providing excellent acid protection, its presence is highly detrimental to diplomacy with draconic entities.

**Tactical Planning**
* **The Rudishva Mapping Expedition:** The company intends to use the rug network to travel to the Beacon. The primary objective is to map the teleporter pad network, specifically targeting R-J, R-L, R-M, R-N, and R-O. 
* **Recommended Loadout:** Yellow Rudishva Card, Amulet of Missile Shield, and Salamander Amulet (or Fire Resistance potions) are advised to mitigate trap damage during rapid transit.
* **Secondary Objective:** After two hours of mapping, the company plans to shift focus to non-Rudishva territory near the Varumani Vats (near teleporter R-K), utilizing *Seek Magic* to identify new leads.

**Unresolved Threads**
* **The Location of Zhorak's Forge:** The search continues for the specific site within the dungeon; some speculate an "Obelisk of Painful Knowledge" could be used to find it.
* **The Black Boar Documents:** The party has not yet located the room opposite the boar statue or attempted the "Thrice Five" invocation.
* **The Hand Reader Key:** The need to recover biological "keys" (hands) from previous combatants to open the Rudishva metal chest.
* **The Bad Neighbors:** Awaiting contact from Wicktrimmer regarding potential hostilities or negotiations.
* **The Resurrection of Cobban:** The legal and magical hurdles for revivifying the fallen remain a significant complication.
* **Exploration Frontiers:** Plans remain to investigate the water levels (requiring swimming gear/helmets of underwater action), the caves behind the waterfall, and the chasm floor.
