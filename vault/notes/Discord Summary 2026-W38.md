**Lore & Discoveries**
* **The History of Arden Vul (from *Lamentations of Hesius*):** Following the death of the last Archon and the retreat of imperial legions, Arden Vul fell into chaos. Hesius and the Inner Order of Thoth fled to the Sanctum, protected by the Avatar Oziman. Upon returning from searching the Lower Halls for food, Hesius found his companions performing perverse rituals to a new incarnation of Thoth known as "The Tentacled One." Hesius subsequently died by suicide via poison.
* **Thothian Records (from *Property of Eustachious the Cantor*):** This text contains Thothian liturgies, instructions for summoning the Guardians of Thoth, and a history of the Temple of Thoth in Arden Vul. It also includes musical notes for "the Cantor's Lament" and two known wizard spells.
* **The Legend of Gnaeus (from *The Founder and His Deeds*):** Details the life of Gnaeus, a Thothian cleric who founded the temple in Arden and later became Ptoh-Ristus. He was present during the Ninth Labor of Arden, where he repelled undead hordes while Arden recovered a magical musical instrument.
* **Authorship Records:** *The Divine Breath of $\text{Thoth}$* was written by Theophania the Devout; *Lamentations of Hesius* by Hesius Tecum; *Practical Liturgies of Lord Thoth* is anonymous; *Property of Eustachius the Cantor* by Eustachius; and *The Founder and His Deeds* by Sabaf the Neophyte.
* **Cultural Observations:** 
    * *Duty Awards 6532* features Captain Leil-Jor presenting awards to: 3 Rudishva, 2 Varumani, 2 Varuda, and 1 Kaliyani.
    * The Kaliyani are noted for their wet skin, strategic prowess, and use of techno-sorcery involving orange minerals.
    * *The Properties of Arcanum (Rudishva)* is the same text as *On the Properties of Arcanum*.
* **Recent Loot:** Collected coins from Thegn Varboka in exchange for Kaliyani teeth and for power supply charging.

**World Rulings**
* **Downtime Training:** There is no limit to the number of skills or spells that can be added during downtime, provided they do not require training. For skills or spells requiring training, a limit of three per week applies. Newly learned skills/spells cannot be utilized until the week following their acquisition.
* **Larel's Sack:** Items retrieved from L/S are accessible more rapidly than items stored in a standard pouch.
* **Staff of Power (p. 839):** Only the spells cast from this staff consume charges; the fixed 1d *Magical Bolt* does not.
* **Equipment Stacking:** The Damage Reduction (DR) provided by a Lion Cloak and the Staff of Power does not stack.
* **Magical Bolt Properties:** This spell hits targets regardless of DR, meaning it can harm golems, spirits, and undead. However, the target is still permitted to attempt a dodge. It does not negate Injury Tolerance for Diffuse or Insubstantial targets, though it may deal 1 HP of damage.

**Item Intelligence**
* **IQ Ioun Stone:** A magical stone used by Ioannes to facilitate the reading of complex historical texts. When floating near a character's head, it provides a +1 bonus to Will. It is recommended that this stone be rotated between Vallium and Uvash weekly.
