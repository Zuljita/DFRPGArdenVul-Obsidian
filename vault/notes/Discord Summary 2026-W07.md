# Weekly Knowledge Base: Neferet’s Tomb Expedition
**Date:** Week ending 2026-02-20

## 1. Summary
The company conducted a high-stakes expedition into Neferet's Tomb, encountering a magical suppression zone and the entity known as Neferet. While the party managed to force Neferet to flee the immediate area, her current status and the nature of her retreat remain unknown. The encounter left the party within a localized "Force dome" that restricts certain magical effects.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **Neferet’s Tomb:** The tomb contains a localized "Force dome" or magical suppression zone that inhibits certain magical effects and prevents easy egress/ingress for casters.
* **Neferet's Condition:** Observations during the encounter suggest that Neferet may have been weakened by the party's actions, as evidenced by her decision to flee down the corridor rather than engage in a sustained confrontation.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Magical Interactions:** The invisibility effect provided by the *Litany of Light* is immediately terminated if the caster uses the *Create Fire* spell on an enemy.
* **Ring of Free Action:** The *Ring of Free Action* does not grant the wearer the ability to pass through solid walls, even when interacting with magical barriers such as the Force dome in Neferet's Tomb.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Ring of Free Action:** During discussions regarding the Force dome, it was clarified that this item provides no utility for bypassing physical obstructions like walls.

## 6. Tactical Planning
* **The Pursuit of Neferet:** The company is weighing several options to intercept Neferet outside of the magic-suppressing zone. Proposed strategies include:
    * Using *Blink Other* to transport a high-mobility ally out of the bubble to initiate a chase.
    * Utilizing *Divine Grace* or other speed-enhancing clerical magic to close the distance.
    * Attacking the entity from the exterior of the suppression zone.
* **Resource Management:** The party noted that pursuing Neferet presents a difficult choice between risking the loss of active *Bless* effects or attempting to hunt her down.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
* **The Fate of Neferet:** Has she truly been weakened, or was her flight a calculated retreat?
* **The Nature of the Suppression Zone:** What is the origin and exact boundaries of the "Force dome" within the tomb?
* **Containment/Pursuit:** How can the company effectively exit the magic-suppression zone to engage Neferet without being caught in a disadvantageous position?
