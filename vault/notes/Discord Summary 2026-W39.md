**World Rulings**

* The Headband of Intellect does not stack with an Ioun Stone.
* Magical Bolt consumes one charge per use.
* Archontos is a coastal city located on the ocean.
* Travel from Arden Vul to Narsileon takes approximately three weeks by land, while travel from Narsileon to Archontos takes roughly one week via ship.
* The company's banking branch does not exist in Archontos; funds must be carried manually or transferred via legacy factors with significant time lag.
* Possession of a Rug of Instant Access is considered illegal within the Empire proper, though legal in Archontos provided Lady Alexia approves.

**Tactical Planning**

The company intends to undertake a major expedition to Archontos and Narsileon. The proposed route involves:
1. Utilizing teleportation beads to travel from the Beacon/Rudishva to Narsileon.
2. Traveling through the dungeon (Drowned Canyon, Halls of Thoth) to reach the Kerbog Khan statue head for bounty collection at the College of Cinders.
3. Using *Pol's Recall* to return to the Beacon.
4. To facilitate this, Vaelitharon will carry all essential gear and the mirror within Larel's Sack (up to heavy encumbrance limits) while the rest of the party remains inside the mirror. 
5. The company plans to travel light in Archontos to avoid suspicion regarding illegal items like Rugs of Instant Access, focusing instead on acquiring specialized magic items (e.g., Dwarf-sized Boots of Levitation or Strength).
