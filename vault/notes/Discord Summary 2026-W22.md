**Lore & Discoveries**
* **The Primordial Deities:** Seven ancient entities ruled before the current era: Rimmaq-Isfet, Frelothian, Mzingaloq, Viirdo Star-Eater, Piqor-Piqaad, Jeqellathon, and Nzimballik (Source: *On the Terrible Old Gods*).
* **The Varumani Identity:** The term "Troll" is an Archontean/Common mistranslation and is considered a slur by the Varumani. They are intelligent, regenerative (unless killed by fire or acid), and possess spacefaring capabilities (Source: Player Research/Vault Records).
* **The Lady's Asylum:** A site of historical importance dedicated to Lady Clementia, wife of Marius (Source: Research via *Book of Priors*).

**World Rulings**
* **Gosterwick Burial Services:** The cemetery managed by Groat offers individual plots ($100), wooden coffins ($200), stone sarcophagi ($1000), and headstones ($200). Inscriptions are limited to Archontean or [[factions/Thorcins.md|Thorcin]] unless a specialized scribe is employed.
* **Varumani Transit:** The upper lift fare is 10 silver per leg; first-time riders may be subject to additional surcharges.
* **Magic/Spells:** Certain spells, specifically *Repel Animal* and *Wall of Lightning*, possess a vertical extension clause; this is not a standard property for all area-of-effect spells.
* **Artifact Distinctions:** The white tear-shaped Chaos Gem and the red Magic Rock of Chaos are distinct entities with different magical properties.
* **Cartography:** The map of the [[locations/Tomb of Archon Marius.md|Tomb of Marius]] indicates undiscovered rooms located to the south, within the cold region of the Halls.
* **Varumani Lifts:** A senior operator at the far west control station was observed carrying a gold baton of office.

**Item Intelligence**
* **The Key (Page 442):** A significant key currently being duplicated by Vallium Halcyon for company use.
* **Marius's Regalia:** While the party has previously lost two pieces, historical records mention the Sky-sled, Tablet of the Beacon, Iron Circlet, and Ebon Spear as part of his legendary arsenal.

**Tactical Planning**
* **The Diplomatic Mission:** The company intends to travel via Rugs to the [[locations/Beacon.md|Beacon]], utilizing the [[items/Rudishva Teleportation Pad.md|Rudishva Teleportation Pad]] to reach the [[locations/Cloister.md|Cloister]], eventually navigating through secret passages to reach the Troll Lifts.
* **Varumani Protocol:** The company plans to approach Thegn [[npcs/Varboka.md|Varboka]] with passports in hand, strictly avoiding the use of the term "troll." They intend to leverage their rescue of [[npcs/Gevorda.md|Gevorda]] as a diplomatic opening.
* **Tribute Strategy:** To appease the [[factions/Varumani.md|Varumani]], the party is considering bringing tribute such as wealth or high-quality foodstuffs (e.g., live goats, chickens, fresh fruit, or fine wines).
* **Exploration:** The company may visit the Fountain of Golden Liquid and potentially explore the Troll Market or the area near the Troll Lifts.
