# Weekly Knowledge Base: Arden Vul
**Week Ending:** 2026-07-31 23:00 CDT

**Summary**
The company focused heavily on the cataloging of recent spoils and the expansion of their scholarly archives. While significant progress was made in researching the history of the Archonal Regalia, much of the week was dedicated to logistical reorganization, the liquidation of surplus heavy equipment to bolster funds, and the acquisition of new texts.

**Events & Actions by Character**

**[[pcs/Uvash Edzuson.md|Uvash Edzuson]]**
* **Town actions taken:** Conducted an audit of his flock, confirming a total of 52 members (42 Dwarves, 8 Humans, and 2 Imperial Goblins). Attempted to refine theological sermons by emphasizing the singular creativity of Zodarrim. Commenced preliminary planning for the construction of a new temple to Zodarrim in Gosterwick.
* **Alchemy/crafting attempts and results:** Attempted to brew a Potion of Strength; the attempt was unsuccessful.
* **Research undertaken and findings:** Consulted *The Intelligent User's $\text{G}$uide to the Mystical Properties of Gems*; noted various magical and alchemical uses for gems categorized by color in Mithric alphabetical order. Researched the history of Dwarves within Arden Vul, resulting in a lead regarding the legendary "Forge of Zhorak," though its exact location remains unknown.
* **Equipment changes:** None this week.

**[[pcs/Vallium Halcyon.md|Vallium Halcyon]]**
* **Town actions taken:** Conducted significant liquidation of surplus heavy equipment to bolster company funds, specifically selling a Suit of Scale Armour ($4,650) and a Heavy Leather Armour Suit ($3,450).
* **Equipment changes:** The following items are currently available for use by other company members during Vallium's absence: Ring of Protection +3, [[items/Ring of Free Action.md|Ring of Free Action]], [[items/Boots of Levitation.md|Boots of Levitation]], [[items/Gauntlet of Flaming Fury.md|Gauntlet of Flaming Fury]], [[items/Torc of Protection.md|Torc of Protection]], and a Small Shield (Fine, Dwarven, Deflect+4, providing a total of +5 DB).

**[[pcs/Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios.md|Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios]]**
* **Town actions taken:** Visited the locksmith Theolopis to oversee repairs on the Members Lounge door; the handle had been sticking and required force to open, but the hinges were adjusted until it could be opened with ease. Shared a moment of affection with [[npcs/Pelteon.md|Pelteon]] before checking in with Onyx. 
* **Alchemy/crafting attempts and results:** Successfully brewed a Potion of Wisdom; achieved a critical success, resulting in the potion being produced at half the standard cost.
* **Research undertaken and findings:** 
    * Read *Symbols of Power, or the Archonal Regalia of Arden Vul* by Beriacius Solus, noting accurate depictions of the [[items/Tablet of the Beacon.md|Tablet of the Beacon]] and the Iron Circlet of Ghanor.
    * Read *History of the Pagan Cults*, gaining insight into the religions of [[factions/Thorcins.md|Thorcin]] and Wiskin, as well as information regarding demonic cults.
    * Read *Words and Symbols: An Attempt to Understand the Symbols of the Old Ones* by Hanon the Scribe, adding a collection of Rudishva glyphs to the company library.
    * Conducted research into the Archonal Regalia; found that the [[items/Ebon Spear of Arden.md|Ebon Spear]] once belonged to [[npcs/Arden.md|Arden]] before passing to Marius Tricotor. Noted that the [[items/Bone Ring of Jagri-Naz.md|Bone Ring of Jagri-Naz]] was described as "creepy as fuck" and was traditionally worn on the left thumb by archons (though Irene Bellatrix wore it on a neck chain). This research yielded no further historical context regarding the items' current locations.
* **Equipment changes:** None this week.

**Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow**
* **Alchemy/crafting attempts and results:** Successfully brewed a Potion of Resist Fire.
* **Research undertaken and findings:** 
    * Read *37 Quips of Balak the Sage*, finding the contents largely trivial.
    * Read *A True Account of the Embassy of Gillethorn Ellagel to [[locations/Archontos.md|Archontos]]* by Belladia Varsdottir, learning that Gillethorn was murdered by a mob, an event that led to the scarcity of elves in Archontos.
    * Read *Legionary Rex and the Hidden Temple* (written by Belladia Varsdottir in Elven), which details a legendary—though likely apocryphal—account of a soldier discovering a vampire-tended temple to Camazotz.
    * Reviewed communications logs; found only unintelligible static with no clear transmissions recorded this week.
* **Character advancement:** Continued study of written Rudishva glyphs.
* **Equipment changes:** Added a new volume of Rudishva glyphs to the company collection.

**Ashe Maykum (Mercenary)**
* **Character advancement:** Increased Magery to 5.

**Other Notable Acquisitions/Changes**
* **Loot:** The company acquired 4 gems from the innards of a Roper.
* **Library Expansion:** Added *16 Steps* by Hoel Gargus to the collection.

**Lore & Discoveries**
* **The Death of Gillethorn:** Gillethorn Ellagel, son of Lady Ellagel, was assassinated in Archontos by a racist mob following the civil war between [[factions/Sortians.md|Sortians]] and Theosophs. (Source: *A True Account of the Embassy...*)
* **The Forge of Zhorak:** A legendary dwarven forge is rumored to exist somewhere within the bounds of Arden Vul. (Source: Uvash Edzuson's research).
* **The Legend of Rex:** A tale exists of a legionary named Rex who cleared a temple of vampires dedicated to Camazotz. (Source: *Legionary Rex...*)
* **Pelteon's Travels:** Pelteon has traveled to [[locations/Newmarket.md|Newmarket]] via Wicktrimmer’s flying disk to procure supplies and books for the Arcane Practitioners' Club.

**World Rulings**
* **Scroll Properties:** The *Scroll of Protection from Demons* is classified as both Charged and Universal in its application.
* **Coffer Access:** To determine the specific contents of the Coffer of Zox, the *Scry Gate* spell must be utilized.
* **Magical Nature of Druidic Areas:** There is no such phenomenon as a "Druidic NMZ" (Null Magic Zone); while spells cast within such areas may suffer a -10 penalty to casting and potential spikes in FP cost, they do not lapse.

**Item Intelligence**
* **Coffer of Zox:** A mysterious container that requires *Scry Gate* to inspect. It has been identified as containing the Mithric command words "store allies" and "summon allies." The company must treat this item with caution, as it may contain trapped undead or other hazards.
* **Cers (Large Falchion):** A massive 4-foot long weapon that bears a striking resemblance to the blade *Mistral*.
* **Ebon Spear:** A weapon featuring an ebony shaft and a double-barbed tip of polished metal. It is inscribed with Mithric prayers to Wadjet. Historical records link it to both [[npcs/Arden.md|Arden]] and Marius Tricotor.
* **Bone Ring of Jagri-$\text{N}$az:** A silver band mounted with knuckle bones. Historically worn on the thumb by archons, though Irene Bellatrix famously wore it as a necklace.
* **Scroll of Protection from Demons:** A versatile, charged scroll usable by any caster.

**Tactical Planning**
* **Teleporter Mapping:** The primary objective for the upcoming session is to aggressively scout and map teleporters, specifically using *Scry Gate* and animal-based scouting (via *Beast Possession*) to identify exits near the cloister and Rudishva areas.
* **Regalia Commissioning:** The company intends to take descriptions of the remaining Regalia items to Temrin to commission high-quality maps.
* **Aquatic Exploration:** There is ongoing discussion regarding a potential expedition into the water levels near the Temple of Marius, supported by the recent acquisition of multiple *Helcium of Underwater Action*.

* **Coffer Contents:** What exactly is stored within the Coffer of Zox beyond the command words?
* **Regalia Locations:** Where are the remaining pieces of the Archonal Regalia currently held?
* **The Behir Lair:** The area surrounding the Behir lair remains unmapped and requires clearing.
