## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **Theology of Magery:** According to the *Canticle of Arcane Knowledge* by Priscus Pulcher, magery is evenly distributed throughout the population and is not a privilege restricted to the nobility.
* **Imperial Goblin History:** The text *The Highly Impressive Story of Frigg the Fabulous* reveals that Frigg was an Imperial Goblin wizard and an apprentice to Vul; he was noted for defeating various monsters, including trolls, ogres, and giant worms.
* **Alchemical Ingredients:** *Practical Animal Magic* serves as a compendium of the properties of various animal parts, useful for potion brewing and item creation.
* **Historical Figures:** Research into recovered documents confirmed that Gemma of Arcturos lived 173 years ago, while Erist Ellagelsson has no recorded history in recent archives.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Returning Javelin Mechanics:** The javelin returns to the owner's hand at Move 12. For throws within a range of 12 yards, it is available for use every turn. If the javelin is dropped or disarmed, it will attempt to return to its owner; however, if the owner's hand is already occupied (e.g., by a sword), the javelin will strike the hand and fall to the ground.
* **Gauntlet of Flaming Fury:** The fire damage provided by this gauntlet applies only to weapons held in the hand wearing the device; the flames extinguish immediately if the weapon is thrown.
* **Ring of One Wish:** This item provides the effects of a *Wish* spell.
* **Efreeti Sand:** This substance, weighing approximately 2 lbs, has the power to dispel creatures from the Elemental Plane of Fire, sending them back to their home plane for one year.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Efreeti Sand (Black Magic Sand):** Identified by Uvash. It is a potent tool for crowd control, capable of exiling fire-based entities for a full year. It is handled similarly to visibility dust and can be used as a grenade-style weapon.
* **Sapphire Eyes:** These eye-shaped gems are noted to deliver a shock (1d-2) to anyone who touches them, though they do not radiate magic.
* **Ring of One Wish:** Analyzed by Vaelitharon. It is a high-value asset capable of fulfilling a single *Wish* spell.
* **Returning Javelin:** A tactical tool that provides reliable ranged capability every turn at short ranges, provided the user does not attempt to switch weapons mid-flight.
