**Lore & Discoveries**
* **Rudishva Technology:** 
    * The *Power Supply Charger* is a plasteel cylinder (4' long, 2' diameter) used to recharge Lesser Power Supplies. It contains a Greater Power Supply, but opening it requires Keyed Tool Number 7 and carries a high risk of fatal electric shock.
    * Known Rudishva weaponry includes laser pistols, rifles, dart throwers, stun batons (powered by Lesser Power Supplies), fragmentation/gas/stun grenades, mines, point defense arrays, rocket launchers, spear guns, sun lances, and vibrablades.
    * Stun grenades utilize concussion; sun lances produce intense heat and provide head protection/breathing filtration.
    * The *Nutri-vore 2500* dispenses nutritional cakes and Rudishva aquavit (which produces no hangovers).
* **The Varumani:** [[npcs/Akla-Chah.md|Akla-Chah]] revealed that the [[factions/Varumani.md|Varumani]] are Rudishva constructs created for security and labor. There are three primary models: Model A (creative/loyal), Model D (aggressive/obedient), and Model G (independent). They are produced via "matrix cards" in specialized pits.
* **The Order of Thoth:** The Order is a powerful, ancient organization. Other notable colleges include the College of Perception (illusionists), the Imperial Academy (nobility-focused), the Collegium of Macrina (researchers), the New School of Arcane Might (necromancers), the Order of the Fifth Circle (summoners), and the Collegium of Cinders (elementalists). No chapters exist in [[locations/Gosterwick.md|Gosterwick]]; several are in [[locations/Newmarket.md|Newmarket]], and all are located in Narsileon.
* **Akla-Chah:** A psychologist for the Beacon who has been confined to her bridge room since the crash. She possesses knowledge regarding Rudishva survivors Talg-RE (in medical containment due to disease) and Triv-Lok (in cryogenic storage due to treason).

**World Rulings**
* Laser-based weaponry deals burning damage; it is unconfirmed if *Protection from Fire* mitigates this specific damage type.
* Stun grenades function via concussion rather than electrical or chemical means.
* The *Nutri-vore 2/500* dispenses nutritional cakes and Rudishva aquavit (which produces no hangovers).
