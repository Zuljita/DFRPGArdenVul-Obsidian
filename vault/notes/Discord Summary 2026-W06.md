# Weekly Knowledge Base: 2994 AEP, Early February

## 1. Summary
The company has focused its efforts on investigating the recent theft of a magical rug from the APC quarters and conducting diplomatic outreach within Goster/sterwick. While research into various undead threats has yielded useful tactical information, political tensions regarding the legitimacy of local leadership remain a complicating factor for future operations.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

### Uvash Edzuson
*   **Research:** Conducted an inquiry into the nature of Liches. The investigation identified them as evil archmages who utilize phylacteries to preserve their consciousness, typically commanding hordes of undead.
*   **Equipment:** None this week.

### Vallium Halcyon
*   **Town Actions:** Departed for business in Newmarket. 
*   **Equipment Changes:** Left several personal items available for the company's use during his absence: the Ring of Free Action, Torc of Protection, and the Gauntlet of Flaming Fury.

### Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Successfully brewed a Potion of Major Healing.
*   **Research:** Conducted an inquiry into Mummies. The research confirmed they are intelligent, necromantically preserved priests who radiate fear and can inflict the fatal "mummy rot" disease; they are notably vulnerable to fire, electricity, and holy water.
*   **Town Actions:** 
    *   Commissioned Fael to perform Beast Speech ($100) to interrogate the cat, Onyx, regarding the theft.
    *   Provided a reward of fish to Onyx for his cooperation.
*   **Character Advancement:** Intelligence increased to 15; Alchemy skill increased accordingly.

### Vaelitharon Sunshadow
*   **Town Actions:** 
    *   Conducted an investigation within the APC quarters, interviewing residents including Lyssandra and Pelteon, and surveying the building for damages or lost items.
    *   Utilized the *History* skill on the stolen rug to reconstruct its recent movements.
    *   Consulted with the lawyer Barnaby Goodbarrel ($100 for legal opinion; $200 to accompany him to meetings) regarding the theft and local political stability.
    *   Met with Tresti (regarding Wicktrimmer’s absence) and Audun Yellow-Eyes to report the burglary.
    *   Presented a formal report to Lady Freydis, attempting to secure a boon while diplomatically managing information regarding the party's use of teleportation.
    *   Visited the Rarities Factor to evaluate the security of the company's stored goods.
*   **Research:** Used *History* on the stolen rug; discovered the rug was moved via magic (Apportation or Invisibility) and placed into a Bag of Holding by a woman with red hair and heavy makeup, who then handed it to a male goblin.

### Chrysalis (Mercenary)
*   **Character Advancement:** Arm Strength increased to 15.
*   **Equipment Changes:** Assigned the Ring of Protection from Vallium's available gear.

### Thronebreaker (Mercenary)
*   **Character Advancement:** Acquired Pickaxe Penchant-4.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **Theft Reconstruction:** Through the use of *History* and *Beast Speech*, it was determined that a thief—described as a woman with red hair, heavy makeup, and fancy armor—handed the stolen rug to a male goblin and a younger woman. The theft involved the use of magic (likely Invisibility or Apportation) and a Bag of Holding.
*   **Undead Biology:** 
    *   **Liches:** Use phylacteries to protect their intellect from decay; information on them is largely speculative due to the rarity of survivors.
    *   **Mummies:** Resistant to cold and most physical weapons; vulnerable to fire, electricity, and holy water.
*   **Political Climate:** The Empire does not formally recognize Lady Alexia’s claim to Gosterwick. Audun Yellow- Eyes is considered a candidate for the Drome post by Lady Alexia, though his appointment by Keko the Lame remains unconfirmed.
*   **Inscriptions & Correspondence:** 
    *   A Magic Mouth was discovered in the Great Hall, stating: *"The path of lord Priscus is the way to enlightenment and fulfilment. The eyes will tell you the way."*
    *   A letter from "Hellas" to Neferet, High Priestess, was recovered. It expresses refusal to relocate the contents of a special library due to the risks of necromancy and the public nature of Neferet's new residence. The author notes that Neferet may have already claimed the Circlet from the archon but warns against stealing the heritage of Lord Thoth.
*   **Commerce:** The Rarities Factor operates a shared vault system; goods are commingled with those of other customers unless a private arrangement is made with Wicktrimmer.

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Potions:** A Potion of Youth is distinct from a Potion of Longevity.
*   **Legal/Service Fees:** Beast Speech via Fael costs $100. Barnaby Goodbarrel charges $100 for legal consultation and an additional $200 to act as an escort to official meetings.

## 5. Item Intelligence
*   **Imperial Field Plate:** Recently acquired; noted for its high efficiency (8 DR at only 36 lbs).
*   **Oathbow:** A weapon of significant name, though its specific magical properties remain unverified.
*   **Helm of Underwater Action:** Recently acquired; provides utility for accessing submerged locations without relying on specialized magic.
*   **Rudishva Technology:** The following items have been cataloged but their functions remain unknown:
    *   Rudishva Breather
    *   Rudishva Battle Helmet
    *   Rudishva Replicube
    *   Rudishva Sonic Fence
*   **The Stolen Rug:** A magical item capable of teleporting objects/persons, though its use carries the risk of "smooshing" occupants if heavy weight is moved through it.

## 6. Tactical Planning
The company intends to execute the following plan for the upcoming session:
1.  **Offerings:** Make an offering to the Avatar of Bastet using Fael and a statue to inquire about the Settites, their weaknesses, and the status of Neferet/the Iron Circlet of Ghanor.
2.  **Transit:** Use magical rugs to travel to the Beacon, then proceed through the Pyramid of Thoth to the Well of Light.
3.  **Logistics:** Deliver food (specifically baboon food) to the apes at the Well of Light.
4.  **Goblin Market:** Traverse goblin territory to visit the market; attempt to hire Temrin to locate a new command bracelet for Vaelitharon.
5.  **Exploration:** Visit the secret wizards near the Goblin Market and eventually move south through beastman territory toward the Great Hall to search for secret doors.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
*   **The Thief's Identity:** While a description of the woman with red hair and the goblin was obtained, their true identities remain unknown.
*   **The Regalia:** Lady Freydis is actively awaiting progress on the recovery of the Regalia.
*   **Wicktrimmer’s Return:** The company must wait for Wicktrimmer to return from his trading trip to renegotiate vault storage at the Rarities Factor.
*   **The Command Bracelet:** The success of hiring Temrin to find a replacement bracelet remains unconfirmed.
*   **The Cult Threat:** The ongoing presence and potential military response to the Settite cult remains an open concern for local stability.
