The group also identified several key items for long-term retention to aid in future reverse-engineering and utility.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **Baboon Level Inscription:** An inscription was discovered carved into a/a wall within the baboon level: *"Let the faithful render honor and glory to the light which reveals all."*

## 4. World Rulings
* **Boots of Elvenkind Properties:** The *Boots of Elvenkind* provide the "Mage-Stealth" effect, allowing the wearer to speak while moving stealthily; they do not impose a "Hush" effect that prevents speech.
* **Valuation of Precious Metals:** Gold leaf and gold dust are valued at 40% of their standard worth.
* **Jewelry Valuation:** Complete pieces of jewelry are valued at 100% of their worth as power items. However, if a piece is disassembled into components for use in workshops or crafting, those individual parts are valued at only 40%.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Ring of Protection (+1 and +2):** It was confirmed that these rings provide both Damage Reduction (DR) and a bonus to resistance checks. They are highly significant for the company's survivability in high-threat environments.
* **Boots of Elvenkind:** The party has retained half of the pair found during recent delves. Their significance lies in their ability to allow communication during stealth operations without breaking concealment.
* **Potion of Gaseous Form:** Identified as a priority item for alchemical study and potential reproduction by the company's mages and clerics.

## 6. Tactical Planning
* The company intends to finalize the valuation of all recent loot to determine which items are worth the cost of retention versus those that should be sold to fund future expeditions.
* Plans are being made to utilize recovered alchemical components (such as the *Elixir of Health*) for further research and development.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
* **Loot Valuation:** The final pricing for several recent items remains pending, which will impact the final distribution of wealth.
* **Research Follow-up:** The specific details and implications of Ioannes's successful research into Roskelly’s location must be integrated into upcoming plans once shared with the group.
* **Equipment Assignment:** A decision is still required regarding whether the *Ring of Free Action* will be assigned to Zuljita or Michael.
