* **Pol's Key:** While the existence of a key associated with "Pol's Recall" is known, no physical description of the key has been found; all searches in *Pol's Book of Laughs* yielded only unrelated jokes.

**World Rulings**

* **Magical Bolt (Scroll/Advantage):** The *Magical Bolt* found on the scroll is an Innate Attack advantage, not a standard Attack. Consequently, it cannot target hit locations and does not allow for Extra Attacks. It is currently available at the 5-point level.
* **Pol's Recall:** This spell allows a mage to teleport themselves and up to a "Heavy" encumbrance of weight (which may include one person) to a specially prepared location in the mage's sanctuary. Preparation requires a 12-hour ritual and $1,000 in materials. The caster must hold an object associated with the destination. A penalty of -2 is applied for every month since the last visit. Failure by 1 results in 1d damage and physical stunning; failure by more than 1 results in arriving at an unknown location determined by the GM. Successful casters must roll vs. Body Sense to act on the same turn they arrive, or suffer disorientation.
* **Potion of Rock Melting:** This potion temporarily transforms rock into mud.
