The company intends to execute a multi-stage expedition for the upcoming session:
1. Utilize existing rugs to reach the Beacon, then utilize teleporters to traverse the Cloister and Hub toward the elevator slab.
2. Investigate the Rudishva coffins in the octagonal room.
3. Seek a teleportation route to the Well of Light.
4. Visit the Goblin Market via potential routes through the Glory of Thoth or the Great Chasm.
5. Commission a map from Temrin for the Ring or Spear of the Regalia.
6. Explore the Arena and the Court of the Troll Thgn.
