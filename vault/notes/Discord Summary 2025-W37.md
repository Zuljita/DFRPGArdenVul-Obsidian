# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Riches Company
**Date:** Week Ending 2025-09-19

## 1. Summary
The "Right for Riches Company" has been formally incorporated in Gosterwick following the approval of the Thesmothete, Eusebia Phokas. A landmark commercial partnership was established with Wicktrimmer, the richest goblin in Gosterwick and founder of the Rarities Factor. The agreement includes the sale of Larel's Pin for $20,000, lifetime banking and vault services for the company's current five members, and a concierge shopping service via mule train.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **Wicktrimmer & The Rarities Factor:** Wicktrimmer is a retired adventurer and the richest goblin in Gosterwick. He founded the Rarities Factor twelve years ago, with locations in Gosterwick, Newmarket, and Narsileon. They provide banking, letters, and mule caravan services. His reputation is solid, though he is rumored to use magical means to stay informed of new magic items appearing in town.
*   **Lost and Found:** A former adventuring group led by the wizard Balthazar the Blue and the paladin Grimley; their historical exploits are considered largely self-promative. Wicktrimmer claims the group's progress ended at the Obsidian Gates.
*   **The Larel Artifacts:** The "pin" is a stiletto disguised as a letter opener, found within a Thothian funerary complex alongside a golden apple and love notes. 
*   **The Drome:** Keko the Lame heads the Drome in Narsileon; the organization’s jurisdiction includes the eradication of the Cult of Set within Arden Vul.
*   **Geography:** Access to a "Sunless Sea" exists underneath the Halls, accessible via an ogre boatman. A staff with "Balthazar" engraved on it was discovered in the sands of the Great Cavern.
*   **New Spell Discovery:** *Brain Full of Small Spiders* (Illusion; R-Will): Creates a sensation of spiders crawling on the subject's head, causing -1 to all rolls and half movement for one minute.

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Company Incorporation:** A company charter in Gosterwick requires approval from Eusebia Phokas, the Thesmothete of Vetucaster; the process costs $500 and takes several weeks for approval.
*   **Legal Retainers:** A retainer with Barnaby Goodbarrel starts at $0 (after initial payment), covering initial work and providing priority access for future needs.
*   **Banking & Security:** The Rarities Factor provides banking, letters, and mule caravan services; their vault is noted for having zero thefts in twelve years.
*   **Magic Item Economics:** Retail pricing for Bracers of Force with DR3 is $40,000.
*   **Alchemical Failures:** Errors in alchemy (such as excess ingredients) can increase the cost of the resulting potion by 10%.
*   **Levitation Physics:** A subject under *Levitation* is pulled downward if they pick up weight exceeding the spell's limit; the descent is slower than a freefall because the spell still offsets five-sixths of the total weight.

## 5. Tactical Planning
*   **The Ruined Watchtower:** The company intends to visit the tower equipped with the *Rod of Turning*.
*   **Great Cavern Exploration:** Plans are in place to access the Great Cavern via a secret entrance and interact with the Philosopher statue using the Magical Silver Eyes.
*   **Fire Column Investigation:** The party plans to investigate the column of fire near the fire imps using the Salamander Amulet and Fire Resistance.
*   **Settite Reconnaissance:** A mission is planned for the Settite outpost, potentially utilizing *Flight* or *Invisibility*.
*   **Procurement:** Requests have been submitted via the Rarities Factor for Bracers of Force (DR2), Giant-Spider Silk, and Weightless Scale of Warding.
*   **Logistics:** The party intends to utilize a "Living Wheelbarrow" to transport heavy gear and loot.
