## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Library of Thoth:** Located near the Well of Light; rumored to be guarded by undead and containing much lost knowledge (Source: Research by Ioannes).
* **Gwelf’s Fate:** Gwelf has been confirmed dead, having suffered a fatal wound in an unknown underground location (Source: *Pathfinder* vision by Vaelitharon).
* **Local Rumors:** The Temple of Set favors the color green; Phleboromas Plumthorn was recently awarded Newmarket's "Most Honest Halfling" award; and the entity known as Craastonistorex is merely an illusion rather than a true dragon (Source: Various rumors/GM).

## 4. World Rulings
* **Currency & Value:** Gold is valued at 10 and silver at 1. Identified gems are considered cash for XP purposes and can be kept as quest items without needing to be sold. Coins are inherently cash and do not require exchange to count toward loot XP. An octagonal platinum piece was found among recent spoils.
* **Loot & Experience:** The company earns 2 XP for exploration and 2 XP for loot (the latter being the maximum cap). All loot must be physically transported back to town to qualify for loot XP.
* **Alchemy:** Reverse-engineering a potion requires an Alchemy roll with difficulty scaling by complexity. A failure prevents understanding the recipe, while a critical failure results in dangerous misinformation. The original potion is not necessarily expended during these attempts.
* **Equipment & Magic:** A delver may wear two rings (one per hand) and one amulet. While armor can be layered, two pieces of the enough the same armor type are unlikely to stack magical effects.
* **Pathfinder Spell:** This spell may be used once per downtime period for searching rumored locations, and once per day per caster during active sessions. Effectiveness decreases significantly for places that have not been visited or are only known through rumor.
* **Provisions:** Elven and Dwarven rations are rare in Gosterwick; Halfling, Human, and Imperial Goblin rations are common.
* **Alchemy Supplies:** Chemicals such as glow vials and visibility dust are available for purchase and can be produced via Alchemy.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Air Jet Wand:** A non-rechargeable wand containing 37 charges. Paelteon provided the identification and the command word to Creon; it is offered for sale by Creon for $13,000.
* **Lion Cloak:** A piece of armor providing DR1, ST +2, and Infravision, but imposing HT -2 and Increased Consumption. It carries the *Berserk* property, which requires a control roll to mitigate. The party has discussed selling this to Grudge for approximately $2,000.
* **Ring of Climbing:** A functional magical ring identified by Vael.
* **See Secrets Wand:** A wand identified via *Analyze Magic* to contain multiple charges.
* **Divine Breath of Thoth (Book):** A rare book that includes a non-detachable, wizardly, uncharged *Mystic Mist* scroll on its reverse side. Its value has been diminished by previous scribblings within the text.

## 6. Tactical Planning
* **The Well of Light Expedition:** The company intends to travel to the Well of Light. The planned approach involves entering via the well shaft using *Flight* magic, attempting to locate the hidden library, and then exploring adjacent areas.
* **Command Structure:** Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow has been appointed as the "Destination Czar" to lead expedition planning.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
* **The Unknown Cave:** The specific location of the dark cave where Gwelf was seen remains unknown.

* **The Creon Offer:** A decision is pending regarding the purchase of the *Air Jet Wand*.
* **Logistics:** Discussion continues regarding the implementation of a "loot train" or specialized transport (e.g., wheelbarrows) to move large quantities of wealth back to town.
* **Pathfinder Utility:** The possibility of using *Pathfinder* in town once per week to search for new entrances or rumored locations remains an open question for the GM.
