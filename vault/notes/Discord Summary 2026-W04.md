## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Bastion:** This area is a fragment of the crashed *Beacon*, specifically containing the quarters for the Captain, the First Officer, and the Security Officer (Akla-Chah).
* **Heqeti:** These are primitive, hostile amphibian creatures native to the world; they were historically kept at bay by the Rudishva until a betrayal occurred (Akla-Chah).
* **The Traitors:** Six-legged beings who betrayed the Varumani to the heqeti (Akla-Chah).
* **Rudishva Energy Research:** Captain Leil-Jor and her engineers were working on modifying a small craft for long-distance space flight, a project that required high-intensity energy sources potentially related to the recovered liquids (Akla-Chah).
* **Recovered Artifacts:** The party has secured several Rudishva ID cards in various colors: yellow, silver, sky blue, pale green, orange, brown, rust, bronze, and platinum.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Ring of Free Action:** This item allows for full movement despite most magical or physical effects that would otherwise slow or stop it.
* **Rudishva Teleportation Panels:** Color-coded systems exist for activation; yellow panels require the Captain's specific identity plaque to function, whereas silver and other colored panels are accessible to any user.
* **Artifact Value:** The platinum Rudishva ID cards contain a small amount of actual platinum, making them valuable, though not of immense wealth.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Yellow Oval Identity Plaque:** Believed by Akla-Chah to be the identity plaque of Captain Leil-Jor; however, its activation power is limited to yellow panels.
* **Black Rectangular Card:** Identified as a security key for Rudishva technology (Akla-Chah).
* **Flasks of Liquid:** While their exact nature is unknown, they are hypothesized to be related to the high-intensity energy research required for space flight (Akla-Chah).

## 6. Tactical Planning
* **The Bastion Expedition:** The company intends to use magical rugs to travel to the Beacon and clear teleportation pads of any obstructions.
* **Infiltration:** A plan is in place to utilize a Salamander Amulet and the yellow access card to bypass the force field and fire trap within the Bastion to reach the conference room containing the six corpses.
* **Mapping Teleporters:** The party intends to use *Trace Teleport* or similar methods (potentially via the Returning Pebble) to determine the destinations of all accessible Rudishva teleporters, prioritizing the circle located behind the force field. Known connections include:
    * **Bastion North Pad:** Leads to the Beacon West Pad.
    * **Bastion East Pad:** Leads up 100ft/North.
    * **Bastion South Pad:** Leads Up 200ft/Southwest.
    * **Obsidian Gates NW South Teleporter:** Leads down 200ft.
    * **Obsidian Gates NW NE Teleporter:** Leads to the Beacon East Pad.
