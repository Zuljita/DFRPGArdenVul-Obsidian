## Session 1 — First Visit to the Ruins of Arden Vul
*Demmasday 14th Toternios 2993 AEP | Real: 2025-03-22*

### Open Threads

- **[[locations/Tower of Scrutiny.md|Tower of Scrutiny]] bronze double doors** — The large square tower's bronze doors are magical and locked with keyholes. The party decided not to risk traps on the first visit. Not yet explored through Session 5.
- **[[locations/Tower of Scrutiny.md|Tower of Scrutiny]] staircase into magical darkness** — Stairs inside the tower descend into a sharp-cutoff magical darkness zone. Uvash suspects it might conceal a mana-zero zone to kill Levitating PCs. Not descended through Session 5.

- **Colossal statue of [[npcs/Arden.md|Arden]]** — Detected as magical (unlike the statue of Vul, which is not). No further investigation attempted.
- **Magic boots (red leather)** — Found on dead dwarf in spider-web room off the Long Stair. Not yet identified.
- **Magic blue powder** — Found with dead dwarf, same room. Not yet identified.

### Pre-Session Discord Rumors (W10, before Session 1)

- **Missing priestess Lillian** — Priests of Mitra are offering a reward posted in Newmarket. A shepherd saw her taken by "walking pigs" up the cliff into Arden Vul. *(Vael asked the Children of Deino about her in Session 3; they denied holding her. Still missing as of Session 5.)*

## Session 3 — Dragons and Baboons and Beastmen, Oh My!
*Demmasday 28th Toternios 2993 AEP | Real: 2025-04-05*

### Open Threads

- **[[npcs/Craastonistorex.md|Craastonistorex]] tribute obligation** — The green dragon demanded tribute be left at the dam regularly. Tribute jewelry was finally left in Session 5. This is an ongoing relationship/obligation.
- **Craastonistorex's tower to the southwest** — The dragon warned to stay away from his tower due to territorial wyverns. Vael's Seek Earth pointed gold and silver in that direction. Hoard to be claimed eventually.
- **Mysterious man with baboons torturing a dwarf** — Observed in a large room containing a glass-red throne; the man was interrogating a dwarf prisoner about how he found an entrance, with albino baboons and two huge four-armed baboons cheering. The group retreated without engaging. Who is he? What is the glass throne?
- **Dwarf prisoner** — Presumably still captive (or dead). The party deliberately left him to his fate. Open moral/plot hook.
- **Four-armed albino baboons** — Two huge ones guarding the baboon area. A major threat blocking this region.
- **Secret stairs behind baboon fresco** — Found a secret door behind a baboon fresco, leading to stairs going down. Not descended.
- **Marble arm of broken statue (near dry fountain)** — Detected as magical by Vael; probably part of a large female statue. Too heavy to carry. Location noted.
- **Krakteros family tomb robbed** — Broke open a tomb sealed with the Krakteros sigil (tower with light in one window), looted the skeleton guardian, and took a jade necklace and three black pearls. The [[factions/Five Families.md|Krakteros]] are one of the five great families of the Archontean Empire; possible consequences for grave-robbing their ancestor not yet materialized.
- **[[factions/Children of Deino.md|Children of Deino]] relationship established** — Trapped the party and released them. Agreed to let the party pass through their territory if they announce themselves loudly first (no sneaking). Ongoing faction relationship.
- **Message in blood: "They are deterred by light. We should restore the well!"** — Found in the room after the teleporter. Who wrote it? What are "they"? Suggests the Well of Light's mirror matters to someone.

### Inter-Session Discord (W12, same week as Session 3)

- **Cult of Set and the "Guild of Service"** — The ancient Set cult ran its own slave trade. Relevant for understanding Set cult encounters.

## Session 4 — Cheese and Crackers and Thoth and Demons
*Demmasday 5th Lucrios 2993 AEP | Real: 2025-04-12*

### Open Threads

- **Selket bust / black gate** — A small room off the Pit of Bones contains a magical void of darkness (a black gate) through which small ball-shaped biting demons emerge. A magical bust of [[npcs/Selket.md|Selket]] acts as a teleporter when touched. The gate is still active; demons could continue to come through.
- **[[locations/Great Chasm.md|Great Chasm]]** — A massive chasm, wider and taller than their light could reach. A room exists on the far side. Vael flew up ~30 yards without reaching the top or seeing daylight. The far side room not entered. A huge ball-shaped demon was seen by Ioannes from a stone chair at the chasm edge.
- **Huge ball-shaped demon** — Seen by Ioannes from the chair at the chasm's edge. The demon's nature and connection to the smaller ones is unknown. The vision caused a Remove Curse-level panic.
- **[[locations/Great Hall.md|Great Hall]]** — Large room with scorch marks indicating magical battle, knocked-down columns, and ibis-headed gargoyles in the rafters. Only passed through; not explored.
- **Ibis-headed gargoyles** — Two killed in the Great Hall. More likely remain. A significant ongoing hazard in beastman/Great Hall territory.
- **One-way door hallway of corpses** — A hallway used as an inadvertent death trap (one-way stone door, too strong to smash). The party looted existing victims but the passage itself is a hazard to note and avoid.
- **Thoth chapel onyx pillar** — Grabbing the pillar and loudly invoking Thoth while asking a question gets an answer — and an electric shock. Ioannes received a Major Wound from the shock. The pillar remains usable as a direct-line to Thoth. Thoth's answer: to make an offering at His statue, loudly invoke His name and offer "the gift of knowledge."
- **Correct offering to Thoth statue** — Thoth answered that the gift must be "the gift of knowledge" offered while loudly invoking His name. The party has not yet successfully activated the statue with this method. Vallium tried offering a mundane book in Session 5 with no result; possibly magic scrolls or something more specific is required.
- **White glass square (8"x8"x0.5")** — Found in trash in a room east of the Thoth chapel. Identified by Pelteon (W13 Discord) as a key to a teleportation device; the magic is in the teleport object, not the glass itself. The destination/device not yet located.
- **[[locations/Well of Light.md|Well of Light]] mirror re-covered** — When the party checked the well from the top in Session 4, the mirror at the bottom appeared re-covered despite being cleaned in Session 3. Someone or something is re-dirtying it.

### Inter-Session Discord (W13, same week as Session 4)

- **Green Lady selling minor noble titles** — Lady Alexia offers land discounts and Warden bounties. Town is "nearing capacity for treasure buying power" — the PCs may eventually need to find markets beyond Gosterwick.
- **Arcane Practitioners Club skirting Collegium monopoly** — They deliberately avoid good recordkeeping because the Collegium's monopoly on magic hasn't reached Gosterwick yet. If the Collegium arrives, it could change access to magical training and services.

## Session 5 — Parleys and The Great Cavern
*Demmasday 11th Lucrios 2993 AEP | Real: 2025-04-19*

### Open Threads

- **Lady Alexia's Regalia** — Four items within Arden Vul that Lady Alexia urgently wants; she will richly reward the finders. Lady Alexia deliberately withheld her intelligence so the party would have fresh eyes. Items:
  - [[items/Bone Ring of Jagri-Naz.md|Bone Ring of Jagri-Naz]]
  - [[items/Tablet of the Beacon.md|Tablet of the Beacon]]
  - [[items/Ebon Spear of Arden.md|Ebon Spear of Arden]]
  - [[items/Iron Circlet of Ghanor.md|Iron Circlet of Ghanor]]
- **[[npcs/Ptarmis.md|Ptarmis]]** — A robed human living on a stone platform in the Great Cavern, calling himself "the master of the cavern." Vael found him deeply unsettling and flew away. His connection to the sealed purple-glowing doors to the south is unknown.
- **Purple-glowing sealed doors** — Massive block-rock doors at the south end of Ptarmis's platform with no visible handles or keyholes and a purple glow around them. What lies beyond?
- **Clockwork dragonfly with blue gem eyes** — Appeared while the party was casting Analyze Magic in the Great Cavern, watched silently, then flew away. Whose construct is it? Is the party being observed?
- **Second magic boot** — Only one boot found in the rock reptile cave. Vael's Seek Magic pointed to the second boot's location as being on or near the big rock in the open Great Cavern (the one with the creepy pillar on top). Not retrieved.
- **Creepy pillar atop the big rock** — Made Vael dizzy when inspected closely. A hole on the other side of the rock drops ~80 feet into another cave below. Both the pillar's nature and the cave below are unexplored.
- **[[npcs/Gribble.md|Gribble]]** — Missing goblin, brother of [[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]]. Sub-chief [[npcs/Bellringer.md|Bellringer]] was searching for him when his group was ambushed by beastmen. Bellringer asked the party to relay news to Weskenim if they meet him first.
- **[[npcs/Weskenim.md|Weskenim]]** — Goblin with a missing brother. Not yet met. The party promised to relay Bellringer's news.
- **Crystal cave entrance** — Spotted high above the Great Cavern pond water, with interesting crystal formations visible inside. Not investigated.
- **[[locations/Great Cavern.md|Great Cavern]] entrance from surface (south end)** — Found via beastman guide. The party now has an alternate entrance that bypasses the halfling toll-zone. The beastmen used this as part of the deal with Count Skleros.
- **Beastman supply agreement with [[npcs/Count Skleros.md|Count Skleros]]** — Vael agreed to: (1) bring food (wood, iron, steel, Cloud Cap mushrooms, fresh fruits/vegetables) on future visits, and (2) put in a good word with Craastonistorex about him eating beastman troops. Both are unfulfilled obligations after Session 5.
- **Adventuring company legal entity** — Vael got a referral to a lawyer who can set up a company as a legal entity. Not yet done.
- **Bank accounts at merchant factors** — The party visited all three factors (Rarities, Prosperity, Silent) to look for banking options but did not set up accounts.
- **Ptarmis's secret tomb** — W15 Discord: Vallium shared a rumor that "the great Thothian wizard Ptarmis" built a secret tomb in the huge mushroom fungus cavern (second level from the surface). The party was driven back by "sheep-headed creatures." They met Ptarmis in Session 5; the location of his tomb is unresolved.
- **Haunted guard tower** — W15 Discord: A town record states the guard tower (~250 years old) was built by Archon Adrienic, sent by Emperor Phocion IV to retake Arden Vul. The expedition was massacred and the tower abandoned; locals say it's haunted. Not investigated.
- **Where to spend accumulating wealth** — Ioannes raised this in W15 Discord; no investment mechanisms or new markets found yet. The group is starting to hit the limits of Gosterwick's buying power.

### Inter-Session Discord (W14, same week as Session 5)

- **Set priests as slavers on the Long Stairs** — Anyone encountered on the Long Stairs leading to the Set Forum risks enslavement, but the Forum itself is apparently safe. Major faction threat.
- **Hall of Forty Pillars / Obsidian Gates** — Right next to each other; the hall is full of albino toads that transform into demons if the wrong person touches the Gates. Future exploration target; significant danger flag.
