# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Ruala Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-08-22  
**Subject:** The Slime Kraken’s Toll and the Search for Lillian

### Summary
The company continues its focused pursuit of the hostage Lillian, navigating through the treacherous territories of the Forum of Set. Recent discoveries near the Slime Kraken's pool have revealed a gruesome scene of carnage, including stripped and mutilated corpses, suggesting Settite ritual activity. While the path toward the prisoner remains clear, the party has expressed concern regarding their precise spatial coordinates and the potential for Settite retaliation. The company has outlined a two-stage plan for upcoming operations, prioritizing the rescue of Lillian followed by an immediate retreat to Gosterwick, with secondary objectives involving the delivery of supplies and further exploration of the Halls of Thoth or the Well of Light.

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Kraken’s Pool:** A collection of mangled, rotting bodies was discovered near the Slime Kraken's pool. The deceased included humans, goblins, and beastmen. All victims had been stripped of their equipment, and various body parts were missing. This suggests that while the Kraken feeds on the flesh, another entity or force is responsible for stripping the gear and removing parts.
*   **Settite Activity:** Evidence suggests the Settites are active in the vicinity of the Slime Kraken and may be utilizing prisoners for ritualistic purposes. The party hypothesizes that the Settites may have a restock point or town inside the dungeon, or are sneaking out to convert loot, as the bodies found were stripped of gear.
*   **Pathfinding:** The route from the party's current position back to the dungeon exit has been confirmed as cleared.
*   **Dungeon Scale:** The GM confirmed that Arden Vul does not have a fixed completion time, though a popular playthrough lasted 115 sessions covering only a fraction of the dungeon. The Forum of Set is rumored to be larger than currently discovered.

### World Rulings
*   **Magical Obstruction Bypass:** Certain architectural features, such as one-way doors, may be bypassed using magical effects like *Shape Earth* or *Thronebreaker*, provided the method does not circumvent the fundamental logic of the dungeon's design. The GM noted that while these methods are allowed, they may not always work as expected, and the dungeon creators may have accounted for such techniques in specific instances.
*   **Post-Mortem Looting:** Bodies found within the Slime Kraken’s territory are consistently found stripped of all gear and equipment, with parts missing, indicating a secondary looting force beyond the Kraken's feeding habits.

### Tactical Planning
The company has outlined a two-stage plan for upcoming operations:

**Plan for Session 23b:**
The primary objective is to engage the Settites in combat. If the company emerges relatively unscathed, they intend to push westward to locate Lillian. Upon her rescue, the party plans to return to the secret sarcophagus room to deliver food to Gog, proceed to the Inn to secure a mule, and then execute an immediate retreat to Gosterwick. Should the encounter with the Settites result in significant injury, the company will prioritize an immediate retreat following the same path.

**Plan for Session 24:**
If Lillian has not yet been recovered, the search will continue. If she has been successfully rescued, the company intends to focus on exploring the Halls of Thoth or the Well of Light, specifically targeting the newly discovered secret rooms for treasure. Additionally, the party intends to fulfill a promise to the Inn by purchasing and delivering fresh produce and hops.

### Unresolved Threads
*   **Spatial Uncertainty:** The party has yet to confirm if their current north-south coordinates are accurate, though east-west coordinates are believed to be correct.
*   **The Distance of the Subject:** The exact distance between the company's current position and Lillian’s prison cell remains unverified, though she is known to be in a cell next to a goblin.
*   **The Mystery of the Stripped Corpses:** There is an ongoing inquiry into whether a secondary force or entity is responsible for stripping equipment from the dead, as the Kraken's feeding habits alone do not fully account for the lack of gear on all victims.
*   **Settite Retaliation:** It remains unknown if the Settites will move or execute their prisoners upon realizing their operations have been compromised.
*   **Settite Logistics:** The party is investigating whether the Settites have an internal restock point or town within the dungeon, or if they are sneaking out to convert loot.
