## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Slime Kraken's Pool:** A collection of rotting, mangled bodies was discovered near the pool. The victims included humans, goblins, and beastmen (no halflings were present). Notably, all victims had been stripped of their equipment and were missing various body parts. 
* **Settite Activity:** Based on the presence of prisoners and the nature of the sacrifices, it is hypothesized that the Settites are sacrificing prisoners to the Slime Kraken as part of a ritual to desecrate the Temple of Thoth. (Source: Hypothesis formulated by Greybrown).

## 4. World Rulings
* **Bypassing Obstacles:** Certain magical abilities (such as *Shape Earth*) or physical tools (such as mining equipment) can bypass one-way doors, provided the creator of the door did not account for such methods. However, magic and tools are not guaranteed to work as expected in all instances.
* **Dungeon Scale:** The Forum of Set is believed to be significantly larger than currently explored, with rumors suggesting much more remains undiscovered.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Cursed Rubies:** Found within the eyes of a recent creature encounter. Uvash Edzuson has identified them as "obviously cursed," though they remain in the company's possession.

## 6. Tactical Planning
The company has outlined two primary operational phases for upcoming sessions:

**Phase I (Session 23b):**
1. Engage the Settite faction in combat.
2. If the company remains relatively unscathed, push westward to locate Lillian.
3. Upon successful rescue, return to the secret sarcophagus room.
4. Deliver food (previously brought to the baboon mosaic) to Gog.
5. Proceed to the Inn to retrieve a mule.
6. Execute an immediate retreat to Gosterwick.
7. *Contingency:* If significantly injured during the Settite encounter, bypass the search and proceed directly to the extraction route.

**Phase II (Session 24):**
1. If Lillian has not yet been rescued, continue the search mission.
2. If the rescue is complete, pivot to exploration of the Halls of Thoth or the Well of Light.
3. Fulfill a standing promise to the Inn by purchasing and delivering fresh produce and hops.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
* **Lillian's Exact Location:** While the direction (southwest) is known, the precise distance and coordinates remain unconfirmed.
* **The Stripping of Gear:** The mechanism or entity responsible for stripping equipment from the bodies at the Kraken's pool—distinct from the Kraken's own feeding—remains unknown.
* **Settite Movement:** It is unknown if the Settites have already moved their prisoners due to the company's recent discovery of their operations.
* **Dungeon Logistics:** The existence of any hidden restock points or towns within the deeper levels of the dungeon remains an unconfirmed hypothesis.
