**Lore & Discoveries**
* **The City of Brass:** A flying city located on the Elemental Plane of and serving as the heart of Efreet culture (Research: Uvash Edzuson).
* **Vampiric Vulnerabilities:** Vampires are susceptible to sunlight, drowning, and stakes through the heart. They possess high Charm capabilities; inflicting self-inflicted pain may aid resistance (Source: *Great Vampire Lords and their Demise*).
* **Anatomical Knowledge:** The company has gained specialized knowledge regarding the vital organs of Rudishva, Varumani, Varuda, and Kaliyani (Source: *Biological Systems: an Owner’s Manual*).
* **Artifacts of Arden Vul:** A drunk guard informed Vallium of powerful artifacts within the dungeon, including the Orb of Krieg, the [[items/Ebon Spear of Arden.md|Ebon Spear]], and the Bone Ring of Jagri-Naz. The Sage of the Middle Levels, a man named Gog, is said to be the best source for information on their locations.
* **Theft from the Tower of Pol:** It was confirmed that various items were taken from the Tower during the period when [[npcs/Choumnos.md|Choumnos]] was enslaved.

**World Rulings**
* **Spell Interaction (Wall of Lightning):** The spell *Wall of $\text{Lightning}$* creates a perimeter around an area; it cannot be cast through a creature to deal direct damage, regardless of the target's size. 
* **Direct Damage Restrictions:** Spells intended for utility or creation (e.g., *I Create Food*) cannot be used to deal direct damage (e.g., causing heart attacks).
* **Smithing Costs:** [[npcs/Torunn the Smith.md|Torunn the Smith]] produces superior "fine balanced" blades at a cost of 110% of the standard list price.
* **Lanyard Terminology:** Chain lanyards and woven steel lanyards are considered functionally identical.
* **Religious Discounts:** Temples provide holy water, and pious individuals may receive discounts on such purchases.

**Item Intelligence**
* **Bone Flute:** The collector Wicktrimmer has expressed interest in purchasing this item.
* **Horn of Demon Command:** Identified as an "evil" item.
* **Lesser Power Supplies:** These are identified as potential leverage for negotiations with the Troll Thegn; the company possesses several fully charged units from the Kerbog Khan's workshop.
* **Fine/Balanced Wooden Stakes (Bokken):** High-quality, well-crafted stakes cost $40 each, whereas standard wooden stakes cost $1.
* **Resurrection Items:** The company currently possesses several resurrection items, including two scrolls and a cup.

**Tactical Planning**
* **The Troll Thegn Negotiation:** The company intends to travel via rugs and teleportation pads to reach the Court of the Troll Thegn. The plan is to present the head of Kerbog Khan and a cache of charged Lesser Power Supplies in exchange for an item from the Thegn's treasury.
* **Vampire Eradication:** Following the negotiation, the company plans to move to the Northwest area to engage vampire populations using prepared silver weapons, holy water, and wooden stakes.

**Unresolved Threads**
* **The Bounty Location:** It remains unconfirmed whether the official bounty for Kerbog Khan is to be claimed in [[locations/Archontos.md|Archontos]] or Narsileon.
* **Thegn Negotiations:** The company has not yet decided whether to disclose the existence of the Lesser Power Supply Recharger to the Troll Thegn, nor which specific item to request from the treasury.
* **Weapon Naming:** Uvash Edzuson is still seeking a suitable name for his new pickaxe.
* **Travel Logistics:** The company has not yet decided whether to use the one-time "fast forward" mechanic to bypass travel time during upcoming exploration.
