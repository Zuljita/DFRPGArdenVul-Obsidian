The company experienced a period of unprecedented prosperity following a massive haul of loot, resulting in the distribution of 4 XP to all active members. ## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **Merchant Economics (Coinbase):** It has been established that the merchant Coinbase provides a 70% sale value for items brought to them (effectively taking a 30% cut from the top).
*   **Valuation of Goods:** Gems are valued at 100% of their worth upon sale, whereas jewelry is valued at only 40%.
*   **Beastmen Territory:** The Beastmen have established clear boundaries to the edges of their territory; they have been observed to be polite but firm regarding these borders.

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Invisibility and Defense:** Active defenses, such as sacrificial blocks or parries, do not break the effect of *Invisibility*, provided the user does not convert the defense into an aggressive action.
*   **Aggressive Parry:** An aggressive parry is generally optional, with rare exceptions for supernatural innate attacks that deal damage via proximity.
*   **Vision and Weather:** The *Water Vision* spell is capable of seeing through the effects of a *Fog* spell.
*   **Racial Statistics:** Racial attributes such as HP, Perception, or Lifting Strength cannot be converted into full base statistics (e.g., Glimmering Goblins possess fixed Perception and HP based on their IQ and HT).
*   **Availability of Consumables:** Potion belts and potions themselves are not available for purchase in town unless acquired through a specific availability roll during play.

## 5. Item Intelligence
*   **Magic Leather Armor:** Identified as magical. While highly desirable for frontline combatants, the party determined that selling it was necessary to maximize gold and XP gains.
*   **Black Pearls & Jade Necklace:** These items were presented to the default merchant for identification, but the attempt failed, leaving their specific properties unknown.

## 6. Tactical Planning
*   **Future Acquisitions:** The company intends to procure iron pikes, longer ropes, and improved solutions for carrying loot.
*   **Spell Utility:** Future scouting operations may rely heavily on *Flight* and *Invisibility*.
*   **Combat Preparation:** Discussion has begun regarding the potential utility of *Concussion* and *Glue* against smaller humanoid threats like halflings.
*   **Buffing Protocols:** If Ioannes acquires *Armor* or *Shield*, it is recommended to cast *Bless* on the target before applying these defensive spells.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
*   **Faction Diplomacy:** The party has yet to decide whether to align with, or oppose, the Beastmen or the Halflings.
*   **Loot Strategy:** A tension remains between the desire to keep useful magic items and the necessity of selling them for XP and gold.
*   **Resource Management:** Whether to prioritize higher-cost spells like *Analyze Magic* versus more immediate utility spells like *Concussion*.
*   **Buffing Synergy:** The potential utility of using *Bless* in conjunction with *Shield* or *Armor* on frontline members.
