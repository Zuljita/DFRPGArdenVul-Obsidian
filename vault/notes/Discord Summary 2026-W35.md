**Summary**
The company engaged in extensive scholarly research and alchemical experimentation, uncovering significant historical details regarding the architecture of Arden Vul and the lineage of local nobility. While preparations for a confrontation with the dragon Craastonistorex have begun, the week was also marked by the acquisition of Rudishva instructional data and the pursuit of long-term construction projects for the temple of Zorradrim.

**Lore & Discoveries**
* **Arden Vul Architecture:** The original 14 round towers of the Arden Vul walls were replaced by more cost-effective square towers during a later renovation. (Source: Azure Keep library documents).
* **The Flooded Tombs:** A massive, treasure-filled flooded cavern exists near the Shrine of Marius, currently largely undefended due to the water levels. (Source: Vallium's carousing).
* **Rudishva Technology:** The use of Rudishva weapons requires strict adherence to safety protocols found in instructional manuals to avoid accidental self-injury or death. (Source: *Military Instructional Manual* data crystal).
* **Political Tensions:** There is growing concern among some groups regarding the resilience of Archontean reliance on specific key families. (Source: *Tibe-Riv’s Primer of Primitive Cultures*).

**World Rulings**
* **Loot and Experience:** Experience points for loot are earned when items are sold as soon as reasonably possible (typically during the first available downtime). If loot is held until a later session, the XP is not realized until that subsequent session.
* **Construction and Artistry:** An 8-foot bronze statue can cost between $1,000 (shoddy) and $10,000 (masterpiece). Construction labor in Gosterwick is subject to a 10% price increase over standard lists.
* **Magic and Movement:** The maximum movement speed ceiling for a character under the effects of both a Flight spell/potion and a Haste spell remains 10.
* **Weaponry Specifications:** Rudishva laser rifles are physically larger, heavier, and more power-intensive than their pistol counterparts.
* **Amulet Properties:** *Resist Fire* amulets provide protection against laser-based weaponry. (Source: Established via testing on a goat and the raid on Kerbog Khan).

**Item Intelligence**
* **Rudishva Power Supplies:** These cells are roughly the size of two stacked silver dollars (the diameter of a D-cell battery but with the thickness of a coin) and weigh 0.25 lbs. Selling these to local buyers may reveal Rudishva secrets; full value is only obtainable from those with complementary technology or instruction.
* **Rudishva Communication Bracelets:** These devices facilitate translation between languages but cannot be used to issue commands to machines. They are tracked by the ship computer (Akla-Cha) for emergency paging of the party.
* **Resist Fire Amulets:** Proven effective against laser fire; essential for protection during high-intensity combat involving energy weapons.

**Tactical Planning**
The company intends to execute a strike against the dragon Craastonistorex in the following session. The plan involves departing Gosterwick using Levitation or *Walk on Air* while wearing *Resist Fire* amulets. Vael will maintain *Invisibility* for the party as they ascend the western cliffside to reach the surface ruins. Upon breaching the tower, the company will either loot the basement hoard or engage the Wyverns. If successful, the company intends to harvest the dragon (claws, teeth, blood, heart) and begin clearing the surface level of Arden Vul.
