# Weekly Knowledge Base: Arden Vul
**Week Ending:** 2026-10-23 23:00 CDT

**Summary**
Following a hiatus in active exploration, the company focused on the liquidation of recent spoils and the scholarly examination of recovered documents. Significant progress was made in repairing damaged weaponry through the use of spider silk, though the deactivation of all active spells has left the party's current trajectory uncertain due to the loss of any magical traces.

**Events & Actions by Character**

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**
* **Town actions taken:** 
    * Conducted research into several recovered texts:
        * *Set Theology*: An anonymous text noted for its poor spelling and grammar.
        * *Writ regarding Kerbog Khan*: A legal document authorizing Asrotelian to apprehend the Rogue Wizard, Cerbactos (also known as Kerbog Khan), either alive or dead. The warrant is issued for charges of theft and treason in Narsileon, bearing the seals of The Collegium of Cinder and Keko, thesmothete of the House of Sight.
        * *Nonsense Scribbles*: A collection of writings that remained indecipherable even when subjected to the *Gift of Letters* spell.
* **Character advancement:** 
    * No new skills or spells recorded this week. Note: Training for the "Resist Evil" advantage costs $40 per point for those possessing the Priest piece, a cost that limits the ability of Ioannes and Uvash to advance in this area.
* **Equipment changes:** 
    * Received a share of loot totaling $12,120 (2 XP).

**Uvash Edzuson**
* **Town actions taken:** 
    * None recorded this week.
* **Character advancement:** 
    * Note: Training for the "Resist Evil" advantage costs $40 per point for those possessing the Priest piece, a cost that limits the ability of Uvash to advance in this area.
* **Equipment changes:** 
    * Received a share of loot totaling $9,430 (2 XP).

    * Participated in discussions regarding the long-term distribution of loot XP to balance the company's resources (aiming for a ratio where Ioannes receives 2 XP for every 1 XP Vallium receives).

    * Received a share of loot totaling $1,197 (1 XP).

**Vaelitharon "Vag" Sunshadow**
* **Equipment changes:** 
    * Received a share of loot totaling $10,960 (2 XP).

**Note on Item Repair**
The repair of the Crossbow of Speed was achieved by applying one pound of spider silk and the *Repair* spell; this process successfully increased the item's value without consuming arcanum.

**Lore & Discoveries**
* **The Narsileon Warrant**: The recovered writ confirms that Cerbactos (Kerbog Khan) is a wanted man in Narsileon, facing charges of theft and treason.

**World Rulings**
* **Training Costs**: For characters possessing the Priest piece, training the "Resist Evil" advantage costs $40 per point. Only one level may be taken at a time, and subsequent levels can only be increased after the current level has been used in play.

**Item Intelligence**

* **Crossbow of Speed**: Previously of diminished value due to a missing magic string. Following repair with spider silk, its value has increased.
* **Unnamed Sword**: This weapon possesses a volatile enchantment; it delivers an electric shock to any individual attempting to pick it up.
* **Universal Scroll of Protection from Acid**: Identified by Vallium Halcyon as a critical asset due to its unique function which cannot be replicated by other known means. The company has expressed a strong desire to retain this item.

**Tactical Planning**

* The company is currently debating whether to press forward with immediate exploration or utilize the upcoming period for downtime and recovery.
* Logistics regarding the feeding schedule of the Apes must be addressed, as their food shipment is due based on the timeline since the last feeding in the month of Ligarios.

**Unresolved Threads**

* **The Lost Trace**: Because all active spells were deactivated, the party currently lacks a magical trace to follow, making it impossible to track their previous path without further investigation.
* **The Kerbog Khan Bounty**: Cerbactos (Kerbog Khan) is dead; a reward for his head and the stolen books is offered in Narsileon (see [[sessions/Session 70 - Shopping in Archontos.md|Session 70]]).
* **The Apes' Sustenance**: The upcoming deadline for the Ape food shipment remains a pressing logistical concern.
