# Weekly Chronicle: The Archives of Gosterwick
**Date:** Week ending 2025-04-11  
**Subject:** The Gem of Insight, Economic Negotiations, and Beastmen Diplomacy

### 1. Summary
The company reached a pivotal decision regarding the "Gem of Insight," voting to sell the artifact to Lady Alexia to secure significant experience and gold for all members. Amidst this economic shift, the company engaged in complex negotiations with local figures like Lyssandra concerning the political and financial implications of the Arcane Practitioner's Club. Preparations are now underway for a diplomatic approach to the Beastmen near the Great Hall, with ongoing debate regarding the method of entry and the handling of dragon tribute.

### 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Gem of Insight:** An artifact that provides +1 IQ, Will, and Per if held continuously for approximately three months. If the holder loses or sells the gem after the effect has taken hold, they retain the increased Intelligence but lose the increased Will and Perception. (Source: Analysis via *Analyze Magic* and Lyssandra’s clarification)
*   **The Glass Square:** A fragile object identified by Pelteon as a key to a teleportation device; however, it requires placement within a larger teleportation apparatus to function. (Source: Identification by Pelteon)
*   **The Set Priests:** Known slavers who patrol the Long Stairs leading to their Forum. Those reaching the Forum itself are considered safe from enslavement. (Source: Local Rumors)
*   **The Pyramid of Thoth:** A structure in the ruined city that connects to numerous underground levels, eventually leading to a massive cavern and lake at the lowest depths. (Source: Local Rumors)
*   **The Great Chasm Cistern:** A massive, 100-foot deep cistern covering an area the size of a small town, ruled by a Kraken. (Source: Local Rumors)
*   **The Imperial Span:** A stone bridge created by Marius Tricotor to allow Archon troops to traverse the Great Chasm. (Source: Local Rumors)
*   **Hall of Forty Pillars:** Located near the Obsidian Gates; it is inhabited by albino toads that transform into demons if the Gates are touched improperly. (Source: Player Observation/Rumor)

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Armor Availability:** The heaviest armor available in this region is heavy scale. Armor types classified as "plate" (such as brigandine or segmented plate) do not exist in this setting. Dwarven armor also does not exist. Elven Heavy Mail is the best available option when in stock.
*   **Magic Item Procurement:** No magical items are available for direct purchase from merchants; all enchanted items must be discovered through exploration. Mundane items (like cloth armor) can be purchased and modified (Ornate/Fine) by tailors, but the enchantment itself must be found.
*   **Fast-Draw Mechanics:** The rules for Fast-Draw are strictly tied to specific weapon specialties. While some "unrealistic" specialties (such as 1H axes, 1H maces, small bucklers, and javelins) are permitted, a character requires the specific skill (e.g., Fast-Draw [Shuriken]) to use that item effectively. Small bucklers are allowed despite weight, but not all shields.
*   **Spell Effects:** The spells *Alertness* and *Keen (Sense)* provide bonuses to Perception-based skills only when those skills are being used in a Per-based manner (e.g., finding a secret door with Traps). They do not help if the skill is not Per-based (e.g., figuring out how to open a secret door). The spell *Restore Memory* can only recall information that the subject had successfully memorized, not merely witnessed.
*   **Shield Arm Mechanics:** A hand on a shield arm can "hold" but not "wield" a weapon. It cannot be used to load or ready two-handed weapons (like Large Nets or Blowpipes).

### 5. Tactical Planning
*   **Beastmen Diplomacy:** The company is debating the approach to the Beastmen. While one option involves approaching via the "back door" to avoid conflict with Halflings, there is a strong counter-argument to use the "front door" (Halfling entrance) to be diplomatic, especially since the Statue of Thoth has been operated and the Halflings are aware of the company's presence.
*   **Dragon Tribute:** There is an ongoing discussion regarding leaving a tribute of gold or jewelry at the dam for the dragon. The company is weighing whether this should be done at the start of a session to ensure accurate loot accounting and whether it counts as a purchase or a reduction in session loot.
