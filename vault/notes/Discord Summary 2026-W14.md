# Weekly Knowledge Base: 21st of Molivios, 2994 AE

## 1. Summary
The company focused on significant research into the Arena Lord and historical figures, alongside successful alchemical endeavors by Vallium Halcyon. While Vaelitharon Sunshadow was absent from recent operations, the remaining members prepared for an upcoming expedition to the Well of Light and the Beacon, with plans to visit Temrin for essential mapping.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**
* **Research:** Conducted an investigation into the identity and nature of the Arena Lord. He discovered that the current Lord has ruled the SS Arena for centuries and is an undead entity with no known name. The Lord’s authority is absolute within the arena, governing the gladiators, the rules of engagement, and the life or death of combatants.
* **Character Advancement:** Immediate goals include increasing Intelligence to 16 and Hit Points and Hit Dexterity to 14. Long-term objectives include reaching 20 Spells, 20 Force Points, and 10 Divine Eminence (Holy), alongside expanding defensive capabilities through Enhanced Block, Enhanced Parry, and increased Shield/Axe & Mace proficiency.

**Vallium Halcyon**
* **Alchemy:** Successfully brewed an *Alkahest Grenade*.
* **Town Actions:** Spent 125 gold pieces to facilitate a successful Carousing roll. Through interaction with Jenks of the Five Fingers of Destiny, he gathered intelligence regarding the "hoppers"—a toad-like race believed to be the original builders of Arden Vul. These creatures inhabit a pitted ziggurat at the foot of the Great Chasm, mark their territory with toxic oil, and are capable of leaping 50 feet before striking with tridents.

The text details the life of Isadora, daughter of a provincial chartoularios, who transitioned from philosopher to defender of Priscus Pulcher. The chronicle concludes with her rescuing Priscus from the gibbet in Narsileon following his execution by order of the Archon.
* **Equipment Changes:** Currently carries Uvash Edzuson's shield (noted in records as erroneously attributed to Vallium).

**Uvash Edzuna**

* **Financials:** Reported a personal treasury of approximately 66,700 gold pieces and expressed a commitment to utilizing these funds for party-wide logistical needs and advancements.

**Zuljita**

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Arena Lord:** An unnamed, ancient undead ruler who maintains absolute control over the Arena’s laws and gladiators (Source: Research by Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios).
* **The Hoppers:** A toad-like race of builders residing in a ziggurat at the Great Chasm. They utilize toxic oil on walls and hunt via long-distance leaps (Source: Carousing/Jenks of the Five Fingers).
* **Isadora & Priscus Pulcher:** Historical figures involving the transition from philosophy to warfare; Isadora famously rescued Priscus from the Narsileon gibbet (Source: *A True History of Isadora...*).
* **Gemma of Arcturos:** A wizard who vanished roughly 210 years ago (Source: Research by Vallium Halcyon).

## 4. World Rulings
* **Flight:** Provides a Move score of 10. The subject maintains control over this movement, incurring no penalties to combat skills. This movement is subject to encumbrance.
* **Haste:** Temporarily increases both the Move and Dodge scores of the subject.
* **Levitate:** A Move score of 3 that ignores all encumbrance.
* **Arena Combat:** Fights against outsiders are typically not conducted to the death, though specific rules apply. While some bouts prohibit magic, this is not a universal rule for all matches.

## 5. Item Intelligence

* **Amulet of Voice:** To be utilized during upcoming communications with the Six Fingers.
