# Weekly Chronicle: The Company’s Progress
**Week Ending:** 2/15/2025

## 1. Summary
The company has transitioned from intensive scholarly research into a period of strategic planning for upcoming expeditions. While recent alchemical endeavors have yielded mixed results, new intelligence regarding the religious significance of local obelisks and the movements of the halfling factions has been gathered. The party is currently preparing for a mission to rescue a priestess of Mitra.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Thothian Obelisks:** Ancient obelisks exist beneath Arden Vul; they are associated with demon-worshipping hoppers and were preserved by the Thothians (*Book of Priors*).
*   **Teleportation Hazards:** Doors marked with an ibis are known to facilitate teleportation, a remnant of the magical era of the Thoth priests (Observation via Carousing).
*   **Pathfinding:** A Pathfinder check regarding the "right" way to the troll lifts yielded no sensed path.

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Security Protocols:** Secret doors must be closed behind the party to prevent pursuit.
*   **Delve Interruptions:** If a delve is paused mid-session, no free healing or loot distribution is permitted; furthermore, characters cannot be changed unless they return to town.
*   **Magic Item Properties:** 
    *   Glass spheres function similarly to potions but must be smashed on the floor to activate.
    *   The Torc of Protection provides a magical force field over the head with a general Damage Resistance (DR) of 6.
    *   The *Levitation* potion allows the drinker to move at Move 3 for a duration of one hour.
    *   The *Ring of Illusion Disguise* is ineffective for significant changes in physical size, as such transformations appear suspicious to observers.
    *   An explosive fireball sphere is designed to explode upon impact.
*   **Alchemy & Skills:** The *Seeker* skill may only be an attempt once per week. Weapon Master training (level 30) covers a small class of weapons, including the staff, spear, and polearm.

## 5. Item Intelligence
*   **Damaged Magic Mail:** This item possesses a slight orange glow unknown to local smiths. While it provides protection comparable to light mail, its structural integrity is compromised, making it difficult to repair with current knowledge.
*   **Boots of Attraction:** These boots are cursed; rather than attracting people, they appear to attract incoming damage.
*   **Dwarven Pick:** A highly potent tool held by the company.
*   **Magic Inventory:** The company's recent acquisitions include a magic helmet and a "magic not-helmet."

## 6. Tactical Planning
The company intends to undertake a mission to rescue Lillian, a priestess of Mitra. The proposed route involves:
1.  Ascending the Long Stair to the surface ruins northwest of the pyramid and proceeding into the basement.
2.  Moving north to reach the Halls of Thoth.
3.  Navigating west around halfling territory toward the Children of Deino.
4.  **Primary Objective:** Negotiate with the Children of Deino using food and offerings as trade for Lillian. The party will use the *Trace* spell or scrying at the beastman entrance to confirm her location.
5.  **Contingency Plan:** Should the primary objective fail, the company will return to the Well of Light to utilize *See Secrets* and explore previously unmapped areas, potentially using the teleporting pyramid in the southwest corner for egress.
6.  **Logistics:** The party intends to bring a mule along to carry necessary supplies and trade goods.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
*   **The Priestess:** The exact location and current condition of Lillian remain unknown.
*   **The Philosopher:** The purpose of the silver eyes and the subsequent steps regarding the eyeless statue ("The Philosary") are unidentified.
*   **The Obelisk Mystery:** The significance of the obelisk featuring a chest and a pentagram remains uninvestigated.
*   **Halfling Diplomacy/Warfare:** Whether to use propaganda to incite a bounty against the halflings or to attempt direct infiltration via the *Ring of Illusion Disguise* (including the proposed plan for Vallium to impersonate two halflings by standing on another's shoulders).
*   **Repairing the Mail:** The method for repairing the damaged, glowing magic mail is currently unknown.
