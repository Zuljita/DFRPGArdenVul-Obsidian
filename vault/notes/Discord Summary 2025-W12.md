## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Hill of the Gods:** Located in the northwest of Gosterwick, this area houses the primary religious centers. The temples of Demma, Mitra, Lucreon, and Heschius Ban are staffed by full-time clerics. A "House of the Gods" serves as a shared temple for other deities, featuring shrines maintained by volunteers and wandering priests.
* **The Yellow Cloak Inn:** A prominent five-story structure near the Central Market with a three-story addition. The establishment is managed by Margot the Red (a Thorcin) and her husband Bricwine, with Helle (a Wiskin) tending the main bar. The inn offers guest rooms and dormitories; a weekly rate of $150 covers lodging and three meals daily. Notably, spellcasting is strictly prohibited within the inn, particularly if it results in magical mishaps.
* **Rumor - The Singing Grotto:** Reports suggest an eighth-level underground grotto containing carved heads that sing recognizable tunes. Success in matching their songs may reveal treasure, but failure is said to be fatal.
* **Rumor - The Cult of Set:** Historical accounts mention the ancient Cult of Set and its "Guild of Service," a slave trade operated under the doctrine of the strong dominating the weak.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Research Mechanics:** Conducting research requires the Research skill, access to a library, and one week of time. A blind research roll is performed at the end of the week to determine if any rumors are uncovered.
* **Clerical Services:** The cost of removing curses varies; clerics of the local temples offer "friendly" discount rates compared to standard book rates.
* **Combat Mechanics (Blind Fighting):** A successful Blind Fighting roll eliminates the -2 penalty for parrying from the rear, effectively making it 0, provided that Peripheral Vision is active.
* **Skill Acquisition:** Certain skills, such as Climbing, do not require formal training to increase in level. However, skills like Search, Stealth, and Swimming require specific instruction. The Gesture skill is considered accessible to all due to the high ambient noise near the local waterfalls.
* **Elven Priesthood:** For a full elf, serving as a priest is considered an Unbelievable [1] feat, requiring either human upbringing or a highly unorthodox religious practice.
