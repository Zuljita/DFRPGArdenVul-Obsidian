# Weekly Chronicle: The Company’s Progress
**Week Ending 2025-05-09**

## 1. Summary
The company remains deep within the megadungeon, operating *in media res*. As the party has not transitioned to town this week, no loot or experience points were distributed. Efforts have been focused on tactical deliberation regarding the halfling-controlled territories and planning for potential extraction from the current level.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

### Uvash Edzuson
* **Character Advancement:** Increased Vitality by +1 HP; through the integration of existing lifting and striking Strength, total regular Strength has increased by +0 (no change to regular Strength).
* **Equipment/Traits:** It has been noted that when traversing caverns, Uvash tends to hold his shield atop his head—a peculiar habit likely born of recent encounters with stalactites.

### Vallium Halcyon
* **Tactical Planning:** Identified the known exit route through the Glory of Thoth, currently under halfling control. Proposed an alternative, high-risk extraction route involving a secret passage behind the baboon mosaic, utilizing a teleport pyramid to reach the Well of Light level, followed by flight or levitation to reach the surface.

### Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios
* **Character Status:** Skill proficiency has decreased to 12 due to the frequent application of *Bless* spells.
* **Tactical Planning:** Intends to utilize *Flaming Weapon* on Vallium Halcyon and Michael before withdrawing from direct combat.

### Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow
* **Character Advancement:** Increased proficiency in Lockmaster (+1) and Stealth (+1).
* **Research/Spells:** Confirmed possession of the *Seek Magic* spell. Expressed intent to acquire the *Pathfinder* spell upon the next return to town, noting its necessity should teleporters prove unreliable.

### Mercenary (Unspecified)
* **Tactical Advice:** Advised the company to prioritize the acquisition of *Pathfinder* if teleportation remains unstable; suggested that if *Seek Earth* is already mastered, the company should seek *Seek Water* (to unlock the Water college) or **Seek Magic*.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Glory of Thoth:** This territory is confirmed to be under the control of halfling factions.
* **Newmarket Connections:** It is hypothesized that the local halfling factions maintain connections within the town of Newmarket, which may provide future avenues for equipment procurement.
* **The Baboon Mosaic:** A secret passage exists behind this feature, potentially providing a route to the Well of Light level.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Exploration and Rewards:** For multi-session delves, there is no multiplier applied to exploration CP or loot thresholds; all findings from a continuous delve are tallied at the conclusion of the session.
* **Session Transitions:** If the company concludes a session outside of town but returns to town and ventures back into the dungeon within the same session, they may claim loot and exploration CP for both distinct delves, with all rewards distributed at the end of the second session.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Pathfinder (Spell):** Identified as a high-priority acquisition for Vaelitharon Sunshadow to mitigate the risks of unreliable teleportation within the dungeon.

## 6. Tactical Planning
The company is currently debating several courses of action regarding the halfling-controlled territories:
* **Diplomacy:** Attempting to negotiate a flat fee for passage or establishing a long-term business relationship with the halflings.
* **Aggression:** A high-risk plan to send specialized combatants (buffed with *Flight*, *Invisibility*, and *Blur*) to assassinate Phlebotomas, thereby decapitating the organization's leadership.
* **Extraction:** If heavily wounded, the company intends to retreat toward Gosterwick, though it is noted a closer inn may exist as a potential destination.
* **Information Management:** If encountering goblins, the company intends to mention "Bellringer" to them.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
* **Halfling Awareness:** It remains unknown if the halfling factions are aware of the company's recent movements or if they can be deceived regarding their identity.
* **Extraction Route:** A final decision has not been reached between using the known exit through the Glory of Thoth or the more dangerous route via the Well of Light.
* **Diplomatic vs. Hostile Stance:** The company has yet to decide whether to pursue a policy of negotiation, strongarming, or assassination regarding the halfling presence.
* **Territorial Succession:** If the halfling faction is successfully dismantled, the company is considering gifting their territory to the "Children of Deino."
