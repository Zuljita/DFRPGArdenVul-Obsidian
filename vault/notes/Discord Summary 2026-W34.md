**Lore & Discoveries**
* **The Hoppers:** It is understood that the Hoppers were the first inhabitants of Arden Vul; they utilize the various toad-like statues throughout the city as teleportation nodes, emerging from the statues' open mouths. (Source: Vallium’s Carousing)
* **Yasidoranicil Text:** A 12lb volume regarding dragon history; it is written in Draconic and cannot be read without magical assistance, though it contains significant illustrations. (Source: GM Ruling)

**World Rulings**
* **Iris Valve Safety:** The iris valves within the Beacon contain internal safety mechanisms that prevent them from closing upon any inserted cords or tethers.
* **The Elvish Rope:** The rope associated with Akla-Cha does not respond to commands issued by the Beacon computer.

**Tactical Planning**
The company intends to execute the following route for the upcoming session:
1. Utilize the Rugs for travel to the Beacon.
2. Use the Rudishva Teleportation Pad to reach the Cloister, then navigate southwest through the secret passage to the acid pool room, proceeding north and east toward the Troll Lifts and Arena W map.
3. Visit the Marius fountain to seek permanent boons.
4. Explore the snow and ice region (requiring Fur Boots and multiple Tripods of Heating) with the objective of locating the Thothian Teleportation Ring.
5. Use *Levitation* or *Walk on Air* to explore the underside of the floating island, targeting gold deposits previously identified via *Seek Earth*.
6. Descend to the Court of the Troll Thegn to exchange charged Lesser Power Supplies for empty ones and deliver the kaliyani teeth to the Varumani Hunters.
