# Weekly Knowledge Base: 2025-05-30

## 1. Summary
Following the expedition to the Great Caverns, the company has focused on processing recent spoils and managing new logistical responsibilities. The week was defined by the acquisition of four cats—including a highly intelligent feline named Onyx—and the identification of hazardous flora and traps within recent loot. Significant progress was made in establishing trade with local entities, including the sale of giant lizard eggs to the Stunned Acolyte.

*   **Equipment Changes:** Received a share of recent treasure; currently holds a portion of the company's recent liquid assets.

*   **Town Actions:** Attempted to locate a "Field Guide to Irthuin Mushrooms" at Astableon’s to aid in future identification efforts; however, no such text was in stock.

**Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow**

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Red Veil Mushrooms:** Identified by Fael the druid; these fungi are highly dangerous, as consumption induces a state of berserk madness.
*   **Onyx the Familiar:** The black cat, Onyx, was revealed to be the former familiar of an elven wizard named Lyra, who was killed by giant reptiles. Onyx possesses high intelligence and can communicate basic instructions to other cats (Source: Fael/Onyx).
*   **The Half-Troll Gog:** Local rumors suggest that a half-troll known as Gog is a reliable source of information for those willing to parlay (Source: Rumor 10).
*   **Statue Eye Traps:** It is known that the gemstones serving as eyes for certain statues in the region are booby-trapped; attempting to remove them causes the statues to animate and attack (Source: Rumor 11).

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Kiai Mechanics:** Kiai functions as a Resisted roll (a subset of Quick Contests), and the Rule of 16 applies to its effects.
*   **Staff Combat:** Enhanced Parry (Staff) only applies to parrying missile weapons if the user also possesses the Weapon Master advantage for that specific weapon.
*   **Skill Specialization:** Survival (underground) and Naturalist are functionally interchangeable for identifying subterranean flora; however, training in the "underground" specialty is rare and difficult to find.
*   **Banking Fees:** The Silent Factor charges a monthly fee of 1.25% on all deposits.
*   **Item Value:** Permanent *Continual Light* on large objects holds limited market value due to the common availability of the spell among adventuring clerics and wizards.

## 5. Item Intelligence
*   **The Blue Staff:** A balanced staff featuring permanent blue *Continual Light*. It bears the inscription "Balthazar" in silver, though speaking this name has no effect. Note: The light cannot be deactivated. Chrysalis (formerly Thorne) has offered to trade her share of loot to acquire this item.
*   **The Trapped Statue Head:** Weighs 50 lbs. It features large gemstone eyes. **Warning:** Attempting to remove the gemstones will trigger the statue to animate and attack.
*   **Red Veil Mushrooms:** Identified as a hazardous biological agent; causes a bersermker state upon consumption.
*   **Magic Long Knife:** A valuable item currently in the company's possession that could be sold for additional XP.

## 6. Tactical Planning
*   **The Great Caverns Expedition:** The company has proposed two primary strategies for upcoming movement:
    1.  A direct assault on the territories of the mushroom-folk.
    2.  Negotiating with the half-troll Gog to secure intelligence and avoid unnecessary combat.
*   **Logistics:** Ioannes intends to use his expertise in Animal Handling to manage the cats, specifically focusing on finding suitable food for them within the APC.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
*   **The Fate of the Cats:** Finding permanent homes for the three remaining cats (the tabby female, the calico female, and the gray male "Bootstraps").
*   **Trap Testing:** Determining if the statue's gemstone trap can be triggered remotely via teleportation, ranged weaponry, apportation, or by levitating a small object (such as a mouse) toward the eyes.
*   **Spell Prioritization:** Deciding whether Ioannes should prioritize *Gift of Tongues* for negotiation or *Resist Fire* for survival.
*   **The Black Cat's Mission:** Identifying the "other wizard" whom Onyx claims needs his help.
