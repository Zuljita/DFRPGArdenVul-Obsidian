**Lore & Discoveries**
* **The II Legion Insignia:** A sheep's head insignia from the II Legion, destroyed during the Adrienic era, was recently recovered from a cave floor under the stalls in the Varumani market (Source: Geleg of Second Chance).
* **Thoth’s Treasury:** The first priests of the Ibis Lord utilized a three-part ritual involving specific poses of the three main images of the God to reveal the treasury (Source: Note in the secret language of Thoth).
* **The Precincts of Thoth:** There are at least two pyramids within the dungeon levels of the Thoth precincts; the topmost causes premature aging, while a lower one is currently occupied by "depraved inhabitants." A third structure, a cursed ziggurat, lies even deeper (Source: Thorwald of Hama's company).
* **The Disappearance of Mt. Juventus:** The Mt. Juventus mine has ceased to exist; it did not merely run dry but physically disappeared from the landscape (Source: Barnaby Goodbarrel).

**World Rulings**
* **Magic Scrolls:** All *Scrolls of Protection from Magic* are universal in application, usable by wizards and/or clerics alike.
* **The Useful Pole:** This implement features three distinct lengths: 3' (a bo), 4' (a staff), and 6' (a spear). Despite its length, it is narrow enough to be carried within a pocket.
