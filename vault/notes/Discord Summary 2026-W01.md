# Weekly Chronicle: The Beacon Expansion & The Dragon’s Shadow
**Date:** 2/Fidios, 2993 AE  
**Subject:** Summary of Company Activities and Intelligence Gathered

## 1. Summary
This week was defined by the logistical expansion of the Beacon annex and the distribution of recent spoils. While the company focused on fortifying their new holdings and refining alchemical recipes, the looming threat of a dragon has begun to shift all strategic focus toward reconnaissance and specialized preparation.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

### Uvash Edzuson
*   **Research:** Undertook an investigation into the properties of the Feathers of Maat. Through the study of ancient human religious texts, it was discovered that these feathers may offer protection against Wights, Specters, and Vampires, provided they are held with steadfast faith.
*   **Town Actions:** Pursued methods to increase Intelligence and invest in Power Investiture; noted the necessity of increasing Strength and Will to resist magical influence.

### Vallium Halcyon
*   **Alchemy & Crafting:** Attempted to brew a Potion of Flight, which resulted in a failed, messy concolant that will require additional resources to rectify.
*   **Research:** 
    *   Studied the "Song of Arden," uncovering a verse regarding the relationship between fear and strength.
    *   Dedicated time to studying Mithric; has achieved proficiency in Spoken/Broken Mithric.
*   **Town Actions:** Assisted Basil with the provision of reading materials for Akla-Chah and Stamelis within the Beacon.

### Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios
*   **Alchemy & Crafting:** Successfully brewed a Major Healing Potion.
*   **Research:** Investigated the nature of "Secret Names." Findings indicate that while true names grant power over demons and devils, these entities frequently use aliases to deceive; the study concluded with a warning against ever engaging in conversation with demons.
*   **Equipment Changes:** Discovered a key during recent explorations, though it has since been lost and cannot be located.

### Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow
*   **Alchemy & Crafting:** 
    *   Successfully brewed a Potion of Gaseous Form (at a discount).
    *   Successfully reverse-engineered the Frost Cage Potion, obtaining its recipe at a cost of $200.
*   **Town Actions:** 
    *   Utilized *Shape Earth* to expand the Beacon annex, adding a larder, privy, and expanded library.
    *   Commissioned a silver signet ring from the jeweler Chairduster ($500 for the ring, plus $100 for the design).
    *   Negotiated the purchase of a Refrigeration Rock (Fridge Stone) for $10,000.
    *   Established a weekly stipend of $100 to Basil for larder maintenance.
    *   Consulted with the miner Bombur Mica regarding ventilation; however, the miner declined the contract due to the dragon threat.
    *   Coordinated the transport of bricks via Basil using the *Viator* rug.
*   **Research:** Continued studies in the Rudishvan language, noting the extreme difficulty of the full glyphic script and the existence of a simplified "Semi-Glyphik" variant.
*   **Equipment Changes:** 
    *   Acquired Spider Silk Robes; paid Pelteon to recharge the item's power.
    *   Sold old robes, with the proceeds shared equally among the company.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Feathers of Maat:** According to ancient human cult texts, holding these feathers with faith protects the bearer from Wights, Specters, and Vampires (Source: Uvash’s research).
*   **Secret Names:** Knowledge of a demon's true name provides leverage, but demons use aliases to manipulate and kill their interlocutors (Source: Ioannes’ research).
*   **The Song of Arden:** An ancient verse stating: *"Fear is the destroyer... Strength counters fear..."* (Source: Vallium’s research).
*   **Rudishvan Script:** The full written language is notoriously difficult; a simplified version known as "Semi-Glyphik" is commonly used by various regional factions (Source: Akla-Chah).
*   **The Floating Sage:** A non-magical scroll written in formal Mithric calligraphy contains the phrase *"Dubito, ergo cogito, ergo sum"* ("I doubt, therefore I think, therefore I am") (Source: Loot analysis).

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Vampiric Bite:** This ability functions upon living beings but is ineffective against constructs, undead, or machines.
*   **Divine Upgrades:** Increasing the level of Divine Grace requires payment for training and prayer.
*   **Alchemical Scaling:** Potions applied to larger creatures are treated as regular spells; this may result in reduced effects or increased difficulty to resist, though no bonus is granted when applying them to smaller creatures.
*   **Shrink Other:** This effect is classified as R-HT.
*   **Jewelry Production:** Jewelers can produce items in the $10 to $10,000 range; amounts exceeding this require the use of rare materials.

## 5. Item Intelligence
*   **Statuette of Feline Friendship:** This item attracts cats and allows the user to summon the Avatar of Bastet once per month during a full moon (Source: Vael’s *Analyze Magic*).
*   **Potion of Frost Cage:** Upon consumption or impact, the target is frozen in place. The target may attempt a Strength roll to break free, with the difficulty increasing by +1 for each subsequent turn.
*   **Smokeless Torches:** Identified as highly valuable; the company has resolved to sell one and keep one for use as a light source/dehumidifier within the Beacon.
*   **Magically Lightened Scale Armor:** Found to be unsuitable for Uvash due to dwarven proportions; slated for sale.

## 6. Tactical Planning
The company’s immediate intentions for the upcoming session include:
*   **Logistics:** Bulk purchasing of food supplies.
*   **Reconnaissance:** Utilizing the "Viator" rug and *Blink Other* to reach the surface ruins; investigating the dragon's lair/treasure hoard to identify weaknesses (specifically targeting flight, breath weapons, and wyvern presence).
*   **Diplomacy:** Meeting with Count Skleros; consulting the Children of Deino regarding support; potentially contacting the blacksmith Temrin for map commissions.
*   **Exploration:** Investigating the Pit of Fire, the Donkey Ears fountain area, and searching the Librarian's quarters for a "Secret Library."
*   **Combat Tactics:** Testing a *Lightning Wall* against the wraiths of the Tower of Scrutiny; potentially using *Shrink* potions to reduce the dragon's size (noting potential resistance).

## 7. Unresolved Threads

*   **The Dragon Threat:** Determining how to effectively counter dragon flight, breath weapons (acid/poison), and potential wyvern interference.
*   **Ventilation:** Finding a permanent solution for Beacon ventilation that does not allow rain or flooding into the annex.
