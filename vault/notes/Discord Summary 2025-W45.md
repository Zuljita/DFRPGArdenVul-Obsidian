### Training & Advancement Rules
- Language learning via intensive study: 1 point per week of downtime spent almost entirely on language study, progressing from Broken (Written or Spoken) to Accented. Native fluency takes significantly longer unless you have Language Talent; most people never get to Native in languages they didn't learn young. [archive | 2025-11-16]
- Languages can be consolidated into Language Talent provided no language gets worse and total points in languages + talent does not decrease. [archive | 2025-11-16; questions-for-gm | 2025-11-16]
- Primary and secondary attributes (ST, DX, IQ, HT, Will, Per) require no training until reaching very high levels. [archive | 2025-11-16; questions-for-gm | 2025-11-16]
- Laser beam weapons are trained by repeatedly pointing what appears to be the right end at a target and manipulating the firing control until proper handling is learned through trial and error. Once you get to that point, you make some targets and fire at them many, many, many times. If you have enough ammo to train enough, you can eventually put a point into the skill. If you run out of ammo first, you can't. This goes much faster if you have a trainer who already knows the skill. [archive | 2025-11-16; questions-for-gm | 2025-11-16]
