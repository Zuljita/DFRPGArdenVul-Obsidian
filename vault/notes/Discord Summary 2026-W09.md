# Weekly Chronicle: The Assault on the Forum of Set
**Date:** Week ending 2026-03-06

## 1. Summary
The company engaged in a high-intensity "Alpha Strike" against the Cult of Set, resulting in significant combat casualties and the loss of an arm for Uvash Edzuson. While the party successfully struck at the cult's leadership, the encounter has left several tactical questions regarding the surrounding chasm and the identity of potential thieves.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

### Uvash Edzuscson
* **Combat Injuries:** Sustained a severe injury during the assault on the Cult of Set, resulting in the loss of an arm.
* **Medical Considerations:** Discussed the use of *Restoration* to regrow the limb, noting that such magic requires a full month to take effect. The party also considered the utility of *Resist Pain* to mitigate ongoing trauma.

### Vallium Halcyon
* **Tactical Planning:** Formulated a comprehensive "To-Do" list for the exploration of the Forum of Set South Central region, focusing on scouting verticality and investigating nearby anomalies (the wood pile, the chimney, and the lever-adjacent hole).
* **Combat Records:** Confirmed the death of the individual known as Theodora during the recent engagement.

### Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios
* **Combat Actions:** Participated in the primary assault against the Settite forces.
* **Social/Religious Considerations:** Evaluated the possibility of converting unarmed cultists to the service of Demma, though the company ultimately dismissed this due to the logistical burdens of managing former enemies and the potential for religious complications.

### Vaelitharon "Verc" Sunshadow
* **Equipment Observations:** Noted the extreme difficulty regarding the use of Merenuithiel’s bow (rated at 23t), suggesting the necessity of alchemical stimulants or drugs to manage the physical strain.

### Chrysalis
* **Combat Status:** Remained under the effects of *Great Haste* through round 13 and again through round 17 of the engagement.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Temple Meeting:** A vision/sketch revealed a male goblin and a human female conversing with a red-haired, middle-aged woman previously seen in the Temple of Set (Source: Greybrown's description of Vaelitharon's vision).
* **Casualty Report:** The individual known as Theodora has been confirmed dead.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Sonic Interference:** The *Message* spell can be effectively countered or blocked by spells that produce silence, such as *Silence*.
* **Unarmed Combat:** For the purposes of the *Berserk* effect/spell, a fist or unarmed strike is classified as a melee weapon.
* **Magical Restoration:** The *Restoration* spell requires a duration of one month to successfully regrow lost limbs.
* **Lore-Master Restrictions:** The *Hidden Lore* skill cannot be acquired by existing characters through the purchase of the Lore-Master piece; this acquisition is restricted to new characters unless the information is discovered through active play.
* **Fatigue Management:** The *Awaken* spell serves as a cheaper, preemptive alternative to *Lend Energy* for addressing fatigue in combat, provided the subject has less than 1/3 FP remaining.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Merenuithiel’s Bow:** This heavy bow (2/3t) presents extreme physical challenges for the wielder. It is hypothesized that its use requires the consumption of alchemical drugs or potions, such as *Dawn*, to overcome the weight and strain.

## 6. Tactical Planning
The company has established several objectives for the exploration of the Forum of Set:
* **Vertical Exploration:** Utilize *Levitation* or *Flight* to survey both the upper and lower elevations of the current chasm, as well as the western chasm, to identify new passages.
* **Scouting:** Deploy a *Wizard Eye* or use a *Clairvoyance* potion to inspect the chimney for connections to other levels.
* **Environmental Investigation:** Examine the wood pile in the current chamber and investigate the hole located adjacent to the two existing levers.
* **Zombie Management:** Explore the zombie-occupied room; the company is considering using a fresh Settite corpse as a distraction to divert the undead from the party's path.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
* **The Identity of the Human Female:** It remains unconfirmed if the woman in the vision is Jadeel or the deceased Theodora.
* **The Goblin's Identity:** The company has yet to determine if the goblin seen in the vision is the individual known as Doffy.
* **The Rug Theft:** There is an ongoing hypothesis that the individuals seen in the vision may be responsible for the theft of the party's rug.
* **Cultist Presence:** The whereabouts and current strength of the remaining acolytes and clerics within the cult remain unknown.
* **Leadership Flight:** There is significant concern regarding whether the cult leadership will attempt to flee the area during future engagements.
* **Rival Adventurers:** The company suspects that previously encountered rival adventurers may be in possession of significant loot.
