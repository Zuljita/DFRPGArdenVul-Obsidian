## In-World Knowledge

### Ghosts & the Supernatural
- Ioannes found a book about ghosts at the Temple of Mitra stating that ghosts are real, typically left by someone who died horribly or with an important task uncompleted; some are benign while others are very dangerous. Turning or even destroying them is usually only temporary and they will often return to their haunted spot until their death is avenged, their task completed, their body properly blessed and buried, etc. Giving a good ghost a proper rest is a blessed deed worthy of a paladin of Mitra, in part because of the danger and in part because each ghost needs different treatment.
