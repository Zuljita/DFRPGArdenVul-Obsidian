# Weekly Chronicle: The Grudge Brigade
**Date:** Week Ending 2025-06-06

## 1. Summary
The company has focused its efforts on the liquidation of recent spoils and the pursuit of historical knowledge regarding recent dungeon encounters. While some members were absent, significant progress was made in identifying the provenance of recovered artifacts and preparing for upcoming spiritual confrontations.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Nature of Ghosts:** According to a text found at the Temple of Mitra, ghosts are entities left behind by those who died violently or with significant unfinished tasks. While some are benign, others are highly dangerous. Temporary measures such as "turning" or destruction are often ineffective, as spirits frequently return to their haunts until their death is avenged, their task completed, or their remains properly blessed and interred. It is established that giving a good ghost a proper rest is a blessed deed worthy of a paladin of Mitra.
*   **The Sheep's Head Rangers:** A herald at the Azure Keep identified a silver sheep’s head plate as the insignia of the 2nd Legion, known as the Sheep's Head Rangers. This unit was reportedly wiped out within Arden Vul approximately 250 years ago.
*   **The Cult of Set:** It is established that the Cult of Set has been banned for a significant period, though their current influence or presence in the local economy remains unconfirmed.

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Loot Liquidation:** Items sold within Gosterwick generate immediate loot experience (XP). While items can be transported to Newmarket for sale, doing so requires a three-day journey and delays the availability of funds and XP for the company.
*   **Market Value:** Standard recovered items are valued at 40% of their base worth when sold to merchants.
*   **Skill Training:** Practical experience in navigation and mapping does not substitute for formal training; specific instruction is required to unlock higher levels of skills such as *Absolute Direction*.

## 5. Item Intelligence
*   **Red "Set" Armor:** This equipment bears the unmistakable hue of the Cult of Set. The party utilized a contact at the docks to facilitate an anonymous sale to avoid alerting the Settites.
*   **Silver Sheep's Head Plate:** Identified as a relic of the 2nd Legion (Sheep's Head Rangers).
*   **Crossbows:** The smith Torunn noted that these crossbows appeared to be normal in construction, but were specifically made for individuals of uncommon strength (or those utilizing exceptionally strong allies).

## 6. Tactical Planning
The company is preparing for an encounter with a spectral entity. The following strategy has been proposed:
*   **Mercenary Deployment:** Chrysalis will be utilized for her ability to counter superior numbers and mitigate mental attacks (mind control). The GOAT will provide additional numbers and utility, specifically using *Swim* and **Breathe Water* to scout the lake beneath the falls.
*   **Spellcasting Strategy:** Ioannes intends to utilize *Affect Spirits* (targeting Michael), *Bravery*, and *Bless*. Uvash has provided a scroll of *Bless* to facilitate this. The GOAT may deploy *Mystic Mist* to obscure the party's presence.
