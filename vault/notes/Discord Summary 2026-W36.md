**Lore & Discoveries**
* **Dragon's Ledgers:** The ledgers recovered from the dragon's tower serve as a record of tribute, listing names, dates, and amounts paid by various adventuring parties and factions. (Source: Analysis by Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios).
* **Magic Keys:** The hoard contains several "Huge Magic Gold Keys" made of gold and platinum. These are not traditional keys but rather magic rods, 15 inches long and 1.5 inches in diameter. (Source: Analysis by Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios).
* **New Spells:** Three new spells were identified within Kel Bormol's Grimoire:
    * *Cleophalus's Look-Away Spell* (Illusion): Makes it difficult for viewers to focus on a target.
    * *Friend of the Hekate* (Knowledge/Information): Detects the nearest creature with Magery.
    * *The Pummeling Fists of Arak-Zhorr* (Air/Area): Fills an area with flailing air fists that deal 1d cr injury and can disarm targets. (Source: Identification by Vaelitharon Sunshadow).

**World Rulings**
* **Attribute Consolidation:** Secondary attribute bonuses that increase a character's point value may be consolidated into primary attributes for the purposes of spending character points.
* **Key Dimensions:** The "Huge Magic Gold Keys" are rod-like in construction, measuring 15 inches in length and 1.5 inches in diameter, with a weight of 15 lbs.

**Item Intelligence**
* **Dragon Hide:** Identified as having high utility for crafting new equipment; the party has decided to retain this material rather than selling it. A buyer, Nikandros the leatherworker, was identified during the appraisal process.
* **Kel Bormol's Grimoire:** A significant magical text containing several previously unknown spells. The party intends to keep this book for study.

**Tactical Planning**
The company has outlined a multi-step plan for the upcoming session:
1. Travel to the Beacon using Purple Rugs, then exit to the surface ruins of Arden Vul via Blink/Blink Other+Green Rugs.
2. Attempt to demolish the Tower of Scrutiny using various methods (e.g., elephant summoning, *Shape Earth*, or Rudishva technology).
3. Meet with Tuftwillig to present proof of Craastonistorex's death and claim rewards for the completed quest.
4. Utilize found fragments (the hands) to attempt a *Repair* spell on the statue of Vul.
5. Search surface levels, both land and water, for additional loot or entrances using specialized magic (*Earth Vision*, *Helms of Underwater Action*, etc.).
