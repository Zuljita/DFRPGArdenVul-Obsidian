# Weekly Chronicle: The Blood of Gosterwick
**Date:** 2025-09-05
**Subject:** Investigation of the Susarra Murder and Declaration of Hostilities

## 1. Summary
The peace of Gosterwick was shattered by the discovery of the headless corpse of the adventurer Susarra, an event that has set the company on a path toward open warfare with the Cult of Set. Following the identification of the crime scene, the company has committed to pursuing the perpetrators and avenging the fallen, even as a bounty has been placed upon their own heads.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

### Vallium Halcyon
* **Town Actions:** Investigated the murder scene at the Yellow Cloak Inn following an alert from Margot the Red; identified the body as Susarra based on her shield device; informed local authorities of Susarra's recent activities against the Settites. Met with Lillian to deliver a letter of introduction to the Temple of Thoth in Newmarket and inquired regarding the Tower of Apes.
* **Equipment Changes:** Currently wears an *Amulet of Missile Shield*, which precludes the simultaneous use of magical belts (such as the *Belt of Strength*).

### Uvash Edzuson
* **Town Actions:** Assisted in the investigation of Susarra's murder; examined the crime scene and noted that the decapitation was performed with significant lack of skill, involving multiple heavy blows from a large axe that left dents in the bedding.
* **Equipment Changes:** None this week.

### Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios
* **Town Actions:** Assisted in the investigation of the murder scene; conducted hurried research into the history and nature of the Cult of Set.
* **Research Results:** Uncovered that Set was a Greater God of the old pantheon (the Ten), presiding over power, knowledge, domination, slavery, vengeance, and serpents. Learned that worship of Set was banned by imperial decree in 1839 AEP and that rumors persist regarding a "Forum of Set" within the ruins of Arden Vul and an active slave trade via the Settite Guild of Service. Discovered an ancient rescript claiming the Set temple in Arden Vul succeeded in breeding a fearsome species of animals (possibly golems) that "shared all the attributes of Lord Set" in a secret cavern near the waterfall.
* **Equipment Changes:** Has taken possession of the *Bracers of Force (+3)* for the current week to achieve DR 3 at all locations and to facilitate unencumbered movement (Move 5, Dodge 8).

### Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow
* **Town Actions:** Received a package from Audun Yellow-Eyes of the House of Sight containing a blood sample from the victim; utilized *Seeker* and *Trace* magic to locate the missing remains.
* **Research Results:** Successfully located Susarra's severed head approximately 10 miles north of Gosterwick, situated within a backpack in a well-furnished room featuring a coffee table, chairs, ewers, and a writing desk.
* **Equipment Changes:** Currently wears the *Amulet of the Silver Tongue*.

### Michael
* **Equipment Changes:** None this week.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Murder of Susarra:** The victim was killed via decapitation using a large axe; the attacker was noted to be unskilled, as evidenced by multiple strikes and damage to the bedding (Investigation at Yellow Cloak Inn).
* **The Cult of Set:** Historically significant as a banned religion; followers utilize "Was" sticks, "Set animals" (described as weird, mean dogs with square ears), and red scale armor (Research by Ioannes). A Temple of Set is known to have existed within Arden Vul.
* **Newmarket Rumors:** The Tower of the Ape is owned by the reclusive wizard Tikun Thane; rumors suggest the presence of white-furred apes within the tower (Information from Lillian).
* **The Bounty:** A letter exists, purchased from the broker Coinbase, offering $2,500 per person to capture or kill the members of the Grudge Brigade: Vael, Ioannes, Michael, Uvor, Vallium, and Susarra. The letter serves as a pass for the Duty Deacon in the Forum of Arden Vul (Information from Coinbase).

## 4. World Rulings
* **Training Restrictions:** Training for skills or spells is unavailable if the company is rushing back to the dungeon to pursue an and active lead.
* **Magery Advancement:** Magery levels may be increased through practice without formal training.
* **Skill Acquisition:** The *Diplomacy* skill cannot be increased via spending points without prior training in the skill.
* **Force DR Stacking:** *Bracers of Force* do not stack with mundane or magical armor DR at a specific location (the higher value is taken); however, they are cumulative with other non-armor magical DR.
* **Torc of Protection:** This item provides Force DR only from the neck upward.
* **Tough Skin:** This trait does not stack with Force DR.
* **Arden Vul Rations (Cakes):** Each cake is 6" in diameter, 1" thick, and weighs 1 lb; one cake feeds one person for one day. They are highly nutritious but unappetizing and remain edible indefinitely if kept dry. Cost: $15 per unit.

## 5. Tactical Planning
* **The Pursuit:** The company intends to follow the *Trace* magic to the location 10 miles north to recover Susarra's head and identify the killer.
* **War on the Cult:** The company plans to aggressively target the Settite Cult, potentially desecrating their temples and seeking vengeance for Susarra.
* **Diplomatic/Support Requests:** Plans are in motion to request support (materiel or intel) from the Green Lady and the Drome, as well as potential coordination with Lillian.
* **Potential Reconnaissance:** The company may visit the Ruined Watchtower to seek an audience with the ghost Yrtol.

* **The Tower of the Ape:** What secrets does Tikun Thane hold, and are the white apes a permanent fixture of his tower?
* **The Forum of Set:** Is the rumored Forum still active within the ruins of Arden Vul?
