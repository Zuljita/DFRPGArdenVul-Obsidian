**Lore & Discoveries**
* **Appraisal Fees:** The priests of Lucreon charge a $100 fee for the appraisal of metals, which is collected via an offering box that deposits funds directly into "Lucreon's Coin Bag." (Source: Priest of Lucreon).
* **The Watcher's Limitations:** The construct known as the Watcher can be commanded to follow individuals or perform a dance, but it cannot be commanded to intentionally knock down obstacles or flee from intruders. (Source: GM Ruling).
* **Arcanum Value:** Thoth's Platinum Scales provide accurate weights and values for Arcanum at approximately $5 and $500/lb respectively. (Source: Testing).

**World Rulings**
* **Thoth's Platinum Scales:** The scales provide both weight and value for coins, metals, gems, and jewels with high accuracy. However, the scales are unreliable for magic items (e.g., valuing an Ioun Stone at only $50) and luxury goods (e.g., valuing a bottle of wine at $1).
* **The Beacon Doors:** The password-protected doors within the Beacon seal so tightly that they possess no keyholes or handles, preventing any objects from passing through the seam unless a channel is physically carved beneath them.
* **Analyze Magic Limitations:** The *Analyze Magic* spell does not provide specific information regarding whether a Staff of Power retains its secondary properties (such as +2 Resist or +0 DR) after its charges have been depleted.

**Tactical Planning**
* **Proposed Expedition Route:** The party proposes using the Purple Rugs to travel to the Beacon, then utilizing the Green Rug to return to the dragon head statue room.
* **Exploration Objectives:** Plans include investigating the North, South, East, and West exits of the dragon head statue room, as well as the secret door and the top of the shaft. 
* **Magical Surveying:** The company intends to use *Analyze Magic*, *Seek Earth* (for Silver/Gold/Platinum), and *Seek Magic* to investigate statues and potential hidden chambers within the sand.
* **Deep Exploration:** If time permits, the party plans to descend into the chamber on the Glory of Thoth level via *Levitate* or *Walk on Air*.

**Unresolved Threads**
* **The Pyrite Hypothesis:** Is the dragon head statue enchanted to make iron pyrite appear as gold to entice adventurers? The party intends to attempt to confirm this with *Analyze Magic*.
* **The Intruder Alarm Test:** Will Vael's proposed alarm system—using a tethered cowbell and the Watcher to alert Akla-Chah—function as intended?
* **Staff Durability:** Does the Staff of Power retain its defensive properties after all charges are spent?
