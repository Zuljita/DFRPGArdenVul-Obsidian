# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Rises Company
**Date:** Week ending 2026-01-23
**Subject:** Ancient Relics, Slave Pit Testimonies, and the Negotiated Sale of Thoth’s Palanquin

### Summary
The company processed significant recent acquisitions, specifically a heavy stone slab and a magical levitation disc known as Thoth’s Palanquin. Through interviews with survivors of the Forum of Set, harrowing details of the local slave trade have come to light. Negotiations with the merchant Wicktrimmer established a complex rental agreement for the company's most valuable new asset, while Vaelitharon Sunshadow successfully dispelled the magic from a captured jeweled snake statue.

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Forum of Set/Slave Trade:**
    *   **Rolf's Testimony:** Detailled a kidnapping from a western farm, transport via basket and cave passages to the Forum of Set, and subsequent sale at auction to Belisarius, the Overseer of Set.
    *   **Basil's Testimony:** Described being kidnapped in Newmarket and transported via wagon and basket to a well-maintained slave pit. He noted an escape led by a cleric of Odin and was eventually sold to Tikun Thane, later being held in the Tower of the Ape.
*   **The "Thoth's Palanquin" (Levitation Disc):** A 6-foot diameter, 6-inch thick disc that permanently levitates 2 feet off the ground. It has a capacity of approximately 5,000 lbs and moves at a walking pace (Move 2) in response to Mithric commands.
*   **The Stone Slab:** A 4,200 lb stone depicting a Rudishva, a Varumani, a Varuda, and a "Traitorous Eater of Its Own Toxic Filth." The imagery depicts the "Beacon" vessel in its intact state prior to its crash.
*   **Local Rumors:** A halfling named Raye claims a wizard named Khan rules the second level from the top of Arden Vul, using ghouls to procure souls under the guise of being helpful.

### World Rulings
*   **The Levitation Disc (Thoth's Palanquin):** The disc is too heavy for a single person to lift and its dimensions exceed those of standard traveling rugs, preventing teleportation via rug. It functions independently of the stone slab it was found with.
*   **Large Bronze Keys:** There are distinct sizes of these keys; one measures 12 inches and another measures 8 inches.
*   **Magic Item Interaction:** The use of *Dispel Magic* on the animated snake statue successfully rendered the object inert and non-magical.

### Tactical Planning
*   **The Next Expedition:**
    *   The company intends to use traveling rugs to reach the Beacon.
    *   Vallium Halcyon will be sent to the surface ruins via the "Viator" rug using *Blink Other*.
    *   The party plans to utilize a Clairvoyance potion to scout the tower to the southwest for dragon activity.
    *   The route will proceed via the Pyramid and the Troll lift toward the Sundered Span, following Temrin's map.
    *   Potential secondary objectives include returning to the Well of Light to perform the Litany of Light or visiting the Ruby Throne to recite the seven secret names of Thoth in Mithric.
*   **Logistics & Commerce:**
    *   The company has negotiated a deal with Wicktrimmer: The Levitation Disc will be sold to him for $20,000, provided the company retains the right to rent it back at $2,000 per day with six weeks' notice.
    *   The stone slab is to be sold separately (offered at $3,000).
