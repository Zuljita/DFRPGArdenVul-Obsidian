# Weekly Chronicle: The Grudge Brigade
**Date:** Week ending 2025-07-18
**Subject:** Arcanum Strategy, Alchemical Innovations, and Familiar Mechanics

### 1. Summary
The Grudge Brigade secured a significant cache of Arcanum during the recent delve into Arden Vul. While the party debated the merits of liquidating the metal for immediate cash versus retaining it as a strategic resource for future enchantments, the goblin merchant Wicktrimmer of the Rarities Factor has expressed interest in purchasing it, raising questions about his end clients. Meanwhile, Ashe (The GOAT) completed two major alchemical projects: the "Living Wheelbarrow" and the "Thaumaturgical Heuristics for Omnisensorial Trickery & Hyperstimulation Labs" (Project Code DAO). The party also clarified several complex rules regarding familiars, concentration maneuvers, and equipment distribution.

### 2. Events & Actions by Character

He spent $125 on a Carousing roll (+2 bonus) and received a rumor via private message. He searched for a Potion Belt at Creon’s but found none in stock this week.

### 3. Lore & Discoveries

**Arcanum**
*   **Properties:** A magical orange metal found within Arden Vul. It can be used to recharge magic items, create magic items, and enhance the range, duration, or effect of spells. It serves as a material component for certain spells.
*   **Economic Impact:** The presence of Arcanum in Gosterwick suggests that magic items may become available for purchase. The party debated whether to sell the Arcanum for experience points (CP) or keep it as a strategic resource. Archon Shiva argued it is a "third currency" that cannot be easily replicated and should be retained.
*   **Wicktrimmer’s Interest:** The goblin merchant Wicktrimmer is actively seeking Arcanum, suggesting a hidden buyer or market for the metal in the region.

**Egrk and the Cavemen**
*   **Lore:** Egrk, the "caveman" encountered previously, is part of an isolated tribe. They speak "of the Tribe" and are naive to the concept of non-tribal beings sharing a common tongue. They are not likely to form a formal faction but are geographically close to the baboon lairs.

**Familiars**
*   **Mechanics Clarified:**
    *   **Energy:** Familiar ER recharges based on the familiar resting, not the master’s Recover Energy. If the familiar cannot rest, the master does not regain ER.
    *   **Mana Zones:** Familiars are unaffected by high/low mana levels but cannot enter no-mana, no-sanctity, or no-nature zones. Entering such zones involuntarily forces an unsummoning.
    *   **Cost:** Common Familiars cost 31–38 points. Learning *Find Familiar* requires Magery, Bardic Talent, or Power Investiture.
    *   **Luck:** Luck [15] and Cat Familiar Luck [9] add together.
    *   **Enhanced Dodge:** Caps at 4, even with a cat familiar.
    *   **Death:** If a familiar dies, the master takes damage. If killed intentionally, points are lost. If killed in combat, the GM may bring it back or provide a new one.

**Concentration Rules**
*   Casting a spell while maintaining a Concentration spell incurs a -3 penalty (not -1).
*   Multitasking (e.g., moving targets with *Apportation* while casting) is non-routine and requires skill rolls; it is not guaranteed to work as intended.

**Statues of the Halls of Thoth**
*   The party humorously suggested naming the seven statues: Doc, Grumpy, Happy, Sleepy, Bashful, Sneezy, and Dopey.

### 4. World Rulings

*   **Arcanum Valuation:** Arcanum is highly valuable. Selling it intact yields more than breaking it into chunks, though chunks are still profitable.
*   **Gem Valuation:** Gems can be sold at full value if properly assessed, contrary to some expectations of a discount.
*   **Shield Mechanics:** Without Shield-Wall Training, large shields impose a -2 penalty to attacks with the off-hand.
*   **Bless Limitations:** *Bless* cannot target the caster. Active blessings persist without the caster, but new blessings require the caster's presence.
