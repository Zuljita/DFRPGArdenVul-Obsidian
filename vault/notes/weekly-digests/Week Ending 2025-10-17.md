### World Rulings

**Wall of Lightning Mechanics**
*   The wall is a single continuous loop of "wire" along the perimeter of the chosen area.
*   It cannot have holes poked in it to create internal walls.
*   It can be shaped into crescents or spirals as long as the outer perimeter is contiguous and follows hex boundaries.
*   Damage is applied **per turn** to any creature touching the wall, not per hex crossed.
*   The wall can pass through non-conductive walls (like stone) but may behave unpredictably near metal.

**Create Fire Mechanics**
*   Damage is **per turn**, not per hex. Spending a full turn in the fire deals 1d-1 damage; spending part of a turn deals 1d-3.
*   It deals **burn (fire)** damage, distinct from burn (electrical).

**Animal Sizes**
*   Baboons are SM 0.
*   Mammoths are SM 3.
*   Elephants are SM 3.
*   No natural land animals in Magae exceed SM 3.
