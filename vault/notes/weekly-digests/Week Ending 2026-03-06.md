### Lore & Discoveries
* **Vision of the Underground Meeting:** Through a vision, Vaelitharon Sunshadow observed a male goblin and a female human conversing with a red-haired middle-aged woman in an underground chamber. Vael recognized the woman from a prior encounter in the Temple of Set. The GM confirmed that Vallium had not seen these individuals.
* **Confirmed Deceased:** The individual known as Theodora is confirmed dead, following a player inquiry regarding which of the two thieves had been slain.
* **Potential Identities:** It is hypothesized that the goblin in the vision may be "Doffy," and the human female may be either Theodora or Jadeel, though this remains unverified.
* **Cultist Leadership:** The GM expressed concern that the cult leadership may flee rather than fight to the death, noting that the red-haired woman (likely the leader) possesses a secret door to Kaliyani in her bedroom.

### World Rulings
* **The Berserk Spell:** The *Berserk* spell applies to combatants using unarmed strikes; fists and feet are considered melee weapons for the purposes of this effect, overriding the strict text requiring a "melee weapon."
* **The Awaken Spell:** As a preemptive alternative to *Lend Energy*, the *Awaken* spell may be used on subjects with low FP (greater than 0 but less than 1/3 of their total) to allow them to ignore the ill effects of fatigue for one hour, at the cost of 1 FP at the conclusion of that hour. It is an area spell, costing 2-3 FP for multiple targets or 0 FP for a single target with a discount.
* **Lore-Master Restrictions:** The *Hidden Lore* skill cannot be acquired by existing characters through the purchase of the Lore-Master piece; such skills are only available to new characters or through direct discovery during adventuring.
* **Restoration Limitations:** The *Restoration* spell requires a duration of one month to fully resolve certain severe injuries, such as the loss of a limb.

### Tactical Planning
Following the eradication of the primary cultist force, the company intends to conduct a thorough sweep of the area to ensure no threats remain before external parties can claim the site. Planned objectives include:
* **Chasm Exploration:** Utilizing levitation or flight to survey both the upper and lower elevations of the current chasm, as well as investigating the western chasm for additional passages.
* **Room Inspection:** A detailed examination of the current chamber, specifically focusing on the wood pile.
* **Chimney Scouting:** Deploying a *Wizard Eye* or a *Clairvoyance* potion to scout the chimney for connections to other levels.
* **Passageway Investigation:** Following the known passageways to determine their destination.
* **Mechanism Search:** Locating the object required to fit into the hole adjacent to the existing levers.
* **Zombie Room Investigation:** Exploring the room containing zombies, with a proposed plan to use a fresh Settite corpse as a distraction, leveraging the zombies' desire for brains.
