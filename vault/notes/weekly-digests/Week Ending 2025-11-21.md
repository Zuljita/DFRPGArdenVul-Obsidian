# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for R/R Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-11-21  
**Subject:** The Rudishva Revelations, Apartment Negotiations, and the Beacon's Secrets

### Summary
The company spent the week processing recent discoveries and negotiating the future of their base of operations. Research into Rudishva technology provided names for previously unidentified artifacts, though the GM cautioned that the texts are confusing and some devices appear identical, suggesting others repeat the research for safety. Significant negotiations took place regarding the renovation and long-term lease of the apartments within the Beacon, resulting in a complex agreement with Lyssandra and Pelteon. Vallium Halcyon engaged in carousing and key duplication, while Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios focused on alchemy and failed to find historical records of the Beacon in town archives. Vaelitharon "Varel" Sunshadow dedicated his downtime to language acquisition, and Uvash Edzulton began intensive study of Rudishva artifacts and the hologram Akla-Chah.

### Events & Actions by Character

**Uvash Edzulton**
*   **Research:** Conducted intensive study of the "Rudishva Devices" text. This research allowed for the identification of several previously unnamed items from recent loot. However, the GM noted the writing is confusing and some devices look identical; Uvash suggested others repeat the research before experimenting to prioritize safety over speed.
*   **Town Actions:** Expressed intent to specialize in the study and use of newly discovered Rudishva weaponry. Informed his family to come to Arden Vul.
*   **Equipment Changes:** None.

**Vallium Halcyon**

While no new intelligence was gained, Vallium felt that other adventurers were actively trying to extract information from him. Negotiated a trade with Lyssandra: provided an Elixir of Health in exchange for a mysterious recipe (received in purple ink with an odd scent). Commissioned copies of three standard keys ($30 total) and one steel copy of the elaborate bronze key ($25; the copy is the same shape but has a less elaborate handle and is made of steel rather than bronze).

*   **Equipment Changes:** Acquired a copy of a mysterious potion recipe (noted for its strange purple ink and scent); holds a copy of the bronze key replica.

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**

**Vaelitharon "Varel" Sunshadow**
*   **Town Actions:** Engaged in high-level negotiations with Lyssandra and Pelteon regarding the renovation of the Beacon's top floor. Proposed a long-term lease for a renovated, unified party quarter.

The GM ruled that one point of proficiency (e.g., moving from Broken to Accented) can be earned per week of dedicated study. Akla-Chah is a fast learner and will have Archontean soon.

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **Rudishva Technology:** The "Rudishva Devices" book provides visual references that allow for the identification of various artifacts found in the workroom. It also contains warnings regarding the dangers of unguided experimentation. The book is confusing, and some devices look identical, so the GM suggested others repeat the research for safety. (Source: Research by Uvash Edzulton).
*   **The Beacon Structure:** The facility consists of six levels, including underground. The northern section (white marble) maintains a stable temperature, while the southern section (gray granite) fluctuates with the weather. The top floor is a wooden addition. There is no privy, requiring use of the Arcane Practitioners Club. A stone door between the granite and marble areas requires the *Lockmaster* spell to operate. Exiting via the stairs requires *Shape Earth*, mining tools with extreme strength, or time. The bronze doors to the stairs require a password. Arden Vul Cakes and Rudishva booze are produced by a machine in Akla-Chah's room; its longevity is unknown. (Source: Internal Survey/Downtime).
*   **Keycard Mechanics:** Oval keycards can open the closet off the reading room; colored oval cards (rust, brown, sky blue, pale green, orange) operate iris doors, while metallic cards (copper, bronze, platinum) do not. Keycards are priced as "weird items" rather than functional keys in the market. (Source: Internal Survey).
*   **The Pantheon of Shadows:** Notes recovered from a Mithric blackboard detail several figures: Crallicarus (an actor organizing dramatic readings), Sakorikus (a surgical assistant with bizarre theories on human/Rudishva fluid compatibility), Ravatorus (a sage of subterranean life), Isocrates (a warrior and advisor who fell out with Melacorius), Melacorius (a warrior of suspect loyalty with a hidden base), and Nacalorus (a legionary). (Source: Recovered Blackboard Notes).
*   **The Great Chasm Rumor:** A rumor persists that a green dragon ruling the surface ruins is the offspring of an ancient, potent wyrm residing at the bottom of the Great Chasm, served by "the survivors." (Source: Local Rumor/Greybrown).
*   **Arcanum:** Arcanum is a native element and serves as a potential power source for Rudishva technology. (Source: Book: *Thirty Uses for Arcanum*).
*   **Additional Books:** *Notes on the Rudishva Spirits* details conversations with two Rudishva Spirits (the Beacon illusion and a remote one) suspected to be the Rudishva gods. *On the Wars* contains maps and info on campaigns between humans and varumani. *Practical Animal Magic* covers harvesting animal organs for Survival/Alchemy/Naturalist purposes. (Source: Book Summaries).
*   **Golem Creation:** The golem book details a specific recipe requiring gems, the blood of an Archontean citizen, and bones of an Archontean soldier who died defending the empire. It is an intensive process, not a weekly alchemy roll, and the book could be a fake. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Wand of Paralyze Limb:** Valued at $10,000 ($20,000 if infinite uses). Requires striking the target's limb to trigger. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Gold Medallion:** Valued at $45. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Tresti:** A half-elf wizard saved from monkey jail, currently renting one of the apartments. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Apartment Lease Terms:** The company negotiated a 2-year lease for up to 6 people. Residents must be APC members. Non-qualified wizards doing alchemy need supervision. Lyssandra gets first call on spell scrolls; Pelteon gets cat visitation rights. The company can transport items through the fire escape but cannot store livestock or corpses. (Source: Negotiations with Lyssandra/Pelteon).
*   **Residence Economics:** Owning a residence is roughly valued at 180 months of rent. Owning reduces Cost of Living by half. Land is free in the sticks if defended, but construction costs apply. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Language Talent:** Can be retroactively applied if no language gets worse and total points do not drop. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Hirelings:** Can be hired for research/alchemy but cannot enter the dungeon. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Bow Restrung:** Changing bow ST from 19 to 27 requires a new bow, not just a modification. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Vallium's Sword Value:** Corrected to $19,800. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Gosterwick Housing:** Gosterwick has the Stunned Acolyte, allowing voluntary double COL for housing. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Map History:** Richard Barton is the author of Arden Vul and did the original maps. Full maps of Newmarket and Narsileon will be released after the campaign. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **DF21/DF23 Confusion:** Stronghold rules are in DF23, not DF21. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Laser Guns:** Do not count as edged weapons. Training requires pointing at a target and hoping to trigger it correctly; skill points require ammo. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Trap Skill Scope:** Traps/Armory are TL3; high-tech items are at a penalty. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Uvash's Family:** Uvash has told his family to come to Arden Vul. (Source: Player Note).
*   **Akla-Chah's Patience:** Akla-Chah can converse with someone who only has Broken Mithric. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Rapier Wit:** Useful for communication as well as combat. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Elemental Strategy:** Lacrymosa is the most reliable damage dealer; Chrysalis is solid defensively. Thronebreaker is too slow; Basilisk requires a killer. (Source: Player Discussion).
*   **Pathing:** The company plans to take the long way around: Great Cavern, Settite Outpost, down stairs, west to blue octagonal pillar room, down stairs to behind the waterfall base. (Source: Player Discussion).

### World Rulings
*   **Downtime Logistics:** The Beacon levels are freely navigable provided teleport rugs are established to connect to town safely. Experimenting with Rudishva artifacts is strictly a during-play activity due to inherent instability.
*   **Language Acquisition:** One point of proficiency (e.g., moving from Broken to Accented) can be earned per week of dedicated study.
*   **Alchemy Constraints:** The creation of golems is an intensive, long-term process involving specific materials and spells; it cannot be performed via weekly alchemy rolls.
*   **Weaponry Properties:** The Wand of Paralyze Limb requires the user to successfully strike the target's limb to trigger the effect. The Rudishva large shield (10 lbs) is transparent and provides no attack penalty.
*   **Real Estate Economics:** Owning a residence in an established town is roughly valued at 180 months of rent. Owning reduces Cost of Living by half. Land is free in the sticks if defended, but construction costs apply.
*   **Hireling Restrictions:** Hirelings can be hired for research and alchemy but cannot enter the dungeon.
*   **Bow Modification:** Changing bow ST from 19 to 27 requires a new bow.

### Tactical Planning
The company intends to execute the following plan for the next excursion:
1.  Utilize the Rug of Access to teleport from Gosterwick directly to the Beacon.
2.  Exit the Beacon via a path created by *Shape Earth* to seal the exit behind them.
3.  Navigate through the Great Cavern and the Settite Outpost, descending toward the blue octagonal pillar room.
4.  Use the Platinum Token of Arden to bypass the guardian near the waterfall area.
5.  Explore the tunnels behind the waterfall and investigate any accessible passageways.
