### World Rulings
*   **Classification of Weapons:** A warhammer or pick may be classified as a blunt weapon if the wielder utilizes the pointed end rather than the bladed/hammer end, following the theological precedent established by Father Durin Obsidian and recognized by the Council of Seven in Kazildor.
*   **Specialized Knowledge:** Skills such as Hidden Lore (Arden Vul) or Area Knowledge (Gosterwick) are not available through standard training; they require the discovery of specific, rare instructors.
*   **Linguistic Instruction:** Instructors for the Mithric tongue can be located within the world.
*   **Training Requirements:** While skill or attribute training typically requires dedicated downtime, this requirement may be waived if a character has demonstrated clear proficiency through active use during play. One week of downtime is required to spend Character Points (CP).

### Tactical Planning
*   **The Halfling Question:** Upon returning to the surface, the company is debating its relationship with the local Halfling faction. Options under consideration include:
    *   Renegotiating existing arrangements from a position of strength.
    *   Declaring all-out war to secure dungeon entrances without the burden of taxation.
    *   Writing "right for riches" on walls to lean into enmity.
    *   Walking through their front door to demand they "shake us down or live."
    *   Buying back loot from the Halflings for 2 CP.
    *   Offering protection from other groups in exchange for a cut of the Halfling loot.
    *   Collecting loot from other adventurers for 2 CP.
    *   Hammering the cult until they stop moving.
*   **Post-Delve Objectives:** The company is considering several paths for the next period of downtime, including traveling to Newmarket to collect Lillian's bounty, investigating the Tower of the Ape, and seeking out the Library of Thoth at the Newmarket temple.
*   **Trade Logistics:** There is an intention to sell any recovered Settite loot discreetly to avoid drawing the attention of cultists within Gosterwick.
*   **Negotiation Tactics:** The company has discussed utilizing Thronebreaker to bolster intimidation efforts during future diplomatic or coercive encounters.
*   **Projected Gains:** The party estimates a point gain of 5-6 points for the current session, contingent on rescuing Lillian and returning their NPC.
