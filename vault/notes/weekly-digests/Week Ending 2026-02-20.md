### Lore & Discoveries
*   **Neferet's Tomb:** The tomb contains a "Force dome" or localized zone where magic is suppressed, preventing the use of magical effects within its boundaries.
*   **The Entity Neferet:** Observations during the encounter suggest that Neferet’s capabilities may have been recently diminished, as evidenced by her retreat through the tomb halls. The party hypothesizes that their previous actions have weakened her, though it remains unknown if this weakness is permanent or if she intends to return.

### World Rulings
*   **Ring of Free Action:** The Ring of Free Action does not permit a wearer to pass through solid walls. Furthermore, it does not aid in interactions with the Force dome's magic suppression effects.
*   **Litany of Light:** Casting *Create Fire* on an enemy dispels the invisibility granted by the *Litany of Light*.

### Tactical Planning
*   **Pursuit Strategy:** The company is considering a strategy to pursue Neferet by using *Blink Other* to transport a high-speed ally from the magic-suppressed zone into the open area to initiate a chase.
*   **Speed Enhancements:** There is an intention to utilize speed-enhancing methods to close the distance during future encounters. Archont proposed casting *Divine Grace* again to catch up to Neferet, though this has not yet been attempted.
*   **Spell Limitations:** It was clarified that there are no clerical spells that increase raw move speed; however, Vaelitharon possesses *Haste* as a prerequisite for such effects.
*   **Regrets:** The party noted that casting *Sense Spirits* one more time before Neferet escaped might have provided more time to react.

### Unresolved Threads
*   The exact nature and boundaries of the magic-nullification "Force dome" within the tomb.
*   Whether Neferet intends to return to the tomb or if her retreat marks a permanent departure.
*   How to effectively engage targets when caught within a magic-suppression zone.
*   **Hypothesis:** The entity's ability to flee suggests she has been weakened, but it remains unknown if this weakness is permanent or merely temporary.
