**Subject:** Alchemical Failures, Ancient Obelisks, and the Search for a Priestess

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Thothian Obelisks:** Ancient structures found beneath Arden Vul, linked to demon-worshipping hoppers (Source: *Book of Priors*).
*   **Teleportation Hazards:** Doors marked with an ibis symbol are known to teleport travelers to new locations (Source: Observations of Thothian priests).
*   **Lacrymosa Trade:** Goods at Lacrymosa are sold at 70% of their standard value (Source: Archon Shiva).
*   **Animal Intelligence:** Cats, such as Onyx, can move through the city without drawing attention, though they possess limited communication capabilities in Archontean, primarily restricted to names for various fish (Source: Observations of Onyx).
*   **New Spell:** *Hekate's Bonds* (Body Control, Regular, R-HT, Duration: 5s, Cost: 8) causes paralysis.

### World Rulings
*   **The Torc of Protection:** This item provides a magical force field over the head that grants general Damage Resistance (DR) 6.
*   **Weapon Mastery:** The "Weapon Master" skill applies specifically to a single line on the weapon table; however, a mastery of [25] covers two weapons used in tandem, and a mastery of [30] covers a small class of weapons (such as "Sticks").
*   **Magical Item Properties:** 
    *   Glass spheres function similarly to potions but must be smashed on the floor to activate.
    *   A Levitation potion causes the drinker to move at Move 3 for one hour.
    *   An explosive fireball sphere detonates upon impact.
*   **The Ring of Illusion:** Using this ring to significantly alter one's size (e.g., appearing as a halfling or a four-armed ape) is ineffective and appears suspicious to observers.
*   **Contingency Casting:** Spells such as *Bless* intended for contingency must be cast during an active session, not between them.
*   **Alchemy Availability:** Lady Alexia maintains reasonable stocks of major and minor healing potions; other alchemical preparations generally require a specific roll to acquire.
*   **Seeker Limitation:** The *Seeker* spell can only be used once per week, whether in town or in-game.

### Tactical Planning
The company has proposed a primary mission to rescue Lillian, a priestess of Mitra. The intended route involves ascending the Long Stair to the surface ruins northwest of the pyramid, entering the basement, and proceeding north through the Halls of Thoth. The party intends to skirt the halfling territory and move south toward the Children of Deino. 

The plan is to approach the Children of Deino with a significant offering of food to trade for Lillian, or to request an escort through their territory while Vaelitharon uses *Trace* to locate her. As a contingency, if the lead proves false, the company intends to return to the Well of Light to explore previously unmapped areas using *See Secrets*.

**Preparation requirements:**
*   Purchase large quantities of food for trade.
*   Secure a mule for transport.
*   Vaelitharon must reserve the use of the *Trace* spell specifically for this search.
