### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Arena Lord (Research):** An unnamed, undead ruler who has controlled the Arena for centuries, possessing total authority over gladiators and fight outcomes. His word is law; he owns the house gladiators and decides the rules of fights.
*   **The "Hoppers" (Carousing - Jenks):** A toad-like race believed to be the original builders of Arden Vul. They reside in an evil, pitted ziggurat at the foot of the Great Chasm. They are known to spread toxic oil on walls and can leap approximately 50 feet before striking prey with tridents.
*   **Gemma of Arcturos (Research):** A wizard who vanished exactly 210 years ago.
*   **Isadora (Reading - *A True History...*):** The daughter of a provincial *chartoularios* and a trained philosopher, she served as the companion and defender of Priscus Pulcher. The book details her taking Priscus Pulcher's body off the gibbet in Narsileon after his execution.
*   **Arena Rules:** Matches are held approximately every three days, though there is no fixed schedule and periods of layoff occur. While some bouts prohibit magic, not all matches are "no magic" affairs. Violations of arena rules can result in punishments such as imprisonment in the stocks; precedent exists for such penalties (e.g., the character Freddy Got Fingered was imprisoned for a week).

### World Rulings
*   **Flight and Haste:** The *Flight* spell provides a Move score of 10, which is subject to penalties from encumbrance. The *Haste* spell increases both the subject's Move and Dodge scores.
*   **Skeletons:** While most skeletons are mana-dependent, there may be rare exceptions to this rule.
