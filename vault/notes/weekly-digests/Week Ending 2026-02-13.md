**Subject:** The Theft of the Rug, Diplomatic Maneuvers in Gosterwick, and Undead Research

### Summary
The company investigated the theft of a magical rug from the APC quarters, tracing its path through Gosterwick via historical magic and animal testimony. ### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Theft of the Rug:** The rug was moved via magic (likely Apportation or Invisibility). Onyx the cat reported smelling an unknown goblin and seeing the rug roll up and disappear. Historical analysis confirmed the rug was handled by a red-haired woman in fancy armor and a male goblin, then transported through underground corridors before appearing in Gosterwick.
*   **The Nature of Liches:** Liches are evil archmages who have cheated death by becoming undead. Their intelligence is moved into a phylactery to protect it from decay. They typically command hordes of undead minions. Information is largely speculative as few survive encounters. (Source: Uvash Edzuson’s Research)
*   **The Nature of Mummies:** Mummies are intelligent, necromantically preserved spirits of powerful priests, often wrapped in bandages and carrying holy symbols. They radiate fear, can cast spells, and inflict the fatal "mummy rot" disease. They are vulnerable to holy water, fire, and electricity, but resistant to cold and most physical weaponry. (Source: Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios’s Research)
*   **Political Climate of Gosterwick:** The Empire does not formally recognize Lady Alexia’s claim to Gosterwick, complicating the authority of officials like Audun Yellow-Eyes. Audun is Lady Alexia’s pick for the Drome post but may not be officially approved by Keko the Lame. Both likely desire the destruction of the Settites, though their willingness to assist is uncertain. (Source: Legal Consultation)
*   **The Regalia:** Freydis inquired about progress on the Regalia, noting the party’s ability to find items quickly. She indicated that maintaining town safety is difficult without Imperial recognition of Lady Alexia’s authority. (Source: Meeting with Freydis)
*   **Vaulting Status:** The Rarities Factor confirmed that the company’s goods are in a shared vault, as they do not store enough for a private one. Changes require Wicktrimmer’s presence. (Source: Rarities Factor Visit)
