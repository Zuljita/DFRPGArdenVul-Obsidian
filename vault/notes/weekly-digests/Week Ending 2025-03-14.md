### 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Dwarven Pantheon:** The dwarven pantheon is comprised of a single deity, Zodarrim.
*   **Goblins of the Region:** Goblins possess lifespans similar to humans; while often treated as second-class citizens in urban centers, they are capable of long lives if occupying stable roles such as banking.
*   **The Temple of Demma (Gosterwick):** Led by Chief Priest Vivian (a Thorcin male), the temple focuses on fertility, crops, and the rural poor. It is guarded by six warriors known as "Harvest Guardians" who wield flails.
*   **The Grudge Brigade:** A mercenary union characterized by the motto "Collectively Bargained, Individually Belligerent." Members are rotated to ensure work for all, and no two members of the Brigade are permitted to work a single job together.
*   **Rumors of Arden Vul:**
    *   A priestess of Mitra named Lillian has gone missing near the overgrown thicket by the cliffs; witnesses claim she was taken by walking pigs into the ruins.
    *   The priests of Thoth are obsessed with light, maintaining brightly lit halls to honor their deity.
    *   A massive road built for much larger creatures runs through the caverns, potentially leading to the Goblin King and the Troll Thegn.
    *   The abandonment of Arden Vul was precipitated by the depletion of a wondrous orange mineral used in arcane processes.
*   **Obelisk Inscription:** "The beacon shall be revealed to those who bring midday life to the sun the moon and the stars!"

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Magic & Spells:**
    *   Points invested in a skill or spell are permanent; they cannot be later converted back into attribute points.
    *   The "Holy" limitation on cleric powers granted by a deity cannot be removed via point expenditure, as doing so would fundamentally alter the nature of the divine gift.
    *   *Blink Other* can be used as a Blocking spell during an active defense roll, but it is subject to resistance (Will or ST) by the target. It cannot be used to block an attack *after* the attacker has already rolled for success unless a "Wait" maneuver was declared beforehand.
    *   *Command* functions as a Blocking spell only when used during a defense roll following a successful attack; otherwise, it is treated as a Regular spell.
    *   *Kiai* can be effectively used to target creatures in adjacent hexes, but cannot be used to target creatures in rear hexes.
*   **Animal Summoning:** Black Bears, Wolves, and Bat Swarms are considered native to the region and can be summoned/created by those who have seen them. Army Ants and Piranhas are tropical species; summoning them requires an "Unusual Background."
*   **Economy & Progression:** The acquisition of consumables costs experience points.

### 5. Tactical Planning
*   **Logistics:** The company is considering the purchase of a wheelbarrow, picks, ropes, and camouflage netting to facilitate long-term dungeon delving. The GM has prepared a wheelbarrow token in Foundry for attachment to PCs.
*   **Combat Roles:** Vael intends to utilize area-denial spells (*Grease*, *Smoke*) to manipulate enemy movement, while Ioannes will focus on maintaining party vitality through alchemy and healing.
