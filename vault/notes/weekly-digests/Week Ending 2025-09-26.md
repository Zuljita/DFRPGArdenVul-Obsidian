During this period, the company engaged in extensive deliberation regarding future maneuvers against the Halfling tribes and the Cult of Set, weighing options from assassination to sabotage. ### Lore & Discoveries
*   **Settite Strength:** The Cult of Set's presence is estimated to be closer to 30 individuals, correcting earlier estimates of 50. This assessment is based on recent session observations where the party faced significant numbers but only eliminated a fraction of the force.
*   **Settite Magical Defenses:** The Cult appears to maintain a specialized "firefighter" brigade of mages and clerics tasked with casting *Dispel Magic* to neutralize hostile spells. However, this is considered a player observation rather than a confirmed structural fact, as the Cult may simply be reacting to threats rather than maintaining a dedicated unit.
*   **Loot Valuation:** The magic glass eyes recovered from recent spoils are made of glass and possess negligible market value. Furthermore, the standard equipment (armor, weapons, and potions) taken from Settite forces is of common, non-magical quality ("book, nothing special").
*   **Halfling Disposition:** The Halflings may have placed a bounty of 5,000 gold each on the heads of the party members who recently passed through their territory, complicating potential diplomatic or manipulative strategies.

### World Rulings
*   **Spell Interruption via Falling:** Dropping or dodging while actively casting a spell causes the spell to break, resulting in falling damage. A caster has a window of approximately 5 yards of fall distance to successfully cast a reactive spell (such as *Slow Fall*, *Levitation*, or *Walk on Air*) before impact occurs.

### Tactical Planning
*   **The Halfling Contingency:** The company is weighing several options regarding the local Halfling population. Proposed strategies include assassinating their leadership to demand surrender and loot, or intentionally manipulating them into a direct conflict with the Cult of Set. There is discussion of forcing the Halflings into a "suicide mission" against the Cult, potentially after eliminating their leaders, Plumthorn and Roskelly.
*   **Settite Sabotage:** Plans are being discussed to destabilize the Cult of Set through non-combat means, including the poisoning of water supplies, the destruction of food stores, and the use of magical area-denial tactics (such as *Create/Shape Fire* or the deployment of *Mystic Mist*) to induce internal dissent. The goal is to observe if the Cult pulls back its guard or reduces its perimeter in response to these pressures.

### Unresolved Threads

*   **The Fate of the Settite-Halfling Conflict:** The outcome of the recent, unobserved clash between the Settites and Halflings is unknown.
*   **Research Objectives:** The party has yet to determine if research should be commissioned into the identity and nature of the individual known as Androsia, with suspicions that she may be a witch, hag, or magic-using nonhuman.
*   **Logistical Needs:** A formal determination of required potion brewing and upcoming town-based preparations is pending.
