# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Riches Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-09-19  
**Subject:** Incorporation Status, Rarities Factor Contract, and Town Activities

### Summary
The Right for Riches Company’s application for legal incorporation was approved by the Thesmothete of Vetucaster on 2025-09-16. However, the company charter has not yet been finalized or signed; the entity cannot yet execute binding contracts in its own name. Consequently, the company’s agreement with Wicktrimmer of the Rarities Factor was executed by three representatives (Vaelitharon, Ioannes, and Vallium) pending final corporate ratification. The agreement involves the sale of Larel’s Pin for $20,000, lifetime banking services, and concierge shopping capabilities.

### Events & Actions by Character

**Uvash Edzuson**
*   **Town Actions:** Participated in discussions regarding the redistribution of wealth to facilitate long-term party growth through increased Wealth and Power Investiture.
*   **Equipment:** Currently managing the company’s significant gold reserves.

**Vallium Halcyon**
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Attempted to brew a Magebane Grenade. The attempt failed due to an excess of nightshade, resulting in a foul odor that required Vaelitharon to cast *Purify Air*. This error increased the cost of the endeavor by 10%.
*   **Town Actions:** Spent $125 on a Carousing roll (successful, +2). During this time, he heard rumors of a "Sunless Sea" beneath the Halls, accessible via an ogre boatman.
*   **Equipment Changes:** Requested the procurement of a Very Fine, Balanced Broadsword with Accuracy +1, Puissance +1, and Penetrating Weapon enchantments (estimated at $29,400). He is transitioning his banking account from the Silent Factor to the Rarities Factor.

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Successfully brewed a supply of Paut.
*   **Research:** Conducted research into "Lost and Found" and the "Rarities Factor." Findings confirmed the existence of these entities but suggested that the history of Lost and Found may be embellished by its former leader, Balthazar the Blue. Research into the Rarities Factor indicated that Wicktrimmer is likely not the true owner, with a wealthy noble acting as the hidden power behind the operation.
*   **Equipment:** No changes recorded this week.

**Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow**
*   **Town Actions:** Conducted investigations using Streetwise and Merchant skills to vet Wicktrimmer and his business. Results confirmed Wicktrimmer is a retired adventurer, possibly the second richest person in Gosterwick, and definitely the richest goblin. He is a collector of historical treasures and magic items with deep connections in Gosterwick, Newmarket, and Narsileon. Vael accompanied the party representatives during negotiations at the Rarities Factor.
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Utilized *Purify Air* to clear the laboratory following Vallium's failed alchemy attempt.
*   **Equipment:** Currently wears Larel's Cloak and the Amulet of the Silver Tongue. These items provide stacking bonuses to reaction rolls and Public Speaking (+2 from the Amulet’s Voice advantage and +3 from the Cloak’s Charisma, totaling +5).

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Rarities Factor:** A banking and commerce entity with branches in Gosterwick, Newmarket, and Narsileon. While Wicktrimmer is the public face, research suggests a wealthy noble is the true owner. They provide banking, message services, and mule caravans.
*   **Lost and Found:** An adventuring group formerly led by the wizard Balthazar the Blue and the paladin Grimley. Historical accounts of their exploits are suspected to be self-promotional.
*   **Larel's Artifacts:** The "pin" is identified as a stiletto that provides +1 DX while wielded. It does not function if held in a shield hand, held non-threateningly, or held between the teeth. It *does* function if held in the off-hand while another weapon is in the primary hand. It was discovered within a Thothian funerary complex alongside other items like a golden apple and love notes.
*   **Political Intelligence:** Keko the Lame serves as the head of the Drome in Narsileon. Political friction between Lady Alexia and her half-brother causes confusion within the Imperial chain of command.
*   **New Spell Discovery:** *Brain Full of Small Spiders* (Illusion; R-Will): Creates a sensation of spiders crawling on the subject's head, causing -1 to all rolls and halving movement.

### World Rulings
*   **Legal Incorporation:** A company charter in Gosterwick requires approval from Eusebia Phokas, the Thesmothete of Vetucaster. The process costs $500 and can take several weeks. The company cannot sign binding contracts as an entity until the charter is finalized and signed.
*   **Larel's Pin Mechanics:** The +1 DX bonus applies while the weapon is wielded. It fails if held in a shield hand, held non-threateningly, or between the teeth. It works in the off-hand if a primary weapon is held.
*   **The Rarities Factor Contract:** The agreement for Larel's Pin includes $20,000 (gold) deposited into the company account, lifetime free banking/vault services for the five current members, and monthly concierge shopping services via *Message*. The shopping service allows for the purchase of items up to the amount deposited in the members' accounts.
*   **Alchemy Costs:** Errors in alchemy, such as incorrect ingredient ratios, can increase the cost of subsequent attempts by 10%.
*   **Levitation Weight Limits:** A subject under the effects of *Levitation* will be dragged downward if they pick up weight that exceeds the spell's maximum capacity.

### Tactical Planning
*   **The Great Cavern Expedition:** The company intends to visit the statue of The Philosopher in the Great Cavern, utilizing the Magical Silver Eyes to trigger unknown effects.
*   **Elemental Exploration:** Plans are in place to investigate the column of fire near the fire imps using the Salamander Amulet and potential fire resistance measures.
*   **Settite Reconnaissance:** The company intends to scout the Settite outpost, potentially employing *Flight* and *Invisibility* for a stealthy approach or utilizing secret passages discovered via the Wand of See Secrets.

### Unresolved Threads
*   **The Assassin of Susarra:** The identity of the individual responsible for the death of Susarra remains unknown; theories suggest either an out-of-town assassin or a Setite operative.
*   **The True Patron of Rarities Factor:** Whether a wealthy noble is indeed the hidden hand behind Wicktrimmer's business remains unverified.
*   **The Sunless Sea:** The existence of a "Sunless Sea" beneath the Halls, potentially accessible via an ogre boatman, remains unverified.
*   **Contract Finalization:** The company must finalize the legal wording for the right of first refusal to ensure they can still trade items with other adventuring companions or mercenaries. The incorporation charter is pending final signature.
