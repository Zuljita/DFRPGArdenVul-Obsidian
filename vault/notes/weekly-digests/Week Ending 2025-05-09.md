### 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **Halfling Connections:** The halfling faction is believed to maintain connections within the town of Newmarket, potentially offering a source for equipment or trade.
*   **Known Exits:** A known exit from the current dungeon sector passes through the Glory of Thoth, which is currently under halfling control.
*   **Secret Passages:** A secret passage is located behind a baboon mosaic. This route leads to a teleport pyramid that provides access to the Well of Light level, though this route is noted as being highly dangerous.
*   **Halfling Awareness:** There is debate regarding whether the halflings know the company’s identity. While some hypothesized they might believe a different crew was responsible for recent activity, it was assessed that the halflings likely know who the party is, and the company lacks the "Face" skills to successfully lie about it.
*   **Dungeon Economy:** Humorous speculation arose regarding the possibility of brigands setting up a fake ATM in the dungeon to take deposits, highlighting the absurdity of the location.

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Multi-session Delves:** Exploration rewards and loot thresholds for multi-session delves are not multiplied based on real-world time elapsed; however, all items recovered during the duration of the delve are tallied.
*   **Session Transitions:** If a party returns to town and embarks on a second delve within the same session, they may earn exploration CP and loot for both excursions, with rewards distributed at the conclusion of the second session.

### 5. Tactical Planning
*   **Halfling Diplomacy/Conflict:** The company is weighing several options regarding the halfling faction:
    *   Negotiating a flat fee for passage through their territory and establishing a business relationship.
    *   Attempting to assassinate Phlebotomas to destabilize their leadership and leverage negotiations.
    *   If the halfling faction is wiped out, considering gifting their territory to another faction, such as the Children of Deino.
*   **Combat Strategy:** Plans include buffing two fighter types with Flight, Invisibility, and Infravision. One caster (Vallium) intends to cast *Flaming Weapon* on allies before backing off. Another plan involves using a power item for a two-fighter assault, with casters refilling the item.
*   **Contingency Plans:** Should the company suffer significant casualties or resource depletion, the primary objective will be a retreat to Gosterwick. If the engagement is successful and the party continues delving, they intend to mention Bellringer if they encounter goblins.
*   **Post-Conflict Objectives:** The party noted the existence of a closer inn as a potential post-conflict objective.
