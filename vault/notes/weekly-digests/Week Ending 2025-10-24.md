# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for R/R Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-10-24  
**Subject:** Alchemical Mishaps, Rug Testing, and Plans for the Well of Light

### Summary
The company spent much of the week in Gosterwick, focusing on logistical preparations and testing newly acquired magical artifacts. While alchemical endeavors met with fiery failure, the company has established a clear tactical roadmap for upcoming explorations within Arden Vul. Significant progress was made in understanding the capabilities of the Rugs of Instant Access, and the party secured vital resources for their impending conflict with the Set cult.

### Events & Actions by Character

**Vallium Halcyon**
*   **Town Actions:** Commissioned a local locksmith to produce three copies of Plumthorn’s keys at a cost of $10 per key ($30 total). Purchased $500 worth of low-quality alcohol (rotgut and moonshine) intended for use with the Goblin tribes to bolster Carousing rolls.

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **Rugs of Instant Access:** Testing has confirmed that by using the command word *"viator,"* a person can teleport between two rugs provided they are laid flat and unoccupied. The effect works while carrying heavy loads, though it cannot transport two people simultaneously. The range is confirmed to be at least 250 yards and does not require line of sight. The rugs have no known cooldown but take one second to activate. (Source: Field Testing).
*   **The Hallow Spell:** This spell increases the Sanctity (Cleric) or Nature's Strength (Druid) of an area by one level, up to a maximum of High Sanctity or +3 Nature's Strength. It has a base cost of 5, a casting time of 1 hour, and requires Perfection of the Intellect 5. It can be used to de-sanctify opposing temples (an act of war), though it can only reduce sanctity down to "Normal." (Source: New Spell Documentation).
*   **Sanctity Mechanics:** Sanctity is tied to specific religions or gods rather than individuals; for example, an area may be low in sanctity for the followers of Set but high for others. A cleric can only sanctify temples for their own god, though a multi-god cleric may choose which god to sanctify for. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Trefko the Baboon:** The guardian of the mirror possesses a dwarven life stone necklace, which he is willing to trade for a live goat. (Source: Intelligence gathered from Trefko).
*   **Other Faction Statuses:** Lycandus’s remains are concealed by illusions. Ptarmis claims control over the Fungal Foresters. Horkhari was shown the tomb of great Theskelon. Amhit has found the Obsidian Gates but cannot teleport behind them. (Source: GM Update).
*   **Bag of Holding Capacity:** Larel's Sack holds 500 lbs or 70 ft³. The other Bag of Holding holds 1000 lbs or 150 ft³. A standard 10-foot ladder fits within these limits but consumes significant space. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Hall of Judgement Pits:** The western pit is less than 12 yards deep, as determined by visibility with Continual Light. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Thoth Temple Status:** The Temple of Thoth has been trashed to the point it is no longer High Sanctity. (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Pebble Properties:** Chrysalis tested her pebble in water, on a stovetop, and in mud. It does not carry temperature or dirt, but it does carry wetness. (Source: Field Testing).

### World Rulings
*   **Equipment Slots:** An Amulet and the Torc of Protection occupy separate equipment slots and may be worn simultaneously; however, the Torc of Protection prevents the wearer from using a helmet slot.
*   **Carousing Costs:** In Gosterwick, spending $125 on supplies provides a +2 bonus to Carousing rolls, while a $500 investment provides a +3 bonus. This purchase allows for in-game Carousing with the goblins rather than just a standard downtime roll.
*   **Alchemy Supervision:** Following alchemical failures involving fire or smoke, the local alchemist requires professional supervision for untrained practitioners.
*   **Spell Properties:** The "eye" component of the Windstorm spell family is optional and can be omitted entirely (e.g., in an Area 1 Windstorm).

### Tactical Planning
The company has outlined a multi-stage plan for the upcoming session:
1.  **Trade Expedition:** Visit the Cavemen west of Arden Vul to trade axes or hatchets for local resources.
2.  **Logistical Testing:** Utilize the Rugs of Instant Access and climbing gear (including Rings of Climbing) to navigate the Long Stair, testing the rugs' efficiency from the base to the summit.
3.  **Well of Light Incursion:** Descend through the kitchen chimney access to reach the Hall of Judgement. The company intends to retrieve the teleporting pyramid lever from the pit containing a goo creature and clear the crocodile pit for potential loot.
4.  **Ritual & Maintenance:** Perform the Litany of Light and check on the status of Stamelis the librarian and Yamki the four-armed ape.
5.  **Exploration:** Test the functionality of the retrieved pyramid lever between levels and explore the western or northeastern sectors of Arden Vul for experience and treasure.
6.  **Commerce:** Visit the Goblin Market to survey available goods.
