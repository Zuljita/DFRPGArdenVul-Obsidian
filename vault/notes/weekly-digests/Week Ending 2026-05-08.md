# Weekly Chronicle: The Company of the Right for Riches
**Date:** Week ending 2026-05-08

## 1. Summary
This week saw the company engage in scholarly research, alchemical experimentation, and logistical preparation for the upcoming push toward the Beacon and the Bastion. Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios and Vallium Halcyon successfully brewed potions and conducted research, while Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow suffered a costly alchemical failure but gained significant historical insights. Remagis accompanied Vael to Gosterwick to procure supplies for his intended restoration of the Temple of Thoth within Arden Vul. The company has finalized plans for the next expedition, including specific protocols for the Tower of Scrutiny.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Successfully brewed a Potion of Fire Resistance.
*   **Research:** Investigated survivors of the Rudishva, specifically excluding those associated with the Arena Lord. This research uncovered a tattered report from the Archon of Arden Vul addressed to the Logothete of the Drome in Archontos. The report details the presence of an extinct, highly dangerous race of sorcerers within the ruins. It notes that one survivor, Crallicarus, has offered assistance to the Archon, while another, Melacorius, poses a palpable military threat.
*   **Town Actions:** Engaged in propaganda efforts to promote the Goblin Forum.

**Vallium Halcyon**
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Successfully brewed an Agility Potion.
*   **Research:** Conducted research into the Ligareon family; no significant information was found regarding this lineage.
*   **Town Actions:** Read the *Book of Night Maneuvers*, a text written in Mithric detailing eight famous historical heists. Spent 125 gold to secure a +2 bonus to his Carousing rolls. Learned that the halfling thug Plumthorn sells a narcotic called "mushroom ale" under the name "Hack's Special," which induces the effects of *suggestion* in those who consume it.

**Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow**
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Attempted to brew a Potion of Wisdom, resulting in a critical failure and the destruction of materials.
*   **Research:** Studied three historical texts:
    *   *The Betrayal*: Revealed that a Kaliyani named Lisstak manipulated his people against the Rudishva out of boredom with peace, leading to the arrival of "the sickness."
    *   *Tales of Larel One-Eye*: A collection of folklore detailing the trickster Larel’s exploits, including tricking a farmer’s wife, distracting wolves with a chicken, visiting wizard Tilgo, and surviving bugbear captivity.
    *   *On the Wars of the Old Ones with the Trolls*: A tactical history of Archontean campaigns against the Varumani, illustrating how the Archontes defeated the trolls and turned them into loyal allies.
*   **Language Study:** Continued studies in spoken Varumani and written Rudishva (using *The Betrayal* as a primer).
*   **Town Actions:** Continued drafting the "Chronicle of the Right for Riches Company By Vaelitharon 'Vael' Sunshadow." Accompanied the mercenary Remagis to Gosterwick, acting as an interpreter for his broken Archontean and facilitating his shopping.

**Uvash Edzuson**
*   **Town Actions:** None recorded this week.

**Remagis (Mercenary)**
*   **Town Actions:** Accompanied Vaelitharon to Gosterwick. Purchased food, holy water, cleaning supplies, and several wooden holy symbols of Thoth. Expressed intentions to reopen the Temple of Thoth within Arden Vul once his Archontean proficiency is sufficient to navigate the goblin population that currently encompasses the site.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Extinct Race:** A report from the Archon of Arden Vul to the Logothete of the Drome confirms that members of an extinct, highly dangerous race of sorcerers reside in the ruins. Crallicarus is noted as a helpful figure, while Melacorius is identified as a military threat.
*   **The Heqeti Ring:** It has been confirmed that the Heqeti Ring was recovered from the finger of the deceased official.
*   **The Mithric Heists:** The *Book of Night Maneuvers* contains accounts of eight famous historical heists.
*   **The "Hack's Special":** It is known that the halfling thug Plumthorn sells a narcotic called "mushroom ale" under this name; consumption leads to the effects of *suggestion*.

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Equipment Classification:** Belts are classified as clothing, not armor; conversely, boots are classified as foot armor.
*   **Dwarf Sizing and Resizing:** Official DFRPG rules require Armoury rolls to resize found armor to fit a character of a different race, with an additional -5 penalty for Fine armor. Magic items are generally not considered one-size-fits-all, though belts and rings are more versatile than fitted armor.
*   **Scaling Magical Items:** The GM has indicated that finding a way to resize magical items (such as the Boots of Ogre Strength) while retaining their magical properties is significantly less likely than finding a new item of appropriate size and power.
*   **Rule Supplements:** The *GURPS Dungeon Fantasy 11: Power-Ups* supplement is not in use for this campaign.

## 5. Tactical Planning
The company has proposed the following objectives for the upcoming session:
*   **Diplomacy:** Consult with Lady Alexia regarding the reward for retrieving the Iron Circlet. The company intends to negotiate for something of significant value, potentially a Wand or a single-charge Minor Wish, leveraging their freedom to remove items from Arden Vul without taxation.
*   **Expedition to the Beacon/Bastion:** 
    *   Utilize Rugs for travel to the Beacon and inform Basil of the delve plans.
    *   Travel to the Bastion via the Rudishva Teleportation Pad, employing Salamander Amulets and Rugs to mitigate fire trap hazards.
    *   Use the black Rudishva card to access the cavern previously occupied by the statue mimic.
    *   Follow Temrin’s map to locate the Tablet of the Beacon.
*   **Contingency:** If forced to retreat, the company will return to the Goblin Forum to continue searching sarcophagi or exploring the western and northern hallways.
*   **Tower of Scrutiny Operations:** The company intends to reveal the surrounding map, consult local Thothian clergy, test existing keys on ground-level doors, analyze the Ibis Head/Statue, and utilize various detection spells (*Seek Platinum/Gold/Silver/Magic*, *See Secrets*) and the Periapt of Finding Heqeti.
*   **Tower Security:** There is an ongoing discussion regarding whether to demolish the Tower of Scrutiny or use *Shape Earth* to seal its basement and camouflage it to prevent incursions from the Children of Deino or the Apes.
