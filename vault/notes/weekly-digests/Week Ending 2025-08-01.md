### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Fate of Gwelf:** Through the use of magical divination, it was revealed that the individual known as Gwelf is deceased. A vision showed his body in an unidentified dark cave, bearing a massive cutting wound across his torso.
*   **The Library of Thoth:** Research indicates this library sits near the Well of Light but is heavily guarded by undead (Source: Research/Ioannes).
*   **Alchemical Supplies:** It has been confirmed that various chemicals, including glow vials and visibility dust, are available for purchase and can be crafted due to the presence of a friendly alchemy lab in town.
*   **Regional Rations:** Elven and Dwarven rations are noted as rare commodities in Gosterwick; however, Halfling, Human, and Imperial Goblin rations are common (Source: Local commerce).
*   **The Divine Breath of Thoth:** This rare book contains an uncharged, wizardly *Mystic Mist* scroll, though its value has been slightly diminished by previous scribbles within the text.

### World Rulings
*   **Magical Jewelry:** A delver may wear up to two magic rings simultaneously (one per hand), though wearing excessive numbers of rings will result in them failing to function. Only one magic amulet may be worn at a time.
*   **Armor Layering:** While armor types may be layered, two pieces of the same armor type are unlikely to stack their magical effects.
*   **Pathfinder Spell:** The *Pathfinder* spell may be used once per downtime period (globally) and once per day during an active game session. The spell provides higher accuracy for known locations and suffers significant penalties when navigating based on mere rumors.
*   **Alchemy Reverse Engineering:** The difficulty of reverse-engineering a potion depends on its complexity. A failure results in the inability to replicate the formula, while a critical failure may result in a dangerous error. The original potion is not necessarily consumed during the attempt.
    *   **Gems and Coins:** Identified gems and coins are treated as cash for the purposes of calculating Experience Points (XP) without requiring them to be sold.
*   **Loot and XP:** The company earns 2 XP for loot and 2 XP for exploration. All loot must be physically transported back to town to be counted toward the company's total.

### Tactical Planning
*   **Expedition to the Well of Light:** The company intends to travel to the Well of Light, entering via the well shaft using *Flight*. The primary objective is to locate the Library of Thoth and explore adjacent areas.
*   **New Command Structure:** To prevent indecision during planning, Greybrown has been elected as the "Destination Czar." He is tasked with making final decisions on expedition routes when consensus cannot be reached.

### Unresolved Threads

*   **Gwelf’s Grave:** The location of the dark cave where Gwelf was seen dead remains a mystery, presenting a potential future lead for investigation.
*   **Logistical Burden:** The management of Vael's extra 7 pounds of gear and its impact on encumbrance during the upcoming delve.
