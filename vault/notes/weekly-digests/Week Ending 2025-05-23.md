**Date:** Week ending 2025-05-23  
**Subject:** Logistics, Armor, and the Mule of Burden

### 1. Summary
The company spent the week preparing for potential conflicts with the halflings and the Great Caverns. Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios conducted research into the halfling factions, though the inquiry yielded no useful intelligence. He also successfully brewed a major healing potion at a discounted rate. Uvash Edzuson assessed his financial standing, determining he possesses approximately 10,000 gold, and planned to purchase a fine mail shirt and helmet to improve his defenses. Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow acquired a new mule, formally naming it *Inventorium Burdenus Maximus* (informally "Bernie"), and had a newly acquired metal shield assessed by an Appraiser, confirming its effectiveness against most foes but noting its significant weight and weakness to demonic entities. The company debated tactical approaches, weighing a direct assault on the halflings—potentially utilizing a mercenary with *Power Blow* capabilities to breach defenses—against a cautious exploration of the Great Caverns.

### 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Drowned Canyon:** Local rumors persist regarding immense wealth within the Drowned Canyon, though seasoned adventurers claim the canyon itself is a myth.
*   **The Cult of Thoth:** Priests of Thoth have erected numerous statues in various forms, including ibis-headed and baboon-headed iterations. These statues feature large gemstones for eyes; it is whispered that removing these gems allows a person to communicate directly with the god Thoth. Many such statues are known to be enchanted.

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Animal Handling:** Learning the skill *Animal Handling (Equines)* requires formal training. However, the act of successfully leading a mule along difficult trails may contribute toward this training.
*   **Fast-Draw (Stick):** The specialty "Stick" under the *Fast-Draw* skill applies exclusively to boomerangs; it does not extend to other implements like staves or clubs.
*   **Power Blow:** The use of *Power Blow* is limited to momentary actions (lasting approximately one second) and is not suitable for sustained tasks such as heavy lifting, though it can double Strength for direct uses and Basic Lift within that brief window.
*   **Geography:** The local cliff face stands at a height of 1,500 feet (equivalent to 500 hexes).

### 5. Tactical Planning
The company is currently debating two primary courses of action for the upcoming week:
*   **Option A: The Halfling Assault.** This plan involves striking the halflings from an unexpected entrance, such as via the chasm or Gog's level, to maximize surprise. The strategy includes the use of strength and agility potions immediately upon reaching the target area. A key component of this plan is the potential recruitment of a mercenary specialist (such as "Thronebreaker") capable of using *Power Blow* to breach walls or the portcullis at the southeast halfling gate, allowing the party to bypass the need for stealth or complex lockpicking. The status of the southeast gate (raised or lowered) remains a variable in this plan.
*   **Option B: Great Cavern Exploration.** A more cautious approach involving the exploration of the Great Caverns via the Long Stair. This plan includes bringing specialized equipment like the Fish Rod and provisions for beastmen to facilitate potential bartering.
