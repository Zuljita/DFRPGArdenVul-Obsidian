# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Rises Company
**Date:** Week ending 2026-01-30  
**Subject:** Rudishva Artifacts, the Bastion, and Consultations with Akla-Chah

### Summary
The company processed a collection of Rudishva artifacts and consulted with the psychologist Akla-Chah to understand their significance. These investigations provided vital intelligence regarding the "Bastion" section of the crashed Beacon and the nature of several newly discovered identity cards and security keys. The party also established logistical plans for upcoming operations, including the mapping of teleportation networks and the management of household expenses in Arden Vul.

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Rudishva Identity Plaques and Cards:** Akla-Chah identified the yellow oval ID as the identity plaque of Captain Leil-Jor, capable of opening any Rudishva door and activating any Rudishva teleporter. The rectangular black card is believed to be a security key for Rudishva technology.
*   **The Bastion:** Akla-Chah identified the area containing the recent discoveries as "the Bastion," a section of the crashed Beacon that originally housed the quarters of the Captain, the First Officer, and the Security Officer.
*   **The Heqeti:** The amphibian creatures found in the Bastion are identified as Heqeti. They are primitive, hostile, underground creatures native to this world.
*   **The Traitors:** The six-legged beings discovered among the corpses are known as "Traitors," who were responsible for betraying the Varumani to the Heqeti.
*   **Rudishva Technology:** It is believed that Captain Leil-Jor and her engineers were attempting to modify a small craft for long-distance space flight, an endeavor that required intense energy sources; the unidentified liquids found may be related to this project.
*   **Loot Inventory:** The company has acquired:
    *   One flask of green water.
    *   Four oval Rudishva IDs (platinum, silver, sky blue, and yellow).
    *   One rectangular black card.
    *   Two melted Rudishva artifacts.
    *   A satchel.
    *   Two additional flasks of liquid (one containing arcanum).
    *   *Note:* The party also retains a larger collection of oval IDs from previous sessions: Pale Green (x2), Orange (x1), Brown (x1), and Rust (x3).

### World Rulings
*   **Rudishva Access:** Yellow square panels on doors or teleporters require the Captain's card to operate; all other colors (such as silver) are accessible to anyone.
*   **Ring of Free Action:** This item allows the wearer full movement despite most effects that would otherwise slow or stop movement.

### Tactical Planning
The company has outlined a plan for upcoming operations in the Bastion and Beacon:
*   The party intends to use rugs to travel to the Beacon and clear any objects from the teleportation pads.
*   They plan to use the yellow Rudishva card to bypass the force field and fire trap within the Bastion to reach the conference room.
*   A primary goal is to investigate the destinations of all accessible Rudishva teleporters, potentially using *Trace Teleport* or *Scry Gate* to map their connections.
*   If exploration of the Bastion is exhausted, the company intends to move toward the Great Cavern at the base of the Great Chasm to seek gold and platinum.
*   **Household Logistics:** The party agreed to treat two apartments as a single residence with a common area. This arrangement sets the weekly Cost of Living (COL) at 200 per week for Ioannes, Vael, and Vallium, and 300 per week for Uvash (who reserves space for his family).
*   **Scheduling Notes:** Vallium will be absent for Session 42 (February 13). The tribute to Craastonistex is due on February 27 (Session 44).
