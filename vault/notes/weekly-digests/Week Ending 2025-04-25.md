### 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Tower Ghost:** Research indicates the spirit inhabiting the tower at the waterfall is not mere folklore but a "wronged soul" that has accused "Settite murderers." (Source: Research by Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios).
*   **The Book of Priors:** A collection of six volumes written in Mithric by Thothian priors between 937 and 1782 AEP. It contains vital histories, including the founding of Arden Vul (893 AEP), the wars against the Varumani (trolls) and Heqeti (hoppers), and the breaking of the Compact of Ennius with dragons (1588 AEP). (Source: The Book of Priors).
*   **Varumani (Trolls):** A race characterized by great strength, intelligence, and regenerative capabilities that require fire or acid to overcome. They are currently loyal Imperial subjects following their defeat by Marius Tricotor. (Source: The Book of Priors).
*   **Heqeti (Hoppers):** An evil, amphibious race. While some are less intelligent "hoppers," others are capable mages or demon-anti-priests. Survivors of the conquest of their Ziggurat remain in the Underearth. (Source: The Book of Priors).
*   **Loot Provenance:** Gold disks with grooves were identified as originating from the hoard within Prior Jacobus's tomb. (Source: Identification by Vaelitharon Sunshadow).

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Loot Valuation:** Silver bars possess a loot value of 100% (equivalent to large coins), while gold statues possess a loot value of 40%. (Established during identification).
*   **Expense Accounting:** Any items lost or given away during an expedition are recorded as company expenses at their full Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (Mage/MSRP).
*   **Attribute Consolidation:** Certain attribute improvements can be purchased by consolidating secondary advantages. For example, ST may be increased via Striking ST, Lifting ST, and HP; DX via Speed +0.25 or High Manual Dexterity; IQ via Will or Perception; and HT via Speed, FP, or Hard to Kill. However, this cannot be done in reverse (e.g., one cannot use Per to buy IQ), and Magery is not subject to these consolidation rules. Move points cannot be folded into Basic Speed.
*   **Ring of Free Action:** This item does not directly mitigate encumbrance but allows the wearer to drop a regular backpack as a free action.
*   **Item Identification Costs:** Any identification fee ending in "50" is paid to Vaelitharon Sunshadow; fees of "100" are paid to external NPCs.
