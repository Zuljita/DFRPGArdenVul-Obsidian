# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for R/R Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-11-07  
**Subject:** The Gauntlet of Gosterwick, Demonological Discoveries, and the Shadow of Cerbactos

### Summary
The company witnessed Vallium Halcyon attempt the legendary "Gauntlet of Gosterwick," a marathon of carousing through the local taverns that left him with fragmented memories of the night's events. Amidst these festivities, research into the soapstone idol revealed the presence of an ancient demon god, while diplomatic efforts with the wizard Asrotelion uncovered details regarding a significant magical theft unfolding within Arden Vul.

### Events & Actions by Character

**Uvash Edzendant**
*   **Town Actions:** Accompanied Vallium Halcyon during his carousing expedition through Gosterwick's establishments to serve as a social chaperone and witness. Provided the necessary funds ($200) to replenish Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios’s power item following its use.
*   **Equipment Changes:** Due to his "Very Wealthy" status, Uvash now manages the company's primary sales, allowing goods to be sold at 100% of their value.

**Vallium Halcyon**
*   **Town Actions:** Undertook the "Gauntlet of Gosterwick," visiting nine taverns and two inns (from the Yellow Cloak to the Stunned Acolyte) in a single night; the endeavor cost $500 but provided significant local intelligence. Vallium survived the ordeal but only remembers about half of the events. Commissioned a custom embroidered patch at Tasha’s Tailor Shop ($25) depicting eleven wooden mugs to commemorate the feat.
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Attempted to brew an Alkahest Grenade; the attempt resulted in failure and incurred additional costs.
*   **Equipment Changes:** Acquired a custom commemorative patch.

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**
*   **Town Actions:** Purchased five Minor Healing Potions from local vendors, clearing the town's stock.
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Successfully brewed a Greater Healing Potion at a discounted cost.
*   **Research:** Conducted research into the soapstone idol, identifying it as an icon of Kauket, an obscure ancient demon god of the salamander variety.
*   **Character Advancement:** Utilized his power item to cast *Bless* upon Uvash Edzendant, consuming 50 units of divine energy and requiring a $200 replenishment cost.

**Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow**
*   **Town Actions:** Provided identification services for the company's recent loot, charging a reduced rate of $50 per item (half the standard NPC cost of $100). Engaged in diplomatic efforts with the wizard Asrotelion, successfully extracting detailed lore regarding Cerbactos being an archmage and former member of the Collegium of Cinders. Vael also inquired about Asrotelion's capability to apprehend an archmage, though the wizard's competence remained uncertain.
*   **Equipment Changes:** None this week.

**Chrysalis (Mercenary)**
*   **Town Actions:** Requested a loan of $90 against future earnings to procure equipment for her companion.

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Demon Kauket:** The soapstone idol found in recent explorations is dedicated to Kauket, an ancient demon god associated with salamanders.
*   **The Second Chance Company:** A new adventuring group—comprising Geleg, Tresti, Klisko, and Samantha the Red—has been active. They report that Plumthorn's Halflings have been defeated by the United Goblins, and the Halls of Thoth have been renamed "Upper Goblintown." The group is currently allowing passage through their territory in exchange for donations of food, alcohol, or coin.
*   **The Theft of the Cinders:** The wizard Asrotelion revealed that an archmage named Cerbactos stole several priceless, unique tomes from the College of Cinders decades ago. Asrotelion has used the *Seeker* spell to track Cerbactos to the vicinity of Arden Vul. Asrotelion is leaving immediately to bring Cerbactos back, expecting a promotion to Associate Department Head upon his return.

### World Rulings
*   **Item Weight:** The company's rugs weigh 2 lbs each, though they remain bulky unless carefully rolled.
*   **Identification Services:** When hired by the company, Vaelitharon provides identification services at a fixed rate of $50 per item (half the standard NPC cost).
*   **Trade and Wealth:** Due to Uvash Edzendant’s "Very Wealthy" status, all items sold by the company are valued at 100% of their worth.
*   **The Scarab of Protection:** This item occupies the same equipment slot as an Amulet. It is capable of absorbing direct death attacks but was ruled ineffective against Wraiths, as Wraiths do not deal direct death attacks.
*   **Gauntlet of Flaming Fury:** The flame effect is activated via a free action. If the weapon is held "with purpose" (squeezed), it remains aflame; simply holding the weapon does not trigger the effect.
*   **Construction Costs:** Building costs for structures follow the LTC3 model, where prices scale linearly with the height of the walls. Reducing wall height scales down prices linearly.
*   **Magic Item Safety:** It remains unproven whether fragile items are protected from physical damage when stored within a Bag of Holding or Larel's Sack, as no such items have been smashed in such containers yet.

### Tactical Planning
The company intends to execute the following plan for their next excursion:
1.  Establish a secure cache in Gosterwick within a room in the APC, leaving one of the rugs behind for stability.
2.  Travel to Arden Vul and ascend the Long Stair to enter the Great Cavern via the secret entrance.
3.  Explore the shaft located near the metal pillar within the Great Cavern and investigate the old Archontean temple atop the stone pillar.
4.  Ambush the Settite outpost in the Great Cavern with the intent to seize their food supplies (specifically targeting Bags of Holding).
5.  Descend the Settite stairs to confront the quartet of Varumani guarding the octagonal room, utilizing fire and acid-based weaponry.
