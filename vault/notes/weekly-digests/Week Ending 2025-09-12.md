# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Ruala Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-09-12  
**Subject:** The Forum, the Retreat, and the Halfling Collision

### Summary
The company pressed deeper into the Cult of Set’s territory, reaching the newly identified area known as the Forum. However, upon encountering a force of approximately fifty Setites, the party was forced to execute a tactical retreat due to unfavorable positioning rather than sheer attrition. In the chaos of the withdrawal, the company evaded their primary target, Roskelly, and inadvertently collided with a group of halflings, driving the cultists into them.

*   **Character Development:** It was clarified that the "stop bleed" ability listed on Uvash’s character sheet is not yet a real capability and should not be assumed to be active.
*   **Combat Actions:** Participated in the heavy engagement against the fifty Setites that necessitated the retreat.

**Vallium Halcyon**
*   **Equipment:** Carries potions of Strength, Agility, Invisibility, Fire Resistance, and Major Healing.

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**

**Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow**

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Forum:** A newly reached major area within the dungeon depths.
*   **The Cult of Set:** The enemy force encountered numbered approximately fifty Setites, indicating a significant concentration of power in this sector.
*   **Religious Verse:** A fragment of verse was observed, describing the "Lord’s Pneuma" molding a champion through humility and wisdom, while noting that the lies of the proud find reward in self-knowing.

### World Rulings
*   **Magebane Potions:** Smashing a Magebane potion emits a sickly smoke that prevents the casting of wizard spells within a few yards. This effect does not suppress clerical magic.
*   **Potion Stability:** Uncorked potions are safe from the effects of *Dispel Magic*.

### Tactical Planning
*   **Retreat Conditions:** The party recognized that retreat may be necessary not only due to damage but also when positioned at a four-way intersection where moving too far out risks having the only known exit cut off by enemies.
*   **Future Engagement:** The company intends to re-engage the Cult of Set to pursue Roskelly, provided they can manage the risk of superior numbers and unfavorable terrain.
*   **Alchemical Strategy:** Magebane potions remain a key asset for neutralizing enemy casters in future skirmishes.

### Unresolved Threads
*   **The Halfling Collision:** The company rammed the Cult of Set into a group of halflings. The nature of this encounter—whether the halflings are hostile, neutral, or potential allies—remains to be determined.
*   **Roskelly’s Fate:** While the company evaded Roskelly during the retreat, his current location and intentions are unknown.
*   **Cult Reinforcements:** It remains to be seen if the retreat will trigger a concentrated pursuit by the Cult of Set.
