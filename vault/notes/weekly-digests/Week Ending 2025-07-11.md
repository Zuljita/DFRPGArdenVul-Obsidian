# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Ruala Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-07-11  
**Subject:** Distribution of Spoils, Alchemical Failures, and Research

### 1. Summary
The company concluded the week by distributing magical artifacts recovered from recent expeditions, including rings, a belt, and boots. Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios suffered a failure in brewing a Potion of Strength, incurring additional costs. Vallium Halcyon and Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow expressed interest in studying the Potion of Gaseous Form, though Vallium lacks the Alchemy skill at present. Ioannes successfully researched a location associated with Roskelly, with details provided privately. The company retained several key items for future study, including a map of the pyramid and the Divine Breath of Thoth.

### 2. Events & Actions by Character

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Attempted to brew a *Potion of Strength*. The attempt failed, resulting in wasted materials and an increased cost for future attempts.
*   **Research:** Focused research on the location associated with Roskelly. The roll was successful; specific findings were communicated via private message.
*   **Equipment Changes:** Acquired the *Ring of Protection +1*.
*   **Loot XP:** Awarded 2 XP for loot valuation.

**Vallium Halcyon**
*   **Town Actions:** Participated in the deliberation regarding the retention of recovered items versus sale. Determined that the *Boots of Elvenkind* (specifically the half found with the Basilisk) are party property and cannot be distributed to mercenaries without full consensus.
*   **Research/Study:** Expressed intent to study and reproduce the *Potion of Gaseous Form*. Clarified that while he possesses the Intelligence to learn Alchemy, he currently lacks the skill due to previous loot rules; acquiring it would require 1 CP and a full laboratory.
*   **Equipment Changes:** No new personal equipment acquired this week.

**Uvash Edzuson**
*   **Equipment Changes:** None. (Note: The *Ring of Protection +1* was acquired by Ioannes, not Uvash.)

**Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow**
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Expressed intent to study and reproduce the *Potion of Gaseous Form*.
*   **Equipment Changes:** Acquired the *Ring of Protection +2*.
*   **Loot XP:** Awarded 2 XP for loot valuation.

**Mercenaries (The Grudge Brigade)**
*   **Note:** The mercenaries expressed interest in acquiring the *Boots of Elvenkind*. However, the company resolved that the boots are party property. Their valuation is pending, as the GM has not yet established a price for them.

### 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **Baboon Level Inscription:** An inscription was discovered on a wall within the baboon level reading: *"Let the faithful render honor and glory to the light which reveals all."*
*   **Properties of Elven Footwear:** The *Boots of Elvenkind* recovered are of the *Mage-Stealth* variety, allowing the wearer to speak while wearing them (unlike *Hush* boots). The first half of the boots was found with the Basilisk.
*   **Centipede Corpse:** A centipede corpse was recovered; its value has not been established, and it remains undistributed.

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Valuation of Precious Materials:** Gold leaf and gold dust sell for 40% of their standard value.
*   **Jewelry Disassembly:** A complete piece of jewelry retains 100% of its value as a power item. If disassembled into components for use in a workshop, those components are valued at only 40%.
*   **Ring of Protection Mechanics:** The *Ring of Protection* provides Damage Reduction (DR) and bonuses to resistances, not an Active Defense bonus.

### 5. Tactical Planning
*   **Reverse Engineering:** The company intends to study and attempt to reproduce the *Potion of Gaseous Form*.
*   **Artifact Retention:** The company has resolved to retain the following items for future use or study:
    *   A map of the pyramid (image attached to records).
    *   A pyramid note.
    *   An *Elixir of Health*.
    *   The *Divine Breath of Thoth* book.
    *   A *Powered Cleric Scroll of Sanctuary*.
    *   The *Potion of Gaseous Form*.
    *   The *Boots of Elvenkind* (pending valuation).
*   **Party Item Distribution:** The following items were distributed: *Ring of Protection +1* (Ioannes), *Ring of Protection +2* (Vael), *Ring of Free Action* (claimed by Vael/Michael), and *Belt of Strength* (to be distributed).

### 6. Unresolved Threads
*   **Valuation Pending:** The final market price for the *Boots of Elvenkind* has yet to be established.
*   **Distribution Authority:** The company has determined that Vallium Halcyon cannot unilaterally distribute party-owned items (such as the boots) to mercenaries without full consensus.
*   **Research Details:** The specific findings from Ioannes's successful research into the location with Roskelly are known only to the GM and Ioannes via private message.
*   **Centipede Corpse:** The value and disposition of the centipede corpse remain unresolved.
