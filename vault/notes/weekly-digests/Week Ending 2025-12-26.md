# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Riches Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-12-26  
**Subject:** Expansion of the Beacon, Mercenary Hiring, and the Legacy of Thoth

### Summary
The company has focused heavily on fortifying the Beacon, expanding the reading room through magical excavation, and hiring new personnel. While significant progress was made in establishing a library and local connections, the recent loss of communal supplies to fire has necessitated a period of reorganization and replenishment. The company has also established protocols for death and incapacitation, including maintaining liquid reserves for resurrection and ensuring personal access to healing potions.

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The History of Kel Bormol:** Identified as the Overseer, Archmage, and Eye of Thoth during the fall of Arden Vul (Source: Research by Ioannes).
*   **Fael’s Mycological Guide:** Detailed information was obtained regarding several mushroom varieties:
    *   *Aqua Vitae:* Can be used as a Minor Healing potion (2-week shelf life).
    *   *Blue Trumpet:* Edible; holds significant water.
    *   *Cloud Cap:* Can be cooked into approximately six Minor Healing potions.
    *   *Eye-Opener:* Enhances senses but reduces judgment.
    *   *Mind-Eater:* Highly toxic; causes insanity.
    *   *Orange Lamps:* Provides emergency light for several hours.
    *   *Red Veil:* Toxic; causes temporary violent madness.
    *   *Slow Stepper:* Emits slowing spores when damaged.
    *   *White Bulbs:* Fatally poisonous.
    *   *White Ribs:* Nutritious, but easily confused with White Bulbs.
*   **The Imperial Tagmata:** A hypothesis has been raised that the "Mailed Fist" insignia may represent a third cohort of this imperial unit (Source: Player observation).

### World Rulings
*   **Resurrection Costs:** The cost to perform a Resurrection remains 15,000 gold pieces. The company has agreed that all members should maintain $15,000 in bank accounts for this purpose, and Ioannes will keep Greater Healing potions on his person for each member.
*   **Land Acquisition:** Near the APC, a 1/5 acre plot costs $10,000; a full acre is priced at $50,000.
*   **Training Requirements:** Training is required for the *Extra Attack* feat, but not for the *Luck* or *Perception* attributes.
*   **The Watcher Statue:** The statue does not provide active notifications of movement; one must periodically observe through its eyes using a light source.
*   **Teleportation Portal:** The broken teleporter is missing its power source and requires both a specific command word and a physical key to function.
*   **Alchemy/Herb Lore:** Herb Lore is the most effective skill for processing mushrooms into medicinal products, though Alchemy or Naturalist may be attempted.
