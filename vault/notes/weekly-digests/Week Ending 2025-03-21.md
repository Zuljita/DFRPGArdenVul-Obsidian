# Weekly Chronicle: The Distribution of Spoils
**Date:** Week ending 2025-03-21
**Subject:** Post-Delve Loot Management and the Establishment of Gosterwick Commerce

### 1. Summary
Following the successful first foray into the ruins, the company focused its efforts on the complex task of auditing loot and distributing spoils within Gosterwick. This week saw the establishment of new mercenary contracts, the formalization of the company's treasury through the sale of surplus gear, and the acquisition of essential logistical assets. The group navigated the financial mechanics of the "Great Haul" bonus, ultimately securing two character points per member by strategically selling mundane items while retaining key magical and utility gear.

### 3. Lore & Discoveries

*   **The Arcane Practitioners Club (APC):** A local institution run by mages Pelteon and Lyssandra. Membership ($13/week or $50/month) provides access to a library, alchemy lab, and lounge. The club offers services including magic item identification, spell training, and the recharging of mage power items. Lyssandra is interested in buying any mage scrolls containing spells she does not already know.
*   **Gosterwick History:** Once a tiny Thorcin farming village, the area was transformed roughly 20 years ago by the arrival of Lady Alexia, an Archontean noblewoman, and her Knights of the Azure Shield. It is currently a booming frontier town with a population of a couple thousand. The walls are largely unfinished, marked only by a dirt berm except for two gatehouses.
*   **The Shrine to Lucreon:** A notable religious site featuring a mystery where coins dropped into an offering box magically ascend to rest in the statue's money bag.
*   **Torunn the Smith:** A big Wiskin woman regarded as the best smith in Gosterwick. She demonstrated the differences between Good, Fine, Balanced, and Dwarven quality work to Vael.
*   **The Temple of Mitra:** A circular stone structure holding a statue of the god with the scales of justice.
*   **The Yellow Cloak Inn:** The party’s lodging, run by innkeeper Margot the Red. It is a five-story building near the Central Market with a 3-story addition, offering rooms and meals for $150/week.
*   **Rumors & Intelligence:**
    *   **The Goblin Gog:** An exiled goblin residing near the arena who trades information for meat.
    *   **Thoth’s Secret:** It is whispered that a chamber exists in the Upper Halls where one can hear the god Thoth speak through a massive horn.
    *   **The Lost Legionaries:** Legends persist of six cohorts lost at the "Cavern of Blood" 250 years ago, along with their battle standards.
    *   **The Troll Bedara:** A hunter was recently seen searching for a troll named Bedara near the troll market lift.

### 4. World Rulings

*   **Training Costs:** The cost to acquire a new skill is $40 per point if the skill is already listed on a character's professional piece, and $80 per point otherwise. Training is free if the trait was used during the previous adventure.
*   **Loot Valuation & Rewards:** To earn maximum advancement rewards for a delve, the company must reach a specific gold threshold (currently $1560 per person for the first bonus, scaling up). Items sold immediately to outsiders yield 40% of their retail value; items kept by the party are valued at 20% for XP purposes. Shortswords sell for $400 retail (used value $160).
*   **Identification & Alchemy:** Well-known potions can be identified via alchemy rolls without a laboratory. The APC provides professional identification services for a fee.
*   **Item Maintenance:** Mage power items can be recharged at the Arcane Practitioners Club; cleric power items are recharged at the temple. Improving the quality of mundane equipment requires specialized smiths or jewelers found within Gosterwick.
*   **Logistics:** Wagons cannot traverse the Long Stair due to its extreme difficulty (compared to the hardest Grand Canyon hikes); only mules or goats can manage it. Levitation is mana-intensive for such distances. Stashing treasure in the dungeon does not generate XP if picked up later.

### 5. Tactical Planning

*   **Logistics:** The company intends to acquire a wheelbarrow and potentially a beast of burden (such as a mule) to facilitate the transport of heavy loot from future delves, as wagons are unsuitable for the Long Stair.
*   **Supply Chain:** Plans are being discussed regarding the bulk purchase of healing potions and group camping gear, such as tents.
*   **Wealth Management:** The group discussed the viability of the Wealth advantage but determined that having a mercenary with Very Wealthy would be inefficient due to the cut taken by the mercenary and the requirement that the merc must be present during the delve to generate XP.
