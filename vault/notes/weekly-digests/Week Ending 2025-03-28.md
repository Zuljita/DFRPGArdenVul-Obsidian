# Weekly Chronicle: The Archives of Gosterwick
**Date:** Week ending 2025-03-28  
**Subject:** Post-Delve Training, Local Lore, and the Management of Cursed Spoils

### 1. Summary
Following the second foray into the ruins, the company focused on skill refinement and the management of newly acquired, albeit problematic, treasures. This week was marked by significant character training in Gosterwick and the formalization of new local knowledge regarding the city's religious landmarks and lodgings. The party also established administrative protocols for loot management and recording consent, while navigating the reliability of their automated knowledge base.

### 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Hill of the Gods:** Located in the northwest of Gosterwick, this hill houses the primary religious centers. Four major temples are staffed by full-time clerics: Demma, Mitra, Lucreon, and Heschius Ban. Other deities are served via smaller shrines staffed by volunteer worshippers or wandering priests.
*   **The House of the Gods:** A large, shared temple in Gosterwick serving various deities not covered by the individual major temples.
*   **The Yellow Cloak Inn:** A prominent five-story structure near the Central Market. It features a lounge, upper-class and main common rooms, guest rooms, and dormitories. The establishment is managed by Margot the Red (a Thorcin) and her husband Bricwine, with Helle (a Wiskin) tending the main bar. Note that spellcasting is strictly discouraged by the innkeepers, particularly if it results in critical failures.
*   **Rumor - The Singing Heads:** A report suggests an eighth-level underground grotto contains carved heads that sing; correct melodies reveal treasure, while incorrect ones result in death.
*   **Rumor - The Guild of Service:** An ancient cult of Set reportedly operated a slave trade under this name, believing in the natural right of the strong to dominate the weak.
*   **Hydrology of Gosterwick:** Recent dam modifications have not yet produced visible changes to the river levels in the city, though early spring flows from melting snow make precise assessment difficult.

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Research Mechanics:** Conducting research requires the Research skill, access to a library, and one week of time. Results are determined by a blind roll at the end of the week; success yields a rumor, failure yields nothing, and a critical failure has severe consequences.
*   **Training Requirements:** Certain skills, such as Climbing, can be gained through experience without formal instruction. Skills like Search, Stealth, and Swimming require dedicated training. Gesture was granted as an exception due to situational necessity.
*   **Blind Fighting:** A successful Blind Fighting roll eliminates the -2 penalty for parrying from the rear, effectively providing the benefits of Peripheral Vision for that engagement.
*   **Clerical Services:** The cost of services like *Remove Curse* varies; clerics may receive a "friendly" discount when visiting their own temple, whereas "heathen" practitioners are charged standard book rates.
*   **Attribute/Skill Limits:** There are no established limits on the acquisition of Luck or Fit points.
*   **Knowledge Base Reliability:** The GM has warned that the party's AI knowledge base (ChatGPT) is prone to hallucinating rumors and fake lore if fed excessive data or long conversation histories. Players are advised to curate inputs carefully and verify information against primary sources.

### 5. Tactical Planning
