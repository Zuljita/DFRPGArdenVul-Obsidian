### World Rulings
* **Imperial Field Plate Legality:** Ownership of Imperial Field Plate is strictly illegal for anyone below the rank of high noble, as it is considered a priceless historical relic stolen from the Emperor. 
* **Social Consequences of Forbidden Gear:** While wearing such gear within the wilds of Arden Vul is unlikely to attract official imperial attention, it remains highly likely to attract robbers or thieves connected to official interests.
* **Equipment Quality:** The recently recovered spyglass is noted to be of inferior quality compared to standard telescopes.
* **Material Properties:** Bronze Rudishva IDs are significantly heavier than other identification discs due to being composed of solid metal.
### Tactical Planning
The company intends to execute the following plan for the next expedition:
1.  **Logistics:** Procure a week's worth of food supplies in town, using recent expense reports from Helena to estimate required quantities.
2.  **Transit:** Utilize the recovered rugs to travel to the Beacon; use *Blink Other* on Vallium to facilitate the transport of the remaining party members to the surface ruins.
3.  **Exploration Path:** Move through the Pyramid and Glory of Thoth levels to locate Temrin and commission a map for the Command Bracelet.
4.  **Forum Re-establishment:** Return to the Forum of Set to unload food stores (offered at cost) and purchase desired items from Horace and Galadnor.
5.  **Targeted Searches:** Explore room #4 on the annotated central map, search the Library of Set for valuable texts, and utilize *Seek* or *See Secrets* spells within the sand-filled chamber.
6.  **Intelligence Gathering:** Investigate the southern stairwell leading from the Guild of Service area to locate any fleeing members.
