# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Ruala Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-07-25  
**Subject:** Loot Liquidation, Cursed Curios, and Alchemical Failures

### Summary
The company spent the week managing the significant spoils from their recent delve, focusing on the liquidation of high-value gear to fund character advancement. A consensus was reached to sell the Fine Mail (with Fortify 1) to generate Character Points for all members, while a vote was held regarding whether to offer tribute to the dragon or sell remaining high-value items. Amidst discussions of tribute and commerce, a cursed artifact was purified, and new alchemical ventures were attempted.

### Events & Actions by Character

**Uvash Edzuson**
*   **Town actions:** Decided to retain the donkey ears acquired during recent travels.
*   **Equipment changes:** Acquired a Medium Shield (Fine, Balanced, Deflect 1, Lighten 1) for use in combat.

**Vallium Halcyon**
*   **Town actions:** Spent 125 silver on a Carousing roll with a +2 bonus, resulting in a Private Message outcome.
*   **Alchemy and crafting:** Attempted to brew a Potion of Fire Resistance; the attempt failed, resulting in additional costs.
*   **Equipment changes:** Continues to utilize a Large Shield (Fine, Balanced, Deflect 1, Lighten 1).

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**
*   **Town actions:** Read the advertisement for Ashe Maykum’s "Living Wheelbarrow" with Onyx; both were befuddled by the prototype’s claims of levitation and plant-based propulsion.

**Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow**
*   **Alchemy and crafting:** Successfully bottled samples of the healing sludge found in the orange room pool for further analysis.
*   **Research results:** Investigated the availability of the *Jump* spell; determined it is categorized under Magic rather than Spells, and ultimately decided it was not worth learning.
*   **Equipment changes:** Utilized a *Remove Curse* service to purify a silver coin. The coin, previously cursed to cause distractions by attracting attention to shiny objects, is now considered a "lucky penny." The GM noted the curse was "oddly overly fixed" and the coin still feels magical.

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **Stirge Lair Notes:** Recovered adventuring notes detail a failed expedition involving the Obsidian Gate, the Chamber of Life, and the Sundered Span. The text records the deaths of individuals named Gregor (near the Troll Lift) and Otto, as well as the disappearance of Jasmine.
*   **The Living Wheelbarrow:** Information regarding a new prototype by Ashe Maykum; a plant-based wheelbarrow designed for easy levitation and movement across difficult terrain. It requires a competent caster and Levitation spell to utilize its levitating feature.
*   **Rumor 17:** While drinking mushroom ale in the Forum of Set, red guardsmen were overheard discussing escaped slaves causing trouble to the north, despite official denials from the Guild of Service.

### World Rulings
*   **The Lover's Token:** A one-use item that allows for two-way voice communication with the giver for up to one hour via concentration. The effect persists until the token is broken or the hour expires.
*   **The "Was" Stick:** An item of low commercial value due to its requirement of a cleric of Set, a deity currently banned.
*   **Spellbook Commerce:** Spellbooks may be sold as collections of unpowered scrolls; however, they are less valuable than true scrolls because they only permit the learning of spells, not their casting.
*   **Shield Mechanics:** Utilizing a Large Shield requires the *Shield-Wall Training* advantage to avoid a -2 penalty to attacks. Medium shields do not impose this penalty.
*   **Training Requirements:** The skills *Search* and *Traps* require formal training despite practical experience in dungeons. Similarly, the *Energy Reserve* (ER) ability requires formal training.
*   **Item Availability:** Balanced sticks/batons are not available for purchase or trade in the current market.

### Tactical Planning
*   **Loot Liquidation:** The company committed to selling the Fine Mail (with Fortify 1) to generate Character Points for all members.
*   **Tribute vs. Profit:** A vote was held regarding whether to keep approximately $1,700 in treasure (gold censer, Thoth statue, dwarven shield) to offer as tribute to the dragon or sell them. The net difference in profit per player was calculated to be approximately $359.
*   **Future Exploration:** The company plans to return to the Great Cave to investigate the locked metal door in the exploding hexagon hallway and explore the area north of the Fidelity stone guardian. There is also an intention to revisit the waterfall area to check for further progress.

### Unresolved Threads
*   **The Tribute Decision:** The outcome of the vote regarding the gold censer, Thoth statue, and dwarven shield remains to be finalized.
*   **The Healing Sludge:** The long-term utility and safety of the bottled sludge remain unverified.
*   **The Obsidian Gate:** The status of the barred gate mentioned in the stirge lair notes remains unknown.
*   **The Northward Path:** The contents of the area north of the Fidelity stone guardian have yet to be scouted.
