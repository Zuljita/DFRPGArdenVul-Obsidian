# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Ruala Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-06-20  
**Subject:** The Restoration of Versania and the Redistribution of Spoils

### 1. Summary
The company successfully facilitated the recovery of the individual known as Lytta through significant charitable donations to the Temple of Mitra. Following a period of intense negotiation, the recent spoils have been redistributed to maximize character point acquisition for the company's primary members. The woman known as Lytta has been revealed to be Versania; she had survived in the caves for several years by fishing and is currently volunteering at the House of the Gods.

### 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Identity of Lytta:** The woman known as Lytta has been revealed to be Versania. She had survived in the caves for several years by fishing. She is currently volunteering at the House of the Gods (Source: Recovery process).
*   **Rumor 13:** A report suggests that a secret entrance to the Forum of Set can be found by shining two lanterns from the left side of the river at night, looking for a basket on the cliff face. The source was identified as dodgy-looking tinkers and trademen who mentioned Stephania paid for supplies (Source: Local traders/tinkers).
*   **Gosterwick Demographics:** There are approximately six dwarves currently residing in Gosterwick. Uvash believes he has met them all, though Archon Shiva notes he has not met Thronebreaker (Source: Local inquiry).
*   **Botanical Magic:** In the traditions of "Ye Olden Days" magic, fungi are classified as plants (Source: GM Ruling).
*   **Political Climate:** The party has begun finding crude political cartoons posted around town depicting large chickens surrounded by small armed men, and rumors are circulating that the company will not pursue the halflings (Source: Grudge Brigade reports).

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Loot Experience Thresholds:** The gold required for 1 XP is determined by the formula $\text{Cost} = (\text{CP}^2) / 100$, or the character's weekly cost of living, whichever is higher. Achieving 2 XP requires ten times that amount. (Source: GM Blog Post).
*   **Alchemy Availability:** Potions other than minor and major healing are not generally available for purchase; they must be brewed by individuals with sufficient Alchemy or Herb Lore skills.
*   **The Belt of Dwarvenkind:** While the belt's dwarven-specific features do not function for dwarves, the +1 Charisma bonus remains effective. Regular use of the belt causes rapid facial hair growth (beards).
*   **Training Costs:** Training for Combat Reflexes is dependent on recent activity; if a character has been fighting frequently, no training cost is required. Training costs for the Luck advantage are waived.
*   **Religious Conversion:** Converting citizens to a specific faith can be attempted once per week via Theology or Fast-talk rolls; a critical success results in a new convert. Theology is preferred as conversions are more likely to stick.
*   **Parish Operations:** Uvash may use the House of the Gods for his proposed dwarven parish for free until he builds his own structure. If he establishes a regular day of service, at least half the local dwarves are expected to attend.

### 5. Tactical Planning
*   **Expedition Targets:** The company is considering several potential destinations, including the Forum of Set, the Surface Ruins, or the Well of Light.
*   **The Great Cavern:** Plans are being discussed to utilize mercenaries (such as The GOAT) for reconnaissance via Beast Possession in upcoming delves.
*   **The Halfling Grudge:** Whether the company will pursue the halflings/Roskelly remains undecided, though public perception is shifting.
