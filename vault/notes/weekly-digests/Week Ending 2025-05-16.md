### 4. World Rulings
*   **Deflect Damage Bonus (DB):** When calculating Deflect DB, a character receives the best physical shield DB applicable to the attack, plus any magical DB provided by that shield. Multiple sources of magical Deflect DB do not stack; only the single best source applies.
*   **Damage Reduction (DR):** All physical armor DR on a specific hit location stacks. However, for magical Fortify effects, only the single best source applies; multiple Fortify spells or enchantments do not stack.
*   **Magical Buffs:** Magical sources of DB and DR are limited to one of each type per character. Similarly, buffs such as *Heroic Might*, *Wisdom*, and *Strengthen Will* **do stack** because they affect different statistics (e.g., Will vs. Will modifiers are distinct in this ruling). The best single effect applies only if they are the same type.
*   **Attribute/Stat Buffs:** Spells that boost different attributes or statistics—such as *Grace* (DX), *Reflexes* (Combat Reflexes), *Haste* (Dodge), and *Shield* (DB)—do stack.
*   **Scrolls:** Unless explicitly stated otherwise, scrolls are unpowered.
*   **Logistics of the Long Stair:** The ascent of the Long Stair is too steep for wagons; any attempt to move a wagon up this path requires extreme magic and involves significant risks of falling.
*   **Market Rates:** A small mule is valued at $1,000, while a large mule is valued at $2,000.
*   **Town Services:** Healing performed by a company member within town is free of charge. Beers are priced at $5 per unit.
