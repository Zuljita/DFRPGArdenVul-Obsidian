# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Ruala Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-06-27  
**Subject:** Alchemical Mishaps, Mercenary Updates, and the Tower of the Ape

### 1. Summary
The company spent the week in Gosterwick preparing for the next descent into Arden Vul. While Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios successfully brewed a *Strength Potion* and secured a discount for future alchemical work, Vallium Halcyon’s attempt to brew an *Invisibility Potion* failed due to measurement errors, requiring Ioannes’ assistance and incurring a price surcharge. Carousing yielded a critical lead directing the party toward the Tower of the Ape in Newmarket. The company also finalized its composition for the upcoming expedition, confirming the arrival of the mercenary Thronebreaker and the potential inclusion of Chrysalis.

### 4. World Rulings
*   **The Concussion Spell:** The spell has been adjusted for balance. The initial stun penalty is HT minus the number of dice of damage, but this penalty is capped at a maximum of -3. The stun radius is equal to the number of dice of damage. Recovery from a concussion stun requires a straight HT roll without modifiers. For casters with Magery 4 or higher, the cap on damage dice can be exceeded, though the HT penalty remains capped at -3.
*   **Wealth and Experience:** Possession of the *Wealth* advantage increases the amount of loot-based experience points earned by 50%.
*   **Loot Distribution:** The party agreed to a distribution method where Archon Shiva defaulted to meeting Vallium and Michael’s 2cp thresholds, with Ioannes receiving 1cp for loot, as the total loot value was insufficient for everyone to meet the 2cp threshold. Demented Avenger agreed to this split for the time being.

### 5. Tactical Planning
*   **Expedition Goals:** The company intends to venture into the Great Caverns or toward Gog's area for the upcoming session. There is specific interest in investigating the Tower of the Ape in Newmarket based on recent rumors.
*   **Combat Strategy:** The party intends to avoid encounters with flying or swimming foes.
*   **Mercenary Composition:** The company is considering a heavy frontline composition, potentially utilizing Vallium, Michael, Thronebreaker, and Chrysalis.
