# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for R/R Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-10-31  
**Subject:** Loot Analysis, Rug History, and Church Planning

### Summary
The company secured significant magical artifacts and resolved the status of the teleporting rugs. Ioannes uncovered the dark history behind the rugs, revealing they are banned in the Empire due to their use in assassination. Vallium attempted to expand the party’s key access and brew potions, but suffered a critical alchemical failure that drew the ire of Lyssandra. Uvash’s proselytizing yielded no new converts, but his carousing provided a lead on a missing knight. The party discussed the feasibility of building a permanent church in Gosterwick, with Uvash drafting blueprints and receiving cost estimates from the GM.

### Lore & Discoveries

**The History of Teleporting Rugs**
The rugs are not merely magical conveniences but artifacts of political sensitivity. Approximately 1,400 years ago in Archontos, Sligo the Devious used a Rug of Instant Access to facilitate the assassination of Leonidas. The resulting ban on rug enchanting and the cultural stigma against used carpets are direct consequences of this event.

**Eyes of Set Goggles**
Vael tested the Eyes of Set goggles. They provide a Dark Vision effect but do not function in magical darkness. They do not allow the wearer to see through spells like *Darkness*.

**Church Construction Estimates**
Building a church in Gosterwick is feasible but varies wildly in cost. A small used house costs approximately $2,000, while a high-end custom building can reach $100,000 or more. Prime real estate on the Hill of the Gods is unavailable, but Outer Gosterwick near the Arcane Practitioners' Club is cheaper. Government approval is required for any construction.

### World Rulings

**Attribute Caps**
The GM confirmed that the cap of 20 applies to both primary and secondary attributes, including advantages that function as attribute boosts (e.g., Acute Vision, Striking ST). Advantages that are not direct attribute boosts (e.g., Mantis Strike) can be upgraded beyond the cap, but permanent attribute values cannot exceed 20.

**Loot from Trades**
Quest rewards count as loot. Purchases from merchants do not. However, favorable ad-hoc trades with NPCs may count as loot at the GM's discretion, though the GM reserves the right to revoke this if exploits are detected.

**Death and Resurrection**
Upon death, characters lose approximately 20% of their character points. Resurrection is possible, and the party intends to fund it unless a Total Party Kill occurs.

### Tactical Planning

**Rug Testing**
The party plans to test the range of the teleporting rugs by leaving one in Gosterwick and attempting to use it from various distances during the walk to Arden Vul.

**Tower Exploration**
The party intends to explore the tower at the surface level forum of the ruins of Arden Vul. Plans include searching for secret entrances, trying discovered keys, and using the Eyes of Set goggles to navigate the magical darkness in the stairwell.

**Church Construction**
Uvash has submitted blueprints for a church. The party is evaluating the cost and location, with a preference for building near the existing dwarven settlement to attract more dwarven adherents.

### Unresolved Threads

**Missing Knight Sixth**
Iris of the Second Chance group is searching for a knight named Sixth. The party has not yet determined if they have seen him or if they wish to pursue the bounty.

**Vallium’s Alchemical Failure**
Lyssandra’s disapproval regarding the supervision of Vallium’s alchemy remains an unresolved tension. The party must decide how to manage future alchemical tasks to avoid similar failures.

**Eyes of Set Limitations**
While the goggles do not work in magical darkness, their exact range and duration in non-magical darkness remain to be fully quantified by Vael.

**Church Funding**
The party has not yet decided on the specific funding mechanism for Uvash’s church, whether through independent income, tithes, or direct party contribution.
