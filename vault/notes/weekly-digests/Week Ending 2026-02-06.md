# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Rises Company
**Date:** Week ending 2026-02-06  
**Subject:** Rudishva Data Decryption, the Kaliyani Betrayal, and Beacon Command

### Summary
The company has made significant strides in comprehending the technology of the crashed Beacon, utilizing newly acquired data crystals to uncover the history of the Rudishva Hegemony. These investigations have revealed a devastating historical betrayal by the Kaliyani species and provided vital technical specifications for the ship's various systems and identity protocols. The crew has also secured the location of the second Command Tablet and identified the method to open the Obsidian Gates.

### Events & Actions by Character

**Uvash Edzuson**
*   **Town actions:** Successfully performed a sermon, convincing two dwarves and one human to attend his services; prepared his residence for his family's arrival in the spring; undertook an alphabetical cataloging of the company's library, though his chosen method caused significant distress to the scribe Stamelis.
*   **Alchemy/crafting:** Successfully brewed a minor healing potion, receiving a discount on the cost due to the success.
*   **Research:** Investigated energy weapons (laser pistols and rifles) via the ship computer. Learned that they require Lesser Power Supplies, have a two-button activation sequence (point and press top, then press front), and that testing them is dangerous and cannot be done during downtime.
*   **Equipment changes:** None.

**Vallium Halcyon**

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Kaliyani Betrayal:** According to Akla-Chah and ship logs, the Kaliyani (a six-limbed, reptilian species) were once allies of the Rudishva but launched a treacherous attack that led to the death of most of the Beacon's crew.
*   **The Beacon Crew Roster:** The vessel carried approximately 200 Rudishva, 75 Kaliyani, 100 Varuda, and 75 Varumani, overseen by two AIs.
*   **Rudishva Identity Protocols:** Access levels are determined by color: Rust (crew/quarters), Brown (NCO/storage), Sky Blue (junior officers), Pale Green (senior Varuda), Pink (senior Varumansi), Orange (senior Kaliyani), Silver (senior Rudishva), and Yellow (Captain; full access).
*   **Data Crystal Contents:** 
    *   *The Shining Beacon of Glorious Heaven:* A six-hour technical archive containing crew interviews regarding life support, weapons, and astrogation.
    *   *Crew Roster:* A five-hour visual archive providing 3D images and service histories of the crew.
    *   *Fire Pits of Creation:* Documentation on a Rudishva/Kaliyani forge located within Arden Vul that utilizes temperature, chthonic energy, and phase controls.
    *   *Untitled Crystal:* A recording of a Rudishva performing ritualistic dance or exercise.
*   **The Heqeti War:** The Rudishva defeated the Heqeti in their mountain pyramids and seized a "demon horn," but the subsequent alliance between the Kaliyani and Heqeti proved fatal to the Rudishva presence on the planet.
*   **Transit Shaft Mechanics:** The shaft used for moving cargo requires magical grouting and a specific liquid application; failure to follow protocols can result in gravity-related damage.

### World Rulings
*   **The Command Bracelet:** This device serves as both a communication and authorization tool. It allows the wearer to communicate with anyone wearing a compatible bracelet or any Rudishva machine within 100 feet. Akla-Chah can communicate with a wearer from vast distances via this link.
*   **Ship Computer Capabilities:** The bridge computer can provide live translation for various languages through the communication station. Proficiency in Archontean and Elvish improves only through regular use on the bridge. The computer currently speaks Archontean with an "angry accent." For security reasons, the bridge computer lacks a direct port for data crystals.
*   **Rudishva Weaponry:** Laser pistols and rifles require Lesser Power Supplies to function; testing these weapons is considered highly dangerous and requires proper training.
*   **Tactical Suit Properties:** The tactical suit has the same weight as heavy leather with a full-face helmet but contains no active technology.
*   **Point Defense Array:** Described as "very dangerous," the array fires tiny pieces of metal at very fast speeds.
*   **Transit Shaft Safety:** The shaft is considered safe only if protocols are followed, including inspecting grout and ensuring 90% liquid application.
