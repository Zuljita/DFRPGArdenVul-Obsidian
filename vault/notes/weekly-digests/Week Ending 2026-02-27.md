### Lore & Discoveries
*   **Sapphires:** These gems are fundamentally linked to the properties of wisdom, magic, vision, cold, and chastity (Source: Vallium’s research).
*   **The Kauketiad:** An epic poem detailing the wars between Archonteans and the heqeti. It emphasizes soldierly virtues—fidelity (ring), protection (armor), vengeance (sword), and nobility (heart)—and mentions the conquest of the Zuggurat of Kauket, located north and east of the chasm (Source: *The Kauketiad*).
*   **Neferet:** A high priestess of Thoth who held significant power in Arden Vul prior to the civil war, serving as second-in-command to the Prior (Source: Ioannes’ research).
*   **Arcanum & Creation Molds:** Arcanum serves as a source of power for creating various items, including struts, plating, fuel cells, teleportation plates, and weaponry. Furthermore, placing a lump of arcanum near certain items can recharge them, though this process depletes the arcanum itself. It does not appear to support direct spellcasting (Source: *Vul Speaks* via Akla-Chah).

### World Rulings
*   **Boots of Levitation:** These boots magically resize to fit any race except halflings; however, they possess a weight limit of 300 lbs.
*   **Teleport Shield:** This effect is only effective at preventing the crossing of a spell's established boundary or perimeter.
*   **Cleric’s Ban on Weapons:** The minimum restriction for the cleric's ban covers cutting weapons; piercing weapons are permitted as they do not cut.
*   **Item Customization:** Moving gems or ornate features between items is not trivial and requires time, money, and a jeweler. A jeweler (such as Chairduster) can move gems for $500.
*   **Torq Pricing:** Silver torqs start at a base price of $100 (anything lower is steel with silver plating). To achieve an Ornate 3 rating, the item must cost at least $1,000.
*   **Newmarket Market:** The market operates five days a week and offers a superior selection of goods compared to Gosterwick.
