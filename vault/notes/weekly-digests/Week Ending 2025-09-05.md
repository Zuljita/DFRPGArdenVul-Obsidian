# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Ruala Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-09-05
**Subject:** The Murder of Susarra and the Pursuit of the Settites

### Summary
The company returned to Gosterwick to find Susarra murdered in the Yellow Cloak Inn. Her headless corpse was discovered by Vallium, Ioannes, and Uvash. Investigation revealed the decapitation was performed with a large axe by an unskilled hand, and magical tracking placed her head approximately ten miles north in a well-decorated room. Vael secured a blood sample from the House of Sight to aid in the tracking.

Coinciding with this tragedy, the company received a bounty letter from Coinbase offering $2,500 each for the capture or death of Vael, Ioannes, Michael, Uvash, Vallium, and Susarra, to be delivered to the Forum in Arden Vul. Vallium requested a letter of introduction to the Temple of Thoth in Newmarket from Lillian before she departed for a meeting with the House of Sight regarding the Cult of Set. Ioannes researched the Settite cult, confirming their history and current presence in Arden Vul.

The company resolved to prioritize the eradication of the Settite Cult and avenging Susarra over other objectives. Due to the urgency of the pursuit, the GM ruled that training is unavailable if the party rushes back to the dungeon, though Vael may raise his Magery trait without training due to constant use. The party is preparing to depart immediately to maintain the Trace on Susarra’s head.

### Lore & Discoveries

**The Murder of Susarra**
*   **Cause of Death:** Decapitation via large axe. The cuts in the bedframe indicate the killer was unskilled and required multiple attempts.
*   **Location of Head:** Ten miles north of Gosterwick, in a backpack within a well-decorated room (containing a coffee table, chairs, ewers, and a writing desk).
*   **Suspect Profile:** The killer is likely not a professional assassin. The location of the head suggests a trophy taken by someone with access to comfortable quarters.

**The Settite Cult**
*   **History:** Set was a Greater God of the old pantheon (the Ten), associated with power, knowledge, domination, slavery, vengeance, and serpents. Worship was banned in 1839 AEP.
*   **Current Status:** A Forum of Set exists in Arden Vul. The Settite Guild of Service is rumored to still trade slaves.
*   **Abominations:** The Arden Vul temple previously bred a fearsome animal species in a secret cavern near the waterfall.
*   **Allies:** Yrtol, a ghost in the Ruined Watchtower, hates Settites and may be an ally.

**Coinbase Bounty**
*   A bounty letter offers $2,500 each for the capture or death of Vael, Ioannes, Michael, Uvash, Vallium, and Susarra. The letter serves as a pass to the Forum in Arden Vul and alerts the Pontifex upon presentation.

**World Rulings & Mechanics**
*   **Training Restrictions:** If the party rushes back to the dungeon without downtime, no skills requiring training can be learned. Skills used recently enough to have been learned in play are exempt.
*   **Magery Upgrade:** Vael may raise his Magery trait without training due to constant use.
*   **Diplomacy:** Points cannot be spent on Diplomacy without training.
*   **Bracers of Force:** Do not stack with armor DR (take the better value). They stack with other non-armor magical DR.
*   **Torc of Protection:** Functions similarly to Bracers of Force but only protects from the neck up.
*   **Hand of Glory:** A mummified hand with candles, designed to be hung from the neck. Carrying it does not trigger the curse; using it does.
*   **Arden Vul Cakes:** 6" diameter, 1" thick, 1 lb. Feeds one person for one day. Highly nutritious, not appetizing, and appears to last forever if kept dry. Cost: $15 each.
*   **Yrtol:** A ghost in the Ruined Watchtower who hates Settites. Vallium plans to visit him to find common ground.

### Tactical Planning

**Primary Objective: Eradicate the Settite Cult**
*   **Rationale:** Avenging Susarra and eliminating the cult is the agreed-upon priority.
*   **Immediate Action:** Pursue the Trace to the location of Susarra’s head (10 miles north). The head is in a backpack in a decorated room; the party intends to ambush the owner upon departure.
*   **Secondary Objective:** Visit the Ruined Watchtower to recruit Yrtol.
*   **Logistics:** The party plans to leave early to maintain the Trace. Vael will carry the Bracers of Force. Michael will wear the Torc of Protection if present. Vallium will wear the Amulet of Missile Shield.

**Secondary Objective: House of Sight & Temple of Thoth**
*   Vallium’s letter to the Temple of Thoth is pending delivery by Lillian.
*   The company intends to seek sponsorship (materiel, personnel, intel) from the House of Sight and the "Green Lady" (likely the Drome or a high-ranking cleric) for the war against the Settites.
