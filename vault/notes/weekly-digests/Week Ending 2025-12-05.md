# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for R/R Company
**Date:** Week ending 202/12/05  
**Subject:** Teleportation Squares, Archontean Legions, and the Well of Light

### Summary
The company spent much of the week conducting vital research into the teleportation squares recovered from recent expeditions and visiting key contacts within the Beacon and Gosterwick to gather intelligence. Preparations are now underway for a descent into the Well of Light and an investigation of the Goblin Market.

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **Teleportation Mechanics:** The glass activation squares are made of Arcanum-enriched glass. There are six colors of squares, and all gates are activated using the same six colors, though different sequences correspond to different destinations (Source: Stamelis/Ioannes).
*   **Military History:** Anaximander noted that the Sheep's Head Legion and the third cohort of the Emperor's Imperial Tagmata were part of Adrienic's expedition to reclaim the Halls of Arden Vul, but he does not recognize other animal-themed legion insignia (Source: Anaximander).
*   **Geography/Verticality:** The vertical distance from the Howling Caves to the Tower of Scrutiny is approximately 250 feet; the distance from the Howling Caves to the Halls of Thoth is approximately 100 feet (Source: Field measurements).
*   **The Druid's Retreat:** A map identified as "Druid's Retreat" was noted, with context suggesting the presence of a druid named Gerrilad (Source: Recovered Map).
*   **Loot Distribution:** It was established that Vallium receives 1 loot XP for the previous week's haul, while all other members receive 2 loot XP (Source: Company Agreement).

### World Rulings
*   **Mercenary Availability:** Only one mercenary is available to the company per week; their services are unavailable on weeks they are not actively in play.

*   **Navigation:** In this region, Navigation (Land) and Navigation (Sea) skills are functionally interchangeable for the purposes of training.
*   **Mapping Visibility:** A location or scene cannot be viewed via a map without a token being present in that scene.
*   **Statue Components:** The stone hands recently recovered are too small to belong to the primary statue of Arden; they likely belong to smaller statues or multiple distinct figures.
*   **Key Reproduction:** Copies of large bronze keys are produced in steel rather than bronze.

### Tactical Planning
The company intends to execute the following for the upcoming session:
*   **Travel:** Use Rugs of Instant Access and Blink/Shape Earth spells to travel to the Beacon.
*   **Well of Light Expedition:** Descend into the Well of Light, potentially performing the Litany of Light to assist with detecting secrets. The company plans to follow Yamki's guidance to locate the entrance used by Tikun Thane and his men.
*   **Teleportation Testing:** Test known color combinations (specifically Red, Black, Yellow, Blue, Green, White) on the teleportation circles, starting with the Glory of Thoth map. There is a plan to "war dial" or scry the exits of various combinations.
*   **Goblin Market:** Visit the Goblin Market to retrieve the magical Temrin map (leading to the gold Rudishva access card). The company may attempt to use Larel's Cloak for invisibility and Blink through the market's secret door.
