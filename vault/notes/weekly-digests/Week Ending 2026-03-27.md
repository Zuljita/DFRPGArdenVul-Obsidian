### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The History of Frigg:** According to *The Highly Impressive Story of Frigg the Fabulous*, Frigg was an Imperial Goblin wizard, an apprentice of Vul, and a renowned slayer of trolls, ogens, and giant worms.
*   **Magery Distribution:** The text *Canticle of Arcane Knowledge* (by Priscus Pulcher) posits that Magery is evenly distributed throughout the population rather than being restricted to the nobility.
*   **Alchemical Reagents:** *Practical Animal Magic* serves as a compendium of the properties of various animal parts, useful for potion brewing and item creation.
*   **The Krakteros Lineage:** Documents found in recent loot suggest Lucius Krakteros had a family in Arden Vul roughly 1200 years ago, though his identity as "Lucius Kruk" remains unproven.
*   **Elf Wizard Rumors:** Carousing intelligence revealed an elf wizard attempting to sell Arden Vul secrets to other parties, but the transaction failed due to price disagreements.

### World Rulings
*   **Returning Javelin Mechanics:** The javelin returns to the owner's hand at Move 12. It can be used every turn if thrown at a range of 12 yards or less, but it does not return quickly enough to facilitate multiple attacks in a single turn. If the javelin is dropped or disarmed, it will attempt to return to its owner unless intercepted by an enemy. If the owner's hand is already full upon the javelin's return, the item falls to the ground. Vallium tested this by throwing the javelin 25+ yards and readying a sword; the javelin returned and fell to the ground because his hand was occupied.
*   **Gauntlet of Flaming Fury:** The fire damage provided by this gauntlet applies only while a weapon is held in the hand wearing the gauntlet; the flames extinguish if the weapon is thrown.
*   **Efreeti Sand:** This black sand, weighing approximately 2 lbs, acts as a grenade-like substance that dispels creatures from the Elemental Plane of Fire back to their home plane for one year.
*   **Ring of One Wish:** The wish contained within this ring is a true *Wish* spell.
