# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for R/R Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-12-19  
**Subject:** Aftermath of the Tower of the Ape and the Cost of Resurrection

### Summary
The company has entered a period of mourning and transition following the heavy losses sustained during the recent engagement at the Tower of the Ape. With Uvash Edzuson deceased, focus has shifted toward the logistical and financial burdens of resurrection, the evaluation of tactical performance, and the potential restructuring of the company's roster. A session recap blog post was published by the GM, detailing the events of the Tower of the Ape.
