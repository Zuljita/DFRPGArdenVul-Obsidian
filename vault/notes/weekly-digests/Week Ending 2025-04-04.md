**Subject:** The Great Liquidation, Adventuring Licenses, and Factional Diplomacy

### 1. Summary
The company experienced a period of unprecedented prosperity following a massive haul of treasure, leading to a strategic decision to liquidate most assets for gold and experience. Amidst the management of new adventuring licenses, the party also navigated the delicate politics of local factions and prepared for future magical augmentations.

### 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **Beastmen Territory:** The beastmen factions have established clear boundaries; they have shown no immediate hostility, merely informing the company of the edges of their territory.
*   **Halfling Vulnerabilities:** Due to their size, halflings are noted to be particularly susceptible to the effects of *Glue* spells.
*   **Diplomatic Strategy:** The company remains undecided on taking a side in the beastmen/halfling conflict, noting that supporting a faction that loses may leave the company vulnerable to the victor.

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Loot Valuation & Exchange:** Gems sell at 100% value, while jewelry sells at 40%. The mercenary "Coinbase" provides a 70% sale value into the company pool.
*   **Experience Point (XP) Acquisition:** Experience is earned primarily through the sale of treasure to vendors. While some magic items provide partial XP if retained, it is significantly less than the value gained from selling them. Magic armor is considered common in this campaign but not valuable enough to justify retaining it over XP.
*   **Invisibility & Defenses:** Active defenses, such as sacrificial parries or blocks, do not break the effect of *Invisibility*, provided the user does not convert their stance to an aggressive one. Aggressive parry is generally considered optional, with exceptions for supernatural innate attacks.
*   **Vision & Spells:** The *Water Vision* spell is capable of seeing through the effects of *Fog* spells.
*   **Racial Statistics:** Racial-specific attributes (such as HP, Perception, or Lifting Strength) cannot be converted into full base statistics; for example, Goblin stats are inherently tied to their IQ and HT unless a specific disadvantage is taken.
*   **Availability of Goods:** Potion belts and prepared potions are not available for purchase in town unless acquired through a successful availability roll during play.
*   **Adventuring Licenses:** Adventuring License Bronze Tokens can be added to a character's sheet upon purchase; these items have a weight of 0.
*   **Spell Utility:** *Levitate* and *Seek Earth* are noted as providing significant advantages. *Seek Earth* has been automated by the GM.
*   **Shield Spell:** The DB from the *Shield* spell does not count for sacrificial defenses, though the spell itself may assist with Block.

### 5. Tactical Planning
*   **Future Spell Priorities:** The company is considering investing in *Concussion*, *Seek Magic*, *Analyze Magic*, *Glue*, and *Blink*. Long-term goals include reaching the threshold for higher-level spells like *Wall of Lightning*. *Levitate* is also a high priority.
*   **Logistical Procurement:** The company intends to purchase iron pikes, longer ropes, and improved solutions for carrying loot.
*   **Factional Strategy:** There is ongoing discussion regarding whether to leverage the beastmen as allies in potential future conflicts with halfling groups, though no formal stance has been taken.

### 6. Unresolved Threads
*   **Defensive Queries:** Whether the *Shield* spell provides benefits to sacrificial defenses (specifically Block) remains unconfirmed by the GM.
*   **Upcoming Threats:** The party anticipates a significant combat encounter in the near future, though the nature of the enemy is unknown.
*   **Diplomatic Direction:** The company has yet to formally decide on a stance regarding the ongoing tension between the beastmen and halfling factions.
