# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for R/R Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-10-10  
**Subject:** The Halfling Strike and the Prophet of Set

### Summary
The company executed a surgical strike against halfling territories, resulting in the surrender of their leader, Plumthorn, and the deaths of Roskelly and a local wizard. Concurrently, town activities revealed a new lead regarding a goblin prophet named Gislu near the Forum of Set, who seeks an ancient artifact. The company also acquired a *Potion of Frost Cage* and confirmed details regarding King Weskenim’s request for the "Scepter of the Goblins."

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Prophet Gislu:** A goblin prophet named Gislu has been spotted near the Forum of Set. He is reportedly searching for an ancient goblin artifact and is certain the object is located near the shrine of Set.
*   **The Scepter of the Goblins:** King Weskenim has requested the recovery of the "Scepter of the Goblins." However, the Wiskin mage Trillip, who provided the information, was unsure if the artifact is indeed a scepter or another type of object.

### World Rulings
