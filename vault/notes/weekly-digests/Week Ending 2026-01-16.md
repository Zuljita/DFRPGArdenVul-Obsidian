# Weekly Chronicle: The Lacrymosa Company
**Date:** Week ending 2026-01-16  
**Subject:** Eye Regeneration, Book Sifting, and the Cult of Arden

### Summary
The company endured a harrowing week marked by magical trauma and extensive logistical management. Vaelitharon Sunshadow suffered a catastrophic injury when a trap in a magic book from the Secret Library ripped out his eyes, sending them to become a scrying device within Arden Vul. After a frantic search for aid, the company paid Jador the Just $5,000 for Instant Regeneration, restoring Vael’s sight at great cost but avoiding the month-long healing time offered by other clerics.

Following the medical emergency, the company focused on sifting through a massive haul of loot from the Secret Library. Key discoveries included a pair of blue eyeballs preserved in a copper bowl of liquid metal, a magic rope with specific Elvish command words, and bones that allow speaking with the dead once a month. The company also identified the contents of Plotina’s Recipe Book and decided to sell the Golemancy book and Plotina’s Recipe Book after copying their contents, granting two loot points to all members except Ioannes.

Downtime activities yielded significant lore regarding the history of Arden Vul, including the fates of Stamelis and Kel Bormol, and new hypotheses connecting the Cult of Arden to military officials. Vallium and Ioannes advanced their skills and brewed potions, while Vael continued his studies on Rudishvan and sought information on Lankios and Guivrel from Anaximander.

### Events & Actions by Character

**Vaelitharon Sunshadow**
*   **Injury & Recovery:** While examining a magic book from the Secret Library, Vael triggered a trap that ripped out his eyes. He was found by Basil and treated by Ioannes with Stop Bleeding and Major Healing. The company sought clerics for regeneration; Jador the Just offered Instant Regeneration for $5,000, while others offered standard Regeneration for less money but with a one-month healing period. Vael accepted Jador’s service, paying the full cost to regain sight immediately.
*   **New SOP:** Vael established a new standard operating procedure: use *Analyze Magic* and other spells on magical items before touching them, and ensure a cleric is always within yelling range.
*   **Downtime:** Studied Rudishvan with Akla-Chah. Planned to interview Anaximander regarding Lankios, K, and Guivrel.
*   **Equipment:** Eyes restored.

**Vallium Halcyon**
*   **Downtime:** Spent the week in the Beacon sorting books and improving Mithric. Read *Secreta Sanctorum* to improve Mithric (Written) and uncover secrets.
*   **Discoveries from *Secreta Sanctorum*:** Found 7 alleged Secret Names of Thoth (including one already known), gossip on ancient misdeeds of the Ligareon and Vasildon clans, evidence that the Silent Factor was a front for the Imperial Private House, and a map of underground rooms.
*   **Advancement:** Purchased Broken Mithric (Spoken and Written) due to sufficient study time.
*   **Alchemy:** Successfully brewed an Agility Potion with a discount.

**Ioannes**

They delved halls within the cliff face to replicate the Twelve Labors of Arden, with the entrance located on the hand of the colossus of Arden.

**Uvash**
*   **Downtime:** Attempted to study "Thoth in All His Forms," but found it written in a secret language he could not read. Researched the Sun-Scarred Knights but found no results.

**Plotina**

### Lore & Discoveries

**History of Arden Vul & Thoth**
*   **Stamelis:** A former Chief Librarian of the Arden Vul Chapter who volunteered to become a Head of Thoth to serve as a Librarian Emeritus. He served through the Civil War, was mistakenly left behind during the evacuation, and was eventually found by Gerrilad.
*   **Kel Bormol:** The Overseer of Thoth, an archmage who rarely visited the library and ruled through fear. He disappeared unexpectedly during the Civil War; Stamelis suspected he may have been murdered or teleported away.
*   **Ligareon Family:** One of the Five Families, described as "super important" in modern times.
*   **Cult of Arden:** An elite cult of Arden devotees existed among military officials in Arden Vul. They built their own headquarters in the Halls and attempted to replicate the Twelve Labors of Arden. The entrance is located on the hand of the colossus of Arden on the cliff face.

**Magic Items & Loot**
*   **The Blinding Book:** A blue leather book with a gold lidless eye. It contains about 10 pages of crystalline material. Looking at a blank page triggers a trap that rips out the viewer's eyes and sends them to become a scrying device for that page. The first few pages show images of sarcophagi, a puppet theater, piles of treasure, a large human in red robes, and a room with beds and a demon statue.
*   **Eyeball Discovery:** A copper bowl containing a pair of blue eyeballs in liquid metal (likely mercury) was found in the same room as the books.
*   **Magic Bones:** Allow the holder to speak with the spirit of the dead person once a month. The protection effect only works if the bones are actively held reverently in both hands.
*   **Magic Rope:** An elven rope that ties/unties itself on command. Command words are "tie" and "untie, untie, untie, yes, I'm sure, untie" in Elvish.
*   **Code Scroll:** Contains color abbreviations: YE-BL-RE-GR-BK-WH (Yellow, Blue, Red, Green, Black, White).
*   **Plant Speech Scroll:** Usable once by clerics, but not learnable by them (only druids can learn it).
*   **Maps:** A new map was found in the *Secreta Secretorum*. It connects to the pentagram room map and the Well of Light area. Two other maps matched existing knowledge (Ruby Chair area and Glory of Thoth area).

### World Rulings

*   **Resurrection Economics:** The GM clarified that resurrection requires a caster, sufficient mana (ceremonial circle/power item), and the body. A "life insurance" service would involve finding the body, retrieving it, and delivering it to a resurrector, likely offered by churches with the necessary capabilities. Noble inheritance laws complicate resurrection for high-status individuals.
*   **Mithric Reading Rates:** Characters with Broken Written Mithric can read one book per week as a downtime activity. Those with Accented/Fluent Mithric can read three per week as a background activity, or seven if it is their sole activity.
*   **Cult of Arden Location:** The headquarters of the Cult of Arden is located on the hand of the colossus of Arden found on the cliff face.

### Tactical Planning

*   **Dragon Strategy:** The company discussed the political implications of killing the dragon. It was noted that the Set faction benefits from the surface being dangerous to drive custom through their baskets. No clear faction benefits from the dragon's death, suggesting the status quo is maintained by those who profit from the danger.
*   **Propaganda:** Ioannes purchased the Propaganda skill to help manage the company's public persona and potentially discredit enemies or attract hirelings.
*   **Next Steps:** The company plans to investigate the Cult of Arden's headquarters on the colossus's hand. They also intend to use the new map from the *Secreta Secretorum* to explore deeper areas of Arden Vul.

### Unresolved Threads

*   **The Blinding Book:** The book that blinded Vael remains unopened. The company has agreed to return to it with appropriate precautions and analysis.
*   **The Eyeballs:** The fate of the blue eyeballs in the copper bowl is undecided.
*   **Anaximander Interview:** Vael plans to interview Anaximander regarding Lankios, K, and Guivrel; the results of this interview are pending.
*   **Sun-Scarred Knights:** Uvash’s research yielded no results, but the nature of this group remains unknown.
*   **Vasildon Family:** The company has not heard of the Vasildon family, despite gossip about their ancient misdeeds.
