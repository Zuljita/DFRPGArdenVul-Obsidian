### 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Eyeless Statue:** The statue resembles "The Philosopher," a classic Archontean archetype.
*   **Red Veil Mushrooms:** Consumption causes a state of "Berserk" that is significantly more severe than standard berserkism.
*   **Onyx & Lyra:** The black cat, Onyx, was once the familiar of an elven wizard named Lyra, who perished at the hands of giant reptiles. Onyx seeks good homes for his feral companions.
*   **Gog:** A half-troll residing in the area; rumors suggest he is intelligent and can be a reliable guide if approached correctly.
*   **Statue Traps:** Statues within the precincts of the ibis-god feature eyes made of huge gemstones; attempting to remove these gems causes the statues to animate and wreak bloody vengeance.
*   **Totem Poles:** Confirmed to be magical in nature.

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Magic & Utility:** Permanent *Continual Light* on large objects holds limited market value, as most adventuring parties possess casters capable of providing the effect for free. It is not worth $8,000 in this world.
*   **Combat Mechanics:** Kiai functions as a Quick Contest where the defense is a resistance roll; therefore, the Rule of 16 applies to the defense roll, not the Kiai roll itself. *Enhanced Parry (Staff)* only applies to missile weapons if the user also possesses the Weapon Master advantage.
*   **Skills & Nature:** Naturalist and Survival (underground) are functionally interchangeable for identifying subterranean mushrooms and default to each other. Training in Animal Handling (Felines) can be acquired through proximity to a feline without monetary cost.
*   **Finance:** The Silent Factor charges a monthly fee of 1.25% (approximately 0.003% of the total balance per week).

### 5. Tactical Planning
*   The company is debating whether to attempt diplomacy with the half-troll Gog to facilitate easier movement through the Great Caverns or to bypass/neutralize the halfling factions entirely.
*   There is a plan to utilize the cat Onyx to assist in managing the new feline arrivals and potentially finding suitable homes for them.
*   Ioannes intends to prioritize the acquisition of *Gift of Tongues* over *Resist Fire*.
