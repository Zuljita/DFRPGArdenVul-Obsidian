# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Riches Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-06-06  
**Subject:** Liquidating Assets and Spectral Investigations

### 1. Summary
The company successfully liquidated a significant cache of red scale armor through anonymous channels, securing immediate loot experience and much-needed gold. While the party prepares for a shopping excursion to Newmarket, research into local legends has provided grim insights into the nature of spirits and the history of long-lost legions. Preparations for an upcoming encounter with a ghost at the cliffs have been finalized, including the purchase of specific spells and the deployment of mercenary support.

### 2. Events & Actions by Character

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**
*   **Town Actions:** Utilized a contact known as "Petros" (associated with the Benevolent Brotherhood) to facilitate the anonymous sale of red scale armor at the docks, ensuring the company's identity remained hidden from potential observers.
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Successfully brewed a *Greater Healing Potion*; the process was completed at a discounted cost.
*   **Research/Study:** Investigated the nature of ghosts via a text discovered at the Temple of Mitra. Learned that spirits are often tethered to violent deaths or uncompleted tasks, and that turning or destroying them is only temporary; they return until their death is avenged or their body properly buried. The text did not contain a "Final Rest" spell or specific ritual, only that proper burial and avenging are required.
*   **Character Development:** Acquired the *Affect Spirits* spell to prepare for upcoming spectral encounters.

**Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow**

### 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Sheep's Head Rangers:** The silver sheep's head plate is identified as the insignia of the 2nd Legion, a group that was wiped out within Arden Vul approximately 250 years ago (Source: Herald at Azure Keep).
*   **Nature of Ghosts:** Spirits are typically left behind by those who died horribly or with unfinished business. While some are benign, others are highly dangerous; providing a "good" ghost with proper rest is considered a blessed but perilous deed. Crucially, the text found at the Temple of Mitra indicates that ghosts return until their death is avenged or their body properly blessed and buried; it does not offer a simple spell to end their existence.
*   **Specialized Weaponry:** Certain crossbows in the company's possession appear standard in design but are specifically crafted for users of exceptional strength (Source: Torunn).

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Anonymous Sales:** Red scale armor can be sold anonymously through a *Streetwise* roll or by using an *Armory* roll to modify its appearance. Selling items in Gosterwick provides immediate loot XP, whereas traveling to Newmarket for potentially better prices would delay the acquisition of XP until the party returns.
*   **Loot and Experience:** Items sold at 40% value generate loot XP. The company earned a total of 2 XP this week (1 for exploration, 1 for loot). The GM clarified that securing 1 loot XP was contingent only on successfully selling the Set armor, regardless of whether other items were sold or identified.
*   **Skill Acquisition:** Navigational experience within the dungeon does not substitute for formal training in skills such as *Absolute Direction*. Paid training is required to advance the skill.

### 5. Tactical Planning
*   **The Ghost Encounter:** The company intends to confront a ghost near the cliffs. The tactical plan involves casting *Affect Spirits* on Michael first, followed by *Bravery* and *Bless* to mitigate the threat of mental attacks and spectral interference. *Affect Spirits* is a two-second cast, allowing it to be prepared before entering. The GOAT will blanket the area in *Mystic Mist* prior to engagement.
*   **Mercenary Deployment:** Chrysalis will be utilized for her ability to counter large numbers of enemies, a necessity given the reduced party size of three. The GOAT will provide area control.
