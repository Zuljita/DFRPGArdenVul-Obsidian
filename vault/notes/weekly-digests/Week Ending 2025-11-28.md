# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for R/R Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-11-28  
**Subject:** The Sortian Legacy, Apartment Negotiations, and the Arden Legend

### Summary
The company secured a two-year lease on three luxury apartments within the Beacon through complex negotiations with Lyssandra and Pelteon, trading the Golem Book for residency rights. During this period, Uvash Edzuson and Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios uncovered significant historical truths regarding the Empire’s past, the legend of Arden, and the dangers of Sortian artifacts. The company also prepared for an upcoming expedition into Settite territory, commissioning maps and supplies while navigating the logistical challenges of their new living arrangements.

### Events & Actions by Character

**Uvash Edzuson**
*   **Research:** Conducted extensive study of the history of Arden. He learned that Arden was one of the twenty Worthies of the Empire and that she and her partner, the sorcerer Vul, discovered the cavern systems of the Halls of Arden Vul. Arden is credited with twelve Labors, including the defeat of owlbears, trolls, undead, dragons, and Wiskin berserkers. While only eleven Labors are well-documented, the twelfth is described as "still to be done at the time of the Empire's Need." Uvash also noted that a Cult of Arden existed among military officials, who allegedly built headquarters in the Halls and attempted to replicate these Labors. He confirmed the historical claim that Arden and Vul disappeared mysteriously at the height of their power, with their bodies never found.
*   **Town Actions:** None this week.
*   **Equipment Changes:** None.

### Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Legend of Arden:** Arden and the sorcerer Vul discovered the cavern systems of the Halls of Arden Vul; Arden is credited with twelve Labors, including the defeat of trolls and dragons. The twelfth Labor is "still to be done at the time of the Empire's Need." A Cult of Arden attempted to replicate these Labors (Source: Research by Uvash).
*   **The Sortian Rebellion:** The Sortians, led by Priscus Pulcher, rebelled against the established magical order, leading to a devastating civil war that shrunk the Empire's borders. The Empire survived, but spell use was eventually opened up (Source: Library of Mitra research by Ioannes).
*   **Sortian Statues:** These magical constructs can be activated via specific eyes placed in their sockets to perform tasks such as passing messages or teleportation. They also have evil effects, such as causing nearby children to disobey their parents. The author advises funding a mission to find and destroy any surviving statues (Source: Library of Mitra research by Ioannes).
*   **Rudishva Funeral Rites:** The preferred methods are Full Recycling and Spacing, though the Beacon currently lacks the facilities for either. Cremation is a viable, if difficult, alternative, requiring the means to build a suitably hot fire outside the Beacon (Source: Akla-Chah).

### World Rulings
*   **The Apartment Agreement:** During the renovation of the APC apartments, the company must compress all belongings into two existing units. The lease grants rights to three luxury apartments upon completion. Lyssandra and Pelteon retain final say over building renovations. Lyssandra has first call on spell scrolls sold while the company is tenanted. Pelteon has visitation rights to all cats on the premises.
*   **Merchant Factors (Gosterwick):** There are three primary storage factors: the Rarities Factor (cheapest), the Prosperity Factor (medium cost), and the Silent Factor (most expensive/secure).
*   **Book Procurement:** Basic instructional books typically cost between $10 and $50. A budget of $1,000 can secure approximately fifteen volumes. $10,000 exceeds the current stock of local shops like Astableon.
*   **Alchemy Interference:** The casting of *Purify Air* during an active alchemical process can cause the attempt to fail and incur extra costs.

### Tactical Planning
The company intends to execute a mission through the following route:
1.  Utilize Rugs of Instant Access to travel to the Beacon.
2.  Descend via the spiral staircase in the northeast area of the Well of Light (Option B) to reach the Glory of Thoth. Option D (flying through the Great Chasm) was explicitly rejected for this trip as it requires testing first.
3.  Visit the Well of Light to deliver food to the apes and consult Gog regarding the Court of the Troll Thegn.
4.  Proceed to the Goblin Market to retrieve a commissioned map from Temrin.
5.  Follow Temrin's map through Settite territory, prepared for potential encounters with the Set Cult, with the ultimate goal of recovering Nyema's necklace or corpse.
