# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Ruala Company
**Date:** Week ending 2025-07-04  
**Subject:** Loot Distribution, Alchemical Failures, and the Stirge Fever Outbreak

### 1. Summary
The company managed the spoils of the recent expedition to Gerrilad, successfully distributing wealth and magic items among the party and the Grudge Brigade. While the division of wealth proceeded without major incident, Vallium Halcyon suffered a failure in brewing a *Potion of Fire Resistance*. On the town front, Vallium attempted to locate a Potion Belt and conducted a high-stakes Carousing roll that yielded a private rumor. In the dungeon, the mercenary Thronebreaker contracted Stirge Fever following a close call with the creatures, leaving his fate uncertain.

*   **Toy Bed Durability:** The GM ruled that the toy bed is delicate; using it as a weapon risks breakage. The *Shatterproof* spell acts more as "Shatter-resistance" than absolute immunity in this context. (Source: GM Ruling).

### 4. World Rulings
*   **Concussion Missile:** This missile creates a ball of highly pressurized air. It possesses Acc 1, deals 1/2D 20 damage, and has a maximum blast radius of 10. Upon impact, it inflicts crushing explosive damage. Anyone within a radius equal to the dice of damage (up to 10) must roll against HT at -1 per die (to a maximum of HT-3) to avoid physical stun. Hearing protection provides its bonus to this roll; earplugs/similar items grant +1 to hearing rolls, up to a maximum of +5. This is classified as a Sound spell. The cost is any amount from 2 to twice the caster's level per second for three seconds. Casting time is 1 to 3 seconds.
*   **Amulet of Missile Shield:** This item functions as an "always-on" enchantment.
*   **Shatterproof Spell:** When applied to delicate objects (such as a magic toy bed), the *Shatterproof* spell acts more as "Shatter-resistance" rather than absolute immunity to breakage.
*   **Attribute Purchases:** It is permissible to purchase points of HT as standalone increments (e.g., +1 FP, +0.25 Basic Speed) provided they are not part of a racial package.
