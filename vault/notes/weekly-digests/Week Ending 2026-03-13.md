### Lore & Discoveries
*   **Cult Demographics:** The Cult of Set comprised over one hundred members, specifically distinguished as guards and cultists.
*   **Cult Security Structure:** The cult maintained a security ratio of approximately two guards for every one slave.
*   **Lifestone Presence:** Lifestone was confirmed to be present within the Cult of Set's headquarters.
*   **Refugee Disposition:** Observations suggest that rescued individuals have varied desires; some wish to return to their homes, while others prefer to resettle nearby.

### World Rulings
*   **Legal Status of Cultist Property:** Vaelitharon Sunshadow specifically asked the GM whether slaves taken and owned by the illegal Cult of Set are considered free persons or property under local law. The GM ruled that Vael does not know the answer, establishing that this information is not available to the character in-world.

### Tactical Planning
*   **Refugee Evacuation:** The company intends to devise a method for the safe evacuation of former slaves from the Forum of Set and their subsequent transport to town.
*   **Resettlement Strategy:** Zuljita proposed offering a sizable grant (a few hundred bucks) to help refugees restart their lives, noting that simply releasing them in town may be irresponsible without further aid.
*   **Refugee Housing Project:** The company is considering requesting land from Lady Alexia to construct a dedicated refugee house. Vaelitharon Sunshadow has proposed using the *Shape Earth* spell to expedite construction if such land is granted.
*   **Integration:** One proposed plan involves offering the liberated individuals positions within the newly established regime at the Forum of Riches/Rarities.

### Unresolved Threads
*   **Legal Ambiguity:** The exact legal status of the liberated slaves under local law remains unknown to Vaelitharon Sunshadow.
*   **Census of the Liberated:** The precise number of slaves successfully liberated remains unconfirmed, though estimates suggest between 25 and 50 individuals based on the known guard-to-slave ratio.
*   **Logistical Coordination:** It remains undecided whether a member of the company should be sent ahead to town to prepare for the arrival of the refugees.
