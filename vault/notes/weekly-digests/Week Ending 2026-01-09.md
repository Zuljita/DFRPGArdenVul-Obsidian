# Weekly Chronicle: The Right for Rises Company
**Date:** Week ending 2026-01-09
**Subject:** Loot Distribution, Dragon Threats, and Expansion of the Beacon Annex

### Summary
The company concluded its recent expedition with the distribution of significant loot, including magical armor, scrolls, and rare artifacts. Vael purchased a Refrigeration Rock for the Beacon annex, while the party decided to retain one Smokeless Torch for utility. Extensive planning was undertaken regarding an upcoming confrontation with a dragon, including reconnaissance strategies and potential summoning of the Avatar of Bastet. In town, Vael successfully expanded the Beacon annex, Vallium learned Broken Mithric and discovered the "Song of Arden," and Ioannes researched the nature of Secret Names.

### Events & Actions by Character

**Vael**
*   **Town & Construction:** Purchased a Refrigeration Rock from Creon for $10,000 (paid solo). Commissioned a signet ring design from Chairduster for $100. Inquired about magical air purifiers and water sources; Creon offered to notify him if such items appear.
*   **Beacon Annex:** Successfully expanded the annex using Shape Earth, adding a larder, privy, and hallway without causing collapses or flooding. Sent bricks to the town via Basil and received correspondence from Freydis, who directed future donation inquiries to the thesmothete, Eusebia Phokas.
*   **Alchemy & Research:** Brewed a Potion of Gaseous Form. Reverse-engineered an Ice Cage Potion (purchased on clearance for $500, retail $2,000) to acquire the recipe for "Potion of Frost Cage" for $200. Consulted Bombur Mica regarding ventilation; Mica refused to work in dragon territory due to his young children but advised on semi-porous rock filters. Learned additional Rudishvan glyphs, noting the difficulty of the full written language and the existence of a simplified "Semi-Glyphik."
*   **Equipment:** Charged new Spider Silk Robes via Pelteon.

**Vallium**
*   **Research & Learning:** Spent downtime assisting Basil with reading materials for the Ardenpedia golems. Learned Broken Mithric (Spoken and Writing). Researched the "Song of Arden," finding lyrics: *"Fear is the destroyer, it bites in the heart. Fear brings me low, makes me writhe in the dirt. Strength counters fear, pushes it deep in its hole. Strength is my strength, let it fill all my soul."*
*   **Alchemy:** Attempted to brew a Potion of Flight but failed, resulting in extra costs.

**Uvash**
*   **Research:** Researched the Feathers of Maat. Found a passage from an obscure human cult stating that holding a Feather with faith protects against Wights, Specters, and Vampires, but dropping it or losing faith leads to death. The origin of the cult remains ambiguous.
*   **Planning:** Discussed potential upgrades to Divine Grace and Divine Might.

**Ioannes**
*   **Research:** Researched Secret Names. Found that they are primarily used for demons and devils to grant power over them; gods do not appear to have secret names. Recalled a cautionary tale of a paladin of Mitra who was killed after using a false secret name on a demon.
*   **Alchemy:** Brewed a major healing potion.

**Basil**
*   **Logistics:** Took $100 for food shopping to stock the new Beacon larder. Transported bricks to the town via the Rug.

**Pelteon**
*   **Services:** Charged Vael’s new robes.

### Lore & Discoveries

**Loot Analysis**
*   **Magically Lightened Scale Armor:** Discussed for potential use; determined it would not fit Uvash (dwarf) and should be sold.
*   **Smokeless Torches:** Kept one for use as a dehumidifier, backup light, and water heater in the Beacon. The other was sold.
*   **Statuette of Feline Friendship:** Allows the summoning of the Avatar of Bastet once a month during the full moon. The next full moon is 11 Fidios. Ioannes expressed interest in gifting it to Pelteon.
*   **Feathers of Maat:** Not well known in town. Uvash’s research revealed their protective properties against undead.
*   **Scroll of the Floating Sage:** Non-margical. Text reads: *"To operate the floating sage, one must slowly and clearly declaim the first principle of epistemology."* Marginalia reads: *"Dubito, ergo cogito, ergo sum"* ("I doubt, therefore I think, therefore I am").
*   **Map:** Depicts a path through underground rooms to an 'X' in unfamiliar terrain.
*   **Wizard’s Staff Club:** Confirmed as a fine, balanced club base item that functions as a wizard's staff.
*   **Salamander Skins:** GM ruled they are mostly useful for making shoes; attempts to start a fashion trend failed.
*   **Lost Key:** Ioannes lost a key to a secret door he previously used; Vallium berated him for the loss.

**World Rulings & Mechanics**
*   **Vampiric Bite:** Does not work on undead, constructs, or machines.
*   **Divine Grace:** Upgrading this trait costs money for training/prayer.
*   **Potions on Large Creatures:** Treating potions as spells on larger creatures costs more and may result in reduced effects or better resistance chances. Shrink Potion resistance is R-HT.
*   **Potion of Frost Cage:** Freezes the target in place; requires an ST roll to break free, with a +1 bonus per subsequent turn.
*   **Jewelry Pricing:** Can be purchased for almost any amount between $10 and $10,000.
*   **Robes:** Vael’s new robes were ready halfway through the week.
*   **Rudishvan:** The full written language is extremely difficult; Semi-Glyphik is a simplified subset used by most Varumani and Varuda.
*   **Gnomes:** No gnomes exist in Arden Vul; only one inventor has been met so far.

**Secret Library & Ruby Throne**
*   **Inscription:** The Ruby Throne bears the inscription: *"Speak the secret name and claim thy reward."*
*   **Letter from Hellas:** Found correspondence addressed to Neferet, high priestess. Hellas refused to relocate the special library to Neferet’s new residence, citing the public nature of the entrance and the risk of exposing Thoth’s heritage. He mentioned Hithra the Paleologue agrees with him and noted Neferet may have already claimed the Circlet from the archon.

### Tactical Planning

**Dragon Confrontation**
*   **Meeting Date:** The party informed Count Skleros they would meet in 7 days (11 Fidios), coinciding with the full moon.
*   **Strategy:** The consensus is to avoid fighting the dragon on the surface. Plans include cornering the dragon in its lair or treasure hoard to negate flight advantages.
*   **Reconnaissance:** Discussed using illusory reconnaissance or Potion of Clairvoyance ($500) to scout the dragon. Commissioning a map from Temrin for the dragon’s hoard was considered.
*   **Combat Prep:**
    *   **Flight:** Need to negate dragon flight. Discussed using Salamander Amulets, Potions of Resist Fire, and potentially Flight Potions for the party.
    *   **Breath Weapons:** Prepared for potential acid or poison gas, in addition to fire.
    *   **Wyverns:** Discussed the possibility of facing multiple wyverns; questioned if any have been seen together before.
    *   **Golems:** Planned to use Ardenpedia golems (Stamelis, Akla-Chah, Anaximander) to research dragon-bane items or Sorian dragon weaknesses.
    *   **Bastet Summoning:** Discussed summoning the Avatar of Bastet for aid, requiring fresh fish and Onyx. Considered whether to perform the ritual in town or outside.

**Beacon Ventilation**
*   Discussed installing vents in the Beacon. Bombur Mica’s refusal to work in dragon territory left the ventilation plan pending. Discussed hiring a congregant or using semi-porous rock filters.
