# Weekly Chronicle: Arden Vul
**Date:** Week ending 2025-06-27

## 1. Summary
The company spent the week engaged in various alchemical pursuits and the investigation of recent spoils, though progress was hampered by measurement errors and a much-needed period of rest for some members. While new rumors have surfaced concerning alternative routes into the depths of the dungeon via the "Shining Well," the party remains focused on preparing for upcoming expeditions into the Great Cavern and toward Gog's area.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Shining Well:** A rumor suggests that because the primary entrance via the Pyramid of Thoth is frequently picked clean by other adventurers, a "Shining Well" may provide an alternative route to significant treasure (Source: Vallium’s Carousing roll).
* **Scorpion Statue:** While direct research into the teleporting nature of the Scorpion Statue yielded no results, secondary information regarding related phenomena was uncovered (Source: Ioannes' Research).

## 4. World Rulings
* **The Concussion Spell:** The mechanics of the Concussion spell are established as follows: damage is calculated at 1d per 2FP; the initial stun penalty is HT minus the number of damage dice, but this penalty is capped at a maximum of -3. Recovery from the resulting stun requires a straight HT roll (not a modified one). Casters with Magery 4 or higher may exceed the cap on damage dice, though the HT penalty remains capped at -3.
* **Identification:** Items identified by the company include a gold dwarf statue and a magic shortsword. The party discussed potentially skipping identification for the "eye" and "statue head," as their destination is already known.

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **Balthazar's Staff:** Now serves as a bonded weapon for Chrysalis.
* **The Eye & The Statue Head:** These items are of significant importance; while their exact properties remain unverified, the company has determined they must be returned to a specific, known location.
* **Scorpion Statue:** Known to possess teleportation capabilities, making it an unpredictable element in the dungeon environment.
* **Recent Loot:** Recent finds include boots, gems, a necklace, and coins.

## 6. Tactical Planning
* **Expedition Goals:** The company is considering routes through the Great Cavern or toward Gog's area, with the intent of avoiding halfling populations. There is also a potential plan to investigate the Well of Light for ape-like entities.
* **Resource Management:** It has been proposed that Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios acquire the Levitation spell to assist in navigating high-altitude or difficult terrain. The party utilizes a mule to assist with weight management.
* **Financial Strategy:** A discussion was held regarding the necessity of a party member investing in the Wealth advantage to increase the gold-to-XP conversion rate for future loot.
