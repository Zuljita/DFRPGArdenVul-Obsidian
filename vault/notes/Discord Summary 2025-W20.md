# Weekly Chronicle: The Company of the Long Stair
**Week Ending 23rd of May, 2993 AE**

## 1. Summary
Following a period of significant loot distribution and the acquisition of new assets, the company has entered a phase of intense preparation and debate regarding future expeditions. While research into local halfling factions has proven fruitless, the naming of a new logistical asset—a mule—and discussions regarding heavy armor upgrades have dominated the recent sessions. The company has successfully secured enough valuables (primarily tapestries and mirrors) to earn 1 XP.

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

### Uvash Edzarysson
* **Town Actions:** Evaluated the necessity of upgrading defensive equipment; specifically considering the purchase of a fine mail shirt and a helmet to mitigate the risk of cranial injuries. He is also weighing the option of wearing heavy leather armor currently held in the party's loot possessions, noting that a full suit of fine heavy leather costs 4,500 gold and weighs 27 lbs.
* **Equipment Changes:** Currently assessing funds (estimated at 10,000 gold) for the acquisition of heavy armor.

### Vallium Halcyon
* **Town Actions:** Actively participated in the strategic planning of upcoming expeditions, weighing the merits of various dungeon sectors.
* **Tactical Planning:** Proposed several routes for the next excursion, including the Great Cavern, an assault on halfling territory, or exploration of Gog’s neighborhood. He plans to consume strength and agility potions at the start of combat during the halfling engagement.

### Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios
* **Alchemy and Crafting:** Successfully brewed a potion of major healing at a discounted rate.
* **Research Results:** Conducted research into Roskelly and the "Doomed Bunch" of Halflings; however, no actionable intelligence was recovered from this endeavor.
* **Town Actions:** Planning the procurement of additional potions (specifically strength and agility) for himself and the party to bolster combat efficacy.

### Vaelitharon Sunshadow
* **Research/Analysis:** Utilized *Analyze Magic* and consulted with the Apothecary/Alchemist Professional Center (APC) to investigate a recently recovered shield. 
* **Town Actions:** Planning to procure additional potions for himself.
* **Equipment Changes:** Identified specific vulnerabilities in the party's new acquisitions.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
* **The Drowned Canyon:** Local rumors persist regarding the existence of a canyon filled with immense wealth, though many seasoned adventurers dismiss the location as mere myth.
* **Cult of Thoth:** It is known that the priests of Thoth constructed numerous statues in various forms, including ibis and baboon heads. These statues feature large gemstones for eyes; it is whispered that removing these gems allows a bearer to communicate directly with the god Thoth.

## 4. World Rulings
* **Equipment Weight:** A medium metal shield possesses a weight of 30 lbs.
* **Skill Requirements:** The acquisition of the *Animal Handling (Equines)* skill requires formal training; however, significant experience leading pack animals may contribute to this progress.
* **Weapon Specialties:** Within the *Fast-Draw (Stick)* specialty, "stick" refers specifically to the boomerang.
* **Combat Mechanics:** The *Power Blow* technique is strictly limited to momentary applications (one second) and cannot be used for sustained tasks such as heavy lifting.
* **Geography:** The verticality of the local cliff face is established at 1,500 feet (equivalent to 500 hexes).

## 5. Item Intelligence
* **The Recovered Shield:** 
    * **Properties:** A heavy metal shield weighing 30 lbs.
    * **Revealed Information:** While highly effective against most standard threats, the shield possesses a notable weakness: it is significantly less effective when facing demons or devils. (Source: Vaelitharon Sunshadow via APC consultation).
    * **Identification Cost:** The cost to identify the magical properties of this item was 100 gold.

* **Inventorium Burdenus Mulex ("Bernie"):**
    * **Role:** The company's primary pack animal and "Czar of Logistics."
    * **Status:** Currently carrying a significant portion of the company's recovered loot.

## 6. Tactical Planning
The company is currently debating several potential courses of action for the upcoming week:
* **Great Cavern Expedition:** An exploration of the Great Cavern via the Long Stair, potentially utilizing the "Fish Rod" and bringing supplies to barter with beastmen.
* **Halfling Assault:** A coordinated strike against halfling territory. The plan involves using *Invisibility* on the entire party to bypass the portcullis on foot. To ensure success, the company may utilize a "ram"—specifically the abilities of the mercenary Thronebreaker—to destroy a wall or door. There is also a proposal to approach via the chasm for extreme surprise value.
* **Gog’s Neighborhood:** Sneaking through the lower levels to explore more of the area surrounding Gog.
* **The Forum of Set:** A potential visit to the Forum of Set.
* **Scorpion Statue:** A suggestion was made to "roll the dice" by testing the teleportation properties of the scorpion statue.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
* **The Portcullis Status:** It remains unconfirmed whether the portcullis in the halfling sector is currently raised or lowered.
* **Mercenary Recruitment:** The company has not yet decided which mercenary to employ for the next mission (options include Thronebreaker, a specialist in demolition, or others).
* **The One-Way Door:** There is an unverified report of a "one-way door" in the dungeon; the party has yet to investigate if any new loot has appeared behind it or if previous chalk markings remain.
* **Hallway Exploration:** The hallway adjacent to the aforementioned one-way door remains unexplored.
