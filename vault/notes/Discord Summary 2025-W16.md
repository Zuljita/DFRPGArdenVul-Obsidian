# Weekly Knowledge Base: The Right for Riches Company
**Week Ending:** 2025-04-25

## 1. Summary
The company focused this week on the formalization of its legal structure through the drafting of a new charter and the management of significant recent spoils. While much of the week was spent in administrative oversight and loot identification, research into local hauntings provided grim insights into the bloody history of the Adrienic Expedition. The company also earned 4 XP (1 exploration, 2 loot, 1 achievement).

## 2. Events & Actions by Character

**Uvash Edzuson**
*   **Town Actions:** Participated in the company vote regarding the method for opening a trapped chest, ultimately supporting the use of magical services over thieves to ensure the security of the contents.

**Vallium Halcyon**
*   **Town Actions:** Engaged in the identification and valuation of recent acquisitions. 
*   **Equipment Changes:** Involved in discussions regarding the optimal distribution of the Ring of Free Action, Belt of Strength +1, and Ring of Protection among the company's frontline combatants.

**Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios**
*   **Alchemy/Crafting:** Attempted to brew a Minor Healing potion; the attempt was unsuccessful, resulting in a 10% increase in the cost of materials used.
*   **Research:** Conducted an investigation into the spirit haunting the tower at the base of the waterfall. The research uncovered records that the tower was built by Archon Adrienic and sent by Emperor Phocion IV as part of an attempt to retake Arden Vul, which ended in a massacre approximately 250 years ago.
*   **Equipment Changes:** None recorded this week.

**Vaelitharon "Vael" Sunshadow**
*   **Town Actions:** Acted as the company's primary identifier for recent loot, earning 700 gold in fees for his services and saving the party 500 gold in potential costs.
*   **Company Work:** Drafted the *Lex Societatis Aureae*, a formal company charter outlining the laws of the "Right for Riches Company," including articles on membership, the division of spoils, and the appointment of officers such as the Loot Czar and Archivist.

**Mercenary: Coinbase**
*   **Status:** Identified as a potential hire for upcoming engagements to bolster the company's magical capabilities.

## 3. Lore & Discoveries
*   **The Tower Ghost:** The tower at the base of the waterfall was constructed during the Adrien and Vul expedition, sent by Emperor Phocion IV, roughly two and a half centuries ago. It was part of an attempt to retake Arden Vul, which ended in a bloody massacre. The resident spirit is known to accuse "Settite murderers" (Source: Research by Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios).
*   **The Book of Priors:** A collection of six volumes written in Mithric, spanning the years 937 AEP to 1782 AEP. It contains a comprehensive history of Arden Vul, including the construction of the Great Pyramid of Thoth and various wars (Source: GM).
*   **The Varumani (Trolls):** A race of large, intelligent, and regenerative creatures that can only be killed by fire or acid. They were defeated by Marius Tricotor in 1043 AEP and are now loyal Imperial subjects (Source: The Book of Empris/Book of Priors).
*   **The Heqeti (Hoppers):** An evil, amphibious race including intelligent mages and demon-anti-priests. Following their defeat by Marius Tricotor, the survivors retreated into the Underearth (Source: The Book of Empris/Book of Priors).

## 4. World Rulings
*   **Loot Valuation:** Silver bars are valued at 100% of their loot value (equivalent to large coins); gold statues are valued at 40% of their loot value.
*   **Selling Rates:** The company is currently selling items at a rate of 40% of their value.
*   **Expense Accounting:** Any items lost or given away during an expedition (such as gifts to NPCs) are recorded as company expenses at their full Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP).
*   **Attribute Consolidation:** Certain advantages can be consolidated into higher attributes (e.g., Striking ST, Lifting ST, and HP may be folded into +1 HT), provided the conversion is a clear-cut case and does not involve skills or violate disadvantage limits. Points cannot be taken from skills to be moved into attributes.
*   **Ring of Free Action:** This item allows the wearer to drop a regular backpack as a free action, though it provides no direct mitigation for encumbrance.
*   **Procurement:** The company may check local pawn shops in-play for items such as healer's kits.

## 5. Item Intelligence
*   **Gold Disks with Grooves:** These items were recovered from the large hoard within Prior Jacobus's tomb. They are worth $200 each but required an identification cost of $150 per disk (one instance involved paying an NPC after Vael was uncertain).
*   **Trapped Chest:** A chest containing over $13,000 was discovered. The trap was triggered by touching the lock. The caster Lyssandra successfully used *Lockmaster* to secure it, but has warned that any future chests brought from across a wall will incur higher fees due to the increased risk of remote tampering.
*   **Orange Robes:** These items were identified as having no significant value for sale or identification.

## 6. Tactical Planning
*   **Logistics & Storage:** The company is discussing the establishment of a permanent "party hall" or secure storage facility (potentially through the Rifters/Rarities Factor) to house essential gear, potions, and heavy equipment, rather than relying on temporary rented rooms.
*   **Equipment Deployment:** Plans are being made to ensure that high-utility items—specifically the Ring of Free Action, Belt of Strength +1, and Ring of Protection—are actively worn by appropriate members during every session.

## 7. Unresolved Threads
*   **The Settite Accusation:** The identity and motive of the "Settite murderers" mentioned by the tower ghost remain unknown.
*   **The Halfling Extortionist:** A rumor persists regarding a halfling named Plummie, who is allegedly extorting "exploration licenses" near the pyramid; the company has yet to decide how to respond to this threat.
*   **Post-Expedition Cleanup:** The party has not yet returned to the tower to search the bedroom for any missed loot after laying the ghost to rest.
*   **Magical Upgrades:** There is no known method or location for purchasing enchantments (such as Accuracy, Deflect, or Fortify) for existing gear.
*   **Charter Legalization:** The new Company Charter requires a formal review by a legal professional to ensure its validity under Archontean law.
*   **Siege Feasibility:** The feasibility of using the *Shatter* spell on portcullises or treasure chests remains uninvestigated.
