## 4. World Rulings
*   **Deflect Damage Bonus (DB):** A character receives the best physical shield DB applicable to the attack, plus the single best magical DB from a source such as a/an shield or spell. Multiple sources of magical DB do not stack; only the highest value applies.
*   **Damage Reduction (DR):** All physical armor DR on a specific hit location stacks. However, magical Fortify/Armor effects do not stack; only the single best source of magical DR applies to that location.
*   **Spell Stacking:** Spells that boost different attributes—such as *Grace* (+DX), *Reflexes* (+Combat Reflexes), *Haste* (+Dodge), and *Shield* (+DB)—do stack. Conversely, spells like *Heroic Might* do not stack with physical Strength-boosting items (e.g., belts or potions).
*   **Scrolls:** Unless explicitly stated otherwise, scrolls are unpowered.
*   **Logistics of the Long Stair:** The Long Stair is too steep and winding for wagons; transporting a wagon up this route requires extreme magic. It is steep enough to necessitate falling checks.
*   **Mule Costs:** A small mule costs $1,000; a large mule costs $2,000.
