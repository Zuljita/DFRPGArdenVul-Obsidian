**World Rulings**
* **Robe of Protection (+4):** Provides inherent DR 1 and Fortify 3 to most of the body, excluding the face, eyes, hands, and feet. This magical DR does not stack with other magic armor or bracers. It is possible to wear a helmet over the robe to utilize the helmet's DR on the head.
* **Armor Stacking Rules:** Magical DR from different sources does not stack. A character receives their best physical DR per location, followed by their best magical DR per location, and finally their best resistance item.
* **Bracers:** These function as forearm armor and are sized to fit the wearer's anatomy.
* **Rudishva Language:** Accented written Rudishva is currently too difficult for the company to acquire or learn.
* **Lesser Wish Scroll:** A powered scroll of *Lesser Wish* allows the caster to apply the effect specifically to a ring, amulet, or jewel.

**Item Intelligence**
* **Robe of Protection +4:** Worn by Vaelitharon Sunshadow. Provides DR 1 and Fortify 3 (excluding extremities). Does not stack with other magical armor.
* **Bracers of Force +3:** Currently unassigned; noted as a potential utility for the mercenary Lacrymosa or for use during delves. They are sized to the wearer.
* **Rudishva Crystal Specimen XRF-6013:** Contains high-value medical footage of "space squid" biological repair.
* **Laser Pistol:** Utilizes small energy cells; requires recharging via the stolen charger from Kerbog Khan.
