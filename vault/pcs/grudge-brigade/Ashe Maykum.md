# Ashe "GOAT" Maykum

## Summary
Ashe "The GOAT" Maykum is an Imperial Goblin druid and inventor. As a member of the Grudge Brigade mercenary company, he is frequently hired by the Right for Riches Company for their expeditions into Arden Vul.

## Personality and Reputation
Ashe has a well-earned reputation as the "goat-summoning druid." He is a reliable and frequently sought-after mercenary who integrates well with the party's operations.

## Skills & Inventions
- **Druid Magic:** Versatile druid famous for summoning goats, bears, and bat swarms
- **Inventor:** Founder of Alcheboretal Animossities
- **Notable Invention:** Living Wheelbarrow (part of his "Shroom for Improvement" prototype project)

## Relationships
- Grudge Brigade: His mercenary company
- Right for Riches Company: A recurring employer
- Camilla: A favored summoned creature

## Notable Actions
- **First Expedition (Session 1):** Hired for the party's very first mission into the ruins
- **Great Cavern Exploration (Session 15):** His summoned bears and bat swarms were instrumental in fighting off local fauna
- **Construction at the Beacon (Session 32):** Helped construct a stone cover over a stairway alongside Vael Sunshadow
