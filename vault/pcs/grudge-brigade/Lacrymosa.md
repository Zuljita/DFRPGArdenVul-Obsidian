# Merenuithiel "Lacrymosa" Armaris

## Summary
Merenuithiel "Lacrymosa" Armaris is a skilled but notoriously aggressive Elven mercenary archer, frequently hired by the Right for Riches Company from the Grudge Brigade. She serves as recurring ranged support and is known for her deadly efficiency in combat and a relentless desire to confront and kill halflings.

## Personality and Reputation
Lacrymosa has earned a reputation for being "bloodthirsty." She is single-minded in her goals, tirelessly advocating for aggressive solutions. This was most evident in her brutal efficiency during the assault on Plumthorn's gang, where she killed stunned, fleeing, or surrendering enemies without pause.

Her defining character trait is a deep-seated animosity towards the extortionist halflings of Phlebotomas Plumthorn, a topic she raises almost every time she is hired.

## Notable Actions
- **First Hiring (Session 4):** First hired from the Grudge Brigade; aided in exploring the Pyramid of Thoth
- **The Great Cavern (Session 15):** Accidentally shot Vallium in the back; later swarmed by stirges
- **The Halfling Takedown (Session 26):** Instrumental in the ambush of Plumthorn's halfling gang; killed Blandveg and Roskelly Winterleaf
- **Official Recognition (Session 32):** Officially recognized by King Weskenim and awarded the title "Halfling Slayer"
