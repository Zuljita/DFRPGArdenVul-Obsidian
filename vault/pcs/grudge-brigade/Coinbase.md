# Ethereum "Coinbase" Thuringwador

## Summary
Ethereum "Coinbase" Thuringwador is an elven illusionist and the leader of the Grudge Brigade mercenary company. Often described by the party as an "annoying elf," Coinbase is a relentless hustler and fixer, constantly trying to upsell clients on his "illusion-based tactical assistance system" or brokering deals.

## History & Interactions
Coinbase has been a recurring hired spellcaster for the Right for Riches Company, stepping in when the party's own mages are unavailable.

His most significant non-combat interaction involved the Cult of Set. He received an anonymous contract offering a substantial reward for the death of the party members and Susarra. Rather than acting on it, Coinbase saw a better business opportunity and sold the information to Vael, providing the party with crucial intelligence about the threat against them ([[sessions/Session 23c - Set Jailbreak and Down to Goblintown.md]]).

## Roles & Influence
- **Leader of the Grudge Brigade:** As the head of the mercenary company, he manages contracts and deploys his subordinates.
- **Mercenary Illusionist:** Provides magical support for hire.
- **Information Broker:** His position gives him access to the undercurrents of Gosterwick's adventurer economy.

## Relationships
- Right for Riches Company: His primary clients
- Cult of Set: He was contracted by them to assassinate the party, but betrayed the contract for a better offer
