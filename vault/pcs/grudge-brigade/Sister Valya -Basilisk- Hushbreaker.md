# Sister Valya "Basilisk" Hushbreaker

## Summary
Mitra‑aligned sister nicknamed "Basilisk," encountered during delves around the Great Cavern and the Library of Thoth. A tiny halfling martial artist with a piercing high-pitched kiai shout.

## Role
Clerical support and expedition logistics. Provides martial arts combat support as a mercenary of the Grudge Brigade.
