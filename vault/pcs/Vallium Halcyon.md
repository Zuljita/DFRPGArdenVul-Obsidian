## Summary

Vallium Halcyon is a 25-year-old Archontean fighter and the primary combatant of the [[Right for Riches]] company, a founding member who has been present since the party's first descent into [[Arden Vul]]. He carries Broadsword-17 with Weapon Master and Extra Attack, making him the most dangerous melee threat in the group and the person everyone else forms up behind when a corridor needs clearing. His disadvantages are as load-bearing as his skills: Charitable, Chummy, Compulsive Generosity, and Responsible make him the party's moral compass, the one most likely to donate food to goblins, warn rival adventurers away from danger, or hand a key to a knight who needs it — sometimes to the quiet dismay of his treasure-focused companions. He has taken up alchemy in earnest and maintains a carefully stocked potion belt, though his IQ-based Alchemy-7 means his personal brew attempts are more aspirational than reliable.

## History

Vallium joined the party as an Archontean fighter in [[sessions/Session 2 - Halfling Rent-Seekers.md|Session 2]] and has appeared in every major engagement since. In the early sessions he established himself as the party's shield-wall anchor and door-breaker, carrying the group through encounters with dragons, beastmen, and baboons while the spellcasters recovered. Over time he accumulated one of the stranger collections in Arden Vul: a master keyring that has grown to hold dozens of keys from every faction the party has looted, including 47 Settite keys taken from the Forum of Set, keys to the Library of Thoth, the Tower of Scrutiny, and a pentagram room. In [[sessions/Session 43c - Looting the Cult of Set.md|Session 43c]], after the party's decisive alpha strike on the Cult of Set, he used his Settite key collection to crack two of the three locks on the Settites' vault — the third required Lockmaster.

Vallium personally led the push into the Forum of Set across multiple sessions, taking the front line through the prolonged assault in [[sessions/Session 43a - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set.md|Session 43a]] and [[sessions/Session 43b - Alpha Strike on the Cult of Set.md|Session 43b]], flying across the chasm to chase fleeing cultists and holding chokepoints while the rest of the party dealt with the response. He killed the behir south of the Goblin Forum in [[sessions/Session 52b and 53 - Behir, Varumani, and the Surgical Construct.md|Session 52b-53]] by trading stabs until it tried to retreat into its electrified mist — too late. He waded through the wraith swarm in the Tower of Scrutiny ([[sessions/Session 50 - The Iron Circlet of Ghanor.md|Session 50]]) using agility and strength potions along with the Affect Spirits spell to make them vulnerable, and spent the better part of a session clearing ghouls and ghasts in the Temple of Thoth area, where his heavy armor made him effectively immune to their paralysis. Against the Vengeance Aspect ([[sessions/Session 51 - The Vengeance Aspect.md|Session 51]]), he again served as the armored fulcrum around which the blind, fire-resistant party managed to survive a fight that included blinding, Symbol of Death, and a cascade of animated ghouls.

One of his more characteristic moments came in [[sessions/Session 49 - Demons and Mummies.md|Session 49]], when the party found a candle-lit room in the Halls. Rather than leave, Vallium stayed alone until the last candle guttered out to see what would happen. A blood-red tentacled demon appeared and gated in two more ape-like demons. Vallium's response: attack first. The party helped from outside and they prevailed. He wears three patches on his clothing — the Halfling-Killer, the Goblin Scepter, and the 11 Tankards — memorializing campaign milestones. He also wore Behir Light Leather after the behir kill, having a full suit crafted from its hide.

In [[sessions/Session 47 - The No Mana Zone.md|Session 47]], he and Ioannes met with Roger the Rapier's company, the [[Five Fingers of Destiny]], explained the Beacon's danger, and warned them to stay away — a notably generous act for a party protective of its base. He has also been present for major diplomatic successes, including the audience with [[Varboka]], Thegn of the Varumani, in [[sessions/Session 55 - Varboka the Varumani Thegn.md|Session 55]], where the party agreed to kill [[Kerbog Khan]] in exchange for one item of Vael's choice from Varboka's personal hoard (plus half of any Rudishva power supplies found on Khan). Both Vael and Vallium were reluctant to trade away the ancient plasteel Varumani passport in the deal, but ultimately declined to do so.

## Goals & Hooks

- Kill [[Kerbog Khan]]. The party has committed to this by formal deal with [[Varboka]], Thegn of the Varumani, who wants Khan dead for “unspeakable things” done to his sister Gevorda; in exchange, Vael gets one item from Varboka's personal hoard. The plan is to use Vael's Seeker spell on Asrotelion — a mage believed to have been sent after Kerbog Khan — to locate him.
- Advance alchemy skill. Vallium is actively trying to improve his Alchemy (currently level 7, IQ/VH) and carries a stocked potion belt. He has Fast-Draw (Potion) at 14 to make combat use reliable even if the brew process is not.
- Continue clearing paths through Varumani territory. The deal with Varboka opens new areas; Vallium was part of the group that wanted to preserve the ancient Varumani passport and remains invested in the relationship.
- Finish mapping and locking down the post-Settite Forum of Set region. The party cleared the Cult but left some unexplored side passages and at least one spiked-shut door they bypassed as potentially too dangerous.
- Find what the collection of magic keys actually unlocks. The Master Keyring holds Large Gold and Large Platinum Magic Keys, among others, whose corresponding locks have not yet been located.
- The Ring of Wish (One Wish) in his purse remains unspent. No one has yet determined the right use.

## Relationships

- [[Vael Sunshadow]]: Party mage and close companion since Session 2. Vael frequently casts Blink Other on Vallium to bypass entry obstacles at the Beacon, and cast Flight on Vallium in early sessions to move the party across terrain. They levitate together over chasms as a reliable pair while the clerics use Teleport Rugs. Vael and Vallium did not donate to the Church of Demma's anti-hunger drive in Session 55 while Ioannes did — a small data point on the difference in their sensibilities.
- [[Ioannes Grammatikos Byzantios]]: The party cleric of Demma and a long-running counterweight to Vallium's ethics. Ioannes regularly casts Bless on Vallium before delves, boosting his combat effectiveness. The two share Archontean background but diverge on piety; Ioannes is more formally religious while Vallium's Charitable and Responsible traits operate on personal rather than institutional grounds.
- [[Thrainor “Thronebreaker” Ironvein]]: Mercenary dwarf, later-joining party member. By Session 55 they are fighting as a matched pair — Vallium and Thronebreaker knocked out two ogres quickly together, with Vallium finishing the third after Thronebreaker routed him. The pairing works well because both are competent front-line fighters who don't need spells to function.
- [[Wicktrimmer]]: The Imperial Goblin merchant-banker of the [[Rarities Factor]] in Gosterwick. Wicktrimmer has handled the party's artifact brokerage, letters of credit, and shipping. He travels to Narsileon on their shopping requests and returned in Session 43 with Vallium catching a ride on the Palanquin of Thoth. Wicktrimmer has been away from Gosterwick often enough that his absences have been remarked upon by third parties.
- [[Gribble]]: Lord of the Goblin Forum. The party helped restart commerce in the Forum after driving out the Cult of Set; Gribble assigned them a room for their use. Vallium's early donation of boar meat to the goblins is consistent with his Compulsive Generosity. Gribble is a regular contact when the party passes through the Forum.
- [[Dworm]]: Guard to [[Galadnor]] the half-elven merchant, stationed at the Goblin Forum. Dworm was drafted into running the party's food stall when no proper merchant was available; he complained he was a fighter, not a merchant, but agreed. A minor but recurring presence in post-Settite Forum activity.
- [[Varboka]]: Varumani Thegn. Newly established relationship as of Session 55. The deal — kill Kerbog Khan in exchange for free passage through Varumani territory and ancillary benefits — is the party's primary current external commitment. Varboka's personal motivation (vengeance for his sister Gevorda) gives the mission an additional moral charge that is unlikely to sit lightly on Vallium.
- [[Kerbog Khan]]: The archmage the party has contracted to kill. Khan has been observed in the Goblin Market and his dragonfly constructs encountered multiple times — he can control or inhabit them. He conducts unauthorized medical experiments on varumani, is wanted by at least one Collegium faction, and demands blood pacts for any dealings.
- [[Five Fingers of Destiny]] / [[Roger the Rapier]]: Rival adventuring company. Vallium warned Roger's group away from the Beacon in Session 47, trading enough information to make the danger credible without fully disclosing their base. A truce rather than alliance.
