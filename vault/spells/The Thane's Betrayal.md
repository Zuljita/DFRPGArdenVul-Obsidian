# The Thane's Betrayal

## Definition

```
The Thane's Betrayal
Movement
Regular; R-ST or Will
Duration: 6s
Cost: 5.  Same cost to maintain.
Prereq: Magery 3, Apportation, 1 spell from each of 10 colleges
Take control of an enemy's weapon.  If resistance fails, the weapon leaves the enemy's hands and attacks that enemy until the spell duration ends or the enemy is destroyed.  The caster may take a Concentrate maneuver to pick a new target.  See Dancing Weapon for other details.
```
