# Pelagion's Bridge of Sighs

## Definition

```
Pelagion's Bridge of Sighs
Air
Area
Casting Time: 3s
Duration: 1 minute
Cost: 1 to cast or maintain
Prereq: Walk on Air
The caster exhales a bridge of air, which the caster and others can walk on.  The caster must breathe precisely (Concentrate maneuvers) as long as the bridge exists, but can move at walking speed while doing so.
```
