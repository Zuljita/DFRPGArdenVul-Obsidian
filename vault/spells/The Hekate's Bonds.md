# The Hekate's Bonds

- **College:** Body Control
- **Type:** Regular; Resisted by HT
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Cost:** 8 (Cannot be maintained)
- **Prerequisite:** Total Paralysis

Causes paralysis. (See DFE p. 66).
