# Nighteyes of Bastet

## Definition

```
Nighteyes of Bastet
Light and Darkness
Regular
Duration: Permanent
Cost: 6
Prereq: Power Investiture 2
Makes a small object (or a small part of a large object) glow permanently. Does not count as a spell on.  This is not an enchantment, so it can be dispelled, is destroyed by no mana zones, etc.  The light is faint moonlight; it gives a -5 darkness penalty over a 6-yard radius.
```
