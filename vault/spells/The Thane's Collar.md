# The Thane's Collar

## Definition

```
The Thane's Collar
Mind Control
Regular; R-Will
Duration: 1 month
Cost: 20.  Same cost to maintain.
Casting Time: 10 minutes
Prereq: Magery 6, Charm 
This is an enhanced version of the Charm spell, with a much longer duration. It requires that the target wear an iron collar as a focus.
```
