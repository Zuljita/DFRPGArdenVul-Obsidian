# The Thane's Vengeful Veil

## Definition

```
The Thane's Vengeful Veil
Body Control
Regular; R-HT (special)
Duration: 1 minute
Cost: 5.  Same cost to maintain.
Prereq: Deathtouch
The caster creates a shimmering field of off-white energy around their body.  Any attacker who injures the caster must R-HT.  If they  succeed, they take half the injury they did to the caster.  If they fail, they take the full injury they did to the caster.  Armor does not protect.  (Note that the caster still takes the injury.)
```
