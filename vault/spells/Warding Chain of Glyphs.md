# Warding Chain of Glyphs

## Definition

```
Warding Chain of Glyphs
Fire
Area
Cost: 5 per glyph times Area
Duration: Permanent until discharged (does not count as a spell on)
Casting Time: 10 minutes per glyph
Prereq: Clerical PI 5
The caster inscribes up to PI glyphs on a surface.  The glyphs become invisible after casting.  Anyone touching the protected surface takes 1d burning damage per glyph.  Each time the spell is triggered, one glyph is removed.
```
