# Great Vampire Lords and their Demise
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Usuon of Agoreon. 7 great vampire lords who were slain.  Apparently it's all about resisting their charm. The main thing Vael gains from this is that Vampires are good at Charm.  Apparently if you think a vampire is trying to Charm you, inflicting serious pain on yourself (such as biting your tongue hard enough to make it bleed) can make it easier to resist.
