*Book*

## Summary
A work in Archontean authored by Helena of Archontos. The accounts are for one month for the Arden Vul chapter of the Cult of Set.  It has the usual accounting stuff, plus gross Set stuff like slaves and sacrificial tools.  One weird part is that something converts 2 slaves to 6 aquamarines.  You're not sure how that worked.
