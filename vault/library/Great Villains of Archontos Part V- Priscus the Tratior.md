# Great Villains of Archontos, Part V: Priscus the Traitor
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Archontean authored by Yeng of Narsileon. Priscus was a foul sorceror who betrayed the Emperor's trust.  He had a hunchback, warts, bad breath, and a morbid fascination with death.  He used evil magic to turn noble Archonteans against each other.  He was aided by a wretched crone named Isodora.  The book ends with an epic battle in Arden Vul where Priscus is slain and Isadora disappears in a puff of smoke.
