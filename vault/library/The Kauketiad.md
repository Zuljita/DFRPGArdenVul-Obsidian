# The Kauketiad
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Anonymous. This is a surprisingly good epic poem about the wars between the Archonteans and the heqeti.  It does not focus on leaders, but rather on the fidelity (symbolized by a ring), protection (armor), vengeance (sword), and nobility (heart) of the Archontean soldiers.  The climax was conquering the underground Ziggurat of Kauket, which was allegedly north and east from the bottom of the chasm.
