# The Nine Hells
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Ixion of Narsilion. A catalogue with descriptions and summoning notes. The book smells like sulfur.  It's bound in what appears to be human skin.  The writing is very dense and it takes days to get through it.  It talks about different kinds of devils and what part of hell they live in and their personalities and ambitions.  You wonder how the author actually learned all this, or if he's making stuff up.
