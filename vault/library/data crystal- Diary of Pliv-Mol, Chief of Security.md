# Diary of Pliv-Mol, Chief of Security
*Data crystal*

## Summary
A work in Rudishva authored by Pliv-Mol. 4 hours long.  It starts after the Beacon crashed.  Pliv-Mol, the Rudishva Chief of Security, talks about how the harmonious work ethic of the star-born hegemony will lead to repairing the Beacon.  Later it appears that won't happen but he's optimistic the engineers can fix the interstellar comms and call for help.  Later that appears not to be working but they have settled in to take over the Halls of Arden Vul and continue their society while attempting to re-invent space flight.  They are also breeding additional varuda and varumani!  And something about using arcanum as fuel.  Over time the entries become shorter and less optimistic.  Toward the end, he suspects Liss-Tak, the kaliyani leader, of plotting treason.  He says it might be necessary to quarantine the kaliyani and he has begun planting mines through the kaliyani area east of the midnight road.  He says the mines are on a dead-man's switch and will go off if he doesn't say the password daily.  That is the last entry.
