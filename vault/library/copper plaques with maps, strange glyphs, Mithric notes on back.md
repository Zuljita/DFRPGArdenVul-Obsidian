# Copper Plaques
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Rudishva/Mithric authored by Anonymous. 1st plaque - 'Power' and 'Flight'; notes on the back in Mithric "Unknown location.  Possibly near administrative center and shrines.  Likely a powerful weapon." 2nd plaque - 'Dwelling' and 'Peaceful'; notes in Mithric on the back.  "Some kind of dwelling?  The Scriptor claims to know its location, as his workmen found it.  But the workmen is devoured and the Scriptor is not talking." 3rd plaque - 'Armory' and 'Mechanical Voice'; notes in Mithric on the back.  "Unknown location.  Reference to a voice is undoubtably a mistake.".
