# The Betrayal
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Rudishva. It claims that a Kaliyani named Lisstak got bored with peace after the victory, and twisted the minds of the Kaliyani against the Rudishva.  Captain Leil-Jor got mad and most of the Kaliyani left and dealt with the "old enemy" for protection.  Finally "the sickness" arrived through the actions of Lisstak.
