# A True Account of the Embassy of Gillethorn Ellagelsson to Archontos
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Elvish authored by Belladia Varsdottir. Gillethorn, son of Lady Ellagel the elven ruler, was sent to Archontos to try to mediate the aftermath of the civil war between Sortians and Theosophs.  He ended up being murdered on the street by a racist mob.  Since this happened, elves have not been seen much in Archontos.
