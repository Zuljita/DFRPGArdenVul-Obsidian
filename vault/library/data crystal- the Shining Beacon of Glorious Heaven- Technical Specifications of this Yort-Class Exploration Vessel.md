# The Shining Beacon of Glorious Heaven: Technical Specifications of this Yort-Class Exploration Vessel
*Data crystal*

## Summary
A work in Rudishva. It is a six hours long.  It consists of interviews, in Rudishva, with several crew members.  (Mostly Rudishva, one Kaliyani, one Varuda, one Varumani.)  They all speak in Rudishva about various aspects of the Beacon such as life support, weapons, communication, medical, astrogation, and shuttles.  Akla-Chah says she can translate any words you don't know, or you can use the bridge comm station's live translation capabilities to translate into over 100 languages.  (She notes that its support for languages like Archontean and Elvish probably isn't great yet, but will improve if you converse in those languages on the bridge.).
