# On the Location of Priscus Pulcher
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Alexandros. Seventeen (17) possible locations for Priscus Pulcher's remains.  He argues for and against each of them.  Possible locations include The Cloud Castle of Earl Reginald, a Charnel Pit on the Outskirts of Arden Vul, the Tower of Scrutiny, and The Villa of Lady Isodora.  He seems to think the latter is most likely.
