# On the Terrible Old Gods
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Sarquin of Archontos. It's about 7 ancient deities who ruled before time and were way more powerful than modern gods.  They were Rimmaq-Isfet, Frelothian, Mzingaloq, Viirdo Star-Eater, Piqor-Piqaad, Jeqellathon, and Nzimballik. The book spends the most time on Rimmaq-Isfet, whose titles included the Devourer, the Worm who Licks the Heart of the Earth, the Extruder of Potent Slimes, the Fecund Sire, and the Corroder.  He was described as a huge worm or a huge salamander or a huge worm-headed biped.
