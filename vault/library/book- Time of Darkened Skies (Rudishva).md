# Time of Darkened Skies
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Rudishva. This book is about the Rudishva/Varumani/Varuda/Kaliyani defeating the Heqeti Hopping Ones.  Tactics include fire / blasting.  Major events include seizing the Obsidian Gates and Hall of Pillars, destroying the heqeti Upper Temple, killing the hoppers in the Chasm, the varuda being defeated in the Cave of Tears, and the final assault on the Heqeti Ziggurat leading to most of the heqeti being killed and the rest fleeing further into the underworld.
