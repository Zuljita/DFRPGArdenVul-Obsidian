# Notes of the Rudishva Spirits
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Two Archontean scholars. A bunch of stuff about conversations with two Rudishva Spirits, the illuision in the Beacon and another they spoke to remotely but never saw.  They think these might be the Rudishva gods. Notes on the Rudishva Spirits was written by two Archontean scholars who investigated the Beacon.  They talked to two spirits.  One had an illusory form of a tall thin demon wearing robes.  The other was invisible, just a voice.  They concluded that these spirits were the gods of the Rudishva.  However, they did not appear to grant any spells, and seemed to aid rather than command their subjects, which was a bit confusing for gods.  Also, the spirits knew a lot about Rudishva technology and the layout of parts of the Halls, but nothing about some other subjects, so either they were not omniscient or they were not perfectly helpful. Akla-Chah remembers those scholars.  She says they were arrogant know-it-alls who didn't listen.  She tried to cooperate with the various ancient Archonteans for a while, then mostly blew them off, as they mostly wanted to use her for their own schemes against each other rather than achieve anything useful. None of them had the Tablet of the Beacon so she didn't have to do what they said so she didn't.
