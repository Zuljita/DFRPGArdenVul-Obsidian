# Secreta Secretorum
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Anonymous. A book full of secrets. 7 alleged Secret Names of Thoth, some gossip about ancient misdeeds of the Ligareon and Vasildon clans, some evidence that the Silent Factor was planned as a front for the Imperial Private House, and a map of some underground rooms.
