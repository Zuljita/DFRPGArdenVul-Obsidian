# Symbols of Power, or the Archonal Regalia of Arden Vul
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Beriacius Solus. Describes a bunch of formal ceremonies, what archons and other participants wore, gestures, calls and responses, etc.  It has pictures of the Tablet of the Beacon, Iron Circlet of Ghanor, Ebon Spear, and Bone Ring of Jagri-Naz.
