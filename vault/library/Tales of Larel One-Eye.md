# Tales of Larel One-Eye
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Anonymous. Larel tricks a farmer's wife into feeding hungry farm hands.  Larel pulls a chicken out of his sack to distract wolves.  Larel hides behind his cloak from an angry husband.  Larel visits the tower of the wizard Tilgo and leaves with Tilgo's spellbook and daughter.  Larel is captured by bugbears, gets them arguing about how to cook him, waits for them to kill each other, then takes their stuff.
