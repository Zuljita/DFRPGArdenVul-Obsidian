# Kel Bormol's Chess Diary
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Kel Bormol. Kel Bormol's chess diary has a lot of chess games.  The last few were against Hithra Paleologos, who apparently relied too much on his High Priests.  You don't see any of the positions from the walls in the book.  Vallium learns a bit more chess, and understands that the symbols on the walls in that room represent chess positions and chess pieces, but not the significance of the exact boards.  Vallium brings his collection of chess diagrams to several different taverns, but does not run into any chess experts there.
