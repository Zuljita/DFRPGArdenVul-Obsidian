# The Ancient Ones
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Laelius the Praetor. The Ancient Ones describes the Rudishva in general and talks about a few survivors: Crallicarus, Salorikus, Ravatorus, Melacorius, Isocrates, and Nacalorus.
