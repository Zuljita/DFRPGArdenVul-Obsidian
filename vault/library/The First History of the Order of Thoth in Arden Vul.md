# The First History of the Order of Thoth in Arden Vul
*Book*

## Summary
A work in Mithric authored by Unknown priest of Thoth. He describes the layout of the Thothian parts of the upper levels of the Halls.  He also talks about "the survivors", strange tall horned beings stranded on Magae after the crash of their boat (of some strange kind), the Beacon.  Apparently there used to be more of them, but after a horrible cataclysm there are only a few left, including Salorikus and Isocrates.
