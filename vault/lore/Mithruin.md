# Mithruin

## Summary
The home island of the old [[factions/Archontean Empire.md|Archontean Empire]], centered in the ocean.

## Notes
- 1200 years ago, after a magical civil war, the survivors of the [[factions/Archontean Empire.md|Archontean Empire]] withdrew to [[Mithruin]] to fight over the home island.
- The capital city, [[Archontos]], is located on this island.
