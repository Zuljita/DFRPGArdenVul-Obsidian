# Varumani

## Who Are the Varumani?

The Varumani are not trolls. They share superficial traits with what fantasy traditions call trolls — large, physically powerful, highly intelligent, capable of regeneration (negated by fire or acid) — but they are wholly distinct from those monsters; their true origin lies with the spacefaring [[factions/Rudishva.md|Rudishva]] (see Origin and Nature below).

The word "troll" is the Archontean Common translation of the Mithric word *varumani*, as confirmed by a copper slate found in the Halls: *"varumani (in Mithric) — that is, troll (in Archontean)."* This translation reflects Archontean ignorance, not Varumani identity. The Varumani consider the word "troll" derogatory.

> *"The trolls preferred to be called varumani."* — Session 26

## Origin and Nature

Per [[npcs/Akla-Chah.md|Akla-Chah]], the Varumani are **Rudishva constructs** — beings engineered by the [[factions/Rudishva.md|Rudishva]] for security and labor, rather than a naturally evolved species. They were produced in three primary models via "matrix cards" in specialized pits:

- **Model A** — creative and loyal.
- **Model D** — aggressive and obedient.
- **Model G** — independent.

This reframes their "otherworldly" character: it derives from their Rudishva makers — a spacefaring people stranded on this world — not from a separate Varumani homeworld. ([[notes/Discord Summary 2026-W23.md|Discord Summary 2026-W23]])

## Language

*Varumani* is also a spoken language, distinct from the written Rudishva script. [[pcs/Vaelethron 'Vael' Sunshadow.md|Vael]] has been studying spoken Varumani under [[npcs/Akla-Chah.md|Akla-Chah]], working toward "Accented" proficiency. ([[notes/Discord Summary 2026-W18.md|Discord Summary 2026-W18]], [[notes/Discord Summary 2026-W22.md|Discord Summary 2026-W22]])

## Why Historical Texts Say "Troll"

Historical sources — the Book of Priors, Archontean military records, lore inscriptions — consistently use "troll" because Archontean authors did not distinguish between the Varumani and generic trolls. Phrases like *"Wars against the Varumani (trolls)"* or the location name "Troll Thegn" reflect the outsider label, not Varumani self-description. A recovered Archontean text, *On the Wars of the Old Ones with the Trolls*, is one such tactical history of the campaigns against the Varumani. The party now knows better. ([[notes/Discord Summary 2026-W18.md|Discord Summary 2026-W18]])
