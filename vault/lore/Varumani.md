# Varumani

## Who Are the Varumani?

The Varumani are a spacefaring people, not trolls. They share superficial traits with what fantasy traditions call trolls — large, physically powerful, highly intelligent, capable of regeneration (negated by fire or acid) — but they are a wholly distinct species with their own history, culture, and origin beyond this world.

The word "troll" is the Archontean Common translation of the Mithric word *varumani*, as confirmed by a copper slate found in the Halls: *"varumani (in Mithric) — that is, troll (in Archontean)."* This translation reflects Archontean ignorance, not Varumani identity. The Varumani consider the word "troll" derogatory.

> *"The trolls preferred to be called varumani."* — Session 26

## Why Historical Texts Say "Troll"

Historical sources — the Book of Priors, Archontean military records, lore inscriptions — consistently use "troll" because Archontean authors did not distinguish between the Varumani and generic trolls. Phrases like *"Wars against the Varumani (trolls)"* or the location name "Troll Thegn" reflect the outsider label, not Varumani self-description. The party now knows better.
