# The Archontean Calendar

## Summary
The **Archontean Imperial Calendar**, also called the **Ennian Calendar**, is the primary timekeeping system in the eastern region of [[lore/Magae.md|Magae]]. Its epoch is the signing of the [[Compact of Ennius]].

## Key Concepts
- **Epoch:** The calendar dates from the Compact of Ennius. Years are denoted as *sine pacti* (SP, before the compact) or *ab Ennii pacto* (AEP, after the compact).
- **Structure:** The year has 372 days, divided into 12 months of 30 days each, with two six-day intercalary periods.
- **Weeks:** A week consists of 7 days.

## Intercalary Periods
- **Mercedonian Days** — "Days of wages." A six-day intercalary period between Fidios (Autumn/December) and Molivios (Winter/January); follows New Year in some reckonings.
- **Voluptarian Days** — "Days of pleasure." A six-day intercalary period between the 8th and 9th months (between Gerakios and Besemios); associated with festivals.
