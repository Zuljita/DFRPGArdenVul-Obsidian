**Concentrate Maneuver**

A Concentrate maneuver allows moving any number of targets with Apportation or Levitation, Shape spells, reshaping Illusions, Tickling people, or other spell effects requiring concentration, plus casting *one spell*, which is at -3 per spell being actively concentrated on, and -1 per other spell on (DFS11).

Additional rolls may be required to properly control spells when multitasking this way.

**Levitation**

*On self*
Without concentration, caster can move a flat 3 hexes each turn in any direction, not subject to encumbrance (which is factored into energy cost), whatever Maneuver they’re using, unless stunned.  You don’t have Move of 3 — the spell is moving you: there is no step, no sprinting bonus, no extra cost to move backwards, no reduction from low HP/FP, and it does not provide a means of Retreating (but you could retreat “manually” if standing in something)

*On others*
Zero movement unless caster takes a Concentrate maneuver (see above).

**Flight**
Provides an *actual* Move of 10, -2 per level of encumbrance; no concentration is involved.  You use this Move normally, just in 3D: steps, facing  changes, sprint bonus, turning radius, extra cost backwards, halve rounded down on low HP/FP, halve rounded up on AOA, can retreat normally, etc.

Neither spell benefits from legs.

"Levitation is a neutral buoyancy spell.  If the subject is not the caster and is within reach of a floor / wall / ceiling they can use it to move themselves, carefully.  If a caster wants to use it offensively to hang someone in the air, they need to Concentrate for long enough to move that person out of reach of anything they could use to move themselves.
If the subject is the caster then they can freely switch back and forth between Levitating (any direction, but only move 3) and other movement (possibly faster).  If the subject is not the caster and is moving under their own power they need to be careful to avoid getting stuck in the air, just like someone moving in zero-G."

*(Q. What happens when 150lbs. Jane is Levitating (max 160 lbs.) and picks up a 30 lbs. backpack?  Does she free fall/land until she drops it?  Breaks the spell?)*
"It drags her down. Slower than a freefall since the spell is still pulling up 5/6 of the weight."
