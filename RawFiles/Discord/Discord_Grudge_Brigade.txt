Once upon a time, there was another adventuring party in the region, but they broke apart because the adventurers couldn’t stand each other.

They became mercenaries, offering their services to supplement other adventuring parties, but they rivalry had them undercutting each other on price to a point where nobody was making any money.

So they unionized. “Collectively Bargained, Individually Belligerent”
