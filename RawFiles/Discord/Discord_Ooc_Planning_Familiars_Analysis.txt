Archon Shiva — 7/13/25, 4:43 PM
Some general pointers on common abilities:

**Flight (controlled gliding)** 
0 FP flight (and some protection from pits and falls) is really good for *anyone*.  It combines with any tool that lets you cheaply gain altitude (Levitation, many Fire or Air spells) to offer excellent 3D movement outside combat.

**Do not** add Enhanced Move (flight or ground), Super Jump, or Super Climbing, they’re just not worth it long term.  ||Super Climbing barely works if you have the Martial Artist’s Spider Climb and no means to fly)||

**Perception**
Stuff that makes you able to perceive foes better in combat is huge, especially if your familiar isn’t wizardly in origin.

Dark Vision is the gold standard for seeing in darkness, with infravision is a decent cheaper alternative. If considering others (Sonar, Ultravision, etc.), make sure you read descriptions carefully to know exactly what they cover.

Night Vision and Telescopic Vision are basically irrelevant.

**”Thief” Stuff**
Flexibility gives very decent bonuses for [3].  A grappled caster is in very deep trouble.
Silence is almost always too expensive at basically [3] per +1 Stealth. 


**Fighter Stuff**
There are a lot of abilities that are great for fighters but not nearly worth the cp for casters.  Hybrid clerics can sometimes get good use out of those.

Archon Shiva — 7/13/25, 4:52 PM
__Familiar Power Source__
If you have access to more than one power source (wizard, cleric, druid) consider getting a familiar for your *worst* source: you’ll keep your familiar powers when your best tools snuff out!

Most of the time, the only downside is that you get the wrong type of ER for your best spells, but this can matter, such as the 50 FP threshold for Bless +2.

Archon Shiva — 7/13/25, 4:56 PM

Archon Shiva — 7/13/25, 5:19 PM
__Specific Familiars__
I will only discuss what I perceive as good options, but may occasionally mention something I see as a trap.

**Bat**
This is probably the best all-around familiar if you don’t know what to pick, but it’s *especially* perfect for clerics willing to embrace it.

Flight: Yay!
Sonar: lets you see invisible foes within 20 yards, and may help with stuff your eyes might miss, such as secret doors.

*Optionals*
Dark Vision: Yay!
Vampiric Bite: This lets you heal, sure, but it also lets you top-up FP from fallen foes in a matter of seconds.
**The cleric’s Loophole**  There is no repeated casting penalty for *Share Vitality*, so clerics can use Vampiric Bite to heal repeatedly, and just top-up your HP off the corpses of your fallen foes.

Archon Shiva — 7/13/25, 5:28 PM
**Cat**
A great bundle of discounts, but the big issue is that it offers very little *exceptional*.  You only get one familiar.  Note that a lot of it is much better if you have *low* dexterity (and possibly high encumbrance).

*Optionals*
Vibration Sense: Kinda like Sonar if every aspect was much worse.  Difficult rolls, vague results, frequent penalties, bad synergy, etc.  I’d recommend taking it, but not taking cat *for it*.

Archon Shiva — 7/13/25, 5:38 PM
**Chimp**
By far the most *versatile* choice.  I think half the Grudge Brigade, being weird-ass hybrid builds, will end up with chimps or monkeys — but very little here for the average caster.  DM me if you think this is for you, avoid otherwise.

*Optionals*
Note that while Arm ST works quite well for combat, Arm DX *specifically* doesn’t.

*Combat*
This familiar is also the most credible *combat* threat, sporting a ST of 11 and actual melee skill.  Give it a staff, flail or crossbow and you have an active participant.

**Macaw**
If you want to do *weird stuff*, there are plenty of tools here.  Most casters won’t care.

Mimicry and Photographic Hearing will let you imitate all the sounds and voices you hear.  Best A Capella bard ever.

*Optionals*
Discriminatory Hearing is a great deal at [9], as the very useful +4 hearing normally costs [8];
That silly tongue can’t melee attack, but you *can* wield a crossbow in each hand and pull the triggers with your tongue, or fast-draw, uncap and drink potions without dropping your staff;
Flight is Flight;
Smooth Operator is expensive, and may or may not help after showing off your tongue gymnastics.
