Lex Societatis Aureae

Let it be known throughout the lands of the Archontean world and beyond, that the undersigned, by oath and coin, do constitute the lawful and sovereign adventuring compact known as the Right for Riches Company. Let none mistake the name for charity, nor its bearers for saints.

**Article I – De Societate Constituta
(On the Formation of the Company)**

The Company is a lawful fellowship of no more than five sworn Members, known henceforth as Socii.

Each Member shall have their name inscribed upon the Charter, signed in ink or blood.

This number is sacred and may not be exceeded. Only upon death, disappearance, or retirement may a new Member be admitted.

Admission of a new Member requires a unanimous vote of all surviving Socii, a formal swearing of the Oath, and acceptance of all debts and legacies attached to the vacated seat.

Zuljita — 4/19/25, 10:10 PM
**Article II – De Sortitione Thesauri
(On the Division of Spoils)**

All treasure, relics, maps, documents, and coin recovered during a sanctioned Company expedition shall enter the Company Reckoning.

The Quartermaster, also known as the Loot Czar, shall:

Catalog all assets

Propose their division

Seek majority consent from attending Members

Spoils shall be divided as follows:

One full share to each Member who participated in the expedition

No share for Members who were absent

Negotiated shares for Temporary Members, per prior agreement

Strategic or unclaimed items may be declared Company Property by vote and held in trust.

**Article III – De Bonis Communibus
(On Company Property)**

The following are deemed Res Societatis (Property of the Company):

Magical implements, maps, documents, or gear used by multiple Members

Items purchased from the Company treasury

Constructs, vehicles, or dwellings tied to the Company name

Such items may be used by Members in good standing but must be returned upon demand.

The Quartermaster shall maintain an inventory of all such items.

**Article IV – De Recessu et Morte
(On Retirement and Death)**

A Member may retire with honor by:

Declaring intent at least one expedition in advance

Gaining unanimous approval

Returning Company property

The retiring Member receives:

One equal share of all current liquid assets

Ten percent of future passive income for a period of three years or until death

Should a Member perish or be unrecoverable:

Their assets revert to the Company

A successor may be selected per Article I

Resurrection may be attempted, but is not required. If the Company declines or fails to revive a fallen Member, their seat is considered vacated.

**Article V – De Succursu Necessario
(On Rescue and Recovery)**

A Member lost, captured, or imperiled shall be considered for rescue by majority vote.

If a rescue is mounted:

No penalty is assessed for loyal Members

Members whose misfortune stems from folly or betrayal may be fined up to half a share as deemed fit

No rescue is to be attempted if the Member has clearly turned traitor or joined the enemy.

**Article VI – De Sociis Temporariis
(On Temporary Members)**

Non-Members (mercenaries, guides, summoned entities) may accompany the Company as Temporary Members.

They shall:

Receive a negotiated share based on terms agreed before departure

Obey Company discipline and carry out assigned duties

Have no seat, vote, or claim on the Charter

Temporary Members may be dismissed at any time by majority vote, or by accident, enemy action, or poor luck.

**Article VII – De Officiis Societatis
(On Company Offices)**

To preserve order and assign blame, the following Offices may be appointed:

Quartermaster / Loot Czar (Mandatory)

Tallies all loot

Proposes divisions of treasure

Maintains the inventory and Company ledger

Archivist (Optional)

Maintains maps, records, expedition logs, and magical research

Custodian of the Charter and copies thereof

Warden of Oaths (Optional)

Oversees swearing-in of new Members

Judges internal disputes, cowardice, or dishonor

Speaker (Optional)

Represents the Company to outsiders

Authorized to negotiate contracts, treaties, and bar tabs

Offices are assigned or revoked by majority vote. One Member may hold multiple Offices. Dereliction of duty may result in fine, ridicule, or reassignment to goat-wrangling.

**Article VIII – De Iure et Sigillo
(On Law and the Seal)**

The Charter shall be kept in a protected vault, chest, or extradimensional space. Tampering with the Charter shall result in death or worse.

All lawful documents issued by the Company shall bear its seal: a gauntlet grasping a coin, encircled by chains.

Amendments to this Charter require a two-thirds vote of the current Membership and must be read aloud in a tavern before they are binding.

Thus bound, we pledge sword, spell, and spirit to the Company and each other. Riches await. Fortune favors the clever. Beware those who read the fine print.
