Archon Shiva — 8/10/25, 1:17 PM
There are abilities that the party needs all the time.  Diverting cp to lower attendance members makes it harder to get those abilities on people who will reliably be around.

The PC with higher cp total *brings more to the table*, and the player’s continuity of awareness is a huge boon.  Vallium’s “Hey, I have that book!” last session was a big help to gauging how to approach a problem.   Occasionally giving these people more money simply to everyone can meet threshold feels justified.  Do not punish players for showing up.

If the *total cp amount across the party* goes up, it’s worth unbalancing loot, especially with a “we’ll get you next time” approach.

Greybrown — 8/10/25, 2:03 PM
CP going up for anyone is good for the party as a whole, and money lacks scarcity in comparison, so I think it's usually a good idea to tweak the numbers so that an extra CP is squeaked in, even if there's a disparity in $.

As for a choice between awarding an available XP between a higher vs a lower CP player, I don't think that should come into the decision making process.

Player A needs one more CP to buy an attribute?  Give it to them.  Player B had given up the potential extra CP the last three times?  Give it to them.  Player C doesn't really need it, but in exchange they'll spend it on a utility spell or skill that will benefit the party?  Give it to them.
