# Campaign Context

This file gives automation enough table and setting context to avoid confusing in-world facts with out-of-world play logistics.

## Source Types

- Blogspot session recaps are canonical GM-authored session summaries.
- Weekly Discord summaries are canonical private-table digests generated outside this repository.
- Entity articles in `vault/npcs`, `vault/pcs`, `vault/locations`, `vault/factions`, and `vault/items` are promoted vault articles, not raw sources.

## Table Roles

- The GM is an out-of-world narrator and rules authority. GM comments, rulings, and blog framing are not in-world character speech unless the recap says an NPC or PC said them.
- Players are out-of-world people controlling player characters. Parenthetical names after player characters usually identify the player, not an in-world alias.
- Player characters are in-world characters. Treat their character names, titles, species, professions, patrons, and actions as in-world material.
- Mercenaries, hirelings, allies, and enemies may be controlled tactically by players but are still in-world entities.

## Verification Guidance

- Do not verify claims from isolated sentences when the surrounding scene determines meaning.
- Use a source window large enough to include the section heading, nearby list context, and several neighboring bullets or paragraphs.
- For entity links, confirm that the mention refers to the target entity in the scene, not merely that the words match.
- For claims, preserve the source's uncertainty. If the source says "believed", "rumored", "tentatively identified", or "might", the promoted claim must keep that uncertainty.
